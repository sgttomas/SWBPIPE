# F1 modal references: mass model and class (a) discrete eigen references

Frozen, independently derived expectations for DEL-04-09 (F1 modal analysis):
the mass model of
[Design §2](../../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-09/Work/modal-analysis.md)
(AC-001), and the class (a) formulation-matched discrete eigen references of
Design §4.1 (AC-003). No product code exists yet that consumes them.

**Status: references with a refutation record.** They were derived and
cross-checked by their author TASK, and a second TASK, reading no product code,
rebuilt all 11 mass cases and all 26 eigen cases by its own routes without
refuting any value ([REFUTATION.md](REFUTATION.md)). No product output has
been compared with them.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs
no product code (nothing under `core/`, `apps/`, `api/`, `tests/`, `schemas/` or
`fixtures/`). It reads no file except the ones it writes. All model data are
invented. No equation, value or example is taken from the
`domains/piping-design/` corpus (DEC-043). The methods are textbook methods,
cited below and restated here, not copied. No weight, centre of gravity,
stiffness scale or acceleration is a catalog or code value. The acceleration
9.8125 m/s² is an invented request input, not a physical constant. Standard
claim fence applies (claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

## Neutral input form

Each case carries its `model`, verbatim:

- `nodes`: `{id, xyz}` in m.
- `materials`: E and G in Pa, and metal density in kg/m³. PF takes G
  directly; ν is not used.
- `sections`: OD, wall t, mill tolerance c_m and corrosion allowance c_a, all
  absolute lengths in m. Also the contents density (null means empty), and
  the insulation thickness and density (both present or both null).
- `members`:
  - `straight` (i, j, section, material, y_reference);
  - `arc` (i, j, `radius_m`, `y_reference` = the bow side, `flexibility_factor`);
  - `connector` and `tie_rod`, which are massless here.
- `components`: kind, node, `maps_member`, `solver_consumption`, `weight_N`,
  `center_of_gravity_m` (global X/Y/Z from the component node, per the R10
  convention), and `stiffness_scaling`.
- `supports`: restrained DOFs by name.
- `constant_effort_supports`: massless and with no stiffness.
- `springs`: node, global DOF and stiffness, in N/m or N·m/rad.
- `weight_to_mass_acceleration_m_per_s2`: a, or null.

Every input number is a binary64 value written exactly in decimal (dyadic
fractions and integers). The generator asserts this and uses the exact
rational value.

DOFs are `ux, uy, uz, rx, ry, rz` per node, in node order. Rotations are
right-handed small-rotation vectors.

## Number formats

- `exact`: a rational, or a + b·π. Used where the value lies in ℚ + ℚπ: the
  straight-member and component matrices, and the category totals without
  arcs.
- `decimal`: 40 significant digits for masses, 36 for eigenvalues and
  frequencies, 25 for mode shapes and participation quantities.
- `value`: the nearest binary64.

The working precision is 80 digits. A shape or participation value whose
magnitude is below 1e-50 is printed as `0`. These are zero in exact arithmetic
to the generator's resolution, by symmetry or decoupling. Arc quantities are
decimal only, because they involve φ and its trigonometric functions.

## Premises the product comparison must match

Each premise below is either stated in the design text, as cited, or assumed
here because the design text does not state it. Assumed premises are marked
**(assumed)**. A product that differs on an assumed premise is not wrong
merely for that reason. The difference goes to the design owner.

### Mass bases

These are restated from DEL-05-01 and DEL-05-03.

- **PF** (`source_od_effective_wall_areas/v1`): metal and contents are both on
  the wall t − c_m. Metal is ρ_m·π/4·(OD² − ID²) and contents ρ_c·π/4·ID²,
  with ID = OD − 2(t − c_m).
- **EX** (`source_od_nominal_wall_pressure_bore_areas/v1`): metal is on the
  nominal wall t. Contents are on the pressure bore OD − 2t_r, with
  t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a. The EX stiffness section (A, I, J) is on the nominal
  wall t (Design §3.4, the D-5 basis); no eigen case uses the EX basis.
- **Insulation** is the annulus from OD to OD + 2t_ins on both bases.
- m̄ is the sum of metal, contents and insulation. Contents are in all three
  translations (D-e).

### Rotary inertia per length

- j̄ = ρ_m·J_metal + ρ_ins·J_ins, with J = π(D_o⁴ − D_i⁴)/32 for an annulus.
  ī = j̄/2. Contents are excluded (Design §2.2).
- J_metal uses the metal annulus of the same basis: t − c_m on PF and t on
  EX (Design §2.2).

### Straight members

The element matrices follow Design §2.2:

- Hermitian consistent translation mass;
- linear axial and torsional mass;
- Rayleigh rotary inertia ī/(30L)·[…] in both planes.

**Bending-plane sign map (Design §2.2).** In member axes, with x along the
member, the transverse plane matrices act on [u_y, θ_z] in the x–y plane and
on [u_z, −θ_y] in the x–z plane. The x–z blocks therefore change sign in
their translation–rotation entries, for the consistent mass and the
rotary-inertia matrix alike.

The matrices here use this map. `elements.py` forms them frame-free from the
unit axis e, with transverse displacement d and slope r = −[e]×θ. For a
member along x that is r = (θ_z, −θ_y), which is exactly the map.
`bending_plane_sign_map` in `mass_references.json` records an exact check: for
a member along +x, mass and stiffness equal the plane matrices placed
explicitly on [u_y, θ_z] and [u_z, −θ_y]. For example, M[u_y,i][θ_z,i] has
consistent-mass part +22·m̄L²/420, and M[u_z,i][θ_y,i] has −22·m̄L²/420.

The section is circular, so the global matrices do not depend on the
member's y/z orientation.

### Stiffness

The stiffness of the eigen cases is the PF linear static K:

- the Euler–Bernoulli 12×12 with A, I and J = 2I on the wall t − c_m;
- a rigid-family body scaled to s·E and s·G (DEL-03-05 §2.1);
- springs on the global diagonal;
- no load-dependent terms.

### Realized arcs: stiffness (DEC-070)

- The arc is fixed by its nodes, R and the bow direction. The arc bows toward
  y_reference projected normal to the chord. Its centre lies on the other side
  of the chord.
- The 12×12 is the exact Castigliano stiffness of the clamped arc for the
  complementary energy density N²/(EA) + T²/(GJ) + k·(|M|² − T²)/(EI):
  - one user factor k on both bending components;
  - no shear deformation;
  - inverted, and spread to both ends by rigid-body equilibrium.

  Design §4.3 now states this as the curved-bend energy of the frozen
  `t4_u4a` and `t4_i7` references.
- Directional k is a deferred unit, and these references do not cover it.

### Realized arcs: mass (Design §2.4)

- Each half-arc, from node i over [0, φ/2] and from node j over [φ/2, φ], is a
  rigid lump about its end node.
- Each lump has mass m = m̄Rα, with α = φ/2 the half-angle, and first moment
  ∫m̄ρ ds.
- Its second moment about the node is
  J_n = ∫m̄(|ρ|²I − ρρᵀ) ds + ∫T(s)·diag(j̄, ī, ī)·T(s)ᵀ ds.
  The first term is the centreline second moment, contents included. The
  second is the section's own rotary inertia, with T(s) the local frame,
  tangent first. It equals ∫(j̄ttᵀ + ī(I − ttᵀ)) ds, with ī = j̄/2, and
  contents excluded from j̄ and ī.
- **Primary values** are the lumps with both terms. They are the values in M
  for every mass and eigen case.
- Each lump also gives:
  - `section_rotary_inertia_kg_m2`, the section term on its own;
  - `variant_centreline_only`, the centreline-only lump. This is labelled as
    a variant and is not the design's lump.

**Half-arc series (small-angle evaluation).**

- `half_arc_local_series` in `mass_references.json` gives every half-arc entry
  in its own local frame as a closed form in α, with its leading series terms.
  The coefficients are exact rationals, from a series of order 70.
- The local frame at the half's end node is: e1 radial, e2 the tangent into
  the half-arc, e3 = e1 × e2.
- Leading terms, each per its scale factor (m̄R for the mass, m̄R² for S,
  m̄R³ for Jc, R for Tt):

  | Entry | Closed form | Leading terms |
  |---|---|---|
  | mass | α | α |
  | S_1 | sin α − α | −α³/6 + α⁵/120 |
  | S_2 | 1 − cos α | α²/2 − α⁴/24 |
  | Jc_11 | α/2 − sin 2α/4 | α³/3 − α⁵/15 |
  | Jc_22 | 3α/2 + sin 2α/4 − 2 sin α | α⁵/20 − α⁷/168 |
  | Jc_33 | 2(α − sin α) | α³/3 − α⁵/60 |
  | Jc_12 | 1 − cos α − sin²α/2 | α⁴/8 − α⁶/48 |
  | Tt_11 | α/2 − sin 2α/4 | α³/3 − α⁵/15 |
  | Tt_22 | α/2 + sin 2α/4 | α − α³/3 |
  | Tt_12 | −sin²α/2 | −α²/2 + α⁴/6 |

  The section term is Js = R[j̄·Tt + ī(αI − Tt)].
- Every arc is cross-checked entrywise in these local frames against the
  series, and the series against the closed forms evaluated directly.
- `M-ARC-SMALL` has sin α = 1e-4 exactly. There, the primary route (exact
  trigonometric-polynomial integration at 80 digits) loses about 25 digits to
  cancellation in Jc_22, which is about α⁵/20. It still agrees with the series
  to 3.8e-55 entrywise, so the published 40 digits are unaffected.
- The product evaluates by series below its own stated switch angle, so these
  are the values its series must reproduce.

### Component bodies (Design §2.3)

- The mass is m = W/a at the centre of gravity:
  [[mI, −m[r]×], [m[r]×, m(|r|²I − rrᵀ)]].
- The body has no rotary inertia of its own.
- The references use the exact rational W/a. The product forms fl(W/a), and
  `range_premise` records fl(W/a) and fl(a·fl(W/a)) for each case.
- Eligibility follows self-weight's predicate, in the order consumption,
  mapping, weight, centre of gravity (DEL-03-05 §3).
- An ineligible rigid-family component keeps its pipe mass. Its
  `component_status` names the expected warning,
  `SELF_WEIGHT_COMPONENT_WEIGHT_NOT_APPLIED` (DEL-03-05's code, the value of
  the product constant `COMPONENT_WEIGHT_NOT_APPLIED`), and the first unmet condition.
- The range premise includes Design §2.3 item 6's fourth part: every entry
  m·r_i and m·r_i·r_j of the spatial block must be finite and either zero or
  normal. `range_premise_witnesses` adds W = 1, a = 1e-300, r = (1e10, 0, 0),
  which fails only that part.
- Stiffness scaling is decided separately. In
  `M-COMP-INELIGIBLE-NO-COG` the pipe mass stays and s still applies.

### Massless families

Connectors, tie rods and constant-effort supports add nothing to M.

### Weight-basis identity (Design §2.5)

- The acceleration is a when an eligible component exists, and otherwise
  1 m/s².
- **Straights:** a·M_e·r_k equals the consistent uniform-load vector exactly:
  a·m̄L/2 at each end, and moments ±a·m̄L²/12·(e × k̂).
- **Arcs:** the lumps' resultant is compared with the arc's weight at its
  closed-form centroid, R·sin(φ/2)/(φ/2) from the centre on the bisector.
- **Components:** the lump is compared with the wrench (W·k̂, r × W·k̂).
- **The whole model:** the resultant is taken about the node centroid.

### Participation (Design §1.5, D-v, manager update of 2026-10-30)

- The excitation is p_k = (M R_k)_f, with R_k the rigid unit translation over
  all DOFs, so the M_fc coupling to restrained DOFs is included.
- Γ = φᵀp_k/m_i and m_eff = (φᵀp_k)²/m_i.
- Fractions are relative to T_k = R_kᵀMR_k over all DOFs.
- Each eigen case publishes T_k, the sum of m_eff over every finite mode and
  over the reported modes, and the part captured by no mode
  (`mass.participation`).

### Normalization (Design §1.5)

- The threshold is τ_t = 1e-6, and ℓ is the bounding-box diagonal of all
  nodes.
- **Case (b)** (`unit_max_rotation`) applies if and only if t ≤ τ_t·ℓ·θ. It
  has no participation condition (Design §1.5 item 2, revised 2026-10-31).
  Its Γ and m_eff are published as computed from the published vector, and
  can be large through the M_fc coupling.
  - Example: `E-SS-1`'s first bending pair has only free rotations and about
    83% of T_Y and T_Z.
  - `MODAL_NORMALIZATION_UNRESOLVED` no longer exists, and no case records it.
- **Units.**
  - Case (a): generalized mass kg; Γ dimensionless.
  - Case (b): generalized mass kg·m²·rad⁻²; Γ rad/m.
  - Effective mass: kg in both cases.
- **Ties.** A tie goes to the lowest global DOF index. **(assumed)**
  Magnitudes within 1e-25 relative count as tied.
  - The tied DOFs are listed in `normalization_tie_dofs`, and the mode is
    marked `compare_sign_invariant: true`.
  - In a binary64 product a tie can break the other way and flip the sign.
    Compare such modes sign-invariantly, or by MAC.

### Clusters

Eigenvalues equal within 1e-30 relative form one cluster. Every cluster here
is an exact repeated pair, by the symmetry of a circular section. In a cluster
the vectors are not unique, so the references give a deterministic basis:
greedy M-projections of unit DOF vectors, with ties to the lowest DOF.

Compare clusters as subspaces, by principal angles. Per-mode Γ and m_eff in a
cluster depend on the basis. Their cluster sums do not, and are given in
`clusters[].effective_mass_sum_kg`.

## Method

### Route A (published)

- **Sturm counts.** The count of eigenvalues below σ is the number of
  negative pivots of an unpivoted LDLᵀ of K_ff − σM_ff (Sylvester's law of
  inertia; Golub & Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*, 4th ed., §§8.4, 8.7).
- **Eigenvalues** are found by bisection on the count, in Decimal at 80
  digits, to a relative bracket width of 1e-42.
- **Vectors** come from inverse iteration, one block per cluster, with a
  shift 1e-26 relative below the eigenvalue and LU with partial pivoting.
- **Reported set.** All finite modes are reported when there are at most 24;
  otherwise the lowest 24, completed to whole clusters. The next eigenvalue
  above the set is also given.
- **Singular M_ff.** When M_ff is singular (`E-POINTMASS-*`, where the tip
  rotations carry no mass), the finite count is the rank of M_ff.

### Exact counts

All counts are recomputed in outward-rounded interval arithmetic at 110
digits, on K and M formed in the same interval arithmetic from the exact
inputs. π, atan and √ are enclosed by evaluating at 125 digits and widening by
one unit in the last place. A count is accepted only if every pivot's
interval excludes zero. A symmetric reordering is tried otherwise; none was
needed.

These counts are certified, given the correctness of the series used for the
transcendentals. They cover:

- `certified_brackets`: λ(1 ∓ 1e-33) around each reported cluster, and below
  the next eigenvalue;
- `sturm_counts`: binary64 shifts the product can use directly, each given
  with its hex form:
  - λ_1/2 (count 0);
  - the geometric mean of each gap, including the gap above the reported set;
  - 2λ_max when the whole finite spectrum is reported;
  - for each of the first 4 clusters (all clusters in `E-RIGID-SCALE-*`):
    λ(1 ∓ 1e-9), and fl(λ) with its two binary64 neighbours.

  The fl(λ) shifts are inertia-risk witnesses. They lie within about 1e-16
  relative of an eigenvalue, and the count given there is exact.

### Route B (cross-check only)

Cholesky K = RRᵀ, then C = R⁻¹MR⁻ᵀ, then cyclic Jacobi on C, then
φ = R⁻ᵀz (Golub & Van Loan §§4.2, 8.5).

### Element cross-checks

- The straight mass is re-derived from the kinetic energy of the interpolated
  fields: linear axial and torsional, a cubic Hermite transverse 3-vector, and
  its slope as the section rotation. This is exact in rationals, after
  Przemieniecki, *Theory of Matrix Structural Analysis* (1968), ch. 11.
- The straight stiffness is re-derived from its exact Castigliano tip
  flexibility.
- The arc flexibility and the half-arc lumps are recomputed by 40-point
  Gauss–Legendre quadrature, with series sin and cos.
- The rigid-body nullity of the arc K is checked.
