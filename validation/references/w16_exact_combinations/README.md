# W-16 references: load combinations on the exact route

Frozen, independently derived expectations for model-authored load
combinations on the exact pressure route (EX, `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`)
(W-16 slice b, FR-SOL-009; DEL-05-02 Design
[`exact-combinations.md`](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-02/Design/exact-combinations.md)
§8). One invented two-member line carries pressure in one member, an axial tip
force through both, or both together; its three primitive cases are combined
by every basis. Each case is solved by
[`core/product_physics/tests/w16_exact_combinations.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/tests/w16_exact_combinations.rs)
and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or
example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods
are the textbook closed forms cited below. Standard claim fence applies
(F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted.

and compare it byte for byte with the committed files; `--write` rewrites
them.

## Model and inputs

A line along global X: A(0, 0, 0) is the anchor `support:a`, B(2, 0, 0) and
C(5, 0, 0); member m1 = A–B (L1 = 2 m) and m2 = B–C (L2 = 3 m), both with
`y_reference` (0, 1, 0). Units: N, Pa, m (the product publishes displacements
in mm and the test converts them).

| Input | Value |
|---|---|
| OD D (R = D/2) | 1/5 m |
| nominal wall t | 1/100 m |
| mill tolerance c_m | 1/800 m |
| corrosion allowance c_a | 1/500 m |
| E, ν | 200 GPa, 3/10 |
| pressure p (internal differential) | 2 MPa |
| tip force F along X at C | −25 kN |

| Case | Pressure regions | Tip force |
|---|---|---|
| `case:A` | m1 (terminals A, B; caps transfer to the wall) | none |
| `case:B` | m2 (terminals B, C) | F |
| `case:C` | m1 and m2 (A's and B's loads together) | F |

## Section bases

With t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a = 27/4000 m, r_n = R − t and r_p = R − t_r:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| nominal area A_n = π(R² − r_n²) (stiffness) | 19/10000·π m² |
| reduced area A_r = π(R² − r_p²) | 20871/16000000·π m² |
| pressure bore A_i = π·r_p² | 139129/16000000·π m² |
| cap thrust P = p·A_i | 139129/8·π ≈ 54635.83 N |

## Closed forms

For a member in a region of its own case, with N the tension-positive
mechanical axial force (the tip force here; caps on the other member cancel
across B):
- N_w = N + P and S = N_w − p·A_i = N;
- σ_m,b = N_w / A_b on each basis b;
- Lamé on each basis, C_b = p·r_b²/(R² − r_b²): σ_θ(r) = C_b(1 + R²/r²),
  σ_r(r) = C_b(1 − R²/r²), at the inner and outer surface;
- the end actions are −N_w at end_i and +N_w at end_j (node on element), and
  so are the global end forces Fx along X;
- the elongation is (N_w − 2νP)·L/(E·A_n) (the straight recovery
  N_w = N_m + 2νp·A_i, Design §2); serial members add their elongations.

A member outside every region of its case takes its pressure-free operand
values (Design §3): N_w = S = N, the end action is the element end row,
σ_m,b = N/A_b and the Lamé values are zero. The support reaction at A is
−F (the caps cancel). Sources: Timoshenko and Goodier, *Theory of
Elasticity* (thick-walled cylinder, Lamé); Timoshenko and Gere, *Mechanics of
Materials* (axial members in series).

## Combinations

| Id | Basis | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| `comb:K1` | mechanics | A + 1.3·B |
| `comb:K2` | mechanics | −0.75·A + 2.5·B (a negative factor) |
| `comb:K3` | mechanics | 0·A + 1·B (a zero factor) |
| `comb:K4` | mechanics | C − A − B (cancelling: exactly zero) |
| `comb:S1` | result_state_subtraction | C − A |
| `comb:E-min`, `-max`, `-min_abs`, `-max_abs` | range_envelope | over A, B, C in each mode |

A mechanics combination is Σ fᵢ·Xᵢ with the represented binary64 factors
(Fraction(float), so 1.3 is the double nearest 13/10); a subtraction is
X_minuend − X_subtrahend; a range envelope selects per row by mode, ties to the
smallest case id. The tip's displacement magnitude is the norm of the combined
components (y and z are zero), or the selected magnitude in an envelope. Each
combination also records the expected count of
`pressure_free_operand_recovery` witnesses (62 per member and operand that does
not pressurize a member another operand pressurizes: 2 end actions, 5 wall and
5 effective forces, 10 membranes, 20 Lamé radial and 20 Lamé hoop values) and of
missing sources (15 per such member: its element axial force at 5 stations and
axial normal stress at 5 stations on 2 bases, which a pressurized member does
not publish), and the zero scale of each family (the largest operand magnitude
of that family).

## π

Every value is q₁·π + q₀ + q₋₁/π with rational qₖ: forces carry π through P,
stresses divide by π through A_b, motions are rational. π is computed in the
generator by Machin's formula (16·atan(1/5) − 4·atan(1/239)) in 100-digit
decimal arithmetic, truncated to 90 digits, and used as the rational bounds
[π₉₀, π₉₀ + 10⁻⁹⁰]. A binary64 value is the nearest double to the exact value,
evaluated at both bounds and refused if they disagree. Decimals print 40
significant digits. No library value of π is read.

## Factor sets (gate 3)

The unit factor-sum rule (Design §4): the exact sum Σf of the represented
factors must satisfy |Σf − 1| ≤ 2⁻⁴⁶, compared exactly and inclusively. Decided
here in rationals:

| Id | Factors | Admitted |
|---|---|---|
| unit | [1] | yes |
| tenths | [0.3, 0.7] (Σ = 1 − 2⁻⁵⁴) | yes |
| below_ulp | [1, 2⁻⁵⁴] | yes |
| upper_boundary, lower_boundary | [1, ±2⁻⁴⁶] | yes (inclusive) |
| two, zero | [2], [0] | no |
| cancelling_2p53 | [2⁵³, −2⁵³] (Σ = 0) | no |
| just_above, just_below | [1, ±2⁻⁴⁶, ±2⁻¹⁰⁰] | no; rounding the sum first would admit them |

## Cross-check

Each primitive case is checked by an independent route: the reaction balances
the applied loads; S = N_w − p·A_i; σ_m,b·A_b = N_w by multiplication; the Lamé
inner radial stress is −p and the hoop difference is p; the hoop resultant over
the wall equals p·r_b (Barlow, by direct integration); the tip displacement is
the sum of the member elongations formed from the published wall forces; and
C = A + B row by row (linearity in the resolved loads, with the pressure-free
operand values standing for the unpressurized members). All 61 checks agree
exactly.

## Product binding and criterion

| Route | As authored |
|---|---|
| EX | `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` with `shear_model` `euler_bernoulli`, the section above, E/ν material, the cases' `pressure_regions` and tip force, the combinations above |

The criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale of its
family. The test runs every case in both solver modes, checks the primitive
cases, the combined rows, the recovery witnesses and the missing-source and
witness counts, and reports the largest relative errors per row family.

Cases without a closed form (connectors with an installed reference, tie rods,
a pressurized arc, load/reference states, component bodies, a seismic case)
are checked in the same test by the exact combination of their published
operand rows (the test's own TwoProduct/TwoSum expansion), not against values
here.
