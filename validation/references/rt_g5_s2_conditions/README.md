[DEL-02-01](../../../execution/PKG-02/DEL-02-01/ScopeOfWork.md), model document 0.9.0 design
§1.5, §1.5.1 and §1.8;
[DEL-05-01](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-01/ScopeOfWork.md), thermal temperature-change design).
They are read by:

  row's ΔT bits or document-level refusal, the integer numerators, agreement with
  `exact_kelvin`, the named mutants, the decimal samples and ties; E1: the lowered

  cases, through the public solve in both solver modes);
- `core/product_physics/tests/common/condition_twin.rs`, the independent twin

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator uses the Python standard
library only, and imports, reads and runs no product code; it reads only the

invented. The product supplies no temperature, weight, wind coefficient or
default: every input is the user's. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2;

## E3: the temperature difference

ΔT = RN(K(T) − K(T₀)) in kelvin, with K the exact kelvin value of the operand's
shortest round-trip decimal under its unit's exact affine definition (K: x;
degC: x + 273.15; degF: (x + 459.67)·5/9; degR: x·5/9), rounded once to binary64,
to nearest, ties to even; an exact zero is +0.0. Each row is computed twice and
the two must agree bit for bit:

- **Route A (the reference).** `fractions.Fraction(repr(v))` for the decimal
  (Python's `repr` is the shortest round-trip decimal, the closer one when two of
  that length round-trip, the even digit on an exact tie) and `float()` of the
  exact difference, which CPython rounds correctly. Admission is read
  independently: the domain is v = 0 or |v| ≤ 10⁶ with decimal exponent ≥ −24;
  below zero kelvin is refused whatever the magnitude.
- **Route B (the cross-check).** A transcription of §1.5.1's fixed-integer
  algorithm, decimal stage included (pre-gate, decade, the d₀/d₀ + 1 search on
  integers scaled by 2^(2−E)·10^max(0,−q), the choice and the stripping), the
  integer form N/D with D = 9·10²⁴, and the rounding with k, q and r. It asserts
  every decimal-stage intermediate is below 2¹⁹² and the rounding's shifted
  numerator below 2¹³⁶.

Coverage:

| Group | Rows |
|---|---|
| each unit, and mixed units | `kelvin`, `celsius_*`, `fahrenheit_*`, `rankine`, `mixed_*` |
| equal temperatures (+0.0) and negative ΔT | `equal_*`, `negative_*` |
| §1.8's examples | `celsius_typed_decimals` (20.3 − 20.1 degC is the binary64 nearest 0.2; the binary64 affine route's 0.19999999999998863 is recorded), `fahrenheit_212_70` (78.88888888888889 K; affine 78.88888888888886 K) |
| the degF/degR disclosure | `fahrenheit_77_70`: 77 − 70 degF gives `400f1c71c71c71c7`; a typed 7 degF interval, through today's value × fl(5/9), gives `400f1c71c71c71c8` (recorded as `typed_interval`) |
| range edges (admitted) | `edge_*`, `width_regression` (1e-24 K − 0 K: N = 9, shifted numerator 9·2¹³² above `i128`, bits `3af357c299a88ea7`), `smallest_negative` |
| the decimal tie | `decimal_tie_regression`: `0x3f80010000000000` reads as 0.007814407348632812, and K − 0.007 K gives `3f4aafbe76c8b435`; the formatter's decimal …813 would give `3f4aafbe76c8b43e` (recorded) |
| refusals | `refused_*`: above 10⁶, e = −25 (1.5e-24), a subnormal, an out-of-range installation temperature (`OPERATING_CONDITION_TEMPERATURE_RANGE_UNSUPPORTED`); below zero kelvin in and out of range (`OPERATING_CONDITION_QUANTITY_INVALID`) |
| near-halfway | `near_halfway_*`: the eight admitted pairs, from a seeded search over typed-looking decimals, whose exact difference lies closest to a binary64 midpoint (an exact tie cannot occur in range, §1.5.1) |

`decimal_samples` are 400 seeded random admitted binary64 values with their
`repr`; `decimal_ties` are the three 16- and 17-digit ties where the canonical
decimal (and `repr`) takes the even digit and Rust's `{:e}` the other one. The
Rust test checks the canonical decimal against both and lists Rust's choice.

## Mechanics

Closed-form textbook results for a straight member along x of length L = 5 m,
OD 0.2 m, wall 0.01 m, E = 200 GPa, G = 80 GPa, α = 1.2·10⁻⁵ /degC (an interval
factor of 1 to /K), A = π/4·(D² − d²), I = π/64·(D⁴ − d⁴), evaluated in exact
rational arithmetic with π as a 60-digit rational and converted to binary64 once.
Each document is solved in both solver modes and also checked to solve as its
twin.

| Case | Input from the condition set | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| `restrained_thermal` | 150 degC operating, 20 degC installation; anchors at both ends | anchor reactions ±E·A·α·ΔT along x (the anchors push the pipe inward), member force E·A·α·ΔT in compression, no midspan motion |
| `free_expansion` | 212 degF operating, 70 degF installation; anchored at x = 0 | tip motion α·ΔT·L; no anchor force |
| `added_weight_cantilever` | added weight −1500 N/m along global_z | Euler-Bernoulli tip deflection w·L⁴/(8EI), tip rotation −w·L³/(6EI) about y, anchor reaction −w·L |
| `wind_cantilever` | `exposure.wind: true`; the case's own 1200 Pa, shape factor 0.7, global_y | q = p·C·OD, tip deflection q·L⁴/(8EI), anchor reaction −q·L |

The comparison is relative 10⁻⁹ with absolute floors of 10⁻¹⁵ m and 10⁻⁶ N
(recorded in the file). The members are two Euler-Bernoulli elements with
consistent load vectors, whose nodal values are exact for these loads, so the
tolerance covers binary64 rounding only.
