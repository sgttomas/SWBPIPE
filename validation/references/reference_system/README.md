# Reference system: EQ-1, EQ-2, EQ-3 and EQ-5

Frozen references and checks for four key results of the reference system (DEL-09-01
[`reference-system.md`](../../../execution/PKG-09/DEL-09-01/Work/reference-system.md) §9.2), on its
tranche-1 fixture [`fixtures/reference_system/reference_system.json`](../../../fixtures/reference_system/reference_system.json).
That fixture is an EX document (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`, `euler_bernoulli`, load/reference-state group)
with cases RS-SUS, RS-OPE and RS-SAM and combinations RS-EXP (OPE − SUS) and RS-dSAM (SAM − SUS).
[`tests/test_reference_system_fixture.py`](../../../tests/test_reference_system_fixture.py) solves it through
`openpipestress-runner` in both solver modes and checks the four results here.

| Id | Check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| EQ-1 | In RS-SUS, RS-OPE and RS-SAM, the sum of the vertical (global Z) reaction components over every support, the spring included, equals W_total | closed form: `references.json` `W_total_N` |
| EQ-2 | In the same cases, the horizontal (X, Y) reaction resultant is zero, and the reaction moment resultant about the global origin equals minus the weight's moment about the origin | closed form: `references.json` `weight_moment_about_origin_N_m` |
| EQ-3 | On every published signed component row, RS-EXP = RS-OPE − RS-SUS; magnitude rows equal the norm of the combined components; withheld rows are excluded and listed | exact identity (this README) |
| EQ-5 | The RS-dSAM reaction resultant force and its moment about the origin are zero | exact identity (this README): no load differs between RS-SAM and RS-SUS |

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs no product code. All model
data are invented (spec §1). No equation, value or example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used
(DEC-043); the methods are elementary statics. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per
DEC-081). Agreement here is evidence, not acceptance (F-PIP-3).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK. They have not yet been
independently refuted.

## What the generator reads

It reads the fixture file as data, with every number parsed as an exact decimal (`Fraction`): the nodes, the
pipe segments with their embedded sections, and the components. It does not read `load_cases`, so none of the
captured self-weight primitives (`load:self-weight:*`), and it does not read `self_weight_capture.json`. The
weight is recomputed from the sections, the material density, the geometry and the component weights.

The generator also holds the specification's own statements and checks the fixture against them: the §4.2
section table, the §5 component weights and centre-of-gravity rule ("body midpoint", the valve's offset
(0, 0, +0.30) m), the §3.1 bend corners and §5's radii, and the members that carry no distributed weight.
g = 9.80665 m/s² along −Z is spec §2's statement; in the model document it appears only inside the captured
primitives, which are not read.

## Mass bases

The bases are EX's stated ones, as the product's documentation gives them (not read from code):
- `core/product_physics/src/exact_admission.rs` `pressure_v3_formulation_basis_with_source`, the formulation
  text EX publishes: "stiffness and pipe metal mass use the nominal wall; contents mass and pressure end-cap
  areas use the reduced bore" (wall minus mill tolerance and corrosion allowance);
- DEL-05-03 `Work/section-bases-and-timoshenko.md` §1 (the D-5 section table) and DEL-05-01
  `Work/exact-equivalent-static.md` §2: "Insulation mass is computed as on PF, over OD to OD + 2·t_ins";
  `validation/references/w16_exact_equivalent_static/README.md` writes the same μ_EX;
- DEL-03-05 `Work/rigid-component-stiffness-scaling.md` §3: a rigid-family component's weight W acts at its
  node plus its centre-of-gravity vector r (global axes, from the node; R10), and its mapped member's
  distributed self-weight is not generated;
- spec §5.1 and §8.1: the replaced span RS-P180 carries no self-weight.

With R = D/2, nominal wall t, mill tolerance c_m, corrosion allowance c_a, insulation thickness t_i, and
densities ρ_m, ρ_c, ρ_i:

    μ = π [ ρ_m (R² − (R − t)²) + ρ_c (R − ((t − c_m) − c_a))² + ρ_i ((R + t_i)² − R²) ]   (kg/m)

| RS-S270 | 57.9299 | 42.5373 | 8.70849 | 109.176 | 1070.65 |

Weight is μ·g times the member length:
- straight members: the chord length; RS-P150 (the tranche-1 plain member in the reducer's slot) is inclined,
  √257/40 m;
- realized arcs RS-P065, RS-P085, RS-P225: R·θ with θ = π/2;
- RS-P020, RS-P030 and RS-P040: none; flange RS-F1 (350 N), valve RS-V1 (2600 N) and flange RS-F2 (350 N)
  replace them;
- RS-P180: none.

The eccentric reducer RS-R1 (300 N) is pending in tranche 1 (spec §10), so it carries no weight here and
RS-P150 weighs as a plain S270 member, as the fixture models it. RS-HYD's weight with its test contents is
referenced when RS-HYD lands.

**Basis readings.** The documentation states each basis above directly. Two points it leaves implicit are read
as follows:
- a realized arc carries the same μ as its section over its arc length, with no bend-specific mass;
- the contents fill the reduced bore over the whole member length, arcs included, with no contents in the
  replaced span.

## Moment of the weight

Each member's weight acts at its centroid: the midpoint of a straight member; for a 90° arc with centre C and
radius vectors e₁, e₂ to its tangent points, ∫ r ds = R[(π/2)C + e₁ + e₂], so the arc's first moment is
μg·R[(π/2)C + e₁ + e₂]. Each component's force −W·Z acts at its node plus its centre-of-gravity vector. With
S = Σ W·r, the weight's moment about the origin is Σ r × (0, 0, −W) = (−S_y, S_x, 0).

## Exactness and evaluation

Every result is an exact sum of terms q·π^k·√n with q rational: k = 1 for straight members, k = 1 and 2 for arcs
(μ carries one π, the arc length another), and n = 257 for RS-P150. `exact` prints that sum; component weights
give the rational term. The transcendental factors are evaluated apart from the exact algebra: π by Machin's
formula, √n by integer square root, each with rational lower and upper bounds 10⁻⁹⁰ apart. `decimal` has 40
significant digits; `value` is the binary64 nearest the exact result, accepted only when both ends of the
bounding interval round to the same double.

Cross-checks, all 20 agreeing exactly: each section's μ in a second factored form (t(D − t), t_i(D + t_i));
each bend's corner and far tangent from its radius against §3.1; each arc first moment against the classical
centroid 2R sin(θ/2)/θ on the bisector (Decimal, 10⁻⁸⁰); each component's weight and centre of gravity
against §5; the omitted members against the specification; and W_total again as per-section length sums.

## Product rows and conventions

Read from the runner's output and `exact_combinations.rs`'s pairing key; the checks use these and no
other product data.
- `support_reaction_component_v2`, components Fx, Fy, Fz (N), Mx, My, Mz (N·m), `coordinate_system: global`,
  `location: node`. Sign convention as published: "support-on-pipe; positive global force and right-hand
  couple about attached node". Every support publishes all six components in every case and combination
  (zero where it does not restrain), the spring RS-VS1 included, so EQ-1 sums all eleven supports' Fz, and
  the moment resultant about the origin is Σ (r_node × F + M).
- `support_reaction_force_magnitude_v2` and `support_reaction_moment_magnitude_v2`: per support, the norms of
  the force and moment components.
- `global_nodal_displacement_{x,y,z}` (mm) and `global_nodal_rotation_{x,y,z}` (rad, right-hand rule), with
  `displacement_magnitude` (no metadata; keyed by its node).
- `element_global_end_force_v1` / `element_global_end_moment_v1`: the global end action on the element at its
  node (node on element), at `end_i` and `end_j`. The element-local section actions, wall and effective axial
  forces, stresses on both section bases, and connector rows are published too and are signed component rows.
- Rows pair across cases by the published fields (kind, unit, entity, location, component, coordinate system and
  the section basis named in `basis`), never by row id.

## EQ-3: rows checked and rows excluded

In each solver mode RS-EXP publishes 3558 rows checked here (3560 less two `combination_modulus_basis_record`
records): 3509 signed component rows of 33 kinds compared with OPE − SUS, and 49 magnitude rows (27 nodal
displacement magnitudes, 11 support force and 11 support moment magnitudes) recomputed as the norm of RS-EXP's
own combined components. Excluded and listed by the test:
- **withheld by the combination**: the two tie-rod rows `connector_tie_rod_force_v1` (rods RS-EJ1-RYP and
  RS-EJ1-RYN share one pairing key), named in RS-EXP's `LOAD_COMBINATION_ROW_WITHHELD` warning; the test checks
  the warning names both;
- **not signed component rows, never combined by the product**: `curved_bend_directional_intensified_bending_*`
  (COMBINATION_INTENSIFIED_STRESS_UNAVAILABLE), `element_local_normal_stress_extreme`,
  `element_local_transverse_shear_stress_maximum`, `pipe_elastic_normal_stress_maximum_v2`
  (COMBINATION_STRESS_MAXIMUM_UNAVAILABLE) and `linear_solver_mode_basis`.

Every other OPE row pairs with exactly one SUS row and one RS-EXP row.

## Criterion

The adopted criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026, as `../w16_imposed_motion/README.md` states it). A nonzero
row passes when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale, the largest nonzero
magnitude of its family in the case. The families:
- EQ-1, EQ-2, EQ-5: the case's published support force components (force) and support moment components
  (moment);
- EQ-3: the row's unit (N, N·m, mm, rad, Pa, MPa, m), over RS-EXP's expected values.
