# W-9 references: PRD §22.2 solver benchmarks

Frozen, independently derived expectations for three solver benchmarks named
in [PRD §22.2](../../../docs/PRD.md): an L-shaped thermal expansion frame, a
simply supported pipe under weight, and an imposed anchor displacement case
(unit W-9 of [the completeness map](../../../docs/COMPLETENESS_MAP.md)). Each
case is solved on a product route by
[`core/product_physics/tests/w9_solver_benchmarks.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/tests/w9_solver_benchmarks.rs)
and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or
example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods
are the textbook methods cited below. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2;
claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted.

## Theory, section and conventions

The theory is a small-displacement, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli space frame
with no shear deformation. The default preview route (PF) forms Euler–Bernoulli
straight members. The exact route (EX) is run with `shear_model:
euler_bernoulli`, so no Timoshenko term enters either route.

Every case uses one annular section: OD 0.2 m, ID 0.18 m (wall 0.01 m), E =
200 GPa. That gives A = 0.0019π m², I = 8.5975e-6π m⁴, EA = 380000000π N and
EI = 1719500π N·m². These are the t3_r1 N-series coefficients, and the
generator asserts them. The in-plane results do not depend on G (PF input
76923076923 Pa) or ν (EX input 0.3); the out-of-plane problem carries no load.

Every result has the form q·π^k with q rational. Forces and moments have k = 1;
translations and rotations have k = 0. `exact` prints q or `q*pi`. `decimal`
has 40 significant digits (π by Machin). `value` is the nearest binary64,
rounded with lower and upper rational bounds of π, which must agree.

The published quantities are:
- `support_reaction`: the global action of the support on the structure, with
  moments about the support node.
- `nodal_motion`: global translations (m) and right-handed rotations (rad).
- `section_action`: the element-local action on the +x face of the i-side
  portion at station s/L, where local x runs from i to j. A node-on-element end
  action is −section(0) at `end_i` and +section(1) at `end_j`.

Local axes follow each pipe's `y_reference`. The generator chooses them so that
local z is global +Z on every member: (0, 1, 0) for members along X and
(−1, 0, 0) for the member along Y.

## Cases

### W9-A: sharp-corner L frame, fixed–fixed, uniform temperature rise

Geometry: A(0, 0, 0) fixed → C(4, 0, 0) → D(4, 3, 0) fixed, built from two
straight pipes with a sharp corner at C and no bend element. Both pipes carry
α = 12e-6 /K and ΔT = 100 K, a free strain of 3/2500.

**Method: force method (flexibility, compatibility).** Hibbeler, *Structural
Analysis*, force-method chapter; Timoshenko & Young, *Theory of Structures*,
statically indeterminate frames.
- Release D. The redundants are X, Y and M: the support's +X force, +Y force
  and +Z moment on the structure at D.
- The bending moment about +Z of the D-side loads at a point P is
  m(P) = M + (x_D − x_P)·Y − (y_D − y_P)·X. On C→D (s = y_D − y_P) this gives
  m = M − X·s, N = Y. On A→C (t = x_D − x_P) it gives m = M + Y·t − X·b,
  N = X.
- Unit-load virtual work over both members, with bending and axial terms
  (a = 4, b = 3), gives:
  - f_XX = (b³/3 + a·b²)/EI + a/EA
  - f_YY = a³/(3EI) + b/EA
  - f_MM = (a + b)/EI
  - f_XY = −a²b/(2EI)
  - f_XM = −(b²/2 + a·b)/EI
  - f_YM = a²/(2EI)
- Compatibility: the released D moves freely by αΔT·(a, b, 0) relative to A,
  so f·R = −αΔT·(a, b, 0).
- A's reactions follow from statics: F_A = −F_D and
  M_A = −(M + a·Y − b·X).
- The motion of corner C comes from tree integration of the cantilever A→C
  under the D-side resultant: F_D and M_C = M − b·X. Then
  u_x = αΔT·a + X·a/EA, u_y = Y·a³/(3EI) + M_C·a²/(2EI) and
  θ_z = Y·a²/(2EI) + M_C·a/EI.

The survey sketch's flexibility formula and sign of the compatibility vector
were derived again here from first principles, and both are correct as stated.
As a self-check, continuing the tree integration along C→D returns D to its
imposed position exactly, (0, 0, 0).

### W9-B: simply supported pipe under its own metal weight

The span is L = 6 m along +X, built from two pipes with a midspan node at 3 m.
- End A restrains UX, UY, UZ and RX.
- End B restrains UY and UZ.

Gravity is −9.80665 m/s² along global Y. ρ = 7850 kg/m³ acts on the metal area
only, so w = ρ·g·A = 585064739/4000000·π N/m = 459.509 N/m. On both routes
the load is the product's own self-weight generation
(`self_weight::generate_self_weight_operations`), applied as drafted. The test
also checks the generated intensity against w.

**Method: Euler–Bernoulli simply supported beam under uniform load.** Gere &
Goodno, *Mechanics of Materials*, beam-deflection tables; Roark's *Formulas
for Stress and Strain*, simply supported beam with a uniform load.
- R = wL/2
- M(x) = R·x − w·x²/2 (+x face, sagging positive)
- V(x) = w·x − R (+x face, global Y)
- δ_mid = 5wL⁴/(384EI)
- θ_end = wL³/(24EI), with θz(A) = −θ_end and θz(B) = +θ_end.

| δ_mid (uy at mid) | −831407787/579200000000 m | −1.43544e-3 m |
| θ_end | 277135929/362000000000 rad | 7.65569e-4 rad |

### W9-C: imposed anchor displacement on the L frame

This is the W9-A frame with no load and no temperature change. Anchor D settles
by d = (UX 0, UY −0.01 m, RZ 0); all other DOFs at D are held at zero.

**Method:** the same force method and flexibility matrix as W9-A. The
compatibility condition is f·R = d: the released D must reach its imposed
position under the redundants alone. A's reactions follow by statics, and C's
motion by tree integration. The self-check returns D to (0, −0.01, 0) exactly.

## Rows and cross-checks

Each case publishes 90 rows:
- all six support components at both supports;
- all six motion components at three nodes;
- all six section components at stations 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1 on both pipes.

Zero rows are exact zeros. Each zero row is compared against 1e-9 times its
family's zero scale: the largest nonzero magnitude of that family (force,
moment, translation or rotation) in the case.

The generator's second route is an exact direct-stiffness solve of the same
plane frame. It uses 3 DOF per node, the held-member thermal end actions
EA·ε, the consistent fixed-end loads wL/2 and wL²/12, and prescribed-DOF
partitioning for W9-C. All 81 comparisons agree exactly:
- every in-plane reaction;
- every in-plane nodal motion;
- every element end action;
- the compatibility self-checks;
- M_mid = wL²/8.

The out-of-plane problem of each case is unloaded and stable, so its
components are exactly zero.

W9-C runs on both routes against the same frozen rows. Its force-method
compatibility F·R = d does not depend on the route, and its out-of-plane
problem is unloaded, so the routes' different shear moduli do not enter. Both
routes accepted the sharp straight–straight junction and refused nothing.

The criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale. Both
solver modes (`SparseInteractive`, `DenseScrutiny`) run against the same
reference.

## Reproduce and change

```
python3 -I generators/w9_reference.py          # check: rebuild, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/w9_reference.py --write  # rewrite references.json and SHA256SUMS
```

Check mode also fails on any cross-check disagreement or unexpected file.

**Re-freeze rules.**
- No value changes to match a product output. A disagreement goes back to this
  reference's owner and an independent refuter.
- A change to the theory or a convention is a re-freeze, with a recorded reason
  and a new refutation.
- Editing this README changes only its hash in `SHA256SUMS`. The test pins the
  hash of `references.json` only.
