# T4-U5a references: untied joint pressure thrust

Frozen, independently derived expectations for T4-U5a, the untied expansion
joint carrying pressure in an equal-bore, equal-pressure region chain
([DEL-04-07 Design, joint-pressure-thrust.md](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Design/joint-pressure-thrust.md)).
Nothing here is generated by the product, and the generator imports, reads and
runs no product code.

## Files

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `generators/u5a_reference.py` | the generator: fixture, exact derivation, requests and oracle |
| `requests/<case>.request.json` | 19 complete product preview requests (model 0.5.0, `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`, explicit local CA 0 m and `pressure_contract.shear_model = euler_bernoulli`, with the U5a fields) |
| `oracle.json` | expected values per case, the J4 and ledger mutants, the offset demonstration and 17 refusal variants |
| `SHA256SUMS` | the hashes of every file above |

Each oracle case names its request and binds it by SHA-256. The refusal
variants are JSON-pointer patches applied to `J3-1`'s request.

## Basis

- **Exact over Q(π).** Every value is a rational function of π with rational
  coefficients, held in lowest terms with a monic denominator, so equality is
  exact. Decimals (30 significant digits) and binary64 values are rounded from
  the exact forms. The general axial solve is checked exactly against JR's
  closed forms on every case that has one (`closed_forms`), including JR's
  literal J3 row 1 and row 3 values.
- **Ideal inputs.** The oracle is exact for the decimal fixture values as
  written (E = 200 GPa, ν = 0.3, OD 0.06 m, wall 0.005 m, p = 0.2 MPa,
  Ae = 0.004 m², k = 2e5 N/m, 2 m stubs, 0.3 m joint). The requests author the
  nearest binary64 values; the difference is of order 1e-16 relative, far
  inside the criterion.
- **Basis-neutral.** Mill tolerance and corrosion allowance are zero, the chain
  is straight and coaxial, and every non-axial displacement, rotation, moment
  and lateral force is exactly zero, so the values do not depend on the
  section basis (D-5, T4-U6) or the shear model (D-6, T4-U8). The connector's
  non-axial diagonal (8e4, 8e4 N/m; 600, 900, 900 N·m/rad) only makes the 6×6
  positive definite.
- **Physics source.** JR (`CORRECTNESS_DESIGN/JOINT_REFERENCE/CONTRACT.md`
  sections 2, 4 and 6, and its independent refutation, sections 2 and 3,
  archived at `11718e61a2^`), re-derived here from the axial free bodies and the
  straight-pipe wall law Nw = EA(ε − ε_T) + 2νpAi. No extracted equation
  artifact is used (DEC-043). All data are invented synthetic controls.

## What is checked inside the generator

- **Representation (A) against (B), exactly (H-2's condition).** For every case
  the generator assembles the pressure RHS twice and independently:
  (A) every pressurized pipe's full mathematical cap pair, its Poisson pair, the
  joint's generalized pressure load Bᵀg_p with g_p = [pAe, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0] from
  JR's B, and a remote-closure cancellation at each separately supported
  terminal; (B) the product's ledger: caps only at transferring terminals,
  Poisson pairs, and at each joint attachment the pair −/+p(Ae − Ai) along the
  connector axis. It asserts the two vectors are equal in Q(π), that Bᵀg_p has
  zero moments for the coaxial zero-offset joint, and that (A) solves to the
  same state. Both vectors are published (`pressure_rhs`).
- **The interface identity.** Wherever the joint is in the region, the
  effective force of the welded pipe equals the joint's, S_pipe = Nw − Pi =
  Tb − Pe = S_joint, at both faces.
- **J4, untied.** The double count (terminal caps plus the full pair pAe, which
  is what dropping the joint's face-cap summands produces) reproduces JR's J4
  values: q = (h + Pe)/(k + kp/2) anchored and q = (Pe + Pi)/k free. In the free
  chain the reactions and Nw are unchanged by the defect (both cases' anchor
  reaction is zero); only q, the joint's elastic force (Pe + Pi instead of Pe)
  and the interface identity expose it. This is JR's tie-force control in its
  untied form: inspect the joint's hardware action, not global balance.

## Mutants and refusals

`j4_and_ledger_mutants` gives the wrong values of four producer-level ledger
mutants on J3-1 and J3-3 (double count, faces exchanged, face caps from the
outside diameter, the effective-area summands dropped) and which observables
each one moves. Faces exchanged (`sign_flip`) reverses both summands at each
attachment, that is the whole pair −/+p(Ae − Ai); reversing the
effective-area summand alone is a different mutant, not listed here. In the
free chain (J3-3) the double count leaves Nw, S_pipe and the reactions
unchanged and moves q, u_C, u_D, g_x, the connector's endpoint rows and `s_x`. `lateral_offset_demonstration` shows why an offset pressurized
joint is refused: with equal lateral offsets of 0.01 m, Bᵀg_p carries end
moments of 8 N·m that a nodal pair cannot represent.

`refusals` gives 17 patches of J3-1 (RV-01 to RV-15 with RV-05a–c and
RV-06b; there is no RV-10) with the exact blocking-code set each must
produce, `MODEL_INCOMPLETE` and no results: the missing region-chain edge, a
terminal at a joint face, the replaced span listed, an unpressurized joint
listed, the effective area absent, zero or without source, lateral and axial
offsets, a straight–joint kink, unequal bore, faces in two regions at different
pressures, rods before U5b, an unknown pressure model, a basis mismatch, a
thermal load on the replaced span and an unknown connector reference. The code
sets assume the Design's precedence: a region with a joint finding is neither
traversed nor assembled, and a listed connector refused by its own model
finding carries only that code. Some refusals have no patch here (a
duplicate `member_connector_ids` entry, a connector listed by two regions, a
negative, nonfinite or wrongly dimensioned area, a face pipe that is not a
straight member); the Design names them as unit tests.

## Criterion

An entry passes when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·max(|exp|, F), F being its family's
floor: 1 N for forces, 1 N·m for moments, 1e-5 m for lengths and 1e-5 rad for
rotations (absolute 1e-9 N, 1e-9 N·m, 1e-14 m and 1e-14 rad on exact zeros).
Run in both solver modes. Blocking-code sets are compared exactly.

Floor family by key:

| Key or row | Family |
|---|---|
| keys ending `_n`; `connector_generalized_force_v1`, `connector_endpoint_force_v1:*`, `connector_pressure_force_v1`, `connector_effective_axial_force_v1`; `pressure_rhs` entries | force (1 N) |
| keys ending `_nm`; `connector_generalized_moment_v1`, `connector_endpoint_moment_v1:*` | moment (1 N·m) |
| keys ending `_m` (q, elongations, axial and global displacements, separations); `connector_generalized_translation_v1` | length (1e-5 m) |
| keys ending `_rad`; `connector_generalized_rotation_v1` | rotation (1e-5 rad) |
| areas (keys ending `_m2`, and evidence areas such as `effective_area_m2` and `face_bore_area_m2`) | no floor: relative 1e-9 only, since every area is positive |
| unitless direction components (`axis_global`, the fixture's rotation) | floor 1, the magnitude of a unit vector |
