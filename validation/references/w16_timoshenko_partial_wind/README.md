# W-16t references: partial-span wind on Timoshenko members on the exact route

Frozen, independently derived expectations for wind marked on a proper fraction
of a straight span under `timoshenko_annulus_energy_v1` on the exact pressure
route (EX). The design is in DEL-05-03 Design
[`section-bases-and-timoshenko.md`](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/Design/section-bases-and-timoshenko.md)
§3.4 and §4 item 4, and DEL-05-01 Design
[`exact-equivalent-static.md`](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-01/Design/exact-equivalent-static.md)
§3 and §5. Each case is solved by
[`core/product_physics/tests/w16_timoshenko_partial_wind.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/tests/w16_timoshenko_partial_wind.rs)
in both solver modes and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs
no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or example
from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods are the
textbook forms cited below. The product supplies no wind coefficient, profile
or default: the pressure, shape factor, direction and marked spans are the
user's inputs. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per
DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author. They
have not yet been independently refuted. Their author also implemented the
product change and had read the product code, so a separate refutation must
precede any reliance on them.

## Model and inputs

The section, material and wind are those of the W16C references
(`../w16_exact_equivalent_static/`): OD 1/5 m, nominal wall 1/100 m, mill
tolerance 1/800 m, corrosion allowance 1/500 m, insulation 1/20 m; E = 200 GPa,
ν = 3/10, G = E/(2(1 + ν)); wind 1200 Pa, shape factor 7/10, direction
global Z, so q_w = p·C·(D + 2t_i) = 252 N/m. Stiffness is on the nominal wall;
stresses on the nominal and reduced (t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a) sections. κ_E is the
energy-matched annular factor of DEL-05-03 §3.1 (16609827/33068756), and
K_s = G·κ_E·A_n. φ = 12EI/(K_s L²) is rational: φ ≈ 0.0450 at L = 5/2 m,
≈ 1.12 at L = 1/2 m.

Units: N, N·m, m, rad and Pa (the product publishes mm and MPa for motions and
station stresses; the test converts).

## Mechanics

For a uniform load w on [aL, bL] of a member of length L:

- feq_T = feq_EB + ΔV₀·[1, L/2, −1, L/2], with
  ΔV₀ = φ/(1 + φ)·(wL/2)·[g(a) − g(b)] and g(ξ) = ξ²(1 − ξ)²;
- feq_EB = [wL[ξ − ξ³ + ξ⁴/2]ₐᵇ, wL²[ξ²/2 − 2ξ³/3 + ξ⁴/4]ₐᵇ, wL[ξ³ − ξ⁴/2]ₐᵇ,
  wL²[−ξ³/3 + ξ⁴/4]ₐᵇ];
- the correction vanishes on a full span, and on a symmetric span in exact
  arithmetic; it tends to zero as φ → 0.

**Three routes, required to agree exactly** (the generator aborts otherwise):

1. **Continuum.** The force method on the whole line, with Macaulay load
   integrals W_n(x) = ∫₀ˣ (x − s)ⁿ/n!·w(s) ds: Q = Q₀ − W₀, M = M₀ − Q₀x + W₁,
   θ = [M₀x − Q₀x²/2 + W₂]/EI, v = [M₀x²/2 − Q₀x³/6 + W₃]/EI + [Q₀x − W₁]/K_s,
   with Q₀ and M₀ from the free end (cantilever) or θ = v = 0 at both ends
   (fixed-fixed). The axial line is N = N₀ − ∫ℓ_x, u = ∫N/EA. No element
   formula is used.
2. **Element.** The exact Timoshenko element (stiffness 12EI/(L³(1+φ)),
   6EI/(L²(1+φ)), (4+φ)EI/(L(1+φ)), (2−φ)EI/(L(1+φ))) with feq_T, assembled
   and solved in rationals; axial bars with their consistent loads.
3. **Closure.** Each element's interior, integrated from its nodal state and
   its recovered i-end actions, returns its j-end motion exactly.

Cantilever tips are also checked by the unit-load method (∫M·m/EI + ∫Q/K_s),
and every case's supports balance its load in force and in moment about A.

Sources: S. P. Timoshenko, "On the correction for shear of the differential
equation for transverse vibrations of prismatic bars", Philosophical Magazine
41 (1921); J. S. Przemieniecki, *Theory of Matrix Structural Analysis* (1968),
the shear-deformable beam stiffness; Z. Friedman and J. B. Kosmatka, "An
improved two-node Timoshenko beam finite element", Computers & Structures
47(3) (1993), the exact interdependent shape functions; S. P. Timoshenko and
J. M. Gere, *Mechanics of Materials*, cantilever deflection and the unit-load
method.

## Values

Every component value is q·π^k with q rational: wind forces and moments
k = 0, motions and stresses k = −1. The combination's seismic term carries one
more power of π. Support magnitudes and elastic maxima need square roots:
they are sums c·√s·π^k, held with a rigorous rational interval (π between
90-digit rational bounds, square roots by integer square roots at 10⁻¹⁰⁰)
whose two ends decide the binary64 value. Component expectations are
represented exactly as rational multiples of powers of π. Norms and maxima
that require square roots or other non-rational operations use independently
derived rigorous intervals. (SKEW's transverse resultant contains √13.)

The elastic maximum is max over the pipe of |N|/A_b + hypot(M_y, M_z)/Z_b on
each basis. Its candidates are the pipe's ends, the load breakpoints inside it
and the zeros of each plane's shear (the interior extrema of M); a 1/256 grid
cross-checks that none exceeds it.

checks each where it applies.

## Conventions and criterion

The row conventions are W16C's: support reactions are the support's global
action on the structure at its node; section actions are element-local
actions on the +x face of the i-side portion (an end action is −section(0) at
end_i); station stresses follow the section action. The criterion is unchanged
(DEC-024/DEC-026): a nonzero row passes when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|, a zero
row when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale, the largest magnitude of that row's family
in the case. The test also applies the K-D5 M3 judging and coverage rules on
every Passed case.
