# T4-U5b references: explicit elastic tie rods

Frozen, independently derived expectations for T4-U5b. T4-U5b lifts the
`untied` hardware restriction of an expansion joint authored as an objective
connector, for explicit elastic tie rods only
(`explicit_axial_rods_v1` with the `bilateral_linear` law;
[DEL-04-07 Design, joint-pressure-thrust.md](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Design/joint-pressure-thrust.md)
§9). Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads
and runs no product code or product output.

**Status.** Refuted on 2026-10-17 by a separate TASK that shares no code with the generator. It used a 100-digit 3D nodal solve with its own B and C, and hand free bodies in reduced coordinates, and confirmed every value. Its one MAJOR and four MINOR findings and HELP_HUMAN's two rulings of 2026-10-17 are applied in this revision. A second separate TASK then confirmed every repair and refuted the four new cases with extended copies of both independent methods: all 6,970 entries of the 38 cases agree (worst 3.1e-112 relative), and all 3,572 discriminator wrong values are reproduced. Of its three MINOR notes, the relocated SK-F value is now stated under the Revision, Design §9 now carries both rulings, and no named p = 0 discriminators are added, because the case values already separate those defects by at least 7.4e7 tolerances. These references do not establish acceptance.

**Consumer.** T4-U5b, not implemented. It lands with T4-U4b on model 0.6.0
([pressure-contract.md](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Design/pressure-contract.md),
the U4b/U5b formats bullet). Model 0.6.0 does not yet define
`explicit_axial_rods_v1`, so the cases are frozen as request-agnostic
descriptions (see "Binding to model 0.6.0").

## Rulings applied (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-17)

Both are recorded in `oracle.json` `rulings`.

1. **Non-parallel rods are admitted. S_joint takes the projected form,
   S_joint = Tb + Σ T_r·(n_r·Q_x) − Pe, in every case.**
   - *Basis.* §9's hardware already defines a general rod direction,
     e = n·(v_bⱼ − v_bᵢ). §9's literal S_joint = Tb + ΣT_r − Pe holds only when
     n·Q_x = 1; on SK-F it breaks the interface identity by 28800/1661 N. The
     projected form is the face equilibrium and reduces to the literal form
     for parallel rods. Refusing non-parallel rods would need a parallelism
     tolerance with no physical basis.
   - *Applied.* SK-F is an admitted reference case. Every case's S_joint
     uses the projected rule; on each of the 37 parallel-rod cases it equals
     the literal form exactly (checked). No value moved: the first freeze
     already published SK-F's projected S_joint (0) and its identity. §9's
     literal form is the discriminator `s_joint_section9_literal`, formed
     on SK-F.
   - *Left to the U5b implementation design.* A rod nearly perpendicular to
     the joint axis (n·Q_x → 0) keeps the rule defined but carries almost no
     axial share; the design may refuse it by name if that is justified.
2. **Rods are admitted with p = 0 at the joint.** This covers rods on an
   unpressurized connector (`pressure_model` `unpressurized`) and rods on a
   constant-effective-area connector that no region lists. With p = 0,
   Pe = 0 and the projected rule applies unchanged.
   - *Basis.* Rods are hardware whose stiffness and reference extension act
     whatever the pressure. JR J-C's mechanical-only tied qualification is
     exactly this case.
   - *Applied.* The controls MO-F, MO-T, RR-U and RR-P (Cases).

## Method

**Rod mechanics** (derived here from JR §5 and Design §9).
- **Attachments.** b = x_node + o, with o the rigid offset from the rod's
  end node. The rod's end nodes are the connector's end nodes.
- **Kinematics.** v_b = u + θ × o; n = (b_j − b_i)/l with l > 0; e = n·(v_bj − v_bi) = C·d.
- **Law.** T_r = k_r(e − e_ref), bilateral. The stiffness is Cᵀk_rC and the
  reference load is +Cᵀk_r·e_ref. The rod's nodal action is CᵀT_r.
- **Connector reduction.** Let m = (o_i + o_j)/2. When the rod's ends are
  the connector's and the connector's offsets are zero, the identity
  b_j − b_i = r + o_j − o_i gives e = c·q with
  c = (Qᵀn, Qᵀ(m × n)). A rod parallel to the axis at transverse offset (y, z)
  in connector axes has c = (1, 0, 0, 0, z, −y). Two consequences follow:
  - a pair at ±a e_y adds K_xx = 2k_r and K_rz = 2k_r·a², and its cross terms
    (∓k_r a) cancel;
  - four rods at ±a e_y and ±a e_z add K_ry = K_rz = 2k_r·a² and no torsion
    stiffness.
- **Pressure.** Rods carry no pressure load. The ledger is t4_u5a's
  representation B: physical caps at the region's transferring terminals,
  ∓p(Ae − Ai) along Q_x at the attachments of a joint the region lists, and
  the Poisson pairs. The tie changes only the stiffness and the interface
  identity: S_pipe = Nw − Pi = Tb + Σ T_r·(n_r·Q_x) − Pe = S_joint (ruling
  1). With p = 0 at the joint, Pe = 0 and Pi = 0 in the face pipes (ruling
  2).

**Two solvers in the generator, compared exactly on every case.**
1. **Nodal stiffness solve.**
   - Members: 3D Euler–Bernoulli frame stubs; the connector's BᵀKB, with B
     evaluated from JR's midpoint kinematics; each rod's Cᵀk_rC, with C
     evaluated from e = n·(v_bj − v_bi).
   - Loads: representation B, the Poisson and thermal strains as equivalent
     eigenload pairs, the rod reference loads and the applied loads.
   - Solution: fraction-free (Bareiss) elimination over Z[π]. Reactions are
     R = Ku − f.
2. **Force method.**
   - The chain is a cantilever from A. When the far end (D, or E in the
     remote-region chain) is anchored, its six actions are the redundants.
   - The pressure loads are written from the free body along the chain
     (closed ends at the region's terminals, the face pair at a listed
     joint). Section actions come from statics and displacements from the
     unit-load method: axial, torsion and bending integrals, with the Poisson
     and thermal strains as initial strains.
   - The connector and its rods form one generalized spring K + Σk_r c cᵀ
     acting on the resultant about the joint midpoint. The rod reference is
     its initial generalized deformation.
   - The connector's end actions come from JR's end-action formulas. The
     pressure RHS is assembled again from the force method's own members.
   - The force method does not use B, C or any assembled matrix.

**Closed forms** are a third check.
- Rows 2 and 4, J4 and e_ref are given as functions of k_R = Σk_r. So are
  MO-T (t4_u5a's thermal relation extended with k_R), the remote-region
  chain (RR-*) and MO-F's axial result.
- OP-* and MO-F give the relative rotations as functions of k_r. The
  generator also checks every node of OP-* and MO-F against a
  cantilever-and-midpoint closed form.
- AS-F* are given as functions of k_r.

They are verified exactly against exact 3D solves (`closed_form_identity`).
As functions of the stiffness, the solves are rational, with numerator degree
at most (number of rods + 1) and denominator degree at most the number of
rods. Every form has degree one, so agreement at (number of rods + 3) distinct
values proves the form identical, not merely equal at the sampled points.
- Rows 2 and 4, correct and J4, are checked at nine k_R values for the
  centreline rod and the pair. The e_ref forms are checked at the same nine
  values for the pair. The correct forms are also checked at k_R = 0.
- MO-T's forms (with and without e_ref) and the remote-region forms (for
  both connector models) are checked at the same nine k_R values for the
  pair.
- The single-offset-rod forms (with and without e_ref), the offset-pair and
  four-rod rotation forms, and MO-F's axial and rotation forms are checked at
  seven k_r values, as the four-rod layout needs (4 + 3).

**Other checks inside the generator** (more than 11,000 exact assertions, counted in `oracle.json` `cross_checks`; any failure stops it):
- **Ledger.** Representation A, with full cap pairs and the joint's Bᵀg_p,
  equals representation B exactly, with and without the Poisson pairs. The
  pressure RHS carries no nodal moment. Global force and moment balance
  holds.
- **Pressure RHS.** Both solvers assemble the same vector, and it equals its
  t4_u5a base's `pressure_rhs.global_n` (below).
- **Interface identity** at both faces of every case, with the projected
  rule.
- **Element level.** Rigid modes: C·d = 0 for all six infinitesimal rigid
  modes of every rod in every case and of a generic skew rod. Connector B
  equals JR's printed B and reproduces the JR refutation's arithmetic case.
  C = c·B, and Bᵀ(Σk c cᵀ)B = ΣCᵀkC.
- **Reversal.** The reversed copies satisfy q′ = Tq and g′ = Tg with
  T = diag(1, −1, 1, 1, −1, 1). Node results, reactions, pipe results, rod
  e and T_r, Tb, S_joint and the pressure RHS are identical. Every other
  published difference is one of these, and the generator checks that this
  list is complete:
  - Q_x (`connector_axis_x_global`) is negated and the connector's end
    nodes swap;
  - the connector endpoint blocks are exchanged;
  - each rod's `direction_global` is negated;
  - each rod's end labels swap: its end_i is at node:C and carries, unchanged,
    the base's end_j action;
  - the interface identity's face labels swap with the connector's ends.
- **Rotation.** The rotated copies have identical local results and
  R-rotated global results.
- **Invariance.** A centreline rod and a ±a pair agree exactly in every
  system result.
- **Bindings.** The t4_u5a values and JR's literal values (below).

    relation gives J3-1T's q, S_joint and Nw. MO-T's k_R → 0 q plus J3-1's q
    equals J3-1T's q (superposition).
- **Remote region (RR-U, RR-P; p = 0 at the joint).** One axial force N runs
  through L, the joint (Tb + ΣT_r) and R. Node D gives Nw_X = N + Pi. The
  pipe laws give elongations N/kp (L and R) and (N + (1 − 2ν)Pi)/kp (X), and
  the anchors make their sum with q zero:
  - (3k + kp + 3k_R)·q = −(1 − 2ν)Pi + 3k_R e_ref, so with e_ref = 0,
    q = −50π/(600000 + 27500000π + 3k_R);
  - N = (k + k_R)·q = S_joint = Nw_L = Nw_R = S_X, R_A = −N and R_E = N;
  - u_B = N/kp, u_C = u_B + q and u_D = u_C + N/kp.

    k_R → ∞: q → 0 and N → −50π/3.
- **J4 tied control** (`j4_tied_control`; the face caps are dropped, so the
  attachments carry ∓pAe).
  - Anchored, at finite k_R:
    - q_w − q = Pi/(D₀ + k_R);
    - Nw_w − Nw = −Pi(kp/2)/(D₀ + k_R);
    - R_A,w − R_A = Pi(kp/2)/(D₀ + k_R);
    - ΣT_w − ΣT = Pi·k_R/(D₀ + k_R).
  - Anchored, as k_R → ∞: q, Nw and the reactions coincide with the correct
    values, while ΣT_r → 800 + 75π instead of 800 − 50π, exactly Pi too much.
  - Free, at every k_R: Nw = Pi and the zero reactions are unchanged,
    q_w − q = Pi/(k + k_R), and ΣT_r → Pe + Pi instead of Pe.
  - S_pipe − S_joint = −Pi in both chains at every k_R.
  - As k_R → 0 the J4 forms reproduce t4_u5a's untied J4 values.

## Binding to model 0.6.0

- A builder authors one 0.6.0 request from each case description. It
  re-expresses the base request (named, with its SHA-256) in 0.6.0 and applies
  the listed changes in 0.6.0's defined rod shape: the hardware, added loads
  and orientation, and for MO-* the removed region, the unpressurized
  connector and MO-T's thermal loads from J3-1T. `hardware` here uses JR's
  proposed vocabulary as a neutral description, not the fixed 0.6.0 shape.
  RR-*'s `member_connector_ids: []` means the region lists no joint (J3-R and
  J3-RP omit the key).
- Oracle values never move. The builder adds each request and its SHA-256
  beside the case's `case_sha256`; only hashes are added.
- t4_u5a's base requests are themselves re-expressed in 0.6.0 by their own
  builder. These descriptions bind to the base's physical content, not its
  0.5.0 bytes.
- If 0.6.0 cannot express a case as described, for example a rod offset frame
  or a moment load, that case is reported back to this reference's owner. It
  is not approximated.

## Re-freeze rules

- No value changes to match a product output. A disagreement goes to this
  reference's owner and its refuter.
- A change to the rod law, the ledger, the kinematics or the fixture is a
  re-freeze with a recorded reason and a new refutation.
- A change to a row convention (frame, sign, node-on-element) re-derives the
  rows. Physical values stay unless the convention changes their meaning.
- Re-authoring in 0.6.0 adds request bytes and hashes only.
- If a bound t4_u5a file changes, the generator refuses until the change is
  examined.

## Covered and not covered

**Covered:**
- `explicit_axial_rods_v1` with `bilateral_linear` rods: centreline, ±a
  pairs about y and z, four rods, one offset rod, and one non-parallel rod
  (admitted, ruling 1);
- axial attachment offsets, pretension, both chains, connector reversal, and
  rigid rotation and translation;
- the finite-rod forms and their k_R → ∞ and k_R → 0 limits;
- the J4 tied control;
- rods with p = 0 at the joint (ruling 2): an unpressurized connector with no
  pressure region under mechanical loads (MO-F) and under uniform stub
  thermal strain (MO-T), and an unpressurized connector (RR-U) or a
  constant-effective-area connector that no region lists (RR-P) beside a
  remote region.

**Not covered**, with reasons and consequences:
- **Rods whose end nodes are not the connector's end nodes.** The c
  reduction (e = c·q) and the face identity S_pipe = Tb + Σ T_r·(n_r·Q_x) − Pe
  assume each rod runs between the connector's end nodes, and the force
  method refuses any other rod. A rod anchored elsewhere (for example on the
  face pipes beyond B or C) puts its force through those pipes, so S_pipe at
  a face no longer equals S_joint. Its expected values are not here: U5b must
  refuse such a rod by name or bring its own reference.
- **JR §5's input refusals:** duplicate rods, zero rod length, missing
  offsets or axes, and contradictory hardware. Their refusal codes and patch
  paths wait for the U5b design and model 0.6.0, so no refusal fixture is
  frozen here. Until then U5b's refusal tests have no oracle patch from this
  set; they are unit tests with the codes the U5b design defines, as t4_u5a
  did for its unpatched refusals.
- **A rod nearly perpendicular to the joint axis.** The projected rule stays
  defined, but the rod carries almost no axial share. Whether to refuse it by
  name is for the U5b implementation design (ruling 1). SK-F (n·Q_x = 4/5)
  is the only non-parallel case.
- **`ideal_inextensible`, the collective axial constraint, tension-only or
  gapped rods, nuts and stops.** They stay refused by name (§9), so there is
  no refusal fixture here: 0.6.0's refusal codes and patch paths are not yet
  defined. The ideal tie appears only as the k_R → ∞ limit. An MPC or
  constraint implementation is a later unit, on the owner's choice only.
- **Penalty stiffness.** None is used; large k_R values are finite rods.
- **Pressure, thermal strain and rods together, q_ref ≠ 0, connector
  offsets, unequal bore, a nonzero allowance or Timoshenko-specific
  responses.** These are t4_u5a's and t4_u8's ground, unchanged by rods.
  MO-T has thermal strain with rods at p = 0 only. Every case here is
  shear-neutral.
- **Finite rotation and rod slackening under compression.** The law is
  bilateral and linear. These rods are in compression, which the bilateral
  law permits: OP-Z's `rod:yp` (−101.72 N), MO-T's rods (−3099.46 N each)
  and RR-*'s rods (−10.686 N each).

## Inferred conventions (confirm with the consumer)

- `connector_axes_global` is the matrix Q whose columns are the connector
  axes, stored row-major. This is inferred from J3-1R, where the chain
  direction is the first column.
- The connector endpoint rows are Bᵀg, node on element, global. This is
  inferred from t4_u5a's J3-1R and J3-1V oracle rows.
- Rod nodal actions use the same node-on-element sign (CᵀT_r), with moments
  about the node. §9 requires only a rod force row per rod (T_r).
- Pipe end actions are node on element, in global and member-local axes
  (x along the member as authored, y = y_reference). This is T4-I8's
  convention as described in t4_u6's README.
- A rod's end_i is at the connector's end_i node, and reversal swaps both.
  e and T_r are invariant under swapping a rod's ends; only the nodal-action
  labels swap.
- Pretension is a negative reference extension, from T_r = k_r(e − e_ref).
- `offset_global_m` is the rigid offset vector from the end node, and
  `offset_connector_axes_m` = Qᵀ times it. If 0.6.0 expresses rod offsets in
  another frame, such as the node's initial axes, the builder converts.
- For an unpressurized connector (MO-*, RR-U), S_joint and Pe = 0 are frozen
  as face-equilibrium quantities. t4_u5a publishes no `pe_x` or `s_x` row for
  an unpressurized connector (J3-R), so whether U5b publishes them there is
  the U5b design's choice. The identity is checked on S_joint either way.

python3 -I generators/u5b_reference.py          # check: rebuild in memory, run every cross-check, compare byte for byte

Check mode fails on any cross-check disagreement, any byte difference or any
unexpected file. It runs in a few seconds and needs no build.
