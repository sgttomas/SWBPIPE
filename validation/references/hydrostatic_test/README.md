# Hydrostatic test references: held supports, test contents and test pressure

Frozen, independently derived expectations for the hydrostatic test load case
on the exact pressure route (EX): DEL-05-01 REQ-014, AC-010 and VER-007, and
DEL-04-07 REQ-011, AC-012 and VER-010. They exist before the engine work that
will be judged against them.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code and uses the Python standard library only. Its sources
are the mechanics below and the governing scope text: the definitions of
*test condition*, *held support* and *mass basis* in
`execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-01/ScopeOfWork.md`, REQ-011 of
`execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/ScopeOfWork.md`, the section bases and thick-wall
stresses of `execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/ScopeOfWork.md`, the mass per length of
`execution/PKG-03/DEL-03-08/ScopeOfWork.md` and the spring, variable spring and
constant-effort laws of `execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-03/ScopeOfWork.md`. All inputs
are invented. The package states mechanics only: it contains no test pressure
factor, test procedure, code rule or tolerance, and no value in it states or
implies compliance. The comparison tolerance is the consuming scope's.

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author. They
have not yet been independently refuted.

### Number representation

Areas and second moments carry π; spring rates, forces and pressures do not.
A reaction is therefore in general a ratio of polynomials in π. The generator
computes in that field exactly (polynomials with rational coefficients,
reduced by their gcd) and evaluates only at output. Each value record carries
`exact` (a rational, `q*pi`, `q/pi` or `(N(pi))/(D(pi))`), `decimal` (40
significant digits) and `value` (the nearest binary64). π is bounded by
rationals 2·10⁻¹⁰⁰ apart (Machin's formula in integers, checked against the
first 49 decimals); a binary64 value is emitted only when both bounds give the
same double, and a sign only when both bounds agree.

## Models

Every model is a straight run along global X with nodes at x = 0, a, 2a, …
named `node:a`, `node:b`, …, and one pipe per span (`pipe:a-b`, …), all of
the section above. The run is one pressure region carrying the case's
pressure and closed at both ends by caps carried by the pipe.

**Model constraint.** Within the pressure region each straight run is one
member, or members joined at interior nodes only where their outside
diameter, wall and direction are identical. Here every interior node is a
support node between two pipes of identical section and direction, so the
engine admits the models whatever the pressure-elongation setting.

| End support | Node | Restrained | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `support:a` | first node | ux, uy, uz, rx | the only axial and torsional restraint |
| last support (`support:b` in M1, `support:c` in M2, `support:e` in M4) | last node | uy, uz | |

Rotations ry and rz are free at every node (pinned for bending). Every
interior support acts on uz only.

| Model | Spans | Interior supports |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | one span of L1 | none |
| M2-SPRING | two spans of a | `support:b`: spring k |
| M2-VSPRING | two spans of a | `support:b`: variable spring k_v with cold load F_c |
| M2-CE | two spans of a | `support:b`: constant-effort support F_ce |
| M4 | four spans of a | `support:b` spring k_B; `support:c` constant-effort F_ce,C; `support:d` variable spring k_v,D with cold load F_c,D |

Support laws (DEL-04-03), as the force of the support on the pipe along +Z,
with u the node's displacement along Z: spring −k·u; variable spring
F_c − k_v·u; constant-effort F_ce. A held support (DEL-05-01, test condition
`springs_and_constant_effort`) is replaced on uz by a rigid restraint at
u = 0 from the authored geometry; neither its stiffness nor its force acts.

## Loads of a case

A case applies its weight per length (w_ordinary, or w_test in a test case)
as a uniform load along −Z on every pipe, and its pressure (p_ordinary, or
p_test in a test case) to the region. The gravity and the pressure are the
case's only loads.

## Pressure elongation

A change in review makes pressure elongation on the exact route a per-case
option (off: the closed-end free pressure strain is omitted and pressure
thrust at unbalanced locations is applied; on: the closed-end free strain
(1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s) is included). Every row of this package is the same
under both settings, and each carries `"pressure_elongation": "independent"`:

1. **Transverse rows** (every `fz`, `fy`, `mx` and `uz`). Pressure acts on a
   straight run only axially: the end-cap thrusts ±p·A_bore along X and, when
   on, a uniform axial free strain. In small-displacement linear theory a
   straight member's axial and bending equations are uncoupled, so no
   pressure term reaches uz, ry, uy, rz or rx. The vertical reactions and
   displacements are those of the weight alone.
2. **Axial reaction `support:a.fx`.** `support:a` is the run's only axial
   restraint, so the axial system is statically determinate. The cap thrusts
   at the two closed ends are equal and opposite (−p·A_bore at the first node,
   +p·A_bore at the last), and a free strain in an axially unrestrained run
   produces no force, so ΣF_x = 0 and `support:a.fx` = 0 under either
   setting. The run is free to grow axially. (Open reading 2 states the
   assumption about the caps.)
3. **Thick-wall stresses.** In a straight cylinder with uniform axial strain
   ε_z (generalized plane strain), u_r = C₁r + C₂/r, σ_r = 2(λ + μ)C₁ −
   2μC₂/r² + λε_z and σ_θ = 2(λ + μ)C₁ + 2μC₂/r² + λε_z. The two boundary
   tractions σ_r(r_i) = −p and σ_r(R) = 0 fix C₂ and the combination
   2(λ + μ)C₁ + λε_z, and σ_θ depends only on those, so the hoop and radial
   values below do not depend on ε_z and hence not on the setting.

Rows that would depend on the setting are not given: the axial displacement
of the free end, member axial forces and the axial membrane stress σ_m.

## Item 1: held supports on two equal spans

Remove the middle support: a simple span 2a under w. With deflection
downward positive:

- δ₀, the midspan deflection under w: 5·w·a⁴/(24·EI), plus w·a²/(2·GA_s) where
  Timoshenko is selected;
- f, the midspan deflection per unit upward force at midspan: a³/(6·EI), plus
  a/(2·GA_s) where Timoshenko is selected;
- k, the middle spring's rate.

The middle reaction R_B (support on pipe, +Z) and the end reactions
R_A = R_C = w·a − R_B/2:

| Middle support | R_B | u_z(B) |
|---|---|---|
| held (any family), Euler–Bernoulli | δ₀/f = (10/8)·w·a, so R_A = (3/8)·w·a | 0 |
| spring, not held | δ₀/(f + 1/k) | −(δ₀ − f·R_B) |
| variable spring, not held | (δ₀ + F_c/k_v)/(f + 1/k_v) | −(δ₀ − f·R_B) |
| constant-effort, not held | F_ce | −(δ₀ − f·F_ce) |

The variable-spring form follows from δ_B = δ₀ − f·R_B and R_B = F_c + k_v·δ_B;
F_c = 0 gives the spring's form, and k → ∞ the rigid prop.

δ₀ ≈ 4.891837e−3 m and f ≈ 5.810921e−7 m/N (Euler–Bernoulli);

**Flexibility method** (used for every model): base span L = n·a, simply
supported; deflection downward positive. Under w,
δ₀(x) = w·x(L³ − 2Lx² + x³)/(24EI) [+ w·x(L − x)/(2GA_s)]. For a unit upward
force at ξ, the deflection at x ≤ ξ is b·x(L² − b² − x²)/(6EI·L) with
b = L − ξ [+ b·x/(L·GA_s)], symmetric in x and ξ. With X_j the interior
support forces on the pipe, δ_i = δ₀(x_i) − Σ_j f_ij·X_j and each support's
law (held: δ_i = 0; spring: X_i = k·δ_i; variable spring: X_i = F_c + k_v·δ_i;
constant-effort: X_i = F_ce) give a linear system; the end reactions follow
from moment and force equilibrium.

All are exact equalities in Q(π) (379 recorded, all agree; the last records the binary64 input check):

- every case and every negative control is solved by three or four routes:
  the closed form (two spans), the flexibility method, a beam stiffness solve

## Open readings

Each is a place where the scope text could carry a value two ways. The
package computes under the first reading; the choice is the integrator's.

1. **A held support's published reaction.** DEL-05-01: "Its reaction is the
   replacement restraint's, published under the support's own identity";
   DEL-04-07: "a held constant-effort support publishes no applied force".
   Computed: the row `support:b.fz` is the replacement's force on the pipe,
   positive along +Z (the constant-effort support's positive degree of
   freedom), and the support's applied force is zero. Other readings: the row
   is the load on the support (sign reversed), or the applied-force row is
   absent rather than zero.
2. **End-cap thrust at a supported dead end.** Computed: both ends of the run
   are closed by caps carried by the pipe, so the thrusts cancel and
   `support:a.fx` = 0. If the cap at the first node were taken as closed
   against `support:a` (no thrust on the pipe there), `support:a.fx` would be
   −p·A_bore: ≈ −419 825.0 N at p_test and −270 298.3 N at p_ordinary. The
   vertical rows and stresses are unaffected either way.
3. **Held choice with nothing to hold.** HT-1S-TEST selects
   `springs_and_constant_effort` on a model with no spring or constant-effort
   support. Computed: the result states the choice and an empty set of held
   supports. Other reading: it states only that nothing was held.
4. **Section that carries contents.** The mass-basis name of DEL-05-01, the
   pressure-bore row of DEL-05-03's table and DEL-03-08's effective wall all
   put contents on the bore of radius R − t_r. Computed on that bore. The
   weaker reading, contents on the nominal bore (radius R − t), gives
   w_ordinary ≈ 1454.779 N/m and w_test ≈ 1534.555 N/m
   (`alternative_readings` in `references.json`).
5. **Scope of a refusal.** The refusal scenarios say the test case is
   refused with no partial result. Whether the other cases of the same
   document still solve, and whether an imposed displacement at a held
   spring's base is a contradiction to refuse or moot (the spring's stiffness
   does not act), the text does not say; no value here depends on it.
