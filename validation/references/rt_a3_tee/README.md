These are frozen, independently derived expectations for RT-A3 increment 1:
a tee built from three pressure regions that meet at one node, on the exact
pressure route
([R-4](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Work/route-unification-r4.md) §00
corrections C-1 and C-3, §2.1 and §3.1 references T-1 to T-4;
[pressure contract](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Work/pressure-contract.md),

[section-bases-and-timoshenko.md](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/Work/section-bases-and-timoshenko.md)

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code or output. All model data are invented, and no value comes
from a standard, catalog or vendor source. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2;
claims taxonomy per DEC-081). The tee's SIFs are user values; no code stress is
claimed.

**Status:** candidate references. Two exact formulations agree on every
published value, and R-4's printed values reproduce (one sign note below). They
have not yet been independently refuted. No product test binds them yet.

## Model

- **Material.** E = 200 GPa, ν = 3/10, G = E/(2(1+ν)) = 10¹²/13 Pa.
  Small-displacement, linear elastic, homogeneous isotropic, Euler–Bernoulli.
- **Geometry.** T = (0,0,0), A = (−4,0,0), B = (4,0,0), C = (0,3,0) m. Pipes
  pipe:A (T→A), pipe:B (T→B), pipe:C (T→C), y_reference (0,0,1).
- **Sections.** Header D = 0.2 m, t = 0.01 m (pipe:A, pipe:B); branch
  D = 0.1 m, t = 0.005 m (pipe:C). c_m = c_a = 0 unless the case says otherwise.
- **Pressure.** p = 10 MPa in three regions, one per pipe. Each region ends at T
  and at its far node, with every terminal `transfers_to_wall` unless the case
  says otherwise.
- **Tee.** A tee component at T with header pipe ref pipe:A, branch pipe ref
  pipe:C, i_header = 2.1 and i_branch = 1.6. The header row is published on
  pipe:A's end at T and the branch row on pipe:C's end at T. pipe:B's end at T
  gets no row.
- **Bore step (T3).** S = (0,0,0), P = (−4,0,0), Q = (4,0,0). pipe:1 (S→P) has
  the header section and pipe:2 (S→Q) the branch section, one region each. There
  is no tee and no reducer component; the bore changes at the node.

## Formulas used

**Sections (D-5, section-bases §1).** For D, t, c_m, c_a:
- r_o = D/2 for both sections;
- nominal wall t, r_i,n = r_o − t;
- reduced wall t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, r_i,r = r_o − t_r;
- each annulus: A = π(r_o² − r_i²), I = π(r_o⁴ − r_i⁴)/4, J = 2I, Z = I/r_o;
- pressure bore r_i,r, with A_i = π r_i,r².

The design is quoted: "stiffness (A, I, J and the Timoshenko shear area), pipe
metal mass, the area As in ε_p = (1−2ν)pAi/(E·As), nominal-basis stresses" use
the nominal section; "reduced-basis stresses" use the reduced section; "end-cap
and joint-face area Ai = π r_i,r²" uses the pressure bore. So stiffness uses
the nominal wall, and mill tolerance enters only the reduced wall and the bore.

**Member law (R-4 §00, §2.1).** Each member k of a pressurized region carries
the wall force N_w and obeys

  E A_n ε = N_w − 2ν p A_i.

The Poisson pair is an axial eigenstrain ε₀ = −2ν p A_i/(E A_n). The fixed
member holds N_w = 2ν p A_i. The mechanical force is N_m = E A_n ε, so
N_w = N_m + 2ν p A_i. The effective force is N_el = N_w − p A_i, which is
S = N_m + (2ν − 1) p A_i (section-bases §1). For a free closed leg N_w = p A_i,
and the elongation is the closed-pipe value ε_p = (1−2ν) p A_i/(E A_n).
Shear, bending and torsion are elastic: EI, GJ on the nominal section.

**Caps.** At a `transfers_to_wall` terminal of member k at node n, a nodal load
−p A_i,k s_k acts, where s_k is the unit vector from n into the member, so the
load points out of the pipe. At a `separately_supported_or_compensated`
terminal no cap acts.

**Junction resultant.** The fitting's wetted interior closed by its openings is
a closed surface, so the net pressure force on it is zero. The fluid force on
the fitting body is therefore minus the force on the openings:

  F_T = −Σ_k p A_i,k s_k.

That is the sum of the transferring caps at the node. For the tee
(s_A = −x̂, s_B = +x̂, s_C = +ŷ), the header terms cancel and F_T = −p A_i,b ŷ.
For a collinear bore step (s₁ = −x̂, s₂ = +x̂), F_S = p(A_i,1 − A_i,2) x̂, as C-3
states.

**Stress rows.**
- Axial membrane stress, per basis b: σ_m = N_w / A_b.
- Tee intensified row, per basis b, at the referenced end:
  σ = i · hypot(M_y, M_z) / Z_b, where (M_y, M_z) are the member-axis
  bending moments of that end. Torsion and axial force are excluded.

## Formulations

**Route 1: direct stiffness.** The 12 × 12 Euler–Bernoulli member stiffness is
formed in member axes. Axial is EA/L and torsion GJ/L. Bending is 12EI/L³,
6EI/L², 4EI/L and 2EI/L, with the x–z plane's coupling signs reversed. It is
rotated with the exact direction cosines (x from `from` to `to`, y the
y_reference less its x part, z = x × y).

The load vector holds the caps, the mechanical loads and minus the held
end actions (−N₀ at i, +N₀ at j, N₀ = 2ν p A_i) of the Poisson eigenstrain.
The free DOFs are solved by exact Gauss–Jordan elimination. Since K = π·K′ with
K′ rational, each power of π is solved separately. Then:
- end actions are K_e u_e plus the held end actions;
- reactions are K u − f at anchored DOFs;
- N_m = E A_n (u_j − u_i)·x / L.

**Route 2: closed forms per case.**
- *Anchored hub, free legs* (T1, T1-CA, T2, T2-CTRL, T3-FREE, T3-COMP). Each
  leg is a cantilever. The far node's loads P (its cap, if any) give, by
  statics, F_j = P, F_i = −P and M_i = −L e × P, with N_w = P·e. The tip moves by
  L ε e + L³P⊥/(3EI), with ε = (N_w − 2νpA_i)/(EA_n). The hub reaction is
  R = −P_hub + Σ F_i, with the moments summed the same way.
- *Every node anchored* (T3-HELD). ε = 0 gives N_w = 2ν p A_i, with no bending.
  Each reaction balances its node's cap and member forces.
- *Free hub, all far ends anchored* (T4, T4-CMCA). The net hub load is
  −(1−2ν) p A_i,b ŷ. The header pair's caps and Poisson pulls cancel, so only y
  is loaded. The hub moves v_T ŷ, with θ = 0 by the x → −x symmetry, as in
  R-4 T-4:
  v_T = −(1−2ν) p A_i,b / (24 E I_h/a³ + E A_b/L_b), with a = 4 and L_b = 3.
  The generator also evaluates R-4's printed radius form
  −(1−2ν) p r_bi² / (E[6(r_o⁴ − r_i⁴)/a³ + (r_bo² − r_bi²)/L_b]) and checks
  equality. It takes r_bi as the bore in the load and the nominal radius in
  the stiffness.
- *Free hub, header anchored, branch free* (T5, T5-CA). The branch is
  determinate. It passes F_C plus its far cap p A_i,b ŷ to T, and the cap at
  T cancels that cap. So the hub load is F_C plus the moment 3ŷ × F_C. The
  equal header legs at ±x̂ have cancelling translation–rotation couplings, so
  the hub stiffness is diagonal:
  (2EA/a, 24EI/a³, 24EI/a³, 2GJ/a, 8EI/a, 8EI/a).
  C moves by rigid transport plus the cantilever laws, rotating by
  L²/(2EI) e × P⊥.
- *Fixed-far end actions* (route 2, free hub). These use the textbook laws for
  near-end translation d and rotation θ:
  - F = (EA/L) d∥ + (12EI/L³) d⊥ + (6EI/L²) θ⊥ × e − 2νpA_i e;
  - M = (GJ/L) θ∥ + (4EI/L) θ⊥ + (6EI/L²) e × d⊥.

  The far end follows by member statics: F_far = −F, M_far = −M + L e × F. The
  hub's equilibrium is checked, not imposed.

**Checks.**
- Both routes publish the same key set, and every value agrees exactly.
- In route 1, every free DOF is in exact equilibrium, the global force and
  moment balance holds, and N_w = N_m + 2νpA_i on every member.
- In route 2, the hub is in equilibrium and both ends give the same N_w.
- R-4's values reproduce, symbolically and as printed decimals.

If any check disagrees, the generator writes and verifies nothing.

## Assumptions (product semantics are not decided here)

- **Terminals at T.** R-4 writes T-1 with a `junction` token at T. Today's
  representation, three `transfers_to_wall` terminals, has the same mechanics
  (R-4 §3.1: the token "is treated in the ledger exactly as `transfers_to_wall`").
- **T2-CTRL.** T2-CTRL is a value for a mutant that drops one junction cap. The
  pressure contract records that the product refuses a compensated terminal at a
  shared node by name, so it is not an admitted case. T2's compensated terminal
  is a single region's closure at an unshared node.
- **Quantity names and frames.** The names and frames are the reference's
  own. The end-action axial component is the physical wall force (−N_w at
  `from`, +N_w at `to`), and N_el and N_m are published separately. Which axial
  quantity a product end row carries, and the row kinds, ids and the ends that
  get rows, are the product's choice.
- **Tee rows.** The tee row is published through its exact square. Each row
  also carries |M|. Its value is unchanged under any sign convention for the
  moments.
- **Bore change.** T3 changes bore at a node with no reducer member, so it
  tests the junction resultant only. The product's reducer representation
  (C-4) is not modelled.
- **Criterion.** `criterion_proposed` and `zero_scale` are a proposal; the
  binding chooses its own.

## Exact form

Each value is a sum of terms q·π^k with q rational, written as `q`, `q*pi`,
`q*pi^k`, `q/pi` or `q/pi^k` and joined by ` + `. Pressure forces carry k = 1,
mechanical forces k = 0 and motions under force k = −1. A tee row is
`sqrt(<exact>)` with `exact_square`. Each value has a 40-digit `decimal` and
`value`, its nearest binary64. The binary64 rounding is decided by rational
bounds on π that are 10⁻⁹⁰ apart.
