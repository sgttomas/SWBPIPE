# W-16 references: equivalent-static generation on the exact route

Frozen, independently derived expectations for DEC-068 seismic and wind
generation on the exact pressure route (EX) and its pressure-free twin (PF)
(W-16 slice c; DEL-05-01 Design
[`exact-equivalent-static.md`](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-01/Design/exact-equivalent-static.md)
§5). One invented cantilever carries a lateral seismic g-factor, or wind over
its whole span or over part of it. Each case is solved by
[`core/product_physics/tests/w16_exact_equivalent_static.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/tests/w16_exact_equivalent_static.rs)
and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or
example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods
are the textbook closed forms cited below. The product supplies no seismic or
wind coefficient, profile or default: the g-factor, gravity, pressure, shape
factor, direction and marked spans are the user's inputs. Standard claim fence
applies (F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted.

## Model and inputs

A cantilever along global X: A(0, 0, 0) is the anchor `support:a`, B(5/2, 0, 0)
is free; one pipe `pipe:a-b` with `y_reference` (0, 1, 0), so local y is
global Y and local z is global Z. L = 5/2 m, so a missing length factor shows.

## Mass bases and the PF/EX difference

With t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, r_n = R − t and r_p = R − t_r:
- **EX** (D-5): μ_EX = π[ρ_m(R² − r_n²) + ρ_c·r_p² + ρ_i((R + t_i)² − R²)], stiffness
  I_n = π(R⁴ − r_n⁴)/4 on the nominal wall; stresses on the nominal and reduced
  (t_r) sections.
- **PF** (no allowance): metal, contents and stiffness on the single wall t − c_m.
- The documented difference μ_EX − μ_PF = π[ρ_m·c_m(D − 2t + c_m) + ρ_c{2c_a(R − t + c_m) + c_a²}]
  is cross-checked against the direct difference.

| μ_PF | 298727/12800·π ≈ 73.3186 kg/m |

## Closed forms

Load q over x ∈ [aL, bL] (seismic: a = 0, b = 1):
- |R| = q·L·(b − a) and |M₀| = q·L²·(b² − a²)/2;
- u_L = q·L⁴·[(b³ − a³) − (b⁴ − a⁴)/4]/(6EI); Timoshenko adds q·L²/(2G·A_s),
  A_s = κ_E·A_n (full span only here; partial spans on Timoshenko are in `../w16_timoshenko_partial_wind/`);
- θ_L = q·L³·(b³ − a³)/(6EI), a section rotation (no shear term);
- V(x) = q·(bL − max(x, aL))⁺ and M(x) = q·[((bL − x)⁺)² − ((aL − x)⁺)²]/2;
- bending normal stress M/Z with Z = I/R on each published basis.

The fractions bound the interval; they do not multiply the intensity a second
time. Sources: Timoshenko & Gere, *Mechanics of Materials*, cantilever
deflection; Timoshenko, *Strength of Materials*, shear deflection of beams.
κ_E is the energy-matched annular factor of DEL-05-03 Design
`section-bases-and-timoshenko.md` §3.1.

## Cross-check

Each closed form is checked against an independent route: load resultants by
direct integration of the load, and the tip motion by the unit-load method
(∫M·(L − x)/EI, ∫M/EI and ∫V/(G·A_s)), with Simpson's rule on each interval
between load breakpoints (exact for these integrands). The section actions at
end_i are checked against the support actions. κ_E's cancellation-free form is
checked against its contract form. All 69 checks agree exactly.

The values are rounded here; `references.json` holds the exact values. Every
result is q·π^k with q rational and k ∈ {−1, 0, 1}: seismic forces carry π;
wind translations, rotations and stresses carry 1/π.

Each case publishes the six support components at A, the six motion
components at A (zero) and B, the six section components at stations 0, 1/4,
1/2, 3/4 and 1, and the active bending normal stress at those stations on each
published basis (EX: `nominal_section` and `reduced_section`; PF:
`preview_section`).

## Product binding and criterion

| Route | As authored |
|---|---|
| EX | `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` with the case's `shear_model`, `corrosion_allowance` 1/500 m, E/ν materials, `pressure_regions: []`, the case's `equivalent_static` |
| PF | no contract or allowance, E and G, the same `equivalent_static` |

Seismic is `equivalent_static.seismic` {gravity_acceleration, g_factor_y}.
Full-span wind is `exposed_pipe_refs: ["pipe:a-b"]`; partial-span wind is one
`exposed_spans` entry with the fractions.

The criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale, the
largest nonzero magnitude of that row's family (force, moment, translation,
rotation or stress) in the case.

The test runs every case in both solver modes and reports the largest relative
errors per route, and the PF/EX seismic support-force difference against
−(μ_EX − μ_PF)·k·g·L.
