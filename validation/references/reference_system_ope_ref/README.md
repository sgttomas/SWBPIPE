reference-system specification §8.1, §8.2 and §9.2) once its fixture holds RS-OPE-REF, the operating thermal state
on the 20 °C modulus, and RS-EXP takes RS-OPE-REF − RS-SUS. They replace nothing: the packages

through `openpipestress-runner` in both solver modes and checks the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs no product code and reads no
fixture model file and no product output; its inputs are the specification's statements (§3.2, §4.1, §5.1, §8,
§8.1), transcribed in it. All model data are invented (spec §1). No equation, value or example from the
`domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods are linear interpolation, the secant expansion law
and elementary statics. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2). Agreement here is evidence, not acceptance
(F-PIP-3). These references state member inputs and equilibrium; they carry no support-state, margin or
integrity-standing value for any case or combination.

## Member states

From the specification alone:

- **Material RS-M1** (§4.1): points 20 °C 200 GPa, 100 °C 195 GPa, 200 °C 189 GPa, 300 °C 182 GPa; ν = 0.30 on
  each; one expansion law, `engineering_secant`, datum 20 °C, constant coefficient α = 12.0 × 10⁻⁶ 1/K.
- **Temperatures** (§8): installation 20 °C; operating 210 °C on every member except the replaced span RS-P180
  (§5.1), 25 members in all. Kelvin values are °C + 273.15.
- **RS-OPE** (§8.1) selects each member's modulus at 210 °C by piecewise linear interpolation between the
  bracketing points 200 °C and 300 °C: fraction 1/10, E = 189 + (182 − 189)/10 = 188.3 GPa, RS-P180 included.
- **RS-OPE-REF** (§8.1) selects the 20 °C point on every member: E = 200 GPa. Each of its 25 members with an
  operating temperature states the specification's reason for a modulus that is not the one at its temperature,
  `RS-OPE-REF takes the operating thermal state on the 20 degC modulus so that the expansion comparison RS-EXP has
  both operands on one material basis (spec §8.1)`.
- **Shear modulus**: G = E / (2 (1 + ν)), from the selected pair: 188.3/2.6 GPa and 200/2.6 GPa.
- **Thermal strain**: the secant law gives the free length at T as L_d (1 + α (T − T_d)). From installation T_i to
  operating T_o the strain is (1 + α (T_o − T_d)) / (1 + α (T_i − T_d)) − 1. With T_i = T_d = 20 °C the denominator
  is exactly 1, so the ratio form and the plain difference form give the same value, α · 190 K = 57/25000 =
  0.00228, on every member of both cases with an operating temperature. RS-P180 keeps zero eigenstrain in both.

The two cases therefore carry the same thermal strain and differ in modulus only, by the ratio 1883/2000
(`distinguishing`). A reader of the published load reference state tells them apart on these grounds: the
consumed point(s), E and G, and RS-OPE-REF's stated reason.

## EQ-1 and EQ-2 for RS-OPE-REF

RS-OPE-REF's weight is generated over the same members, with the same gravity and component weights, as RS-SUS,
RS-OPE and RS-SAM (§8.1). A free-length thermal state is an eigenstrain and applies no external load, whatever the
modulus; every pressure closure transfers to the wall, so each region is self-equilibrated (§9.2); RS-CE1's
9000 N is the same support action in every case and adds no weight. So RS-OPE-REF's vertical reaction sum and its
reaction moment about the origin are those of the other three cases.

The values are not recomputed here: they are [`../reference_system_r1_ce/references.json`](../reference_system_r1_ce/references.json)'s
`key_values`, carried into `eq1_eq2.key_values` record for record. The generator binds them by checking that file's
bytes against that package's own `SHA256SUMS` (the hash is recorded as `bound_sha256`), that it states EQ-1 and
EQ-2 for exactly RS-SUS, RS-OPE and RS-SAM, that its W_total is the exact sum of its member and component weight
records, and that its weight and reaction moments follow from its first moment.

## EQ-3

RS-EXP is a `result_state_subtraction` of RS-OPE-REF and RS-SUS (§8.2). Both operands select the 20 °C point on
every member, so the difference is one of two states of one stiffness. The identity is the tranche-1 package's
EQ-3 on these operands: on every published signed component row RS-EXP = RS-OPE-REF − RS-SUS; magnitude rows
(resultant displacement, reaction magnitude) are recomputed from the combined components; rows the combination
withholds, such as tie-rod forces, are excluded and listed. RS-EXP declares no `difference_basis` (§10).

The identity establishes the combination's algebra and nothing about either solve. RS-EXP is the difference of two
independently solved equilibria on one material basis, labelled `RS-EXP expansion: RS-OPE-REF - RS-SUS, the
difference of two solved states on the 20 degC modulus`; it is not the solution of the system under the load
difference, and where supports respond nonlinearly the two need not agree.

## Controls

The cross-checks in `references.json` (every one must agree for `--write`): RS-OPE's 188.3 GPa; RS-OPE-REF's
200 GPa on every member; the strain α · 190 with the ratio form's denominator exactly 1; the same strain in both
cases; RS-P180's zero eigenstrain; the moduli ratio 1883/2000 on every member; G = E / 2.6; 25 members with an
operating temperature; and the four bindings to the r1_ce package above.

## Criterion

EQ-1, EQ-2 and EQ-3 use the r1_ce package's criterion (DEC-024/DEC-026): a nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|, a zero row when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale. For the member states the consumer states its
own a priori rounding bound; the values here are exact rationals with their nearest binary64.
