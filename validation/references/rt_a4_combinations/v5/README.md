## Closed forms

Euler-Bernoulli, small displacement, A = π(D² − d²)/4, I = π(D⁴ − d⁴)/64.
Each load q = w·g is split into its axial part (q·e)e and transverse part
q_t along the member axis e = (3/5, 4/5, 0): tip displacement
(q·e)L²/(2EA)·e + q_t·L⁴/(8EI) (the transverse tip deflection wL⁴/8EI),
tip rotation (e × q_t)L³/(6EI), anchor force qL (wL) and anchor moment
(L/2·e) × qL (wL²/2 transverse). The X and Y cases both excite the tip's X and
Y displacements, its Z rotation and the anchor's Z moment, where the absolute
sum and the SRSS differ.
A finite-element cantilever with cubic beam elements and consistent element
loads reproduces these nodal values exactly, so the comparison tests the
combination, not a discretization.

For factors fₖ over the three cases and each tip or anchor component q, the
absolute sum is Σₖ|fₖqₖ| and the SRSS is √Σₖ(fₖqₖ)²; the tip displacement and
anchor force and moment magnitudes are the norm of the combination's own
components. Signs do not enter.

## Independence and precision

Every value is an exact rational in which π is a rational within 10⁻⁸⁰ of π
(Machin's formula on integers). A root is the integer square root at a scale
of 10⁻¹⁶⁰. Each published value is the nearest binary64 and a
30-significant-digit decimal. Criterion: relative 10⁻⁹; a zero expectation is
compared with 10⁻⁹ times the family's largest case value.
