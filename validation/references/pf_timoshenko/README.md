## Method

- **Exact arithmetic.** Every value is an element of Q(π) in lowest terms
  (t4_u8's representation and text forms). Decimals carry 40 significant digits
  (π by Machin, 140-digit context); `expected` is the binary64 rounding.
- **Inputs.** Decimal inputs are exact rationals (r_i = 9/100 m, and so on); the
  requests author the nearest binary64 values (relative effects of order
  1e-16). E = 200e9 Pa and G = 77e9 Pa are exact binary64 integers, so the
  derived ν is exact for the authored values. The φ = 2 cases author dyadic OD,
  wall and L, exact in binary64.
- **ν and κ_E.** On PF, ν = E/(2G) − 1 from the authored, unscaled E and G,
  computed exactly (pf-timoshenko.md §2). κ_E = 6(1+ν)²(1+m²)² /
  [(7+14ν+8ν²)(1−m²)² + 48m²(1+ν)²], m = r_i/r_o, on PF's single annulus
  r_o = OD/2, r_i = OD/2 − (t − c_m); A, I = π(r_o⁴−r_i⁴)/4 and J = 2I are of
  that annulus; A_s = κ_E·A. The generator checks that this form equals
  SHEAR_REFERENCE CONTRACT's form on a grid and reproduces t4_u8's frozen
  κ_E(9/10, 3/10) = 16609827/33068756.
- **Element.** section-bases-and-timoshenko.md §3.3, per bending plane:
  φ = 12EI/(G·A_s·L²), k_vv = 12EI/(L³(1+φ)), k_vθ = 6EI/(L²(1+φ)),
  k_θθ = (4+φ)EI/(L(1+φ)), k_θθ' = (2−φ)EI/(L(1+φ)); axial EA/L and torsion
  GJ/L with the metal area and no κ. Plane map as t4_u8: v = u_y with θ = r_z,
  v = u_z with θ = −r_y. Local axes: x from i to j, y = `y_reference` less its x
  part, normalized, z = x × y. Every member's global K annihilates its six rigid
  motions (checked on the skew run). The Euler–Bernoulli twin is the same
  element with no shear term.
- **Loads.** Full-span uniform loads (authored `distributed_force` and
  `weight`) use [wL/2, wL²/12, wL/2, −wL²/12] per plane and wL/2 axially at each
  end (§3.4). Thermal loads are the held end forces ∓s·E·A·αΔT
  (rigid-component-stiffness-scaling.md §2.1 for s). Nodal loads enter directly.
- **Supports and solve.** Restraints and imposed support displacements are
  prescribed DOFs, eliminated by partition, K_ff u_f = f_f − K_fc u_c; springs
  add to the diagonal. Reactions are the support-on-pipe actions R = K u − f at
  the prescribed DOFs, −k u for a spring. Every solve is checked for global force
  and moment balance about the origin and for a symmetric K.
- **Nonlinear supports.** DEC-067 as stated in kd5-nonlinear-active-set.md §0,
  with r = (K u − f)_d the support-on-pipe force:
  - one-way, active when positive: active iff r > 0, released iff u_d > 0 (and
    the mirror for negative);
  - gap closing in −, clearance g: closed (u_d = −g) iff r > 0, open iff
    u_d > −g (and the mirror for +);
  - friction with an explicit normal N > 0: sticking (u_t = 0) iff
    |r_t| < μN; sliding with the applied force −dir·μN at the DOF iff
    dir·u_t > 0; inactive is excluded for N > 0.

  Every combination of states is solved exactly. Each case has exactly one state
  that meets every condition strictly and none that meets them only with a zero
  margin; that state's rows are frozen and named in `support_states`. A
  nonlinear support's reaction slot is its constraint force when engaged, the
  applied friction force when sliding and zero when released.
- **Recovery.** f = K d − feq with the same feq; stations are the +x-face
  section action of the i-side portion, from statics (§3.5).

## Discriminators and controls

Every row of both twins carries `discriminating`: true where the Timoshenko
and Euler–Bernoulli expectations lie more than 1e-7 relative apart (a zero
against a nonzero counts). `PFT-AUTHNU` rows also carry
`authored_nu_discriminating`, against the solve that takes κ_E from the
authored ν. Each Timoshenko load case lists the mutants that move its rows by
more than 1e-7, with their wrong values. The catalogue
(`discriminator_catalogue`) covers: the Cowper, 5/6 and κ = 1 factors; κ_E or
the whole section on the nominal wall (mill tolerance ignored); the authored
ν; swapped plane signs; κ on GJ; A_s in EA; θ replaced by the slope v′; the
Euler–Bernoulli recovery of end actions; a W-15 body's unscaled shear
stiffness. Long exact texts of wrong values (rational functions of π in the
spring cases) are given as 40-digit decimals.

**Controls.**
- The Euler–Bernoulli twins are the Euler–Bernoulli expectation: an
  Euler–Bernoulli product must meet them, and the explicit `euler_bernoulli`
  documents must meet the same rows as their absent-member twins.
- A Timoshenko product judged against the Euler–Bernoulli twin's rows must
  breach on every row marked `discriminating`; on `PFT-AUTHNU`, judged against
  the authored-ν rows, it must breach on every row marked
  `authored_nu_discriminating`.

## Criterion

t4_u8's policy, with no new threshold:
- nonzero rows pass when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|;
- zero rows pass when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·|zero scale| (`criterion.absolute_tolerance`,
  in the row unit); each zero scale is the largest nonzero magnitude of the same
  family (force, moment, length, rotation, stress) in that load case, with the
  fallbacks in `zero_scale_definitions`;
- **cancellation rows** (`criterion.kind = cancellation_zero_scale`): the six
  PHI2 θ_B rows are nonzero but smaller than their family's zero tolerance,
  because (2 − φ) is a few ulps. Ordinary binary64 formation and solution
  cannot be expected to meet a 1e-9 relative test there: evaluating the closed
  forms in binary64 gives 18–100 % relative error, though a correctly rounded
  answer would pass. So they are judged like zero rows: |obs − exp| ≤
  `absolute_tolerance`, and their nonzero expectations are kept. The
  Euler–Bernoulli value (−4.85e-7 rad) still breaches by far. This is the only
  departure from the plain relative test; the round-1 refuter judged it
  numerically justified;
- every discriminator row must satisfy |obs − wrong| > 1e-9·max(|obs|, |wrong|).

Rows are keyed by (kind, entity_ref, component, location); `basis_ref` names
the load case, or for a combination `{ref_type: combination, ref_id}`.
`expected` is in the row unit; `exact` and `decimal` are in `reference_unit`,
and `factor` converts them exactly (m → mm 1000, Pa → MPa 1/1000000).

## Inferred conventions (confirm with the consumer)

- **Row vocabulary.** The frozen kinds are the PF result keys listed in
  `row_conventions`: element-local forces and stresses at five locations,
  global nodal displacements (mm) and rotations (rad),
  `support_reaction_component_v2` and its magnitudes, and
  `pipe_elastic_normal_stress_maximum_v2` (frozen only where the five-station
  maximum is provably the member maximum: 7 member maxima per twin in the skew
  and weight cases are not frozen and are listed in `omitted_rows`). Not frozen:
  `element_global_end_*`, `element_local_normal_stress_extreme`,
  `element_local_transverse_shear_stress_maximum`, `displacement_magnitude`,
  whose formulas are not in a design read here. For combinations the
  magnitudes and maxima are not frozen.
- **Combinations.** Rows are the factor-weighted sums of the terms' rows, with
  `basis_ref` `{ref_type: combination, ref_id}`.
- **Nonlinear supports.** The reaction slot as stated under Method; the
  product's per-support state rows are not frozen (their kinds are not known
  here), and `support_states` gives the expected state. Gap-bearing cases
  carry the product's `NUMERICAL_INTEGRITY_RECOVERY_BASIS_UNQUALIFIED` standing
  (kd5-nonlinear-active-set.md §0); the rows here are their physical
  expectation either way.
- **Authoring.** Partial restraints use `family: guide`; springs use
  `family: spring` with `restraints: [dof]` and `stiffness {dof, value}`; a zero
  mill tolerance is written as absence; thermal primitives are
  `temperature_interval` in degC with direction `global_z`; weight and
  distributed loads are element `force_per_length` primitives; the W-15
  component carries `rigid_pipe_ref`, `stiffness_scaling_user_value` (unit
  "none"), a `source_reference` and `solver_consumption:
  mechanics_geometry_only`.
- **Self-weight.** The weight case authors the intensities as primitives:
  w_m = g ρ_m π(r_o² − r_i²) = 52932962439/400000000·π N/m and
  w_c = g ρ_c π r_i² = 1624177373/20000000·π N/m, with r_i = OD/2 − (t − c_m),
  g = 9.80665 m/s², ρ_m = 7850 and ρ_c = 1000 kg/m³ (PF's one effective-wall
  section for every use, section-bases-and-timoshenko.md §1). A test that
  generates self-weight from densities instead should compare its intensities
  with these.

**Re-freeze rules.**
- No value changes to match a product output. A disagreement goes back to this
  reference's owner and its refuter (design §5).
- A change to the theory or a definition (κ_E, ν, the element, load
  equivalents, recovery or the support law) is a re-freeze with a recorded
  reason and a new refutation.
- A request re-authoring (for example the final 0.8.0 member shape) regenerates
  the requests; oracle values must not move, only request hashes do.
- Editing this README changes only its hash in `SHA256SUMS`.
