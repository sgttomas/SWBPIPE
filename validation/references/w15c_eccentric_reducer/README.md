# W-15c references: the eccentric reducer offset

These are frozen, independently derived expectations for DEL-03-05 Design
[`eccentric-reducer-offset.md`](../../../execution/PKG-03/DEL-03-05/Design/eccentric-reducer-offset.md)
§7 (PRD §22.2, the rigid-link family; unit W-15 slice c). The product binding
is `core/product_physics/tests/w15c_eccentric_reducer_benchmarks.rs`.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented, and no value comes from a
standard, catalog or vendor source. Standard claim fence applies (F-PIP-2;
claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

**Status:** candidate references. Two exact formulations agree on every value,
and the design's hand values reproduce. They have not yet been independently
refuted.

## Model and formulations

A cantilever runs from an anchor N0 (0, 0, 0) to A (1, 0, 0).
- **Body.** A reducer's body runs from A to B. B is A plus (0.12, −0.05, 0),
  or plus (0.12, 0, −0.05) in the z case: a 5-12-13 chord between the two
  centerlines.
- **Pipe.** A downstream pipe runs from B to C = B + (1, 0, 0).
- **Sections.** S1 (OD 0.2 m, ID 0.18 m) on N0–A and on the body; S2 (OD
  0.1 m, ID 0.09 m) on B–C.
- **Material.** E = 200 GPa, ν = 1/4, G = 80 GPa, α = 12e-6 1/K.
- **Scale.** The reducer at A carries the DEC-045 scale s and the offset
  under convention R-c1 option A, measured from the component's node.

**Route 1.** Unit-load virtual work on the space frame: section actions by
statics, motions as exact integrals of real × virtual section actions over each
member's compliances. The anchored thermal case uses the force method, with the
six redundants at the far anchor.

**Route 2.** An exact direct-stiffness solve: 12 × 12 Euler–Bernoulli or
Timoshenko member stiffness, rotated with exact direction cosines, held
free-strain end actions.

**Checks.** Every motion, reaction and end action of route 2 equals route 1's.
The design table (§7.2) reproduces to its 14 printed digits. The rigid-link
limit satisfies m(s) = m_rigid + c/s, and B follows A as a rigid body. The
W15C-Z statics of §7.4 reproduce, and the convention rows R and O solve as
W15C-P. If any cross-check disagrees, the generator writes and verifies
nothing.

## Cases

| Case | What it tests |
|---|---|
| W15C-P-S{1,1000}-{EB,TIM} | axial tip force 10 kN at C: P·‖D⊥‖ in the upstream run |
| W15C-Z-S{1,1000}-{EB,TIM} | z-offset with a transverse tip force of 1 kN: torsion Q·‖D⊥‖ in the upstream run |
| W15C-P0-S{1,1000}-EB | concentric control (B and C on the X axis) |
| W15C-R-S1000-EB, W15C-O-S1000-EB | convention rows: the body member reversed, and the component at B (offset +50 mm from B); they solve as W15C-P |
| W15C-T-S1000 | free thermal growth, ΔT = 100 K |
| W15C-TF-S{1,1000}-{EB,TIM} | thermal growth with both ends anchored |

**Exact values.** Every result is q·π^k with q rational. Under given forces,
forces carry k = 0 and motions k = −1. Under thermal strain, forces carry
k = 1 and motions k = 0.

**Conventions.** These are W-9's and W-15's. Member axes follow the product's
convention: x from `from` to `to`, y the `y_reference` less its x part,
z = x × y.

## Reproduce and change

```
python3 -I generators/w15c_reference.py          # check: rebuild, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/w15c_reference.py --write  # rewrite references.json and SHA256SUMS
```

**Re-freeze rule.** No value changes to match a product output. A
disagreement goes back to this reference's owner and an independent refuter.
