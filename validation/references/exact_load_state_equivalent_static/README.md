# Exact-route generated loads in a load and reference-state document

Frozen, independently derived expectations for generated wind and static
seismic loads solved on the exact pressure route (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`)
in a document that also carries a temperature state (a load and
reference-state document), with internal pressure present. Today the product
refuses this combination. These references exist so that the repair is judged
against them.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code, and reads no file other than the ones it writes. The
values are derived only from linear-elastic frame mechanics and from the
governing scope text: DEL-05-01 (static-equivalent generation, mass basis,
REQ-008, AC-007), DEL-04-07 (routes, pressure on the exact route, REQ-011),
DEL-03-08 (mass per length) and DEL-05-03 (section bases, shear model). The
assigner also supplied the per-case pressure-elongation option's text (see
Pressure). All inputs are invented, deliberately non-standard and exact
rationals. No code coefficient, combination or default is used: gravity,
g-factors, wind pressure, shape factor, direction and spans are inputs.

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted. The comparison tolerance is not
set here.

## Files

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `generators/exact_load_state_reference.py` | the generator. It uses exact rational arithmetic, two solution methods, the cross-checks, `references.json` and `SHA256SUMS` |
| `references.json` | inputs, derived sections and masses, generated loads, 16 cases (864 rows), 16 load components (864 rows), refusals, the wrong-implementation control and 146 cross-check results |
| `SHA256SUMS` | hashes of the files above (`shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS` in this directory) |

`python3 -I generators/exact_load_state_reference.py` rebuilds every output in
memory and compares it byte for byte with the committed files. It also fails
if any cross-check disagrees. `--write` rewrites the outputs. The generator
uses the Python 3 standard library only.

## Units, conventions and number format

- SI throughout: m, N, N·m, rad, Pa, K, kg/m³.
- Global axes X, Y, Z. Every member is parallel to a global axis.
- **Motion** (`motion/<node>/ux…rz`): the node's global translation and its
  right-handed rotation. On Timoshenko members a rotation is the section's
  rotation, not the slope of the centreline.
- **Reaction** (`reaction/<support>/Fx…Mz`): the global force and moment that
  the anchor exerts on the structure, with the moment taken about the anchor's
  node.
- **Member end** (`member_end/<pipe>/<i|j>/Fx…Mz`): the global force and moment
  that the end node exerts on the member, with the moment taken about that end
  node. End i is the member's first node. Global components are given so that
  no choice of local axis enters. The axial force of a member along +X is −Fx
  at end i and +Fx at end j, tension positive.
- **Values**: `exact` is a sum of rational multiples of π^k, k ∈ {−1, 0, 1}.
  Areas and second moments of an annulus carry π, so seismic loads, thermal and
  pressure forces carry π, wind forces are rational, and wind motions carry
  1/π. `decimal` gives 40 significant digits. `value` is the correctly rounded
  binary64, decided by rational bounds of π 10⁻⁹⁰ apart.
- **Pressure-setting dependence**: a row of a pressurised case carries
  `"pressure_elongation": "independent"` and one `expected` value, or
  `"dependent"` with `expected_pressure_elongation_off` and
  `expected_pressure_elongation_on`. The generator compares the two settings'
  exact values to decide which; the reasons are under Pressure.

says nothing about whether, or how, they are listed.

## Models

Both are anchored, all six degrees of freedom, at both ends. Each is
statically indeterminate to degree six, so a temperature or pressure free
strain produces forces.

**STRAIGHT.** A(0,0,0), M(1.9,0,0), B(5.2,0,0).
- Members `pipe:a-m` and `pipe:m-b`, both section S1.
- Anchors `support:a` at A and `support:b` at B.
- Node M is free. Its six motions decouple into: axial (ux), torsion (rx),
  bending in XY (uy, rz) and bending in XZ (uz, ry).

**FRAME.** A(0,0,0), C(4.1,0,0), B(4.1,2.7,0).
- Members `pipe:a-c` (along +X) and `pipe:c-b` (along +Y), section S2.
- Anchors at A and B.
- Node C is free. Its motions decouple into in-plane (ux, uy, rz) and
  out-of-plane (uz, rx, ry). Out-of-plane they couple bending of one leg with
  torsion of the other.

**Element states and cases.** Every member of a model shares one material and
one operating temperature, so within a case all members have the same E.

- **Material selection**: the material point at the installation temperature
  (E = 203.7 GPa, called "cold") or at the operating temperature
  (E = 181.9 GPa, "hot"). The selection is exact at a point, so no
  interpolation enters.
- **Thermal state**: none, or the coefficient form α·(T_operating −
  T_installation), applied as the free strain α·ΔT on every member.

| Case (per model) | Selection | Thermal | Generated | Pressure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REF | installation | none | none | none: a zero-action reference state, published as zero without a finding (DEL-04-07 REQ-007) |
| THERMAL | operating | α·ΔT | none | yes |
| WIND-COLD | installation | none | wind | yes |
| WIND-HOT | operating | none | wind | yes |
| SEISMIC-X, -Y, -Z | operating | none | seismic, one axis | yes |
| STRAIGHT-THERMAL-WIND | operating | α·ΔT | wind | yes |
| FRAME-THERMAL-SEISMIC-Y | operating | α·ΔT | seismic Y | yes |

**Superposition.** A combined case's expected response is the sum of its
components:

- STRAIGHT-THERMAL-WIND = `thermal@operating` + `wind@operating`
  [+ `pressure_elongation@operating`];
- FRAME-THERMAL-SEISMIC-Y = `thermal@operating` + `seismic_y@operating`
  [+ `pressure_elongation@operating`].

The bracketed term applies with elongation on only. Each case's `superposition`
names its components for each setting. `components` gives every component's
full rows, and a cross-check confirms that one direct solve equals the sum.

**Cold against hot.** In each model, WIND-COLD and WIND-HOT differ only in the
selected modulus. Every stiffness term is proportional to E: G is too, since ν
is the same at both points. So the forces are the same and every motion
differs by the factor E_operating/E_installation = 1819/2037 (cross-checked).
For example STRAIGHT node M uy is 3.05686·10⁻⁵ m cold and 3.42322·10⁻⁵ m hot.

## Pressure

**Text relied on.**
- DEL-04-07: a region runs between two terminals, each transferring its end
  load to the wall or separately supported or compensated. Pressure enters a
  case as loads only.
- The per-case option supplied by the assigner from a change in review. With
  pressure elongation **off**, "the closed-end free pressure strain is omitted;
  pressure thrust at unbalanced locations is applied". With it **on**, "the
  closed-end free strain (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s), with A_i on the pressure bore
  and A_s the nominal section area".
- The same change, also supplied by the assigner: "Leaving it out removes the
  free pressure strain only; pressure thrust at unbalanced locations is applied
  in both settings, and the wall force N_w = N_el + p·A_i and the effective
  force S = N_el hold in both."

**Regions.**

| Model | Regions | Use of the assigner's constraint |
|---|---|---|
| STRAIGHT | one region `region:a-b` over `pipe:a-m` and `pipe:m-b`, terminals A and B, both `transfers_to_wall` | one straight run in two members, joined at interior node M where OD, wall and direction are identical |
| FRAME | `region:a-c` over `pipe:a-c` (terminals A, C) and `region:c-b` over `pipe:c-b` (terminals C, B), all `transfers_to_wall`; C is a pressure junction | each straight run is one member; two regions meet at the corner C |

**What is computed.**
- **On**: on every pressurised member, the free strain
  ε_p = (1 − 2ν)p·A_i/(E·A_n), with E the member's selected modulus in the
  case. No thrust is applied, because these models contain no location where
  the closed-end condition fails: every terminal transfers its end load to
  the wall.
- **Off**: the free pressure strain only is removed. These models have no
  unbalanced location, so pressure contributes nothing more. The "off" rows
  are the expected values, not one reading among two.
- **Forces in both settings.** The member-end rows give the element force
  N_el. The effective force is S = N_el and the wall force is
  N_w = N_el + p·A_i, under either setting.
- ε_p values: S1 3.35881·10⁻⁵ (installation) and 3.76135·10⁻⁵ (operating);
  S2 3.77323·10⁻⁵ and 4.22544·10⁻⁵.

**Why "on" is the physical state.** For a closed-ended pipe whose ends
transfer to the wall, Lamé's thick-wall solution gives the axial wall strain
(N_w − 2ν·p·A_i)/(E·A_n), with N_w the wall force. The free closed-ended pipe
(N_w = p·A_i) therefore elongates by ε_p. In the effective-force description,
S = N_w − p·A_i, the pipe-plus-contents member has free strain ε_p. At any
node where every incident terminal transfers to the wall, the fluid force on
the fitting balances the jump in p·A_i between the members' wall and
effective forces. No external thrust remains, whatever the fitting's angle.

The generator checks this a second way, in the wall-force formulation of
DEL-04-07's current text:
- Poisson shortening −2ν·p·A_i/(E·A_n) on every member;
- each terminal's end load p·A_i along the member's outward axis;
- at C, the junction fluid force −Σ p·A·s = p·A_i(X − Y).

That formulation gives exactly the same motions and reactions. Each member-end
axial force exceeds the effective one by p·A_i (cross-checked).

**Which rows depend on the setting, and why.**
- **STRAIGHT.** ε_p is uniform and restrained by the anchors, so with
  elongation on no node moves. Only the axial force changes:
  S = −(1 − 2ν)p·A_i, with reactions ±(1 − 2ν)p·A_i = ±54 647.7 N along X.
  The six rows that depend on the setting are the two reaction Fx rows and the
  four member-end Fx rows. Every motion, every transverse force and every
  moment is the same with the setting off or on.
- **FRAME.** Elongating both legs between the anchors bends the frame in its
  own plane only. The 21 in-plane rows (C's ux, uy, rz; Fx, Fy, Mz at both
  anchors and at both ends of both members) depend on the setting.
- **Unaffected rows.** The 33 out-of-plane rows of every FRAME case (C's uz,
  rx, ry; Fz, Mx, My) do not depend on the setting. Neither do the
  out-of-plane wind and seismic-Z responses (planar decoupling).
- **Modulus.** With elongation on, the pressure response's forces are the same
  at both modulus selections, and its motions scale by 1/E, because
  ε_p ∝ 1/E (cross-checked).

**The corner C is balanced.** Every terminal at C transfers to the wall, so
the fitting's fluid force balances the incident wall forces p·A_i. It is not a
further load in the effective-force equations, in either setting. Neither
model exercises a genuinely unbalanced pressure thrust.

**Wrong-implementation control.** `wrong_implementation_control` gives, for
each model, the response to each terminal's end load p·A_i and each
junction's fluid force applied as loads with no wall free strain. It is not
an expected value. It is the response a product would publish if it applied
those forces in the effective-force equations. In FRAME this is a corner load
of 82 409.2 N along (X − Y) at C, against the zero expected with elongation
off.

**No rigid-family component.** Neither model has a rigid-family component, so
the later decision that static seismic carries a component's weight where the
weight case applies it changes nothing here.

## Methods

**Method 1: direct stiffness.**
- Each member is split at its load breakpoints: the wind-span fractions, plus
  0 and 1. Every member load is then uniform over whole elements.
- The 12×12 element in local axes is exact for a prismatic member: EA/L,
  GJ/L, and bending EI/((1 + Φ)L³)·[12, 6L, (4 + Φ)L², (2 − Φ)L²]. Here
  Φ = 12EI/(G·A_s·L²) for Timoshenko and 0 for Euler–Bernoulli, using each
  element's own length.
- Fixed-end actions of a uniform load w over a whole element are −wL/2 at
  each end, with end moments ∓wL²/12 (the same for Timoshenko, by symmetry).
- A free strain ε gives the fixed-end actions +EAε at i and −EAε at j along
  the member's x axis.
- Local axes are x along the member, y = ẑ_ref × x and z = x × y (ẑ_ref = Z);
  for a circular section they do not affect the results.
- The system is solved in exact rationals with π factored out.
- Reactions are the sum of member end actions at the anchor node. Member end
  actions are k·u plus the fixed-end actions.

**Method 2: force method with unit-load integration.**
- The released structure is the path from A to B, cantilevered from A. The
  redundants are the six components of the anchor action at B.
- Flexibilities and displacements are ∫(N n/EA + V_y v_y/GA_s + V_z v_z/GA_s
  + T t/GJ + M_y m_y/EI + M_z m_z/EI) ds + ∫ n ε ds. The shear terms apply to
  Timoshenko only.
- The integral runs over each interval between load breakpoints by Simpson's
  rule. This is exact: the integrands are at most cubic.
- Section actions are the resultant of everything beyond the cut (loads, nodal
  thrusts, redundant).
- Compatibility (B's motion = 0) gives the redundants. Nodal motions come from
  unit loads at the node, member end actions from the section actions at the
  member ends, and A's reaction from the end action at A.

**Re-deriving by hand.**
- Each case reduces to six unknowns at the one free node (M or C), in
  independent blocks. STRAIGHT has 1+1+2+2. FRAME has 3 in-plane and
  3 out-of-plane.
- Seismic loads are full-span, so textbook fixed-end forces apply.
- For the partial wind spans:
  - FRAME `pipe:c-b`, Euler–Bernoulli: use the partial-UDL fixed-end forces,
    or split the member at 0.25 and 0.8 of its length.
  - STRAIGHT `pipe:m-b`, Timoshenko: split at 0.15 and 0.7 and condense the
    two interior nodes. Alternatively use method 2 with redundants at B.
- Thermal and pressure strains enter as ±EAε pairs.

## Open readings

1. **Closed: what "pressure thrust at unbalanced locations" covers.** The
   governing text settles it (Pressure): the "off" rows are the expected
   values, and the former alternative is now the wrong-implementation control.
2. **E in ε_p.** Computed with the member's modulus as selected in the case.
   With a fixed reference modulus instead, pressure-on motions in operating
   cases would scale by 2037/1819. Forces are unaffected.
3. **Which axial force a member end publishes on a pressurised member.**
   Partly closed: in both settings the governing text gives S = N_el and
   N_w = N_el + p·A_i, and the member-end rows are N_el.
   - Still open: whether the product's published member-end axial force is
     N_el, as here, or DEL-05-03's mechanical force N_m. DEL-05-03's relations
     make N_m = N_el + (1 − 2ν)p·A_i.
   - If it is N_m, the axial member-end rows of pressurised cases differ from
     those here by that constant.
4. **Meaning of a load and reference-state document.** No scope states it
   (DEL-05-01 "Not decided"; DEL-02-01). The schema's member names are used
   only to name inputs. Computed reading:
   - an element state's `material_selection` gives the member's E, with
     G = E/(2(1 + ν)) and the same ν;
   - its `thermal_state` gives the free strain α·ΔT;
   - generated loads use the section's densities, with no `mass_state_ref`;
   - each case is solved with its own element states.
5. **"A wind span on a member that cannot carry it."** The text available
   here does not say which members cannot carry a span. The refusal is stated
   for a span whose target is not a pipe of the model, and for an inverted
   span. Which other members (for example arcs or components) are refused is
   not determined here.
6. **Seismic sign.** The load acts along +axis for a positive g-factor, as the
   definition is written ("g-factor × gravity × mass per length" in that axis
   direction).
