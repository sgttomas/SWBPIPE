# W-16 references: an anchor (nozzle) movement on the pressure-free route

Frozen, independently derived expectations for imposed displacement on the
pressure-free route (W-16 slice a, stage 1; DEL-04-03 Design
[`pf-imposed-displacement.md`](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-03/Design/pf-imposed-displacement.md)
§5). An invented L frame is anchored at both ends, and one anchor (the nozzle)
moves in three translations and one rotation. Each case is solved by
[`core/product_physics/tests/w16_imposed_motion.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/tests/w16_imposed_motion.rs)
and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or
example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods
are the textbook methods cited below. The product supplies no nozzle or
equipment movement: the motion is the user's input. Standard claim fence
applies (F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted.

## Theory, section and conventions

The theory is a small-displacement, linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli space frame
with St Venant torsion and no shear deformation. The pressure-free route (PF)
forms Euler–Bernoulli straight members. The exact route (EX) is run with
`shear_model: euler_bernoulli`, so no Timoshenko term enters either route.

The frame has one annular section: OD 0.25 m, wall 0.01 m (ID 0.23 m), E =
200 GPa and ν = 1/4, so G = E/(2(1 + ν)) = 80 GPa exactly. PF takes G = 80 GPa
as its input and EX derives the same value from E and ν, so E, G and the
section theory are the product's on both routes. Then A = 0.0024π m²,
I = 1.731e-5π m⁴ (Iy = Iz) and J = 2I, so EA = 480000000π N,
EI = 3462000π N·m² and GJ = 2769600π N·m².

Every result has the form q·π^k with q rational. Forces and moments have
k = 1; translations and rotations have k = 0. `exact` prints q or `q*pi`.
`decimal` has 40 significant digits (π by Machin). `value` is the nearest
binary64, rounded with lower and upper rational bounds of π, which must agree.

The published quantities are:
- `support_reaction`: the global action of the support on the structure, with
  moments about the support node.
- `nodal_motion`: global translations (m) and right-handed rotations (rad).
- `section_action`: the element-local action on the +x face of the i-side
  portion at station s/L, where local x runs from i to j. A node-on-element end
  action is −section(0) at `end_i` and +section(1) at `end_j`.

Local x runs from i to j, local y is the pipe's `y_reference` less its x part,
and z = x × y. The references choose (0, 1, 0) for the member along X and
(−1, 0, 0) for the member along Y, so local z is global +Z on both.

## Model

Geometry: A(0, 0, 0) is the fixed anchor `support:a`. The frame runs to the
corner C(5, 0, 0) and on to D(5, 4, 0), the nozzle anchor `support:d`. Both
members are straight and meet at a sharp corner; there is no bend element.
Both anchors restrain all six DOFs.

The nozzle movement g at D is UX = 3 mm, UY = −5 mm, UZ = 8 mm and
RY = 0.002 rad. RX and RZ at D are held at zero. The thermal load is a uniform
rise ΔT = 80 K with α = 12e-6 /K on both members, a free strain of 6/6250.

## Method 1: six-redundant force method

Complementary energy and the unit-load method: Timoshenko & Young, *Theory of
Structures*, statically indeterminate frames; Hibbeler, *Structural Analysis*,
force-method chapter.
- Release D. The frame is then a cantilever from A. The six redundants R are
  the actions of the support on the structure at D: Fx, Fy, Fz and Mx, My, Mz
  about D.
- Statics on the released frame gives, at any point P of either member, the
  action on the +x face of the i-side portion: force F and moment
  M + (r_D − r_P) × F. Its local components are the axial force n, the
  torsion t and the bending moments m_y and m_z.
- The flexibility at D is
  F_ij = Σ_e ∫ (n_i·n_j/EA + t_i·t_j/GJ + m_yi·m_yj/EI + m_zi·m_zj/EI) ds
  for the unit redundants i and j. The integrands are quadratic in s, so
  Simpson's rule is exact.
- Compatibility at D: the released D moves by d_T + F·R, where d_T =
  αΔT·(r_D − r_A) is its free thermal motion (no rotation). It must reach g, so
  R = F⁻¹(g − d_T).
- A's actions follow by equilibrium of the whole frame:
  F_A = −F and M_A = −(M + (r_D − r_A) × F).
- The corner C moves by αΔT·(r_C − r_A) plus the unit-load integral over A→C
  of a unit action at C against the actual section actions.
- Self-check: C's motion carried rigidly to D, plus αΔT·(r_D − r_C), plus
  member C→D integrated as a cantilever from C, returns D to g exactly in all
  three cases.

## Method 2: direct stiffness (cross-check)

The generator's second route is an independently written three-dimensional
direct-stiffness solve. It uses the Euler–Bernoulli 12 × 12 member stiffness
with GJ/L torsion, transformed by the local axes above. Each member carries the
held thermal end actions ±EA·αΔT. The solve partitions on prescribed DOFs,
K_ff·u_f = f_f − K_fc·g with u_c = g, as the product's reduction does. The
reactions are the sums of the member end actions at the restrained DOFs.

All 255 comparisons agree exactly:
- the three compatibility self-checks;
- every nodal motion (18 per case);
- every support action (12 per case);
- every member end action (24 per case);
- superposition: each row of W16-MOTION-THERMAL equals the sum of the
  W16-MOTION and W16-THERMAL rows (90 rows).

## Rows

Each case publishes 90 rows:
- all six support components at both supports;
- all six motion components at three nodes;
- all six section components at stations 0, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and 1 on both pipes.

Zero rows are exact zeros. Each zero row is compared against 1e-9 times its
family's zero scale: the largest nonzero magnitude of that family (force,
moment, translation or rotation) in the case.

## Product binding and criterion

| Route | Motion as authored | Thermal as authored |
|---|---|---|
| PF | four primitive loads, `category: imposed_displacement`, target `{type: "support", support: "support:d", dof}`, `direction` the same DOF, dimension `displacement` (m) or `rotation` (rad) | element `thermal` primitive loads of 80 degC with material α 1.2e-5 /degC |
| EX (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`, `euler_bernoulli`, load/reference-state group, `pressure_regions: []`) | `support_states[].boundary_motion` on `support:d`, `absolute_reference_displacement` | `constant_alpha_interval` element states, 1.2e-5 /K × 80 K |

The test runs every case on both routes in both solver modes
(`SparseInteractive`, `DenseScrutiny`) against these references, then compares
PF with EX row for row. It also covers non-SI inputs and combinations with the
motion cases.

The criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale.
