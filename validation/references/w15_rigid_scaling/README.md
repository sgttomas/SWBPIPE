## Theory, section and conventions

A component body is the straight member a valve, flange, reducer, rigid or
specialty component maps. With the user's DEC-045 stiffness scale s, the
member's stiffness is s·K: s·E·A, s·E·I, s·G·J and, for Timoshenko, s·κ·G·A.
Free strains keep their meaning (a scaled body still grows εL), and section
actions follow from equilibrium.

The theory is a small-displacement, linear-elastic space frame, Euler–Bernoulli
or Timoshenko with the energy-matched annular shear factor
κ = 6(1+ν)²(1+m²)² / [(7+14ν+8ν²)(1+m²)² + 4m²(5+10ν+4ν²)], m = rᵢ/rₒ (the
Saint-Venant shear strain energy V²/(2κGA) of the annulus). Every case uses one
annular section: OD 0.2 m, ID 0.18 m, E = 200 GPa, ν = 1/4, G = E/(2(1+ν)) =
80 GPa (the preview route's authored G and the exact route's derived G
coincide), A = 0.0019π m², I = 8.5975e-6π m⁴, J = 2I, bore area Aᵢ = 0.0081π m².

Every result has the form q·π^k with q rational. In W15-R the loads are given
forces, so forces carry k = 0 and motions k = −1. In W15-T and W15-P the loads
are formed from E·A or p·Aᵢ, so forces carry k = 1 and motions k = 0. `exact`
prints q, `q*pi` or `q/pi`; `decimal` has 40 significant digits; `value` is the
nearest binary64, rounded with lower and upper rational bounds of π, which must
agree. The conventions (support reaction on the structure, global motions,
element-local section action on the +x face of the i-side portion, a
node-on-element end action −section(0) at `end_i` and +section(1) at `end_j`)
are W-9's. `wall_axial_force` is the pressure-2 wall force N_w of a member.

Every member lies along +X with `y_reference` (0, 1, 0), so local y is global
+Y and local z is global +Z.

## Cases

### W15-R: rigid-link cantilever (s = 1, 1/4 and every decade from 1e3 to 1e8; EB and TIM)

A(0) fixed → B(1) → C(3/2) → D(3) free: pipe A–B, body B–C (a valve at B with
scale s), pipe C–D. Tip loads at D: Fx = 2000 N, Fy = 1000 N, Mx = 500 N·m.

**Method: unit-load virtual work** (Timoshenko & Gere, *Mechanics of
Materials*; Hibbeler, *Structural Analysis*, virtual work): with compliance
sums over the segments,
- u_x(P) = Fx·∫₀ᴾ dx/(sEA), θ_x(P) = Mx·∫₀ᴾ dx/(sGJ);
- u_y(P) = Fy·∫₀ᴾ (L−x)(x_P−x)/(sEI) dx, plus Fy·∫₀ᴾ dx/(sκGA) for Timoshenko;
- θ_z(P) = Fy·∫₀ᴾ (L−x)/(sEI) dx (section rotation);
- section actions by statics: N = Fx, V_y = Fy, T = Mx, M_z = (L−x)·Fy;
- reactions at A: (−Fx, −Fy, 0, −Mx, 0, −L·Fy).

Section actions and reactions do not depend on s (the cantilever is
statically determinate); the motions do. **Rigid-link limit:** with the body's
compliance removed and its moment arms kept, each tip motion m satisfies
m(s) = m_rigid + c/s with c independent of s (checked exactly at s and 2s);
`rigid_link_tip_*` are the limit values. At s = 1 the case is plain pipe
(checked against FL³/(3EI)). The Timoshenko runs use the exact route only.

### W15-T: thermal and fit series

A(0) fixed → B(1) → C(3) fixed: pipe A–B and body B–C (scale s), each with a
free strain ε. **Method: series compatibility** (the free plus elastic
elongations of the chain sum to zero): N = −Σ(εᵢLᵢ)/Σ(Lᵢ/(sᵢEA)), tension
positive; u_x(B) = ε₁L₁ + N·L₁/(EA). Bases and producers:
- PF-MAT (s = 1/4 and 4), PF-NAMED, PF-INTERP: the preview route's element
  thermal load, ΔT = 40 K, on the base material, a named temperature point
  (E = 190 GPa, α = 13e-6) and the linear interpolation at 425 K between
  points at 300 K (200 GPa, 12e-6) and 500 K (180 GPa, 14e-6);
- EX-MAT-THERMAL, EX-NAMED-FIT, EX-INTERP-FITONLY, EX-CANCEL: the exact
  route's load/reference-state member pair, with an explicit interval strain
  2⁻¹¹ and a fit strain on the body (−2⁻¹²; fit only +2⁻¹²), on the base
  material, an exact point and the 425 K interpolation. The composition is
  λ_fit·λ_thermal − 1 = f + t + f·t. EX-CANCEL authors the fit strain
  −t/(1+t) on both members as its binary64; the residual eigenstrain is the
  exact f + t + f·t of the binary64 inputs (of order 1e-20), so N is a small
  nonzero value with its own relative criterion.

W15-T-FREE: the same chain anchored at A only, ΔT = 40 K on both members, body
scale 1e6. The body grows αΔT·L, the tip moves αΔT·(L₁+L₂), and no force
arises; the force zero scale is the body's held force s·E·A·αΔT.

### W15-P: closed-end pressure cantilever (s = 4)

A(0) fixed → B(1) → C(3): pipe A–B and body B–C (scale 4), one pressure region
over both, terminals A and C transferring their caps to the wall, p = 2 MPa.
**Method: closed-end vessel statics and Poisson strain**: N_w = p·Aᵢ on both
members; the mechanical force N_m = (1−2ν)p·Aᵢ; the elastic axial strain
(1−2ν)p·Aᵢ/(s·E·Aₙ), s times smaller on the body; u_x by accumulation. The
closed vessel puts no force on the anchor.

## Cross-checks

The generator's second route is an exact direct-stiffness solve of each chain
with the axial, torsion and in-plane bending problems decoupled, every element
scaled by its s (Timoshenko: Φ = 12EI/(κGAL²), unchanged by s), held-member
eigen end actions for the thermal, fit and Poisson strains, and the caps as
nodal loads. Every nodal motion, support reaction and element end action is
compared with the closed form, together with the far-anchor compatibility of
each series, the rigid-link limit, the s = 1 plain-pipe value and, for W15-P,
N_m and N_w. All 1071 comparisons agree exactly. The out-of-plane problem of
each case is unloaded and stable, so its components are exactly zero.

## Product binding and criterion

| Case | Routes in `w15_rigid_scaling_benchmarks.rs` |
|---|---|
| W15-R EB | PF; EX (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`, Euler–Bernoulli, `pressure_regions: []`) |
| W15-R TIM | EX (`timoshenko_annulus_energy_v1`) |
| W15-T PF-* and FREE | PF (element `thermal` primitive loads) |
| W15-T EX-* | EX (load/reference-state group, `pressure_regions: []`) |
| W15-P | EX (one pressure region, both terminals `transfers_to_wall`) |

The body is a valve at its member's `from` node, mapping that member, with
`solver_consumption` `mechanics_geometry_only` and the case's scale. The
criterion is unchanged (DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale. Both
solver modes run against the same reference.

The decades from 1e3 to 1e8 locate the product's precision threshold
(DEL-03-05 Design §2.5): below it a run is held to the criterion; at and above
it the test asserts the product's precision warning and its unchanged standing
only, never an accuracy, and prints its measured errors. These references are
exact at every s; the decades 1e4, 1e5, 1e7 and 1e8 were added (a re-freeze
that leaves every earlier case byte for byte).

## Reproduce and change

```
python3 -I generators/w15_reference.py          # check: rebuild, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/w15_reference.py --write  # rewrite references.json and SHA256SUMS
```

**Re-freeze rules.** No value changes to match a product output; a
disagreement goes back to this reference's owner and an independent refuter. A
change to the theory or a convention is a re-freeze, with a recorded reason
and a new refutation. Editing this README changes only its hash in
`SHA256SUMS`; the test pins the hash of `references.json` only.
