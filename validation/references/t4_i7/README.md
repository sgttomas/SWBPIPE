# T4-U2 bend references (T4-I7, repair round 01; re-frozen for D-5)

realized bends under `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` (H-2). Derived by the direct
unit-load (flexibility) method over the whole line, with no curved-element
stiffness, no K·u_free and no thermal analogy; nothing here is generated by
the product. Consumed by
`core/product_physics/tests/t4_u2_pressure_references.rs`.

## D-5 re-freeze (T4-U6)

The owner ruled D-5 on 2026-10-10 ("D-5 and D-6 as recommended"; DEL-05-03
Design `section-bases-and-timoshenko.md` §§1, 2.1, 2.5 and 7). Before it, one
section (wall − mill tolerance) served stiffness, the pressure load path and
stress. The 72 cases whose section carries mill tolerance 0.000889 m (71
valued and the mitre refusal) are re-frozen in every row:

- **Stiffness** (A, I, J, and the As of the Poisson eigenstrain
  −2ν pAi/(E·As)) uses the nominal wall 0.00711 m. Displacements move in all
  71 valued cases (by at least 1.1e7 tolerances). Forces, moments and
  reactions move wherever the line is indeterminate: in the 44 anchored
  lines support A moves by at least 1.04 % relative (the in-plane
  redistribution depends on EA against EI; the out-of-plane one only on
  EI against GJ, which does not change). In the 27 free-tip lines every
  force, moment, reaction and reduced-basis stress is unchanged exactly.
- **Ai** stays on the pressure bore OD/2 − (wall − mill), since the
  corrosion allowance is 0 here (the documents carry none, and the
  current-format re-authoring writes 0).
- **Stresses** are recovered on both section bases. In a re-frozen case each
  station's `sigma_m`, `sigma_b_y`, `sigma_b_z` and `tau_t` are under
  `stresses_by_section_basis.{nominal_section, reduced_section}`, a straight's
  `lame_surface` is keyed by basis (each basis's own annulus; the reduced
  values equal the frozen ones exactly), and the stress zero-scale groups are
  per basis (`membrane_stress@nominal_section`, …). Arcs keep
  `withheld_on_arcs` on both bases.
- **Unchanged.** The 8 CBPT cases (no mill tolerance, allowance 0) keep their
  entries byte for byte; their bases coincide, and their single stress family
  is the value of both families. `u2_document_sketches.json` is
  byte-identical: the same documents mean the nominal wall plus mill
  tolerance under D-5, and the authored self-weight intensity (derived before
  D-5 from the single section) is an input, to which D-5's mass rule does not
  apply.
- The negative controls and the polygon-limit control are recomputed on the
  same rules; every control keeps its listing rule (≥ 1e3 tolerances).
- **Zero-scale floors.** The rule is unchanged: each group's floor is
  1e-9 × that group's own largest |expected| in the case. Where the re-frozen
  restraint forces and moments rose, so did the floors: 162 of the 894 groups
  present before and after rose, by at most ×1.174
  (`U2-L-ANCH-ALL-K2-SKEW-X5E6`, `elastic_end_force_chord_frame`), and the
  reduced-section bending/torsion groups by at most ×1.112 against the former
  single group. Displacement and nominal-section groups fell. This is not a
  loosening: each floor stays tied to its group's own maximum.

The re-freeze was derived without reading product code and awaits
refutation by a separate TASK before any product output is compared with it.
`../t4_u4a/u4a_generate.py` re-derives every valued case independently
(stations, arc end rows, support A) and binds this file by its SHA-256.

## How the tests compare

|observed − expected| ≤ 1e-9·max(|expected|, zero_scale(group)) in both
solver modes, with each case's `zero_scale` block (translations converted to
the product's mm). Each stress row is compared against its own basis's value
and group. Arc end rows are compared in the chord frame
(`chord_frame_elastic`) for the elastic `element_local_*` rows and along the
end tangent for `pipe_wall_endpoint_action_v2`; Lamé rows and the
straight-statics maximum must be absent on arcs.

## Reproduce

Standard-library Python 3 only:

```
python3 -I -B _run_records/u2_generate.py <output_dir>
python3 -I -B _run_records/u2_generate.py --check
```

The first writes both JSON files; the second regenerates them in a temporary
directory and compares them byte for byte with the committed files.
