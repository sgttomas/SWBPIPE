# Exact-route references: the declared operating minus sustained difference

Frozen, independently derived expectations for a result-state subtraction
declared `operating_minus_sustained` on the exact pressure route, with its gates
G-1 (containment) and G-2 (one modulus basis):
[DEL-05-02](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-02/ScopeOfWork.md) REQ-012, AC-003
and AC-012, with pressure as [DEL-04-07](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/ScopeOfWork.md)
defines it and the section bases of
[DEL-05-03](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/ScopeOfWork.md). The
pressure-free neighbour is [`op1b_difference_basis`](../op1b_difference_basis/README.md).
This package shares no code or values with it.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator uses the Python standard
library only and imports, reads and runs no product code or output. All inputs
are invented and deliberately non-standard. No value comes from a design code,
catalogue or vendor. No code compliance is claimed.

**Status.** Candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author.
They have not yet been independently refuted, and no product test binds them.
The product refused a declared basis on the exact route when they were written.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `generators/exact_difference_basis_reference.py` | the generator: exact arithmetic, two solution methods, the G-1 and G-2 verdicts |
| `references.json` | inputs, derived quantities, 17 numerical pairs, 17 G-1 rows, the M1 hand values and the cross-check counts |
| `SHA256SUMS` | hashes of this README, the generator and `references.json` |

```
python3 -I generators/exact_difference_basis_reference.py          # rebuild in memory, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/exact_difference_basis_reference.py --write  # rewrite references.json and SHA256SUMS
shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS
```

**Re-freeze rule.** No value changes to match a product output. A disagreement
goes back to this reference's owner and an independent refuter.

## Exact form and rounding

Each value is a finite sum of rational multiples of integer powers of π, written
as in `3/4*pi + 1/2 + 5/7*pi^-1`. It is published as `value`, the nearest
binary64 (ties to even). The rounding is decided from rational bounds on π about
1e-160 apart (Machin's formula, with a proven error). A magnitude is published
through its `exact_square`. Every input is a dyadic rational, so every input is
exactly its own binary64.

## Inputs (all invented)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Material points | 300 K: E = 194 750 000 000 Pa, G = 76 000 000 000 Pa, α = 3·2⁻¹⁸ /K; 700 K: E = 184 500 000 000 Pa, G = 72 000 000 000 Pa, α = 3·2⁻¹⁸ /K |
| Poisson's ratio | ν = 9/32 (the member's selected value) |
| Section | D = 35/128 m, t = 9/1024 m, mill tolerance c_m = 1/2048 m, corrosion allowance c_a = 1/1024 m |
| Thermal primitive | ΔT = 475/2 K on every member of an operating case |
| Pressures | p = 5 750 000 Pa; the pressure-change pairs use p_o = 6 500 000 Pa |
| Modulus bases | `REF`: exact basis reference to the 300 K point. `HOT`: solve temperature 600 K, interpolated between 300 K and 700 K at fraction 3/4: E = 187 062 500 000, G = 73 000 000 000, α = 3·2⁻¹⁸ |
| Shear model | `euler_bernoulli`. Bourdon option off. No bends. |

E/(2G) − 1 = 9/32 at both points, so ν does not depend on the basis. α is the
same at both points, so it does not matter whether a case at an exact basis
reference takes α from that point. These choices remove two readings that the
text leaves open.

**Sections** (DEL-05-03, as the bases table defines them):

- r_o = D/2 = 35/256;
- nominal inner radius r_i,n = r_o − t = 131/1024;
- reduced wall t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a = 15/2048;
- pressure bore r_i,r = r_o − t_r = 265/2048.

From these:

- A_n = π(r_o² − r_i,n²) = 2439/1048576·π;
- I_n = π(r_o⁴ − r_i,n⁴)/4 = 89660079/4398046511104·π, and J_n = 2I_n;
- A_i = π r_i,r² = 70225/4194304·π.

Stiffness uses the nominal section. A_i uses the pressure bore. Each member has
EA = E·A_n, EI = E·I_n and GJ = G·J_n, so every stiffness is a rational multiple
of π.

**Free strains.**

- Thermal: ε_T = α·ΔT = 1425/524288.
- Closed-end pressure: ε_p = (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_n). At p = 5.75 MPa on `REF`
  this is 452249/4863951360. It is rational because A_i/A_n is.

## Pressure on these models, and the elongation setting

DEL-04-07 says pressure enters a case "as loads only: the Poisson shortening of
the wall, the terminal end loads, and the remainders at bends and joints". The
wall form of those loads, as `rt_a3_tee` writes it, has three parts:

- each member has the eigenstrain −2ν·p·A_i/(E·A_n) on the law E·A_n·ε = N_w − 2ν·p·A_i;
- each `transfers_to_wall` terminal of member k at node n carries the cap −p·A_i·s_k,
  where s_k is the unit vector from n into the member;
- a junction's end loads are the sum of its caps, −Σ p·A_i·s.

**On these models the wall form equals the closed-end free strain.** Every region
here is one straight member, or (in M2) members joined only where diameter, wall
and direction are identical. At any node, add each incident member's end force
N_w·s to that end's cap −p·A_i·s (if the end is a terminal). The sum is
Σ (N_w − p·A_i)·s = Σ S·s, where S = N_w − p·A_i is the effective force. At an
interior joint of a region there are no caps, but s₁ = −s₂ and the bores are
equal, so the p·A_i terms cancel in the same way. The nodal equations are
therefore those of members with no pressure loads at all, whose axial law is

  E·A_n·ε = S + (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i,

that is, members with the free strain ε_p. That is the governing statement for
the setting **on**: "the closed-end free strain (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s)". The
generator checks this on every pressurised case with the setting on. It solves
the wall form with the caps and the Poisson eigenstrain, and confirms that every
motion and reaction equals the free-strain solution and that N_w = S + p·A_i
(19 cases).

For the setting **off** ("the closed-end free pressure strain is omitted;
pressure thrust at unbalanced locations is applied") there are two readings:

- **W (computed; the text best supports it).** Off is on without ε_p. On these
  models that leaves no pressure load on the nodal equations. The corner thrust
  −Σ p·A_i·s at a junction is still present, but the walls' end-cap tension p·A_i
  balances it. That holds because every terminal transfers to the wall. On is
  then exactly DEL-04-07's current loads, as the tee reference reproduces them.
- **J (alternative, given where it differs).** Off applies the junction resultant
  −Σ p·A_i·s at each junction node as an external nodal load on members whose
  axial law carries no pressure strain. On adds ε_p to that. Under J, on is not
  DEL-04-07's wall form: it counts the corner thrust a second time. Reading J
  also treats the corner as an unbalanced location, which is how the request for
  this package described it.

**Unbalanced thrust.** Under reading W, a corner where two regions meet with
transferring terminals is not an unbalanced location. Its thrust is balanced
through the walls and deforms the model only through ε_p. A genuinely unbalanced
thrust under W needs a `separately_supported_or_compensated` terminal. Such a
terminal is not modelled here, because for a region that is not closed at one end
the text does not say what off omits.

**Wall and mechanical forces.** With either setting, `N_wall = S + p·A_i` (the
statics of a closed region) and `N_mech = S + (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i` (DEL-05-03's
identities). Under the setting on, N_mech is E·A_n·ε, as the tee reference defines
it. Under off, the identity is applied as written; which recovered force a product
calls N_m with the setting off is an open reading.

## Pairs

Each pair has a sustained case and an operating case. The sustained case has the
weight primitives and the pressure regions. The operating case has the same
weight primitives and pressure regions plus the thermal primitive ΔT on every
member. The setting each case is solved with is labelled. For each pair the file
gives:

- the `difference` rows with exact forms;
- the `magnitudes`;
- the two cases' own rows as binary64 (`cases`);
- `consists_of`, which says what the difference is the response to;
- the reading-J values where they differ from reading W.

| Pair | Outcome (reading A of G-1) | The difference is the closed-form response to | Setting dependence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / off–off | published | ε_T alone | identical to on–on on every row; identical under W and J |
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / on–on | published | ε_T alone | as above |
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / operating on, sustained off | published | ε_T + ε_p(p) | differs from on–on by the response to ε_p; identical under W and J |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / on–on | published | ε_T + ε_p(p_o) − ε_p(p_s); N_wall, N_mech also carry their Δp·A_i terms | J differs (adds the response to the junction resultant of Δp) |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / off–off | published | ε_T alone under W; N_wall, N_mech carry Δp·A_i | J differs (response to the junction resultant of Δp) |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / operating on, sustained off | published | ε_T + ε_p(p_o) under W | J differs |
| M1 / g2 / mixed basis (S on `REF`, O on `HOT`, both on) | **refused (G-2)** | — (the would-be subtraction is given as `withheld_would_be_difference`) | — |
| M1 / g2 / same-basis twin (both on `HOT`, both on) | published | ε_T on `HOT`'s moduli | — |

Why the equal-pressure differences do not depend on the setting: the two cases
of a pair share every pressure load, so the loads cancel in the subtraction under
either setting and either reading. Why an on–off pair differs by exactly the
response to ε_p: on is off plus ε_p under both readings. Under reading B of G-1
(below) the pressure-change pairs are refused. Under reading A they are
published, as the table shows.

**Not trivially zero.** In M1 and M2 the thermal difference has non-zero
reactions, displacements, member end forces, and (in M2) torsion. The generator
asserts this. M0 is the determinate control: its difference has motions,
C moving by (ε_T + ε_p,if any)·(15/4, 9/4, 0), and every force is exactly zero.

**G-2 distinguishability.** Take the mixed-basis would-be subtraction and the
twin's published difference. They differ in the motion rows (u_B,x is 0.0141541… m against 0.0141343… m):
under the mixed basis ε_p and the weight response do not cancel (ε_p ∝ 1/E). Out of plane,
u_B,z = −4.36e-5 m is the uncancelled weight response. The twin's force rows are
those of the `REF` thermal difference times E_HOT/E_REF = 0.960526…, and its
motions equal the `REF` thermal motions, because a free-strain response's motions
do not depend on E when G/E is fixed.

## Rows

- `u/<node>/<UX|UY|UZ|RX|RY|RZ>`: global translations (m) and rotations (rad) of
  every node, anchors included (zero).
- `reaction/<anchor>/<FX..MZ>`: the force and moment the support exerts on the
  pipe, in global axes, with the moment about the anchor node.
- `end_force/<member>/<i|j>/<FX..MZ>`: the force and moment the node exerts on the
  member end, in global axes.
- `resultant/<member>/<0|1>/<N|VY|VZ|T|MY|MZ>`: DEL-05-03's sign convention, which
  is the actions on the positive-x cut face at stations 0 and 1, in the member
  frame (x from `from` to `to`; y the y-reference less its x part; z = x × y).
  Here N is the effective force S.
- `axial/<member>/<S|N_wall|N_mech>`: as defined above.

Which of these a product publishes, and in which frame, is the product's choice.
A product end row whose axial component is the wall force differs from
`end_force` by p·A_i in each case, and by Δp·A_i in a pressure-change difference.

**Row text.** Every published difference reads `operating minus sustained difference`.

**Magnitude rule.** A magnitude row of a difference is the norm of the
difference's own components: `from_combined_components`, with its exact square.
It is never the difference of the operands' magnitudes. The file also gives that
forbidden value as `forbidden_subtracted_magnitudes`, so that a check can tell the
two apart. For M1's B translation, for example, the correct magnitude is
0.016828… m and the forbidden one is 0.016229… m. Magnitudes are given for
translation and rotation per node and for force and moment per anchor.

## G-1: what "contained by content and with multiplicity" means

REQ-012: the subtrahend's *primitive loads* must be contained, "by content and
with multiplicity", in the minuend's. DEL-05-01 defines a primitive load as an
identity, a category, a target, a direction, a dimension, a magnitude with its
unit, an optional span, and provenance. "By content" is read as everything except
the identity: category, target, direction, dimension, the magnitude's binary64
bits, and the unit. "With multiplicity" means that, for every content, the
sustained case's count does not exceed the operating case's. Provenance is not
counted, and no member load with a span is used here.

Rows marked "fixed" have the same verdict under both readings below. These are
the expectations:

| Row | Sustained | Operating | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| contained | W | W, T(AB), T(BC) | published |
| sustained_only_load | W, F_y(B) | W, T, T | refused (G-1) |
| twice_in_sustained_once_in_operating | W, W′ (same content, other id) | W, T, T | refused (G-1) |
| twice_in_both | W, W′ | W, W′, T, T | published |
| same_target_different_magnitude | W = −1843.75 N | W = −1843.5 N, T, T | refused (G-1) |
| same_target_magnitude_one_ulp_apart | W | W one binary64 further from zero, T, T | refused (G-1) |
| operands_swapped | W, T, T | W | refused (G-1) |
| no_primitives_in_sustained | (pressure only) | T, T | published |

### Open readings: what counts toward containment on the exact route

On the exact route a case's actions are more than its primitive loads. DEL-04-07
defines *Action* as "a primitive load, an equivalent-static load, a non-empty
pressure-region list, a boundary motion, a member state of non-zero free strain,
or a preload present in every case". Pressure is authored in regions, which
DEL-05-01 leaves to DEL-04-07. G-1 names primitive loads only, and
`docs/theory/load_combinations.md` states for the pressure-free route that "its
G-1 compares primitive loads only". The two readings:

- **A (primitive loads only).** This is REQ-012's literal words, and the generator
  computes outcomes under it.
- **B (every action, by content and multiplicity).** A pressure region counts by
  its members and its pressure's bits, the closed-end pressure strain counts when
  the setting is on, and member states, support-record motions and preloads count
  too.

| Row | A | B |
|---|---|---|
| pressure_differs (p_s ≠ p_o) | published | refused |
| pressure_absent_in_operating | published | refused |
| elongation_off_sustained_on_operating | published | published |
| elongation_on_sustained_off_operating | published | refused |
| support motion authored as an imposed-displacement primitive, sustained only | refused | refused |
| support motion authored on the support record, sustained only | published | refused |
| member thermal state (load-state document), sustained only | published | refused |
| constant-effort preload in both | published | published |

Every numerical pair records both verdicts. The pressure-change pairs and the
sustained-on/operating-off setting are published under A and refused under B.

**The question exposed.** Reading A is what the text says. It also publishes
differences whose operating case does *not* have every load of the sustained
case: pressure dropped, or pressure elongation on only in the sustained case.
AC-003 says the published difference is "the closed-form response to the loads
the operating case has beyond the sustained case". Under reading A that phrase
has to cover a negative "beyond", the response to a removed pressure. Reading B
keeps G-1's purpose but goes beyond REQ-012's words. Not decided here:

- whether pressure regions, the elongation setting, a support-record motion and a
  member state count toward G-1;
- if pressure counts, whether a pressure *increase* (p_o > p_s, same regions) is
  "contained".

Two more facts bear on this:

- **Support motion.** The request for this package assumed a prescribed support
  motion is not a primitive load. DEL-05-01 lists imposed displacement among the
  eight primitive categories, with a support degree of freedom as its target, so
  authored that way it counts under both readings. It falls outside G-1 under
  reading A only if it is authored as a support-record value, which DEL-04-07's
  "boundary motion" may mean.
- **Temperature state.** As a thermal primitive, including one resolved from an
  operating condition (DEL-05-01 REQ-013 turns conditions into ordinary loads
  before anything reads the case), it counts. As a member free-strain state of a
  load and reference-state document it does not under reading A. DEL-05-01 says
  that which deliverable states such a document is not decided.

## G-2: one modulus basis

The mixed pair (sustained on `REF`, operating on `HOT`) is refused. The twin with
both on `HOT` is published. Its difference is the thermal response with
E = 187 062 500 000 Pa and G = 73 000 000 000 Pa. G-2 is read as the two cases
selecting the same basis: the same exact reference, or the same solve temperature.
Whether a reference and a temperature that resolve to equal moduli count as one
basis is not exercised here.

## M1 by hand

Release the anchor at C. A–B–C becomes a cantilever from A: AB has length
a = 21/4 along x, BC has length b = 27/8 along y. The thermal free strain moves
C by δ₀ = ε_T(a, b, 0) and rotates nothing. Unit loads at C give these in-plane
flexibilities (EB, axial deformation included, EA = E·A_n, EI = E·I_n):

- f_xx = b³/(3EI) + a·b²/EI + a/EA;
- f_yy = b/EA + a³/(3EI);
- f_zz = (a + b)/EI (for M_z);
- f_xy = −a²b/(2EI);
- f_xz = −b²/(2EI) − a·b/EI;
- f_yz = a²/(2EI).

Solve f·(X, Y, Z) = −δ₀ for the support force and moment at C on the pipe. Then
A's reaction is −(X, Y, 0) with M_z,A = −(a·Y − b·X + Z), and every other row
follows by statics. The generator solves this 3 × 3 system separately and checks
it against the full method 2 on `REF` and `HOT`:

| Basis | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REF` | −36 616.002… N | −17 056.358… N | −72 147.412… N·m |
| `HOT` | −35 170.634… N | −16 383.081… N | −69 299.488… N·m |

For ε_p the same formulas hold with ε_p in place of ε_T.

## Cross-checks inside the generator

If any check fails, nothing is written. The counts are in `cross_checks`.

1. **Two methods.** Direct stiffness (12 × 12 Euler–Bernoulli elements, exact
   Gauss–Jordan, free strains as held end actions) and the force method (release
   the far anchor, virtual work by Simpson's rule, which is exact for these
   quadratic integrands, a 6 × 6 compatibility solve, statics) agree exactly on
   every row of every case (6 696 rows).
2. **The difference is the closed form.** For every published pair, operating
   minus sustained from method 1 equals method 2 applied to the operating loads
   less the sustained loads, on every row (1 722 rows).
3. **Equilibrium.** Global force and moment balance holds exactly for every case
   (66) and every difference (16).
4. **Wall form.** DEL-04-07's wall form equals the closed-end free strain on every
   pressurised case with the setting on (19).
5. **Setting independence.** Equal-pressure differences are identical under
   readings W and J. Pressure-change differences differ, and the J values are
   given.
6. **Hand formula.** The M1 hand formula reproduces the redundants on both bases.
7. **Not trivially zero; G-2 distinguishable.** The M1 and M2 thermal differences
   have non-zero motions and reactions, and the withheld G-2 values differ from
   the closed form of the twin.
8. **Binary64 substitution.** Every input is exactly its binary64 (63 inputs).
   Every G-1 verdict, recomputed from the emitted bits, is unchanged. The two
   bases' moduli are binary64 and differ.

## Limits

- Linear Euler–Bernoulli models without bends, springs or nonlinear supports.
  No certificate bound (REQ-015) is given.
- Tolerances, refusal codes and row identities are not set here. Outcomes are
  stated as `published`, `refused_g1` and `refused_g2`.
- Weight is authored as weight-category concentrated forces, not generated
  self-weight. The operating and sustained weights are identical, so the
  differences do not depend on how weight is formed.
- No case uses a hydrostatic test, an operating-condition use, the Bourdon option
  or the held-status subtrahend.
