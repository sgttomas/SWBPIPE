# Exact-route references: the declared operating minus sustained difference

Frozen, independently derived expectations for a result-state subtraction
declared `operating_minus_sustained` on the exact pressure route, with its gates
G-1 (containment) and G-2 (one modulus basis):
[DEL-05-02](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-02/ScopeOfWork.md) REQ-012, AC-003
and AC-012, with pressure as [DEL-04-07](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-07/ScopeOfWork.md)
defines it and the section bases of
[DEL-05-03](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/ScopeOfWork.md). The
pressure-free neighbour is [`op1b_difference_basis`](../op1b_difference_basis/README.md).
This package shares no code or values with it.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator uses the Python standard
library only and imports, reads and runs no product code or output. All inputs
are invented and deliberately non-standard. No value comes from a design code,
catalogue or vendor. No code compliance is claimed.

**Status.** Candidate references. An independent reviewer re-derived every
operand, difference, magnitude and hand value with exact rational equality. Two
findings were then repaired: the G-1 expectations now follow the decided rule,
and each subtraction row carries the value formed from the published operands.
No product test binds them yet. The product refused a declared basis on the exact
route when they were written.

## Exact form and rounding

Each value is a finite sum of rational multiples of integer powers of π, written
as in `3/4*pi + 1/2 + 5/7*pi^-1`. It is published as `value`, the nearest
binary64 (ties to even). The rounding is decided from rational bounds on π about
1e-160 apart (Machin's formula, with a proven error). A magnitude is published
through its `exact_square`. Every input is a dyadic rational, so every input is
exactly its own binary64.

reference takes α from that point. These choices remove two readings that the

**Sections** (DEL-05-03, as the bases table defines them):

- r_o = D/2 = 35/256;
- nominal inner radius r_i,n = r_o − t = 131/1024;
- reduced wall t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a = 15/2048;
- pressure bore r_i,r = r_o − t_r = 265/2048.

From these:

- A_n = π(r_o² − r_i,n²) = 2439/1048576·π;
- I_n = π(r_o⁴ − r_i,n⁴)/4 = 89660079/4398046511104·π, and J_n = 2I_n;
- A_i = π r_i,r² = 70225/4194304·π.

Stiffness uses the nominal section. A_i uses the pressure bore. Each member has
EA = E·A_n, EI = E·I_n and GJ = G·J_n, so every stiffness is a rational multiple
of π.

**Free strains.**

- Thermal: ε_T = α·ΔT = 1425/524288.
- Closed-end pressure: ε_p = (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_n). At p = 5.75 MPa on `REF`
  this is 452249/4863951360. It is rational because A_i/A_n is.

## Pressure on these models, and the elongation setting

DEL-04-07 says pressure enters a case "as loads only: the Poisson shortening of
the wall, the terminal end loads, and the remainders at bends and joints". The
wall form of those loads, as `rt_a3_tee` writes it, has three parts:

- each member has the eigenstrain −2ν·p·A_i/(E·A_n) on the law E·A_n·ε = N_w − 2ν·p·A_i;
- each `transfers_to_wall` terminal of member k at node n carries the cap −p·A_i·s_k,
  where s_k is the unit vector from n into the member;
- a junction's end loads are the sum of its caps, −Σ p·A_i·s.

**On these models the wall form equals the closed-end free strain.** Every region
here is one straight member, or (in M2) members joined only where diameter, wall
and direction are identical. At any node, add each incident member's end force
N_w·s to that end's cap −p·A_i·s (if the end is a terminal). The sum is
Σ (N_w − p·A_i)·s = Σ S·s, where S = N_w − p·A_i is the effective force. At an
interior joint of a region there are no caps, but s₁ = −s₂ and the bores are
equal, so the p·A_i terms cancel in the same way. The nodal equations are
therefore those of members with no pressure loads at all, whose axial law is

  E·A_n·ε = S + (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i,

that is, members with the free strain ε_p. That is the governing statement for
the setting **on**: "the closed-end free strain (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s)". The
generator checks this on every pressurised case with the setting on. It solves
the wall form with the caps and the Poisson eigenstrain, and confirms that every
motion and reaction equals the free-strain solution and that N_w = S + p·A_i
(19 cases).

For the setting **off** ("the closed-end free pressure strain is omitted;
pressure thrust at unbalanced locations is applied") there are two readings:

- **W (computed; the text best supports it).** Off is on without ε_p. On these
  models that leaves no pressure load on the nodal equations. The corner thrust
  −Σ p·A_i·s at a junction is still present, but the walls' end-cap tension p·A_i
  balances it. That holds because every terminal transfers to the wall. On is
  then exactly DEL-04-07's current loads, as the tee reference reproduces them.
- **J (alternative, given where it differs).** Off applies the junction resultant
  −Σ p·A_i·s at each junction node as an external nodal load on members whose
  axial law carries no pressure strain. On adds ε_p to that. Under J, on is not
  DEL-04-07's wall form: it counts the corner thrust a second time. Reading J
  also treats the corner as an unbalanced location, which is how the request for
  this package described it.

**Unbalanced thrust.** Under reading W, a corner where two regions meet with
transferring terminals is not an unbalanced location. Its thrust is balanced
through the walls and deforms the model only through ε_p. A genuinely unbalanced
thrust under W needs a `separately_supported_or_compensated` terminal. Such a
terminal is not modelled here, because for a region that is not closed at one end
the text does not say what off omits.

**Wall and mechanical forces.** With either setting, `N_wall = S + p·A_i` (the
statics of a closed region) and `N_mech = S + (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i` (DEL-05-03's
identities). Under the setting on, N_mech is E·A_n·ε, as the tee reference defines
it. Under off, the identity is applied as written; which recovered force a product
calls N_m with the setting off is an open reading.

## Pairs

Each pair has a sustained case and an operating case. The sustained case has the
weight primitives and the pressure regions. The operating case has the same
weight primitives and pressure regions plus the thermal primitive ΔT on every
member. The setting each case is solved with is labelled. For each pair the file
gives:

- the rows, under the field the pair's `values_field` names: `difference` for a
  published declared difference, `undeclared_subtraction` for a pair G-1 refuses
  (the values of the plain subtraction, not a published declared difference),
  `withheld_would_be_difference` for the pair G-2 refuses;
- the magnitudes, under `magnitudes`, `undeclared_subtraction_magnitudes` or
  `withheld_would_be_magnitudes`;
- the two cases' own rows as binary64 (`cases`), which are the published operands;
- `consists_of`, which says what the subtraction is the response to;
- the reading-J values where they differ analytically from reading W
  (`reading_J_alternative`);
- for every same-basis pair, the reading-J operands (`reading_J_cases`) and, for
  every row, the reading-J publication value formed from them
  (`reading_J_publication`, with `reading_J_publication_summary`).

Statements in this section that two subtractions are identical, or that one does
not depend on the setting or on the reading, are **analytical**: they compare
the exact differences. The publication values (`from_published_operands`) are
each formed from rounded operands. Different operands with the same exact
difference can therefore give publication values that differ because their
operands were rounded, in some rows by many units in the last place.
Where a reader compares publication values bit for bit, the file says row by row
which are bit-identical (below).

| Pair | Outcome | The subtraction is the closed-form response to | Setting dependence |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / off–off | published | ε_T alone | analytically identical to on–on, and under W and J, on every row; publication values: see "Publication values across settings and readings" |
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / on–on | published | ε_T alone | as above |
| M0, M1, M2 / equal pressure / operating on, sustained off | published (elongation is a separate action the sustained case does not apply) | ε_T + ε_p(p) | differs analytically from on–on by the response to ε_p; analytically identical under W and J |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / on–on | **refused (G-1)**: undeclared subtraction given | ε_T + ε_p(p_o) − ε_p(p_s); N_wall, N_mech also carry their Δp·A_i terms | J differs (adds the response to the junction resultant of Δp) |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / off–off | **refused (G-1)**: undeclared subtraction given | ε_T alone under W; N_wall, N_mech carry Δp·A_i | J differs (response to the junction resultant of Δp) |
| M1, M2 / pressure change / operating on, sustained off | **refused (G-1)**: undeclared subtraction given | ε_T + ε_p(p_o) under W | J differs |
| M1 / g2 / mixed basis (S on `REF`, O on `HOT`, both on) | **refused (G-2)** | — (the would-be subtraction is given as `withheld_would_be_difference`) | — |
| M1 / g2 / same-basis twin (both on `HOT`, both on) | published | ε_T on `HOT`'s moduli | — |

Each pair also records `g1_contained` under the decided rule and, as a control,
`control_primitives_only_g1_contained` (primitive loads only), which is true for
every pair.

Why the equal-pressure differences do not depend analytically on the setting:
the two cases of a pair share every pressure load, so the loads cancel in the
exact subtraction under either setting and either reading. Why an on–off pair
differs analytically by exactly the response to ε_p: on is off plus ε_p under
both readings. The pressure-change
pairs are refused because a region with another pressure is other content; their
mechanics is kept as the undeclared subtraction.

### Which field is compared how

DEL-05-02 forms a subtraction from the published operand rows: the exact
difference of the two published binary64 values, rounded once (its method
choices, AC-001, REQ-017). So each row of a subtraction carries two values:

| Field | What it is | How a product row is compared with it |
|---|---|---|
| `from_published_operands` | the two operands' emitted binary64 values (`cases`), subtracted exactly, rounded once to binary64 | **bit for bit**: this is what a published difference must equal |
| `value`, with `exact` | the analytical difference of the exact (unrounded) solutions, rounded once | the mechanics reference, compared at the analytic tolerance |

The two differ in the last bit on **263 of the 1 722** rows of the 16 same-basis
pairs (129 of 1 020 published `difference` rows, 134 of 702
`undeclared_subtraction` rows), and on 18 of the 84 `withheld_would_be_difference`
rows. The reviewer's example is M1, equal pressure, on–on, u/B/UX. The operands
0.014617794698056548 and 0.0004835227236798495 give 0.0141342719743767 from the
published operands, against the analytical 0.014134271974376698. The reading-J
publication values are formed the same way from the reading-J operands, which
the file gives as `reading_J_cases`.

**Magnitude rule.** A magnitude row of a subtraction is the norm of the
subtraction's own components. It is never the difference of the operands'
magnitudes. Each magnitude record has these fields:

| Field | Components it is formed from | Compared |
|---|---|---|
| `from_published_operand_components` | the row's `from_published_operands` values (binary64), squared and summed exactly, square root correctly rounded | bit for bit |
| `from_combined_components`, with `exact_square` | the analytical components (`exact`), the norm correctly rounded | at the analytic tolerance |
| `forbidden_subtracted_magnitudes` | the result of the wrong algorithm: the difference of the operands' analytical magnitudes, instead of the magnitude of the difference | a distinguishing negative control, usable only where `forbidden_value_distinguishes` is true |
| `forbidden_value_distinguishes` | computed: true when the wrong algorithm's value differs in its bits from both `from_combined_components` and `from_published_operand_components` | — |

For M1's B translation, for example, the correct magnitude is 0.016828… m and the
forbidden one is 0.016229… m. Magnitudes are given for translation and rotation
per node and for force and moment per anchor.

The wrong algorithm often gives the right answer: for example, when the vector
is zero in both operands, as for a fixed anchor's translation. A record is a negative control only
where its flag is true. Of the 106 published magnitude records,
60 distinguish and 46 do not. Of the
72 undeclared ones 48 distinguish, and of the 10
withheld ones 6.

The two correct fields differ in
the last bit on 11 of 106 published magnitude records, on 10 of 72 undeclared
ones, and on 1 of 10 withheld ones.

## G-1: what "contained by content and with multiplicity" means

The rule, as decided for DEL-05-02 REQ-012: a declared subtraction is published
only when "everything the subtrahend case applies is contained, by content and
with multiplicity, in what the minuend case applies". On the exact route what a
case applies is:

- its primitive loads;
- each pressure region with its pressure;
- pressure elongation, where the case includes it;
- each generated load;
- each prescribed motion;
- each member's authored state of non-zero free strain.

The generator reads that as follows (function `applied`):

- **A primitive load's content** is everything except its identity: category,
  target, direction, dimension, the magnitude's binary64 bits, and the unit.
  Provenance is not counted, and no member load with a span is used here.
- **A pressure region's content** is its members and its pressure's bits. A region
  with another pressure is other content.
- **Pressure elongation** is a further action of its own, not part of a region's
  content. A sustained case without it is contained in an operating case with it.
- **A member state** is compared as authored: member, kind, expansion law
  reference, and the temperature's bits and unit. It is not compared by the
  resolved strain.
- **A prescribed motion** is compared by target, degree of freedom, value bits and
  unit, whether it is authored as an imposed-displacement primitive or on the
  support record.
- **A preload** present in every case is not among the listed actions.
- **With multiplicity** means that, for every content, the sustained case's count
  does not exceed the operating case's.

Every row's `outcome` is the expectation under that rule. The earlier reading,
primitive loads only, is kept as `control_primitives_only` and is not an
expectation.

| sustained_only_load | refused (G-1) | the sustained occasional force is absent from the operating case | refused |

## G-2: one modulus basis

The mixed pair (sustained on `REF`, operating on `HOT`) is refused. The twin with
both on `HOT` is published. Its difference is the thermal response with
E = 187 062 500 000 Pa and G = 73 000 000 000 Pa. G-2 is read as the two cases

## M1 by hand

Release the anchor at C. A–B–C becomes a cantilever from A: AB has length
a = 21/4 along x, BC has length b = 27/8 along y. The thermal free strain moves
C by δ₀ = ε_T(a, b, 0) and rotates nothing. Unit loads at C give these in-plane
flexibilities (EB, axial deformation included, EA = E·A_n, EI = E·I_n):

- f_xx = b³/(3EI) + a·b²/EI + a/EA;
- f_yy = b/EA + a³/(3EI);
- f_zz = (a + b)/EI (for M_z);
- f_xy = −a²b/(2EI);
- f_xz = −b²/(2EI) − a·b/EI;
- f_yz = a²/(2EI).

Solve f·(X, Y, Z) = −δ₀ for the support force and moment at C on the pipe. Then
A's reaction is −(X, Y, 0) with M_z,A = −(a·Y − b·X + Z), and every other row
follows by statics. The generator solves this 3 × 3 system separately and checks
it against the full method 2 on `REF` and `HOT`:

| Basis | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| `REF` | −36 616.002… N | −17 056.358… N | −72 147.412… N·m |
| `HOT` | −35 170.634… N | −16 383.081… N | −69 299.488… N·m |

For ε_p the same formulas hold with ε_p in place of ε_T.

## Cross-checks inside the generator

If any check fails, nothing is written. The counts are in `cross_checks`.

1. **Two methods.** Direct stiffness (12 × 12 Euler–Bernoulli elements, exact
   Gauss–Jordan, free strains as held end actions) and the force method (release
   the far anchor, virtual work by Simpson's rule, which is exact for these
   quadratic integrands, a 6 × 6 compatibility solve, statics) agree exactly on
   every row of every case (6 696 rows).
2. **The difference is the closed form.** For every same-basis pair, published or
   undeclared, the analytical operating minus sustained from method 1 equals
   method 2 applied to the operating loads less the sustained loads, on every row
   (1 722 rows).
3. **Equilibrium.** Global force and moment balance holds exactly for every case
   (66) and every difference (16).
4. **Wall form.** DEL-04-07's wall form equals the closed-end free strain on every
   pressurised case with the setting on (19).
5. **Setting independence, analytically.** Equal-pressure exact differences are
   identical under readings W and J, and between off–off and on–on.
   Pressure-change exact differences differ, and the J values are given. For
   publication values, the generator computes and emits which rows are
   bit-identical (see "Publication values across settings and readings").
6. **Hand formula.** The M1 hand formula reproduces the redundants on both bases.
7. **Not trivially zero; G-2 distinguishable.** The M1 and M2 thermal differences
   have non-zero motions and reactions, and the withheld G-2 values differ from
   the closed form of the twin.
8. **Binary64 substitution.** Every input is exactly its binary64 (63 inputs).
   Every G-1 verdict, under the decided rule and the control, is recomputed from
   the emitted records (values by their bits) and is unchanged. The two bases'
   moduli are binary64 and differ.
9. **Published operands.** Every subtraction row's `from_published_operands` is
   formed from the emitted operand values in `cases`. The rows where it differs
   from the analytical `value` are counted in `cross_checks`.

## Limits

- Linear Euler–Bernoulli models without bends, springs or nonlinear supports.
  No certificate bound (REQ-015) is given.
- Tolerances, refusal codes and row identities are not set here. Outcomes are
  stated as `published`, `refused_g1` and `refused_g2`.
- Weight is authored as weight-category concentrated forces, not generated
  self-weight. The operating and sustained weights are identical, so the
  differences do not depend on how weight is formed.
- No case uses a hydrostatic test, an operating-condition use, the Bourdon option
  or the held-status subtrahend.
- No pair carries a generated load. The decided G-1 rule counts generated loads,
  but this package does not exercise them.
- Prescribed motions have gate coverage only. Two G-1 rows exercise them, but no
  numerical pair has a prescribed motion.
