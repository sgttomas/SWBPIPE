## Convention

The owner's ruling on the centre-of-gravity convention (DEL-03-05 R10,
2026-10-24): "For the centre-of-gravity I approve option A as recommended."
`center_of_gravity` is r, a vector in the model's global X/Y/Z axes measured
from the component's `node`, in model length units. The weight W (a force)
acts at the node as F = W·ĝ, ĝ the unit vector of the case's gravity axis with
the sign of its acceleration, together with the moment M = r × F. The
acceleration's magnitude does not scale W: W is the user's force.

## Cases

### W15-B-WRENCH: the nodal wrench (24 cases)

W = 600 N with r = (−3/4, 0, 0), (0, 1/8, 0), (0, 0, −1/16) and
(−3/4, 1/8, −1/16) m, under gravity along ±X, ±Y and ±Z. **Method: statics
of a point force** (Hibbeler, *Engineering Mechanics: Statics*, equivalent
force systems). With F = f·e_k, f = ±W:
- along X: M = (0, r_z·f, −r_y·f);
- along Y: M = (−r_z·f, 0, r_x·f);
- along Z: M = (r_y·f, −r_x·f, 0).

`rows` are the six global components, zeros included. `primitives` are what the
generator must emit: the force along the gravity axis, then one moment per
nonzero component in x, y, z order, each with its signed value. A zero moment
component has no primitive.

### W15-B-CANT: a cantilever carrying a component body's weight (12 cases)

A(0) fixed → B(1) → C(3/2) → D(3) free along +X: pipes A–B and B–C and the
body C–D, mapped by a valve at D with stiffness scale s. Only the body is
selected for self-weight, so the only loads are its component's wrench at D.
The cases are every gravity axis and sign with the combined offset (s = 4,
Euler–Bernoulli); gravity −Y with each offset axis alone (s = 4); gravity −Y
and −Z with Timoshenko (s = 4, exact route); and gravity −Y with no scale
(s = 1: the weight is generated for an unscaled body).

**Method: statics and unit-load virtual work** (Timoshenko & Gere, *Mechanics
of Materials*; Hibbeler, *Structural Analysis*, virtual work). With the tip
wrench (F, M) at x = L = 3:
- section actions on the +x face of the i-side portion: N = F_x,
  V_y = F_y, V_z = F_z, T = M_x, M_y(x) = M_y − (L−x)·F_z,
  M_z(x) = M_z + (L−x)·F_y;
- u_x = F_x·∫dx/(sEA), θ_x = M_x·∫dx/(sGJ);
- θ_z = ∫M_z/(sEI)dx, u_y = ∫M_z·(x_P−x)/(sEI)dx, plus F_y·∫dx/(sκGA) for
  Timoshenko;
- θ_y = ∫M_y/(sEI)dx, u_z = −∫M_y·(x_P−x)/(sEI)dx, plus F_z·∫dx/(sκGA)
  (a right-handed rotation about +Y turns +X toward −Z);
- the support's action on the structure at A: −F and −(M + d × F),
  d = (L, 0, 0).

The section is W-15's: OD 0.2 m, ID 0.18 m, E = 200 GPa, ν = 1/4,
G = 80 GPa, the energy-matched annular κ. Forces and moments are rational;
motions are rational multiples of 1/π. `exact` prints q or `q/pi`; `decimal`
has 40 significant digits; `value` is the nearest binary64, rounded with lower
and upper rational bounds of π, which must agree. The row conventions are
W-9's and W-15's.

## Cross-checks

The wrench is recomputed by the determinant form of r × F, and its static
equivalence with the point force at the centre of gravity is checked about an
arbitrary point. Each cantilever is solved again by an exact direct-stiffness
route: axial, torsion and the two bending planes decoupled, every element
scaled by its s (Timoshenko Φ = 12EI/(κGAL²), unchanged by s), the x–z plane
mapped onto the x–y bending matrix through (u_z, −θ_y) ↔ (u_y, θ_z). Every
nodal motion, support reaction and element end action is compared with the
closed form, together with free-DOF equilibrium and the balance of the
reactions against the tip wrench about the anchor. All 1080 comparisons agree
exactly.

## Product binding and criterion

| Case | Route in `w15_component_weight_benchmarks.rs` |
|---|---|
| W15-B-WRENCH | the self-weight generator (`self_weight::generate_self_weight_operations`), its primitives and evidence |
| W15-B-CANT EB | the generator's primitives, solved on PF and on EX (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`, Euler–Bernoulli, `pressure_regions: []`) |
| W15-B-CANT TIM | the same on EX (`timoshenko_annulus_energy_v1`) |

The body is a valve at D mapping `body:c-d` with `solver_consumption`
`mechanics_geometry_only`, its weight and centre of gravity, and (except the
s = 1 case) the case's stiffness scale. The criterion is unchanged
(DEC-024/DEC-026). A nonzero row passes when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero
row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale. Both solver modes run against the
same reference.

## Reproduce and change

```
python3 -I generators/w15b_reference.py          # check: rebuild, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/w15b_reference.py --write  # rewrite references.json and SHA256SUMS
```

**Re-freeze rules.** No value changes to match a product output; a
disagreement goes back to this reference's owner and an independent refuter. A
change to the theory or a convention is a re-freeze, with a recorded reason
and a new refutation. Editing this README changes only its hash in
`SHA256SUMS`; the test pins the hash of `references.json` only.
