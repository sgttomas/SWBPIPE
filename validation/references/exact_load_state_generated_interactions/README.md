# Generated loads and the rest of a load and reference-state document (exact route)

Analytic expectations for selected generated-load interactions required by
release criterion 1, prepared for comparison with a later exact-route
implementation (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`). The fixtures put generated static
seismic and wind loads beside a constant-effort support, the seismic terms
formed by subtraction and their SRSS and absolute-sum combinations, a lift-off
support that seismic uplift lifts, and a span that a connector replaces. No
product test reads them yet.

[`../exact_load_state_equivalent_static`](../exact_load_state_equivalent_static/README.md)
and [`../seismic_component_weight`](../seismic_component_weight/README.md):
the sibling packages supply dedicated checks of generated loads and component
weight. These fixtures exercise those loads beside other actions and
boundaries.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs
no product code, and reads no file other than its own package's. All inputs are
invented and deliberately non-standard; no value or example from a catalogue,
code or vendor source is used. There is no internal pressure in this package:
no pressure region and no pressure load. The standard claim fence applies.

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
The comparison rule is under "Product binding".

## What is decided, and what this package rests on

- **A generated load on a load and reference-state document** is what it is on
  a plain exact-route document: the same generator, the same identities, the
  same mass basis, factor one. The binding expects each generated source once
  at factor 1 in the case record, alongside its declared sources, with the
  generated source's identity (DEL-04-07 REQ-011, its last sentences; decided
  by SWBPIPE's Agent 0).
- **The seismic rule**, as SWBPIPE's Agent 0 decided it on 12 November and as
  `../seismic_component_weight/README.md` records it: every member carries, for
  each global axis, a load per length equal to the g-factor times the seismic
  case's gravity times the member's mass per length. A member whose component
  weight the self-weight plan applies carries instead the g-factor times the
  component's entered weight W at the component's node, with the moments of
  that force about the node from the centre-of-gravity vector. W is a fixed
  force. A weight of zero is not applied. A connector-replaced span carries no
  generated seismic load.
- **The constant-effort force in a subtraction.** In the active-support CEF and
  LO fixtures the constant-effort force is the same in both operand cases. Its
  own row cancels in the subtraction.
- **A generated identity that collides with an unlisted stored primitive is
  refused.** SWBPIPE's Agent 0, 18 November 2026, among its terms for
  generation on a load and reference-state document: "a generated identity colliding with an unlisted stored primitive is refused before assembly, so the ordinary and certified paths cannot attribute one identity to two sources." The decision was
  made for the engine unit's brief and is not yet in any scope of work; the
  scope takes it in the pull request that lifts the refusal of generation on
  such a document.

Text relied on: DEL-05-01 (static-equivalent generation, mass basis,
self-weight plan, REQ-008, REQ-015, AC-002, AC-007), DEL-04-07 (REQ-011,
"Action"), DEL-03-05 (a rigid-family component's weight, centre of gravity,
consumption declaration and stiffness scale), DEL-03-08 (mass per length),
DEL-05-03 (section bases), DEL-04-04 (the lift-off support and its
state-switched test), DEL-05-02 (subtraction, SRSS, absolute sum, the
fixed-load count κ and REQ-011's gates), and the reference system's
specification (`fixtures/reference_system/SPECIFICATION.md`: RS-LO1 "contact on
positive reaction", RS-CE1, the replaced span RS-P180, EQ-4 and EQ-6).

own documents.

## What the expectations are about

The mechanics rows give analytic values. The outcome fields give the support
states, gates and refusals expected for the fixtures. Source labels and row IDs
are local to this reference: the labels (`pipe:a-b/seismic`, …) and the
`identity` members name what each generated load is generated from, and are
not the product's identity strings.

## Units, conventions and number format

- SI throughout: m, N, N·m, rad, kg/m³, Pa, m/s².
- Global axes X, Y, Z; Z is vertical, up. Every member is parallel to a global
  axis.
- **Motion** (`motion/<node>/ux…rz`): the node's global translation and its
  right-handed rotation.
- **Reaction** (`reaction/<support>/Fx…Mz`): the global force and moment that
  an anchor exerts on the structure, with the moment about the anchor's node.
- **Constant effort** (`constant_effort/<support>/Fz`): the force the support
  applies on the pipe, along +Z.
- **Lift-off** (`nonlinear_support/<support>/reaction_Fz` and `relative_uz`):
  the support's force on the pipe (zero when lifted), and the node's uz less
  its ground's. The ground does not move here.
- **Member end** (`member_end/<pipe>/<i|j>/Fx…Mz`): the global force and moment
  that the end node exerts on the member, with the moment about that end node.
  End i is the member's first node.
- **Axial rows.** An axial row is the global component, along the member's
  own axis, of the node-on-member end action (the member-end convention above)
  associated with the elastic (effective) axial force N_el, as in
  `../exact_load_state_equivalent_static` ("the member-end rows give the
  element force N_el"; there the wall force is N_w = N_el + p·A_i). It is a
  global component, not a tension-positive axial force: on CEF `pipe:d-e`,
  along −Z, the sustained case's Fz is −1675.38 N at end i and +2515.71 N at
  end j. No case in this package carries pressure, so N_el and the pipe-wall
  force are equal here. Each signed axial member-end row carries
  `axial_quantity` saying so; on CEF-EQ-SRSS and CEF-EQ-ABS the same rows are
  labelled as magnitudes aggregated from those elastic axial components.
- **Values**: `exact` is a sum of rational multiples of π^k. Areas and second
  moments of an annulus carry π, so distributed loads carry π, a component's W
  and a constant-effort force are rational, and motions carry 1/π. An SRSS
  value's `exact` is `sqrt(…)` of such a sum. `decimal` gives 40 significant
  digits. `value` is the correctly rounded binary64, decided by rational
  bounds of π less than 10⁻⁹⁰ apart (and, for a square root, by lower and
  upper bounds of the root on a grid of spacing 2⁻⁴²⁰); the two bounds' nearest
  binary64 values agree in every row.
- A positive g-factor loads along +axis, as in the two packages named above.

## Sections and mass per length (exact-route bases)

t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, r_n = R − t (nominal wall) and r_p = R − t_r (pressure
bore).

- Stiffness on the nominal section: A_n = π(R² − r_n²), I_n = π(R⁴ − r_n⁴)/4,
  J_n = 2I_n.
- Mass basis `source_od_nominal_wall_pressure_bore_areas/v1`:
  μ = ρ_m·A_n + ρ_c·π r_p² + ρ_i·π((R + t_ins)² − R²).
- Projected diameter D + 2t_ins.

## Model CEF: a constant-effort support and the three seismic terms (part i)

A(0,0,0) – B(2.9,0,0) – C(3.55,0,0) – D(3.55,2.3,0) – E(3.55,2.3,−1.7).

- Members `pipe:a-b` (S1), `pipe:b-c` (S1, the valve body), `pipe:c-d` (S1,
  along +Y), `pipe:d-e` (S2, a riser along −Z). Euler–Bernoulli.
- Anchors `support:a` at A and `support:e` at E, all six degrees of freedom.
- `support:ce-b`: a constant-effort support on UZ at B, 2730 N along +Z. It has
  no stiffness and adds no restraint.
- `component:valve-c`: a valve mapping `pipe:b-c`, declared
  `mechanics_geometry_only`, its node C. Its weight is applied by the
  self-weight plan and its scale 5/2 multiplies EA, GJ and EI of `pipe:b-c`
  (DEL-03-05).
- The seismic gravity equals the weight plan's here, so this model exercises
  the valve's generated force k·W at C but cannot tell a fixed W from one
  wrongly rescaled by the ratio of the two gravities.
  `../seismic_component_weight` is the package that separates the two.

**Cases.** Each case of the document selects the same material point for every
member, carries no thermal strain, and has every support participating.

| Case | Declared ledger (factor 1 each) | Generated (consumed beside it, factor 1 each) |
|---|---|---|
| CEF-SUS | the self-weight plan's weight primitives at g along −Z: −μg on `pipe:a-b`, `pipe:c-d`, `pipe:d-e`; at C the force (0, 0, −W) with r × F = (−130.9, −355.3, 0) N·m; nothing on the valve body | none |
| CEF-EQX, -EQY, -EQZ | the same weight primitives | the case's seismic load on its one axis: k·g·μ on `pipe:a-b`, `pipe:c-d`, `pipe:d-e`; at C the force k·W along the axis with r × F; nothing on the valve body |

The constant-effort force acts in every case. Each case's `load_sources`
gives the declared ledger and the generated sources: each generated load
appears exactly once, at factor 1, and no generated load is named in the
declared ledger.

**Terms.** CEF-dEQX, -dEQY and -dEQZ are `result_state_subtraction`s, each
seismic case minus CEF-SUS, with no declared difference basis. The model is
linear, so in exact mechanics each term equals the response to the case's
generated seismic load alone, and that is cross-checked against a direct solve
of the generated load alone on every row. The constant-effort row of every term is exactly zero; the
force is 2730 N in every case.

**Reaction resultants (the EQ-6 form).** For each term, the sum over the two
anchors of the reaction force, and of its moment about the origin, equals minus
the generated resultant: k·g·Σ(μ·L) along the axis, plus k·W along the axis,
with the moment of each about the origin, the component's r × F included.

| Term | Generated force (N) | Generated moment about the origin (N·m) |
|---|---|---|
| CEF-dEQX | (2628.56, 0, 0) | (0, −188.175, −1590.49) |
| CEF-dEQY | (0, 3765.23, 0) | (269.548, 0, 10450.9) |
| CEF-dEQZ | (0, 0, 2060.22) | (1246.60, −5718.43, 0) |

**Combinations.** CEF-EQ-SRSS and CEF-EQ-ABS combine the three terms at factor
1, on every signed row: √(Σ dEQ²) and Σ|dEQ|, as exact values. Each row also
carries `binary64_from_rounded_operands`: DEL-05-02's exact-then-rounded
arithmetic computed on the ideal rounded analytic operands, the nearest
binary64 of each exact term row. Those ideal operands can differ in the last
place from term rows a product forms by subtracting its own rounded case rows
(for CEF-dEQX they differ on 41 rows), so this field is not the value a
product must publish; see "Product binding". Every operand's κ is exactly 0 (1 − 1) and every reached case
selects the same material point, so both are expected to be published, not
withheld. CEF-EQ-SRSS-OF-CASES, the SRSS of the three cases themselves, has
operands with κ = 1 and is expected to be withheld whole by REQ-011's
fixed-load count gate, with no rows.

**The device acted: single-case rows and the twin without it.** The terms
alone cannot show that the constant-effort support acted, since they are the
same with it and without it. The single-case rows (reactions, motions, member
ends of CEF-SUS and each seismic case) and the device's own row (2730 N in
every case) can. CEF-CTRL-DEVICE-REMOVED-FROM-EVERY-CASE is the same document
with the support removed from every case. It records which rows move: in each
of the four cases, 78 of the 90 rows other than the device's own (every row but
the anchors' motions, which are zero both ways), each by exactly the device's
own response. It also records which do not: every row of the three terms, of
CEF-EQ-SRSS and of CEF-EQ-ABS. Its twin case rows are frozen too.

**One directional row, three combinations.** `directional_row` is
`motion/node:b/uy`. All three terms are nonzero there, and dEQX
(−3.89421e-5 m) is of opposite sign to dEQY (6.38107e-4 m) and dEQZ
(2.11366e-4 m). So the SRSS (6.73329e-4 m), the absolute sum (8.88415e-4 m)
and the signed sum (8.10531e-4 m) all differ. All three are frozen with the
terms. As products of the algebra: CEF-EQ-SIGNED-SUM, a mechanics combination
of the three terms, has κ = 0 and is expected to be withheld whole by the
fixed-load count gate in this model with a constant-effort support.
CEF-SUS-PLUS-EQ, the mechanics combination CEF-SUS + dEQX + dEQY + dEQZ, has
κ = 1, publishes, and carries the signed sum over SUS. These are identities of
the exact mechanics: every exact row of CEF-SUS-PLUS-EQ equals a direct solve of
the weight, the device and all three seismic loads together, and on every row
CEF-SUS-PLUS-EQ − CEF-SUS equals dEQX + dEQY + dEQZ, which equals a direct
solve of the three seismic loads alone (each cross-checked exactly). They are
not identities of binary64 arithmetic. The nearest binary64 of the direct
solve, an exact-then-rounded sum of rounded operands, and a rounded chain from
cases through terms to the combination can differ in the last place. At
`reaction/support:a/My` they give 338.9035124320791, 338.9035124320794 and
338.9035124320785 N·m, and on the directional row the analytic signed sum
rounds to …5423 m where rounded SUS-PLUS-EQ minus rounded SUS gives …5424 m.

## Model LO: a lift-off support under seismic uplift (part ii)

A(0,0,0) – B(3.7,0,0) – C(5,0,0), members `pipe:a-b` and `pipe:b-c` (S1,
Euler–Bernoulli), anchor `support:a` at A.

- `support:lo-b`: a lift-off support on UZ at B, in contact on a positive
  reaction (it pushes the pipe up and cannot pull it down), as RS-LO1 is
  specified. In contact it is a rigid UZ restraint; lifted it is free
  (DEL-04-04 "State").
- `support:ce-c`: a constant-effort support on UZ at the tip C, 1110 N along
  +Z, acting in every case.
- The declared ledger of every case lists the weight primitives −μg on both
  members. EQZ-SEATED and EQZ-LIFTED also generate k·g·μ along +Z on both.

**The state test** (DEL-04-04 "State-switched test"): contact holds while the
reaction is positive; a lifted support re-engages when the node would move
below its ground (uz < 0). The reference stores both candidate states of each
case in `state_test` (and the other state's rows in `other_state_rows`);
exactly one qualifies, with a strict margin. The engine expectation concerns
the qualifying state only: its status and its `rows`.

| Case | k_z | Seated reaction at B (N) | Lifted uz at B (m) | Expected status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LO-SUS | 0 | 865.333 | −2.34553e-3 | in contact |
| LO-EQZ-SEATED | 0.23 | 276.457 | −7.49349e-4 | in contact |
| LO-EQZ-LIFTED | 0.71 | −952.503 | 2.58181e-3 | lifted |

**The threshold.** In the seated state the reaction at B is
R(k) = R0 + k·R1, with R0 = 964935565822961559/1184000000000000·π − 1695 ≈
865.333 N (weight and the constant-effort force) and R1 ≈ −2560.33 N (the
generated seismic Z load per unit g-factor). It lifts at

  k* = −R0/R1 = 1 − 668960000000000000/(321645188607653853·π) ≈ 0.337977.

The threshold is frozen as a boundary value, derived two ways: from the solves
as −R0/R1, and from the propped cantilever's closed form with R_B = 0, where EI
cancels:

  k* = 1 − P·(a²(3L − a)/6) / (μ·g·a²(6L² − 4La + a²)/24).

The two are exactly equal. The two seismic cases lie strictly on either side
of it: in contact below (0.23), lifted above (0.71). **No state or token is
expected at k* itself.** The mechanics there is only that the seated reaction
and the lifted separation are both exactly zero (cross-checked). Without the constant-effort force k* would be 1: the
weight and the generated load share one mass and one gravity. So k* also tests
that the force acts in the seismic case.

**Rows.** Each case's `rows` are those of its qualifying state;
`other_state_rows` gives the other state's, for a reader to see why it does not
qualify. Key values:

| Case | B uz | B ry | C uz | lift-off Fz | A Fz | A My |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LO-SUS | 0 | −2.51400e-4 | 4.08890e-4 | 865.333 | 2249.54 | −1810.46 |
| LO-EQZ-SEATED | 0 | −2.42862e-4 | 4.08929e-4 | 276.457 | 1866.70 | −1560.00 |
| LO-EQZ-LIFTED | 2.58181e-3 | −1.27172e-3 | 4.35150e-3 | 0 | 115.215 | 2486.96 |

**Terms.** LO-dEQZ-SEATED = EQZ-SEATED − SUS equals the seated structure's
response to the generated load alone. LO-dEQZ-LIFTED = EQZ-LIFTED − SUS is the
difference of two independently solved equilibria and does not. Its
`linear_superposition_control` gives the seated structure's response to the
generated load alone, which a product would publish if it formed the term on
one structure. The control solves the generated load on the seated
structure. It differs from the switched-state subtraction by up to 3.94249 mm
at C uz.

**Closed forms (a third way).** Lifted: a cantilever of length L under a
uniform load w = μg(k − 1) and a tip force P,
uz(x) = w·x²(6L² − 4Lx + x²)/(24EI) + P·x²(3L − x)/(6EI), and ry = −duz/dx.
Seated: a propped cantilever with an overhang, R_B = −uz_B·3EI/a³ from the
released cantilever, and uz_C adds R_B·a²(3L − a)/(6EI). Both equal the solves
at B and C in every case.

## Model JR: a connector-replaced span (part iii)

A(0,0,0) – M(1.45,0,0) – B(3.1,0,0), then the span B–C(3.9,0,0) that
`component:joint-bc` replaces, then C – N(3.9,1.15,0) – D(3.9,2.6,0).

- Members `pipe:a-m`, `pipe:m-b` (S2, along +X), `pipe:c-n`, `pipe:n-d` (S1,
  along +Y). Euler–Bernoulli.
- `pipe:b-c` (S2) is replaced by the connector. It carries no load or member
  result, and its element state carries no eigenstrain (the reference system's
  replaced span, spec §5.1).
- Anchors at A, B, C and D. **Both connector faces are anchored**, a valid
  physical boundary condition that sets the connector's relative attachment
  motion to zero. Each run is a beam fixed at both ends, with one interior
  node. (Movable faces would change the live spans' reactions and
  deflections; this fixture does not have them.)
- **The connector's inputs.** `component:joint-bc` is a consumed, stress-free,
  unpressurized connector with zero reference wrench, and either no rods or
  rods with zero reference extension. The binding supplies valid connector
  data. With both faces anchored, no row depends on which admissible connector
  stiffness is supplied, and the connector carries no force.

| Case | Declared ledger | Generated |
|---|---|---|
| JR-EQ | empty | seismic on three axes, k = (0.41, 0.27, 0.33): k·g·μ on the four pipes; nothing on `pipe:b-c` |
| JR-WIND | empty | wind 830 Pa × 0.77 × (D + 2t_ins) along global Z, a whole-span mark on each of the four pipes |
| JR-WIND-PART | empty | as JR-WIND, but the mark on `pipe:c-n` is a part span over [1/5, 13/20] |
| JR-EQ-UNLISTED-PRIMITIVE | empty; the case also stores a nodal force (0, 0, 5150) N at M that the ledger does not list, under an identity no generated load has | as JR-EQ |

**A case whose only action is generation.** For JR-EQ, JR-WIND and
JR-WIND-PART, expect a case result with each generated load applied once at
factor 1. Generation is this fixture's only action: no pressure, no preload
(the connector has zero reference load and extension), no constant-effort
support and no stored primitive selected. Grounds: DEL-04-07's definition of
Action and the schema's empty source array. The reactions at B
and C come only from the runs' own loads (closed form: −wL/2 at each end of
each run, for the whole-span cases).

## Methods

**Method 1: direct stiffness.**
- The 12×12 Euler–Bernoulli member matrix in local axes (EA/L, GJ/L and
  EI/L³·[12, 6L, 4L², 2L²]), rotated by exact direction cosines (local x along
  the member, y = ẑ × x, or ŷ × x for a vertical member, z = x × y; for a
  circular section the choice does not affect the results).
- Each member is split into elements at its load breakpoints (a part-span
  mark's fractions), so every member load is uniform over whole elements; the
  interior nodes are free and not published.
- A uniform element load w (global) enters as work-equivalent nodal loads:
  wL/2 at each end, with moments ±(L²/12)·x̂ × w.
- A component's force and moment, and a constant-effort force, enter as nodal
  loads.
- The system is solved in exact rationals with π factored out. Member end
  actions are k·d minus the equivalent nodal loads. A support's action is the
  sum of the member end actions at its node less any nodal load there.

**Method 2: force method with unit-load integration.**
- Each model is one chain or two chains, cantilevered from a root anchor (A in
  CEF and LO; A and C in JR). The redundants are the six actions of the other
  anchor (E, B and D) or, with the lift-off support in contact, its UZ
  reaction. The lifted LO is statically determinate.
- Section actions are the resultant of everything outboard of the cut (member
  loads, nodal loads, redundants), about the cut point.
- Flexibilities and motions are ∫(N·n/EA + T·t/GJ + M⊥·m⊥/EI) ds, by Simpson's
  rule on each interval between a member's load breakpoints. That is exact:
  the integrands are at most cubic there. A
  circular section has one EI, so only the bending moment perpendicular to the
  member enters, whatever its local axes.
- Compatibility at the redundants gives them. Member end actions come from the
  section actions at the member ends, and the root's reaction from the
  resultant of everything.

**What the two methods share, and are therefore not independent in:** the
inputs; the section and mass formulas; the load formation (the weight
primitives, the generated seismic and wind loads, the component wrench and the
constant-effort force); the exact π arithmetic and its bounds; and the exact
Gauss–Jordan solver, which forms the displacements in one method and the
redundants in the other. They differ in how the response is formed: element
assembly in one, statics and virtual work in the other. The load formation is
checked separately: by the closed-form generated resultant of each CEF term,
by the closed-form beams of LO and JR, and by the mass computed from radii and
from diameters.

## Open readings

1. **The model-check warning.** SWBPIPE's Agent 0 decided that a model-check
   warning on each seismic case names each entered weight-category load, and a
   seismic gravity that differs from the applied self-weight's when a
   component weight is applied (`../seismic_component_weight/README.md`). The
   gravities are equal here, so the gravity clause is not met. Whether the
   weight primitives that the self-weight plan created, listed in each seismic
   case's declared ledger, are "entered weight-category loads" that the
   warning names is not settled by the text read. **This package freezes no
   warning expectation.** The generated seismic load is not formed from those
   primitives in any reading: it is formed from the members' masses and the
   component's W.
2. **The meaning of the document's fields.** As in
   `../exact_load_state_equivalent_static` (its open reading 4): each element
   state selects the material point, with G = E/(2(1 + ν)); no thermal state
   is applied; generated loads take the sections' densities, with no
   `mass_state_ref`; each case is solved with its own states.
3. **The component's node.** The valve's node is an input, C, the end of its
   body away from A, as in `../seismic_component_weight`. W acts there with
   r × F about it, in the weight primitives and in the generated load alike.
4. **JR's connector.** Anchoring both faces keeps the connector's stiffness out
   of every row, with the connector's inputs as stated under the model. If the
   product refuses anchors at a connector's faces, the JR cases cannot be bound
   as they stand, and that is a finding for this package's owner, not a reason
   to change a value.
5. **The lift-off sense.** "Contact on a positive reaction" is RS-LO1's. A
   support that bears the other way negates the threshold's meaning.
6. **The seismic sign.** +axis for a positive g-factor. The opposite convention
   negates every generated row, leaves the CEF SRSS and absolute sum unchanged
   and keeps LO-EQZ-LIFTED seated.
7. **A fixed W against a rescaled one.** The CEF seismic gravity equals the
   weight plan's, so this package cannot tell the valve's fixed W from one
   wrongly rescaled by the ratio of the case's gravity to the weight plan's.
   `../seismic_component_weight` separates the two; no case is added here.
8. **A generated identity equal to a listed primitive's or a synthesized
   identity.** Open. The decision covers an unlisted stored primitive only, and
   no outcome is frozen for these.

Not covered here: pressure (so the effective and wall axial forces coincide
in every row); Timoshenko members; a hydrostatic test case; gap and friction

  packages `../w16_exact_equivalent_static` and
  `../w16_timoshenko_partial_wind` state (DEC-024/DEC-026), with the scale
  taken per case, term or combination:
  - a row whose expected value is nonzero passes when
    abs(observed − expected) ≤ 1e-9 × abs(expected), with no floor (1e-9
    relative is DEL-05-01 AC-001's analytic tolerance);
  - a row whose expected value is exactly zero passes when
    abs(observed) ≤ floor, the floor being 1e-9 times the largest magnitude
    of that row's family (force, moment, translation, rotation) in the same
    case, term or combination.

  Neither sibling states a floor for a family that is entirely zero in an

  `comparison_floor` gives the
