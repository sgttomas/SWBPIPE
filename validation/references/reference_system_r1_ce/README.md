# Reference system with RS-R1 and RS-CE1: EQ-1, EQ-2, EQ-5 and RS-CE1's support action

Frozen references for the reference system ([DEL-09-01](../../../execution/PKG-09/DEL-09-01/ScopeOfWork.md),
reference-system specification §9.2) once its fixture holds the eccentric reducer RS-R1 and the constant-effort
support RS-CE1 (specification §5, §6 and §10). They replace nothing: the tranche-1 package
[`../reference_system/`](../reference_system/README.md), with RS-P150 a plain S270 member and no RS-R1 or RS-CE1,
stays frozen as it was, and its EQ-3 and EQ-5 identities and its product-row conventions apply here unchanged.
[`tests/test_reference_system_fixture.py`](../../../tests/test_reference_system_fixture.py) solves the fixture
through `openpipestress-runner` in both solver modes and checks the values here.

| Id | Check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| EQ-1 | In RS-SUS, RS-OPE and RS-SAM, the sum of the global Z support action components over every support, RS-VS1 and RS-CE1 included, equals W_total | closed form: `references.json` `key_values.W_total_N` |
| EQ-2 | In the same cases, the horizontal (X, Y) resultant is zero, and the support action moment resultant about the origin equals `key_values.reaction_moment_about_origin_N_m`, minus the weight's moment | closed form: `references.json` `key_values` |
| EQ-5 | The RS-dSAM resultant force and its moment about the origin are zero | exact identity: no load differs between RS-SAM and RS-SUS, RS-CE1's constant force included |
| KR-03, RS-CE1 | RS-CE1's `constant_effort_support_applied_load` row and its published support action Fz each equal its entered `constant_load`, 9000 N, and its Fx, Fy, Mx, My and Mz are zero, in every case | identity with the entered input (DEC-049): `references.json` `constant_effort` |

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs no product code and reads no
fixture model file and no product output; its inputs are the specification's statements (§3.1, §3.2, §4.2, §5,
§6), transcribed in it. All model data are invented (spec §1). No equation, value or example from the
`domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the methods are elementary statics. Standard claim fence
applies (F-PIP-2). Agreement here is evidence, not acceptance (F-PIP-3).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK before the adopted fixture was
solved against them. They have not yet been independently refuted.

## What the generator reads

Only the two frozen independent packages, for consistency controls; nothing is taken from either:
- [`../reference_system/references.json`](../reference_system/references.json): its tranche-1 W_total, first
  moment and RS-P150 member record, each of which the generator recomputes from the specification and must
  equal exactly;
- [`../reference_system_pending_cases/references.json`](../reference_system_pending_cases/references.json): its
  `context` RS-SUS full-system (RS-R1 in place) W_total and weight moment, frozen before RS-R1 entered the
  fixture, which the adopted values must equal exactly.

## Bases

The mass bases are the tranche-1 package's ("Mass bases"; EX's stated ones): pipe metal on the nominal wall,
contents over the reduced bore, insulation from OD to OD + 2·t_i, realized arcs over R·π/2 with the same μ, the
replaced span RS-P180 carrying no weight, and a rigid-family component's weight at its node plus its centre of
gravity vector in place of its mapped member's distributed weight (DEL-03-05's stiffness-scaling design §3).

**RS-R1** (spec §5) is a rigid-family component on RS-P150 at RS-160: 300 N at the body midpoint. Its entered
centre of gravity from RS-160 is the midpoint (6, 20.2, 6.9875) m less RS-160, (0, −0.2, 0.0125) m, so its
weight wrench at RS-160 is F = (0, 0, −300) N and M = r × F = (60, 0, 0) N·m. RS-P150 carries no distributed
weight: the adopted W is the tranche-1 W less RS-P150's √257 term plus 300 N, and it holds no √257 term. The
declared `eccentric_offset` (0, 0, +25) mm is the transverse separation from RS-160 to RS-150 about the +Y run
axis both neighbours share; the body chord is √257/40 m; the drop is half the end-size difference.

**RS-CE1** (spec §6) at RS-100, UZ, entered `constant_load` 9000 N, acts on the pipe along +Z in every case
(DEC-049). It adds no weight. In EQ-1 and EQ-2 it is one support among all, counted once: the vertical sum over
every support is W, the supports other than RS-CE1 carry W − 9000 N, and RS-CE1's moment about the origin is
(6, 3, 7) m × (0, 0, 9000) N = (27000, −54000, 0) N·m. RS-CE1's force is the same in RS-SAM and RS-SUS, so EQ-5's
identity is unchanged.

**Generated-weight inventory.** Per case 29 primitives: 21 line loads (every member but RS-P020, RS-P030,
RS-P040, RS-P150 and RS-P180) and, for each of RS-F1, RS-V1, RS-F2 and RS-R1, a force along −Z and one moment
(the one nonzero component of r × F). Tranche 1 had 28.

## Controls

The cross-checks in `references.json` (every one must agree for `--write`):
- each section's μ in a second factored form;
- the bends' tangent nodes and centres against the specified corners and radii;
- RS-R1's chord, drop, offset, centre of gravity and wrench;
- the adopted W and first moment by the replacement route (tranche 1 less RS-P150 plus RS-R1), and the adopted W
  again as per-section length sums;
- the tranche-1 W, first moment and RS-P150 record recomputed here against the frozen tranche-1 package, exactly;
- the adopted W and weight moment against the pending-cases package's full-system value, exactly;
- RS-CE1's moment about the origin.

`negative_controls` gives the values that the bookkeeping errors in the threat model would produce: RS-R1 added
with RS-P150's weight kept, RS-R1 omitted, RS-CE1 counted twice, and RS-CE1 left out of the vertical sum. Each
differs from W by far more than the criterion allows.

## Criterion

DEC-024/DEC-026, as the tranche-1 package states it. A nonzero row passes when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|. A zero
row passes when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·zero_scale, the largest nonzero magnitude of its family in the case. The RS-CE1
identities are exact.
