## Number format

- **Inputs.** Every input number is a binary64 value written exactly in
  decimal (dyadic fractions and integers), taken as an exact rational.
  - The exceptions are ζ, the binary64 nearest 0.05 taken exactly, and the
    connector guide, which is integers.
- **Exact formation.**
  - ℚ + ℚπ for the straight-member stiffness (`K_exact`).
  - ℚ for the connector and rods.
  - 80-digit Decimal for the arc, whose chord R√2, frame and half-angle
    functions are irrational, and for everything after formation.
  - π comes from Machin's series and the arc angle from f1's arctangent
    series. Square roots, ln and exp are Decimal's correctly rounded
    operations. No libm value enters any published number.
- **Published reals.** Each is a pair: a 40-significant-digit decimal, then
  the nearest binary64.
  - A value below 1e-50 of the largest magnitude of its unit in its block is
    printed `0`. Such values are zero by symmetry or decoupling.
  - The generator refuses any value between 1e-50 and 1e-35 of that scale.
- **Convergence and rounding.**
  - All 86,516 scalar leaves in the convergence comparison are identical at 80
    and 100 digits. This is a mixed population, not a numerical-pair count:
    the package has 51,284 decimal/binary64 pairs, 31,154 zero-string leaves,
    377 other numeric strings, 3,418 other strings, 17 booleans, 264 integers
    and 2 nulls. The paired values agree in decimal and binary64.
  - The nearest-binary64 rounding of each published value is decided with a
    relative margin of at least 1.47e-21 from the nearest rounding boundary.
    The generator requires more than 1e-60.
- **Units.** SI: m, rad, kg, N, N·m, s, Hz and m/s².

## The model (invented)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Section `s3` | OD 0.171875 m, wall 0.0078125 m, c_m = c_a = 0, contents 1000 kg/m³, no insulation |
| Steel | E 2e11 Pa, G 7.8125e10 Pa, ρ 7850 kg/m³ |
| Nodes | A (0,0,0), B (1.5,0,0), C (1.875,0.375,0), D (1.875,1.375,0), E (1.875,1.875,0), F (1.875,3.125,0) |
| P1 | straight A–B, 1.5 m along X |
| BEND1 | realized 90° arc B–C, R = 0.375 m, centre (1.5,0.375,0), y_reference (1,−1,0), one flexibility factor k = 4.25 |
| P2, P3 | face pipes C–D (1.0 m) and E–F (1.25 m) along Y |
| J1 | massless connector replacing span D–E (0.5 m): zero offsets, Ls = 1 m, Q from guide (4,0,3), H = diag(150e3, 400e3, 250e3, 500e3, 1.5e3, 1.0e3) N·m in normalized coordinates |
| R1, R2 | linear rods D–E at ±0.1875·Q_y, k = 1.2e8 N/m, e_ref = 0 |

H contains work coefficients for dimensionless q̂ = D⁻¹q. The physical
stiffness is K = D⁻¹ H D⁻¹: at Ls = 1 m and rotation scale 1 rad its diagonal
has the same numerical entries, with translational stiffness in N/m and
rotational stiffness in N·m/rad. The units of H and K are not interchangeable.

The present arc endpoint frames are signed permutations. They do not distinguish
rotation of signed contributions before combination from the corresponding
absolute permutation of combined magnitudes. The additive case V-ARC-OBL
supplies the non-permutation arc orientation control; see
[Additive arc frame-order control](#additive-arc-frame-order-control-v-arc-obl).

**Why these values.**
- They are dyadic, so every coordinate, sum, chord and offset is exact in
  binary64. They take the place of the reference-system inspirations R 0.4 m,
  k 4.2 and rod offset 0.2 m, which are not exactly representable.
- P2 and P3 are unequal, so the joint variants have no mirror symmetry that
  would zero the connector forces.
- The connector's unequal lateral and bending terms keep the rods-absent
  straight line free of exactly repeated pairs, without adding a support. They
  also make the rotated frame enter K_e itself.
- k is one factor on both bending terms, as f1 forms it. Directional k is
  not covered.

## Conventions a product must match or map

1. **Reaction sign.** A reaction is the force and moment the anchor applies
   to the piping, in global axes: (K u − M g) at its DOFs. A product
   reporting the force on the support has the opposite sign of every signed
   reaction. Combined magnitudes are unaffected.
2. **End actions.** Every element, connector and rod end action is the
   generalized force acting on that element at its end node (node on
   element), in the order Fx Fy Fz Mx My Mz, node i then node j. An internal
   section force at end i is the negative of the end action there.
3. **Arc end actions.**
   - The total is elastic minus `lump_load`: (K_arc − ω² M_lumps) u per mode,
     and K_arc u − M_lumps g in the rigid response. M_lumps acts only at the
     end node of each lump.
   - The `_t` rows are the same totals in each end's U4a end-tangent frame:
     x the traversal tangent, y toward the centre, z = x × y. The frame rows
     are published per end.
   - Signed contributions are rotated before they are combined. Combined
     magnitudes depend on the axes they are combined in. The global rows are
     kept as independent observables; their presence does not decide what a
     product publishes.
4. **Connector.**
   - g is in connector axes, in the order axial, lateral y, lateral z,
     torsion, bending y, bending z. It is work-conjugate to q = B d.
   - `J1.i.*` and `J1.j.*` are Bᵀg in global axes.
   - These are incremental dynamic quantities. They exclude static pressure
     thrust, preload and reference load, and they are neither wall force nor
     stress.
5. **Rods.** T is tension positive. `R*.i.*` and `R*.j.*` are b·T in global
   axes, with the moment about the node including the offset.
6. **Straight members in the five original variants** are axis-aligned. Their
   global rows map component to component onto any member frame that is a
   signed permutation of the global axes. The additive V-ARC-OBL straights
   have oblique rational unit axes and rational lengths, preserving exact
   stiffness formation; they publish global rows only.
7. **Interpolation abscissa, grouping boundary and 100-40-40 with 2/5.**
   These are as in `../discrete/` (conventions 3 to 5), unchanged.

## Mapping to the rows DEL-04-10 publishes

Addendum of 18 November 2026. This package was derived before DEL-04-10
REQ-006 fixed which rows a spectrum result publishes and how each is
identified. No value, sign or quantity here contradicts that requirement.
Where this package's identities or record structure differ from it, the
package is unchanged and this section states the mapping a comparison uses.
It adds no value and changes none.

1. **Reactions.** A key `N.C` is REQ-006's reaction of the rigid constraint at
   node `N`, on the component that `C` restrains (Fx to UX, Fy to UY, Fz to
   UZ, Mx to RX, My to RY, Mz to RZ), in global axes, held by the one anchor
   at `N`. This package gives its anchors no identity of their own; the
   holder's identity is the one the compared model gives that anchor.
2. **Tie rods.** In V-JOINT-R and V-COMB-R, R1 and R2 are the tie rods of
   connector J1. REQ-006 identifies each by its connector and its own
   identity: J1 with R1, and J1 with R2. The rod axis runs from the rod's
   attachment at its end i to its attachment at its end j, here (0, 1, 0).
3. **Straight members in member axes.** Straight members carry global rows
   only. In the five original variants their member axes are signed
   permutations of the global axes: P1 has x = (1, 0, 0), y = (0, 0, 1) and
   z = (0, −1, 0), so Fx_m = Fx, Fy_m = Fz and Fz_m = −Fy; P2 and P3 have
   x = (0, 1, 0), y = (0, 0, 1) and z = (1, 0, 0), so Fx_m = Fy, Fy_m = Fz
   and Fz_m = Fx; moments map the same way. Signed values map with these
   signs and combined magnitudes by the same permutation without sign. That
   is exact on this geometry only, and is a mapping for comparison: REQ-006
   never forms a row by permuting or rotating a combined magnitude. In
   V-ARC-OBL the straight members are oblique and their member-axis rows are
   not exercised.
4. **The arc tangent frame.** REQ-006 takes the arc tangent frame from the
   static result (DEL-04-01 REQ-018) and does not define it. The `_t` rows
   here are in the frame this package defines and publishes per end
   (`tangent_frame_rows_end_i` and `tangent_frame_rows_end_j`, in
   `derived_model.stiffness_constituents`): x the traversal tangent, y toward
   the centre, z = x × y. Before comparing spectrum values, verify the static
   result's tangent frame at each end against the arc geometry and the static
   frame convention under DEL-04-01 REQ-018. Under REQ-006, the comparison
   fails if the axes published for a spectrum arc tangent row do not match
   that verified frame. When the verified frame matches this package's frame
   at that end, compare the package's `_t` values directly. When it differs,
   rotate the reference's signed contributions into the verified frame for
   the signed comparisons and recombine those contributions there before
   comparing magnitudes.
5. **The record of one direction.** The periodic magnitude and the
   combination are carried. The rigid magnitude is the absolute value of
   `rigid_response_signed`. Each `mutations` entry carries only the
   magnitude over the directions. The per-direction periodic magnitudes are
   in `periodic_modal_combined` with the correction applied and
   `periodic_modal_combined_correction_off` with it off.
   `per_direction_combined` gives the base rules' combined values. The
   oblique frame-order control carries selected `correct` magnitudes under
   `strongest_witness_b_by_path_family`: match a `periodic_with_rigid`
   witness by its path's modal rule, rigid rule and direction, and by its
   `component`.
6. **Governing direction.** Not carried.
7. **Extracted rigid modes.** A mode of `contributions_signed` that
   `mode_status` marks `rigid (extracted)` is not a contribution row when the
   missing-mass correction is applied. It is one only for the data with the
   correction off.
8. **Clusters.** Contributions do not name their cluster. Each extracted mode
   is its own cluster, so there are no cluster sums.
9. **Coupled pairs.** The ten percent method's coupled pairs are not carried,
   and the grouping method's groups are listed only where a group has more
   than one mode. Each eligible mode absent from that list is a singleton
   group. `cqc_correlation_periodic_clusters` gives the unordered pairs with
   the correction applied. Coefficients for pairs involving an extracted
   rigid mode in correction-off runs are not carried.
10. **Zero.** A response is printed `0` when its magnitude is below 1e-50
    times the largest magnitude of the same unit in its block, or when that
    unit's scale is zero (Number format). A product value compared with it is compared with zero under the
    verification tolerance's absolute floor.

The arc's separate elastic and inertia parts and the rods' end actions are
this package's diagnostics and correspond to no row. This package does not
exercise: member-axis rows of an oblique straight member, compliant supports,
snubbers, a rigid restraint other than an anchor, a constraint with more than
one holder, a support with no row, an exact-route pressure member's axial
row, straight members of rigid-family components, tees, reducer chords, bodies, a cluster of more than
one mode, or a restraint along a skew direction.

## Additive arc frame-order control (V-ARC-OBL)

**Why it exists.** REFUTATION.md C1 shows that V-ARC's end-tangent frames are
signed permutations, so its values cannot discriminate rotating signed
contributions before combination from mapping combined magnitudes afterwards.
The arc end recovery path is the admitted path this control examines. It
examines no mid-arc station.

**The case** (`generators/w2_cases_oblique.py`; published in
`w2_v_arc_oblique.json`, with the same blocks and checks as the other variant
files).
- **Geometry.** A realized 90° arc in an oblique plane. Its traversal tangents
  are t1 = (3,4,0)/5 at B and t2 = (−12,9,20)/25 at C, with R = 25/32 m.
  - The straights P1 (1.25 m) and P2 (1.5625 m) are tangent to it.
  - It is anchored at A and D, with 12 free DOFs.
  - Every coordinate is dyadic.
- **End frames.** They are exact rationals and not signed permutations:
  - at B: x = t1, y = t2, z = (16,−12,15)/25;
  - at C: x = t2, y = −t1, the same z.
- **Section, spectra and rules.** The section, spectra, directions, ζ and base
  rules are those of the other variants.
- **Request.** The cut-off f_c = 320 Hz extracts 8 modes, from 35.48 to
  275.07 Hz. The rigid frequency f_r = 250 Hz makes 6 of them periodic.
  - The periodic set has the chain 192.2, 208.2 and 224.6 Hz. GROUPING forms
    {4, 5}, while TEN_PERCENT also pairs 5 and 6. The two differ on 248
    periodic components, by up to 0.138 relative.
  - The odd modes participate along the plane normal and the even modes in
    the plane, so every global direction excites both.

**The control** (`w2_control_arc_frame_order.json`). There are 12 arc end
components per response (6 at each end), all in the end-tangent frames.
- **Correct path.** Rotate every signed arc end contribution (each periodic
  mode and each rigid response) into its end frame, then combine component by
  component. These are the `_t` rows.
- **Wrong path a.** Combine the global component magnitudes by the same rules,
  then apply the frame rows to those magnitudes.
- **Wrong path b.** The same, with the absolute values of the frame rows. This
  is the generalization of the absolute signed permutation that C1 could not
  reject.
- **Witness.** A component is a witness when it differs from the correct value
  by at least 1e-2 relative, and is at least 1e-3 of its end-and-unit maximum.
  For scale, REQ-008 seeds the verification tolerance at 1e-9 relative, and
  the spectrum screening class is 5e-3 on forces and moments.

**Contrast and non-mutants.**
- **The same control on V-ARC.** Its frames are signed permutations. Wrong
  path b differs from the correct path by at most 6.4e-78, which reproduces
  C1. Wrong path a differs there only by sign, in 117 of 126 paths.
- **Linear reorderings are not mutants.** Rotation commutes with linear signed
  summation, so the control does not flag a linear reordering. It checks two:
  - rotating a signed sum against summing the rotated contributions (6.8e-80);
  - rotating the elastic and lump parts separately against rotating their
    total (1.5e-79).
- **Precision.** The additive outputs have their own 80/100-digit comparison:
  12,072 decimal/binary64 pairs, within 18,333 scalar leaves, all identical.
  The smallest binary64 rounding margin is 5.53e-21.

**What it does not show.**
- It discriminates these two wrong orders on one geometry. Agreement on it
  does not prove that a product forms arc end magnitudes correctly for every
  orientation.
- The witnesses rely on unequal, non-collinear contributions, which this case
  has by design.
- The other U4a frame mutants (frame swap, sign flips, chord frame) are
  targeted by `../../t4_u4a/`, not here.
- The oblique straights P1 and P2 publish global rows only, so their
  member-axis path is not exercised here. `../discrete/` C9 publishes
  member-axis rows for an oblique straight.
- There is no connector or rod in this case.

## Limits

- **The arc model.** It is DEL-03-03's thin curved-beam model with one k,
  which inherits LIM-021. These references show agreement with that model,
  not its adequacy.
- **Section bases.** PF and EX coincide here (c_m = c_a = 0), so this family
  does not qualify the distinction between the nominal and reduced bases.
- **The connector frame.** It is an exact derived rational frame from an
  integer guide. A product that authors the frame as rounded unit vectors
  forms a slightly different frame. How a product represents the frame is
  outside these references.
- **No exact repeats.** There is no exactly repeated eigenvalue and no
  multi-mode cluster. `../discrete/` C7 and C10 cover those.
- **What is not covered.** Compliant supports, snubbers, nonlinear states,
  Timoshenko members, directional k, nonzero rod pretension, nonzero
  attachment offsets and pressure stiffness.
- **The pressure and reference control** is the fixed-state increment only
  (above).
- **Stresses.** None are formed.
- **Tolerances.** None is set for product comparison. These references state
  only their own cross-check residuals.
- **Comparability.** Every extracted periodic mode is simple and well
  separated, so per-mode contributions and combined magnitudes are both
  comparable at a verification tolerance.
