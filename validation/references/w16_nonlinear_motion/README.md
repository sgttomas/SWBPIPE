# W-16 stage 2 references: imposed motion with nonlinear supports

Frozen, independently derived expectations for imposed displacement in a
model with nonlinear supports on the pressure-free route (W-16 stage 2;
DEL-04-03 Design
[`pf-nonlinear-imposed-motion.md`](../../../execution/PKG-04/DEL-04-03/Design/pf-nonlinear-imposed-motion.md)
§5). Each request is solved by
[`core/product_physics/src/w16_nonlinear_motion_tests.rs`](../../../core/product_physics/src/w16_nonlinear_motion_tests.rs)
in both solver modes and compared with the values here.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and
runs no product code. All model data are invented. No equation, value or
example from the `domains/piping-design/` corpus is used (DEC-043); the
methods are the textbook methods cited below. Standard claim fence applies
(F-PIP-2; claims taxonomy per DEC-081).

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author
TASK. They have not yet been independently refuted.

## Units, theory and inputs

SI throughout: N, N·m, m, rad. The theory is a small-displacement,
linear-elastic Euler–Bernoulli space frame with St Venant torsion
(Przemieniecki, *Theory of Matrix Structural Analysis*; McGuire, Gallagher and
Ziemian, *Matrix Structural Analysis*). Members are axis-aligned, so every
element entry and direction cosine is exact.

- **Section.** OD 0.206 m, wall 0.011 m. The leaves are the normalized
  binary64 values the product forms from them: A = 0.0067387162419501045 m²,
  I = Iy = Iz = 3.213188372067858e-05 m⁴, J = 2I. The product-side consumer
  checks them bit for bit.
- **Material (invented).** E = 5·2^35 Pa (171.8 GPa) and G = 2^36 Pa. With
  these, every boundary case is an exact boundary of the system formed from
  the binary64 leaves: loads and motions are binary64 and no rounded
  analytical threshold is used.
- **Lengths and motions.** Power-of-two lengths (4 m cantilevers, a 2 m
  L-frame) and dyadic motions.

## Method

For each request and load case the generator forms the exact stiffness,
reads the case's motion (a primitive on a rigid restraint is a base
prescribed value g_b; on a nonlinear support, its ground offset g_s), and
enumerates every support state: one-way, lift-off and gap supports active or
inactive; friction supports inactive, sticking, or sliding in either sense
(with either normal sign when the normal is derived). Each state is solved
exactly by the partitioned reduction K_ff·u_f = f_f − K_fc·u_c with u_c the
base values and each active or sticking support's prescribed value; a sliding
support carries −dir·μ·|N| (a derived normal is coupled exactly). A state
qualifies when every DEC-067 condition holds on δ_s = u_d − g_s: strictly
(every margin positive), or weakly (a zero margin). Its published rows are
recorded with their binary64 bounds: member and support group norms, each
nonlinear support's signed slot, and every nodal displacement.

**A-3's reduced closed form.** With K = [[a, b], [b, d]] the condensed
stiffness on (n, t) = (tip UY, tip UX) and u_n = g_n:
α = (a − b²/d)·g_n + (b/d)·f_t − f_n, γ = b/d, c_f = −dir·μ·β,
φ = c_f·α/(1 − c_f·γ), u_t = (f_t + φ − b·g_n)/d. The generator checks φ and
u_t against the full solve, Coulomb equilibrium |φ| = μ|R_n| opposing the
slip, and a nonzero denominator. An independent L-frame with L = EA = EI = 1
and the tip rotation free gives K = (1/103)·[[84, 18], [18, 48]] and
γ = 3/8. Two mutants are recorded: an influence system that carries g_n
(γ' = γ + (a − b²/d)·g_n) and a Stage A that omits the motion
(α' = (b/d)·f_t − f_n). Both change φ.

## Product binding and criterion

The requests are product documents; motion is a primitive load with
`category: imposed_displacement` and target `{type: "support", support, dof}`.
The criterion is the K-D5 ball bound: each certified member, support and
nonlinear slot row lies within its bound B of the exact value. Nodal
displacements are compared at 1e-9 relative, an exact zero against 1e-9 of
the case's largest translation or rotation, and every prescribed DOF bit for
bit. A weak case must not be certified. A-2's weak case fails closed in the
confirmation ("stick capacity not established"). At A-1's exact boundary the
loop alternates between the two weak states and does not converge, so the
case is withheld before any confirmation.

Gap cases (R1) are never confirmed: the exact strict-gap decisions select
the state and the standing stays `NUMERICAL_INTEGRITY_RECOVERY_BASIS_UNQUALIFIED`;
their displacements and slot reactions are compared at 1e-9 relative. B-1's
weak case fails closed in the confirmation on either weak state, by solver
mode; B-3's is the weak sticking boundary ("stick capacity not established").
Each moved ground's `nonlinear_support_final_relative_displacement` row must
equal fl(U − g_s), with U the published displacement, and lie within 1e-9
relative of the exact δ_s (exactly 0 where the support is at its ground).

Observed on the W-16 stage 2 branch (both solver modes gave the same
figures): every certified group within B; the largest relative error of a
nonlinear slot row was 1.6e-16 (A-3) and of a nodal displacement 3.3e-16
(A-3) on R-a, and 0 and 1.7e-16 on R-b; the gap projections matched to 0.
