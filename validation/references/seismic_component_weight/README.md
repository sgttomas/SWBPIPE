# Seismic component-weight references: seismic takes the masses the weight case takes

Frozen, independently derived expectations for the static-equivalent seismic
rule of [DEL-05-01](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-01/ScopeOfWork.md) as
SWBPIPE's Agent 0 decided it on 2026-11-12:

> Seismic: for each global axis, the user's g-factor times the weight the same
> gravity gives the model: on every member, a load per length equal to the
> g-factor times gravity times the member's mass per length; and for a member
> that a rigid-family component maps, in place of that load, the force equal to
> the g-factor times the component's weight W at the component's node, with the
> moments of that force about the node from the centre-of-gravity vector.

As the self-weight plan does (DEL-05-01 REQ-015; DEL-03-05 "Consumption
declaration"), only a member whose component weight is applied gets k·W in
place of its distributed load; a member whose component weight is not applied
keeps its distributed load. The references were written before the engine
change they will judge. No product comparison binds them yet.

Nothing here is generated by the product. The generator imports, reads and runs
no product code. It reads only the files it writes. All inputs are invented and
deliberately non-standard. No value or example from the `domains/piping-design/`
corpus or from any catalogue is used. **There is no internal pressure anywhere
in this package**: no pressure region and no pressure load. The seismic load
does not depend on pressure. The exact route's pressure bore enters only as
the contents' area. The product supplies no g-factor, gravity or weight: all
are the user's inputs here. The standard claim fence applies.

Status: candidate references, derived and cross-checked by their author TASK.
They have not yet been independently refuted.

## Generated loads

Under a seismic case with gravity g and g-factors k = (k_x, k_y, k_z):

- **AB and CD** (unmapped, or mapped by the not-consumed flange) carry
  q = μ·g·k in N/m, uniform over the whole member.
- **BC** carries no distributed load.
- **Node C** carries F = W_v·k and M = r_v × F.

Under the weight case, AB and CD carry q = (0, 0, −μ·g_w), BC carries nothing,
and C carries F = (0, 0, −W_v) with M = r_v × F. That is the self-weight plan
of DEL-05-01, with W a force that the size of the acceleration does not scale.

The valve wrench in the XYZ cases (both routes) is F = (705, 822.5, 940) N and
M = r_v × F = (799/80, 2021/8, −18283/80) = (9.9875, 252.625, −228.5375) N·m.
Under the weight case, M = (−282, −411.25, 0) N·m.

## Re-deriving by hand

**Statics (CANT).** The reaction at A is minus the resultant. Example,
CANT-PF SEISMIC-Y:

- R_Ay = −k_y(μg·(L_AB + L_CD) + W_v) = −0.35·6797.384 = −2379.08 N.
- M_Az = −k_y[μg(L_AB²/2 + L_CD(x_C + L_CD/2)) + W_v(x_C + r_vx)]
  = −0.35(1111.846·9.05 + 2350·2.675) = −5721.96 N·m.
- M_Ax = −(−r_vz·k_y·W_v) = 102.8125 N·m.

**Section actions.** At x, the section action is the resultant of everything
outboard of x, about (x, 0, 0), acting on the +x face of the inboard part:
N = F_x, V_y = F_y, V_z = F_z, T = M_x, and M_y and M_z with the lever
(x_n − x, 0, 0). The member end force at i is −S(x_i⁺) and at j is S(x_j⁻).

**Motions (unit-load virtual work).** Integrate from 0 to x_P over every
member, with EI, EA and GJ multiplied by 3 on BC:

- u_x = ∫N/EA
- θ_x = ∫T/GJ
- θ_z = ∫M_z/EI
- u_y = ∫M_z(x_P − x)/EI
- θ_y = ∫M_y/EI
- u_z = −∫M_y(x_P − x)/EI

A right-handed rotation about +Y turns +X toward −Z, which is the reason for
the sign of u_z.

**FIXED.** The axial, torsion, x–y and x–z problems decouple. Release D and
take its six actions as redundants. The CANT motions at D under the loads
(δ₀), with the 6×6 flexibility of unit actions at D (f), give the actions at D
from f·R_D = −δ₀. Then superpose. Axial and torsion have one redundant each;
each bending plane has two.

**Second method in the generator.** It also assembles the 24-DOF direct
stiffness system with work-consistent nodal loads, for a uniform q on length l:

- q_y: (ql/2, ql²/12) at i and (ql/2, −ql²/12) at j on (u_y, θ_z);
- q_z: −ql²/12 at i and +ql²/12 at j on θ_y.

It solves that system exactly, and every motion, reaction and end force of
the two methods is equal as an exact value.

## Parity (item 4)

For each seismic reference case, take the weight that gravity g_w gives the
model along +e_a: the total weight 𝒲 and its moment about the origin M_w,a.
Then the identities are:

- resultant force = Σ_a k_a·𝒲·e_a;
- resultant moment = Σ_a k_a·M_w,a.

Each case's `parity` block carries the expected values, the differences and
`holds`.

- **The parity holds exactly in all 20 cases with g_s = g_w.** These are X, Y,
  Z, XYZ and TWO-SEISMIC-XYZ on each model and route.
- **Row by row:** every response row of SEISMIC-Z equals −k_z times the same
  row of WEIGHT-NZ (66 rows on CANT, 72 on FIXED; `row_identity`).
- **The two-mapped references equal the one-mapped references exactly.**
  TWO-SEISMIC-XYZ equals SEISMIC-XYZ and TWO-WEIGHT-NZ equals WEIGHT-NZ. The
  not-consumed flange changes nothing, in weight or in seismic.
- **Every control fails the parity**, keyed-on-mapped included. There, the
  weight the parity compares with is the self-weight rule's: AB keeps its pipe
  weight.

## Different gravities (item 6)

In SEISMIC-XYZ-G10 the seismic case states 10 m/s² and the weight case uses
g_w = 9.79 m/s².

- **Under the decided text**, AB and CD carry μ·10·k, and the valve force is
  k·W_v = (705, 822.5, 940) N, with no division by any gravity.
- **Control CTRL-G-RESCALED** uses k·(W_v/g_w)·10 = (720.123, 840.143,
  960.163) N. The two differ by k·W_v·(10/g_w − 1) = (14805/979, 34545/1958,
  19740/979) ≈ (15.1226, 17.6430, 20.1634) N in force and (0.214236, −52.0469,
  45.3803) N·m in moment, on both routes and models.

**What this means for parity.** When the two gravities differ, the seismic
resultant is not k times the weight case's total. It differs by
k·(g_s − g_w)·μ·(L_AB + L_CD) per axis:

- PF: ≈ (28.6195, 33.3895, 38.1594) N, with moments (0, −86.3356, 75.5436) N·m;
- EX: ≈ (31.2381, 36.4444, 41.6508) N, with moments (0, −94.2349, 82.4555) N·m.

That difference is only the pipes' share. The component's share is k·W in both
cases. Parity holds exactly against the weight that the seismic case's own
gravity gives the model, μ·g_s·(L_AB + L_CD) + W_v, with W unscaled
(`at_the_seismic_case_gravity.holds` is true in all four). That is the
reading in which "the weight the same gravity gives the model" means the
seismic case's gravity.

## Open readings

- **O-1. "the same gravity".** The decided text says "the user's g-factor
  times the weight the same gravity gives the model".
  - Reading A, computed: the seismic case's own gravity, the only gravity
    seismic generation takes as input.
  - Reading B: the gravity of a weight case. Its values for G10 are
    ALT-O2-WEIGHT-GRAVITY, which equal SEISMIC-XYZ. The two readings coincide
    whenever the gravities are equal.

  The text supports A better: the generation definition says "times gravity"
  in the seismic case's own terms, and REQ-008 says seismic is generated from
  the user's inputs.
- **O-2. When a component's weight is not applied.** DEL-03-05 states one
  condition explicitly: declared not consumed by the solve ("solves as its
  pipe, with its pipe weight"). That condition is the one modelled here.
  DEL-03-05 AC-005 also has a component that declares mechanics geometry only,
  or nothing, "and whose scale or weight is not applied", and it "states the
  first condition that was not met". Those other conditions are not listed in
  the text, and no case here models them. The rule it follows is the same: its
  body solves as its pipe, and its member keeps its distributed load.
- **O-3. Sense of the g-factor.** The text says "for each global axis" and
  gives no sign. Here a positive g-factor loads along +axis, as in
  `w16_exact_equivalent_static`. The opposite convention negates every seismic
  row.

## Product binding

The test that compares the product with these references has not been
written, and its tolerance belongs to the integrator. The DEL-05-01 analytic
tolerance is 1e-9 relative, with a stated absolute floor.

The models use these schema members:

- the component kinds `valve` and `flange`, with `rigid_pipe_ref`, `weight`
  and `center_of_gravity`;
- `solver_consumption`, `mechanics_geometry_only` for the valve and
  `not_solver_consumed` for the flange;
- `stiffness_scaling_user_value` 3 on the valve;
- the seismic inputs `gravity_acceleration` and `g_factor_x`, `g_factor_y`,
  `g_factor_z`;
- on EX, `corrosion_allowance` 1/400 m, `euler_bernoulli`, and no pressure
  regions.

**Re-freeze rules.** No value changes to match a product output. A
disagreement goes back to this reference's owner and an independent refuter.
A change of rule or convention is a re-freeze, with a recorded reason and a new
refutation.
