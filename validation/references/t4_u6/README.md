# T4-U6 references: the D-5 section bases on one straight pipe

Frozen, independently derived expectations for T4-U6 (D-5 section bases) and
the Timoshenko variant shared with T4-U8
([DEL-05-03 Design, section-bases-and-timoshenko.md](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/Design/section-bases-and-timoshenko.md)
§§1, 2.1, 2.5, 3.1, 7 and 8; rulings in
[section-and-shear-decisions.md](../../../execution/PKG-05/DEL-05-03/Design/section-and-shear-decisions.md)).
Nothing here is generated by the product, and the generator imports, reads
and runs no product code or output.

## Files

| File | Role |

Each variant names its request and binds it by SHA-256.

## Cases

All invented: OD 0.2 m, wall t = 0.01 m, E 200 GPa, ν 0.3, member A (x = 0)
→ B (x = 2 m), y_reference (0, 1, 0). With mill tolerance c_m = 0.00125 m
and corrosion allowance c_a = 0.002 m the nominal section is ri 0.09 m (the
annulus of SHEAR_REFERENCE S-B), the reduced section t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a =
0.00675 m and the pressure bore ri,r = 0.09325 m.

Frozen per load case (1,449 rows in 12 load-case evaluations): the wall and
effective forces, wall end actions and element axial force; the five section
actions at five stations; every stress row on both sections (axial membrane
or axial normal, Lamé radial and hoop, bending y and z, torsional shear,
each with `section_basis`) and the per-basis maximum; displacements and
rotations of A and B; support components and norms; the terminal cap thrust
(`contract_evidence.pressure`); and per variant the `pipe_sections` evidence
(nominal, reduced and pressure-bore geometry and `beam_theory`).

## Basis

- **D-5 geometry (Design §1).** Stiffness A, I, J, the Timoshenko shear area,
  pipe metal mass and the As of ε_p = (1 − 2ν)pAi/(E·As) on the nominal wall;
  Ai (caps, Poisson pair) and contents on the pressure bore; stresses on both
  sections, each section's As = πw(OD − w), I = As(ro² + ri²)/4, J = 2I,
  Z = I/ro. `section_geometry.dual.binary64_order` applies the fixed binary64
  order t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, ri,r = OD/2 − t_r to these inputs; the other
  order, ((OD/2 − t) + c_m) + c_a, rounds one ulp differently here.
- **Stress per basis (Design §2.1).** σ = N/A_b (N_w/A_b on pressurized
  members), M/Z_b, T·ro/J_b; Lamé of each basis's own annulus (radial −p and
  0; hoop 2p·π ri,b²/A_b outside and that plus p inside); maximum
  |N/A_b| + hypot(M_y, M_z)/Z_b at the root (|M_y| and |M_z| are checked to be
  nonincreasing along the member).
- **Shear (Design §3.1; SHEAR_REFERENCE §§1, 3, 4).** κ_E =
  16609827/33068756 (S-B's 33.219654/66.137512), A_s = κ_E·Aₙ, Ks = G·A_s;
  tip deflection FL³/(3EI) + FL/Ks, uniform load wL⁴/(8EI) + wL²/(2Ks);
  rotations are section rotations and do not change. `beam_theory` also
  carries an exhibit of κ evaluated in binary64 in the Design's operation
  order from the binary64 leaves (one ulp from the rounding of the exact
  value), for the evidence readers.
- **Conventions** are T4-I8's: element-local frame = global here; end rows
  node-on-element (end_i = −cut(0), end_j = +cut(L)); station rows and all
  stress rows from the j-side cut; element-local stresses in MPa, displacements
  in mm (references in Pa and m); supports support-on-pipe with moments about
  the node; x–z plane v = u_z, θ = −r_y.
- **Exact numbers.** Every value is a sum of rational·π^k, or a square root of
  one (support norms and the combined maxima), evaluated at 130 digits. The
  decimal inputs are exact rational targets; their binary64 rounding is
  absorbed by the criterion.

## Checks inside the generator (1,634)

Annulus identities; the pressure bore = OD/2 − t + c_m + c_a; κ_E in the
cancellation-free form equals the CONTRACT form and S-B's value; an exact
direct-stiffness solve with the SHEAR_REFERENCE element (1, 2 and 4 elements,
both bending planes, tip force and uniform load, Euler–Bernoulli and
Timoshenko) reproduces every tip translation and rotation; global force and
moment balance; end_i node-on-element actions equal the support action; the
general Lamé family on each basis; the axial compliance identity on the
stiffness As; the restrained wall force 2νP − E·Aₙ·αΔT; unique row
identities with both families on every stress kind and no basis elsewhere;
every discriminator moves at least one row by ≥ 1e3 tolerances; under
pressure alone the stiffness-section mutants do not move the restrained wall
force while the ε_p-area mutants do.

## Discriminators

`wrong_result_discriminators` lists, per case, variant and load case, each
wrong mapping a product must not produce: its rows moved, the largest
distance, and up to 16 listed rows (the farthest of each kind, component and
basis), each with its wrong value and its distance in tolerances (all
≥ 1e3; the smallest listed is 2.8e6).

| Id | Wrong mapping |
|---|---|
| `mill_tolerance_in_stiffness` | stiffness section (A, I, J, the ε_p area, the shear section) on t − c_m |
| `allowance_in_stiffness` | stiffness section on t − c_a |
| `stiffness_on_reduced_section` | stiffness on t_r (the retired single section) |
| `cap_area_on_nominal_bore` | Ai for caps and the Poisson pair on the nominal bore |
| `eps_p_area_on_reduced_section` | ε_p formed with the reduced As (restrained Nw = P − (1−2ν)P·Aₙ/A_r) |
| `poisson_eigenstrain_area_on_reduced_section` | the Poisson eigenstrain formed with the reduced As (restrained Nw = 2νP·Aₙ/A_r) |
| `contents_on_nominal_bore` | contents mass on π ri,n² (self-weight cases) |
| `metal_mass_on_reduced_wall` | metal mass on the reduced wall (self-weight cases) |
| `reduced_rows_copied_from_nominal`, `nominal_rows_copied_from_reduced` | one basis for both families |
| `allowance_omitted_from_reduced_section` | a mill-only reduced wall and bore, OD/2 − (t − c_m), in stresses, caps and contents |
| `nominal_lame_at_pressure_bore` | the nominal-basis Lamé rows formed with the pressure bore |
| `kappa_on_reduced_section` | κ_E and A_s on the reduced annulus (Timoshenko variant) |
| `shear_deformation_omitted`, `shear_deformation_applied_under_euler_bernoulli` | the other shear model's deflection |

The coincident case has none: with c_m = c_a = 0 every D-5 mapping coincides.

## Criterion

The existing T4 rule, in both solver modes: a nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|; a zero row when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·|zero scale| in the row
unit (`criterion.absolute_tolerance`, precomputed). The zero scales are per
load case and family (axial and transverse forces, force norms, moments,
axial and transverse translations, rotations, and per basis the axial,
bending/torsion, Lamé and maximum stresses), each the family's largest
|expected| or, if the family is identically zero, the sibling scale named in
`zero_scale_definitions` (as T4-I8 takes P and P/As). A listed discriminator
row passes when |obs − wrong| > max(absolute tolerance,
1e-9·max(|obs|, |wrong|)). No new threshold.

## Open items

1. **Generated self-weight input (not determinable without product files).**
   The exact route's generated self-weight from pipe metal and contents
   densities (the `stable_mass_per_length` / `MassPrimitive` exact branch,
   Design §1) has no schema or reference document this author may read: the
   preview-model fields for the pipe metal density, the contents density and
   gravity are unknown. `case:self-weight` and `case:combined` are therefore
   frozen with their operands (`self_weight`) and discriminators, but are not
   in any request (`request_status` pending). Authoring them needs that
   spelling; the expected values do not depend on it.
2. **Unpressurized row identities (inferred).** `case:axial-tip` publishes
   `element_local_axial_force` (component `axial_force`, node-on-element at
   the ends, j-side cut at the stations) and
   `element_local_axial_normal_stress` (component `axial_normal_stress`, MPa,
   five locations), by the sibling kinds' pattern. Their spelling and
   locations are in the pressure-1 table, which this author did not read.
   A mismatch shows as an unresolved row, never as a changed value.
3. **Plain-form thermal primitive.** `U6-BASES-002`'s thermal load is a
   `thermal` primitive in a 0.5.0 document without the load/reference-state
   group, as in the re-authored T4-I8 case 2.
