## Basis

- **D-5 geometry (Design §1).** Stiffness A, I, J, the Timoshenko shear area,
  pipe metal mass and the As of ε_p = (1 − 2ν)pAi/(E·As) on the nominal wall;
  Ai (caps, Poisson pair) and contents on the pressure bore; stresses on both
  sections, each section's As = πw(OD − w), I = As(ro² + ri²)/4, J = 2I,
  Z = I/ro. `section_geometry.dual.binary64_order` applies the fixed binary64
  order t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, ri,r = OD/2 − t_r to these inputs; the other
  order, ((OD/2 − t) + c_m) + c_a, rounds one ulp differently here.
- **Stress per basis (Design §2.1).** σ = N/A_b (N_w/A_b on pressurized
  members), M/Z_b, T·ro/J_b; Lamé of each basis's own annulus (radial −p and
  0; hoop 2p·π ri,b²/A_b outside and that plus p inside); maximum
  |N/A_b| + hypot(M_y, M_z)/Z_b at the root (|M_y| and |M_z| are checked to be
  nonincreasing along the member).
- **Shear (Design §3.1; SHEAR_REFERENCE §§1, 3, 4).** κ_E =
  16609827/33068756 (S-B's 33.219654/66.137512), A_s = κ_E·Aₙ, Ks = G·A_s;
  tip deflection FL³/(3EI) + FL/Ks, uniform load wL⁴/(8EI) + wL²/(2Ks);
  rotations are section rotations and do not change. The frozen
  `pipe_sections_evidence` object has exactly the Design §2.5 keys; beside it,
  the Timoshenko variant's `binary64_exhibits.shear_factor` gives κ evaluated
  in binary64 in the Design's operation order from the binary64 leaves (one
  ulp from the rounding of the exact value), for the evidence readers.
- **Conventions** are T4-I8's: element-local frame = global here; end rows
  node-on-element (end_i = −cut(0), end_j = +cut(L)); station rows and all
  stress rows from the j-side cut; element-local stresses in MPa, displacements
  in mm (references in Pa and m); supports support-on-pipe with moments about
  the node; x–z plane v = u_z, θ = −r_y.
- **Exact numbers.** Every value is a sum of rational·π^k, or a square root of
  one (support norms and the combined maxima), evaluated at 130 digits. The
  decimal inputs are exact rational targets; their binary64 rounding is
  absorbed by the criterion.

## Criterion

The existing T4 rule, in both solver modes: a nonzero row passes when
|obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9·|exp|; a zero row when |obs| ≤ 1e-9·|zero scale| in the row
unit (`criterion.absolute_tolerance`, precomputed). The zero scales are per
load case and family (axial and transverse forces, force norms, moments,
axial and transverse translations, rotations, and per basis the axial,
bending/torsion, Lamé and maximum stresses), each the family's largest
|expected| or, if the family is identically zero, the sibling scale named in
`zero_scale_definitions` (as T4-I8 takes P and P/As). A listed discriminator
row passes when |obs − wrong| > max(absolute tolerance,
1e-9·max(|obs|, |wrong|)). No new threshold.

## Generated self-weight

`case:self-weight` and `case:combined` were frozen first, with their operands
(`self_weight`) and discriminators, and are now carried by
`U6-BASES-001-EB-SELF-WEIGHT` and `U6-BASES-001-TIMO-SELF-WEIGHT`
(`request_self_weight` in each variant). No frozen value changed. The
spelling follows a prepared exact 0.5.0 self-weight input document read as
data; `schemas/model.schema.yaml` constrains only `schema_version` and
`pressure_contract` of these documents and defines none of the fields below:
- the section carries `material_density` 7850 and `contents_density`
  1000 kg/m^3 beside its explicit mill tolerance and allowance;
- each load case carries one `distributed_force` primitive,
  `load:self-weight:<len>:<case>:<len>:<pipe>`, along `global_z`, whose
  `provenance` is the generation record: method
  `pipe_mass_per_length_times_explicit_axis_acceleration/v2`, mass method
  `source_od_nominal_wall_pressure_bore_areas/v1`, gravity −9.80665 m/s² on
  `global_z`, the section's mass inputs and dependencies, `mass_kg_per_m`,
  the generated payload and an all-zero `source_model_hash`, as in the
  example.

The authored binary64 operands are inputs, not frozen values: mass
74.17476969712266 kg/m and magnitude −727.4060052502879 N/m, 1.1e-16
relative from the exact w (`generated_self_weight` records the order). Other plausible
binary64 orders of the same D-5 rule give 74.17476969712267 and
−727.406005250288, one ulp away; the example document does not separate
them. If the product rejects the generated load as modified or stale, the
record is re-authored with the product's order or hash; the rows do not
move. The request records the intensity the product uses, so the
`contents_on_nominal_bore` and `metal_mass_on_reduced_wall` discriminators
expose a wrong mass basis only where the product re-derives the intensity
from the section inputs (the exact branch's re-formation or its consistency
check) rather than taking the recorded magnitude.
