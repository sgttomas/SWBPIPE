## EQ-4: SRSS and absolute sum, an identity

RS-EQ-SRSS and RS-EQ-ABS combine RS-dEQX, RS-dEQY, RS-dEQZ (factors 1, spec §8.2). On every signed component row
(support force and moment components, translation and rotation components, element end-force components), with
x_X, x_Y, x_Z the operand rows as published, DEL-05-02's definitions give:

- **absolute sum** A = RN(Σ |fᵢ·xᵢ|): the exact sum of the exact products, rounded once to binary64;
- **SRSS** zᵢ = RN(fᵢ·xᵢ), R = RN(√Σ zᵢ²): the square root of the exact sum of squares, correctly rounded once.

With fᵢ = 1, zᵢ = xᵢ exactly. Since √Σ xᵢ² ≤ Σ |xᵢ| exactly and RN is monotone, R ≤ A holds on every row after
rounding too, with equality when at most one operand row is nonzero. Rows of either are unsigned (magnitudes, not
equilibrium states). Magnitude rows (resultant displacement, reaction magnitude) are not combined
component-wise; they follow the combination's own recomputation rule, as for EQ-3. Rows the combination withholds
are excluded. No values are frozen. The generator carries a reference implementation (`absolute_sum`, `srss`,
`rn_sqrt`, exact `Fraction` arithmetic with a correctly rounded square root, ties to even) and checks it on six
invented triples.

## Specification observations (not open readings)

These are with the lane that holds the specification, as they stand:
- **Seismic gravity.** §2 says the seismic case "enters `gravity_acceleration` explicitly" but gives no value. These
  references use 9.80665 m/s², the weight case's gravity, as a stated assumption; that lane is writing the value
  into §8.1. Until it is on `main`, every seismic value here is conditional on it. Under the decided rule a seismic
  force is a·W_s, and W_s is proportional to g on the distributed members only (the component weights are forces),
  so a different value would move each seismic value, but not in simple proportion.
- **Sense of a positive g-factor.** Taken along the positive global axis. The opposite sense (for Z, along gravity)
  reverses the sign of each seismic force and moment.
- **RS-P150 under the reducer.** RS-P150 carries section S270 (§3.2) though RS-R1 runs from 270 to 220 mm. Its wind
  diameter, and its distributed seismic load and weight in the fixture today, use S270.

## Exactness and evaluation

Every value is an exact sum of terms q·π^k·√n with q rational: π from μ, π² from μ on an arc, √257 from RS-P150's
length, and rational terms for component weights and wind on straight members. `exact` prints that sum. π is
bounded by Machin's formula in integer arithmetic and √n by the integer square root, each to 10⁻¹⁰⁰. `decimal`
has 40 significant digits and `value` is the binary64 nearest the exact value. Each is emitted only when both
ends of the bounding interval give the same.

Cross-checks, all 34 agreeing: the transcription against the specification (four); each arc's corner,
tangency and centre exactly, and its first moment against Simpson quadrature (six); RS-P150's length √257/40 and
its drop as half the OD difference (two); each section's μ in a second factored form, operating and test (six);
