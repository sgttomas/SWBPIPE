compared with them. A separate TASK refuted them on 2026-10-16 and reported

two were repaired in `5e84d520a7` without changing any frozen value, product

independent refuter confirmed both repairs and showed that the third finding
was not a defect:
- the S-E energy terms are correct;
- the TWIN-A reaction-magnitude discriminator's frozen value F(1+φ) is
  correct; the original refuter's value had kept the (1−2ν)pAi axial load at
  node A;
- the reported unlisted moved row was TWIN-A Fx. The
  `euler_bernoulli_recovery` mutant does not move it, because EA/L is the
  same in both theories, so leaving it unlisted was correct and nothing was
  repaired.

The `status` strings inside `element_controls.json` and `product_cases.json`

## Method

- **Exact arithmetic.** Every value is an element of Q(π) held in lowest terms
  with a monic denominator; text forms are `p/q`, `r*pi`, `r/pi` and
  `(n0 + n1*pi)/(d0 + d1*pi)`. Norms outside Q(π) are `sqrt(...)`. Decimals carry
  40 significant digits (π by Machin, 140-digit context). Binary64 values are
  rounded from the exact forms.
- **Element.** CONTRACT §3's exact static element, placed per design §3.3: v = uy
  with θ = rz, and v = uz with θ = −ry. Axial EA/L uses the metal area and
  torsion GJ/L carries no κ. κ_E is computed on the nominal (stiffness) annulus
  with the case's resolved ν. Load equivalents come from CONTRACT §4's
  integrated solution (V′ = −w, M′ = −V, θ′ = M/EI, v′ = θ + V/Ks) by
  fixed-fixed compatibility, not from Hermite formulas. Recovery is f = K d − feq
  with the same feq; stations are the +x-face section action from statics.
- **Assembly.** The generator builds a 12-DOF member in each member's rational
  local frame and assembles nodal loads, uniform member loads, thermal and
  pressure-Poisson eigenloads, transferred caps and linear springs. It solves
  exactly and takes reactions as R = K u − f; a spring's reaction is −k u.
- **Section bases (D-5).** Stiffness and κ_E use the nominal wall. The reduced
  section is (t − c_m) − c_a, and the pressure bore is the reduced bore. Every
  stress row is published on both bases. With zero mill tolerance and allowance
  the two values are identical.
- **Inputs.** The new cases treat their decimal inputs as exact rationals
  (ri = 9/100 m, and so on). Their requests author the nearest binary64 values,
  which moves results by about 1e-16 relative. Twin A uses binary64 inputs, as
  its fixture does; its ri bits equal the fixture's `ri_bits_hex`, which confirms
  design §1's order D/2 − t on that case.
- **Binary64 κ.** Each Timoshenko member also records κ evaluated in design §3.1's
  fixed binary64 order from the leaves r_o = D/2 and r_i = D/2 − t
  (`shear_factor_binary64_design_order`, with its hex). A_s is frozen exactly
  only. Its binary64 leaf fl(κ·A_n) depends on the product's annulus-area
  operation order, which this generator does not read.

## Discriminators

Each load case lists the mutants that move its rows (by more than 1e-7
relative), with the wrong values, and names those that do not move them there:
- shear omitted or wrongly added;
- the Cowper, solid-circle (Cowper and energy), 5/6 and κ = 1 factors;
- θ = v′;
- the locked linear element;
- swapped plane signs;
- κ on GJ;
- A_s in EA;
- stiffness-only replacement (Euler–Bernoulli recovery);
- κ, or the whole stiffness, on the reduced section, and the pre-D-5 wall − mill
  section;
- one basis for both stress families;
- the base ν instead of the case ν;
- one case's shear terms reused.

Two discriminators need care:
- **`euler_bernoulli_recovery` reaction rows.** The 76 reaction rows listed for
  this mutant assume a product sums reactions from the Euler–Bernoulli-recovered
  member end actions. A product that takes reactions from R = K u − f with the
  assembled Timoshenko K publishes correct reactions under this mutant. The
  mutant's end-action, station and stress rows kill it on either reaction path.
- **`base_nu_for_kappa`.** This is the weakest discriminator: it moves the tip uy
  by 2.6e-6 relative in case:cold and 3.3e-6 in case:hot. That is still far
  outside the 1e-9 criterion, so it remains valid.

The stale Euler–Bernoulli load vector is an element-level discriminator only
(S-A). The exact route has no partial-span member load, and for full-span loads
the exact vector equals the Euler–Bernoulli vector.

## Criterion

The criterion is unchanged and adds no threshold:
- nonzero rows pass when |obs − exp| ≤ 1e-9 |exp|;
- zero rows pass when |obs| ≤ 1e-9 |zero scale|, with the tolerance precomputed
  in the row unit (`criterion.absolute_tolerance`);
- every discriminator row must satisfy |obs − wrong| > 1e-9 max(|obs|, |wrong|).

Run both solver modes against the same reference. Rows are keyed by (kind,
entity_ref, location, component), plus `section_basis` on stress rows. Expected
values are in the row unit; `exact` and `decimal` are in `reference_unit`, and
`factor` converts them exactly (m → mm 1000, Pa → MPa 1/1000000).

New cases take each zero scale as the largest nonzero magnitude of the same
family in that load case. The I8 case keeps T4-I8's definitions; its
`scale_transverse_length` is |uy(D)| of the Timoshenko solve, by that
definition.

## Reproduce and change

```
python3 -I generators/u8_reference.py          # check: rebuild everything, compare byte for byte
python3 -I generators/u8_reference.py --write  # rewrite the outputs (intentional changes only)
```

Check mode also fails on any cross-check disagreement or unexpected file.

**Re-freeze rules.**
- No value changes to match a product output. A disagreement goes back to this
  reference's owner and its refuter (design §7).
- A change to the selected theory or its definitions (κ_E, the element, load
  equivalents or recovery) is a re-freeze with a recorded reason and a new
  refutation.
- A change to a row convention (kind, unit, sign, D-5 basis rules) re-derives
  the rows. Physical values stay unless the convention changes their meaning.
- A request re-authoring (for example a later model version) regenerates the
  requests. Oracle values must not move; only request hashes do.
- If either bound T4-I8 block or the twin-A fixture changes, the generator
  refuses until the change is examined.
