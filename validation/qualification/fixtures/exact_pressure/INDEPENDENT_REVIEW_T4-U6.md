## Independent checks

I wrote two checkers for this review. Neither imports the generator. Each
pending and U4a package was regenerated by its own generator from its own
commit, outside the tree. Each reproduces its committed MANIFEST byte for byte
(`--check`: 861 and 841 files, 0 differences). `--check` also passes in the
reviewed tree. The static checker derives every expected selector key, value,
criterion, negative and absence from the frozen references by its own mapping,
then compares them with the case files and with the U4a package. It ran
1,700,770 checks, and none failed.

1. **Binding.**
   - All 172 cases bind their 860 files by the MANIFEST's SHA-256.
   - Each analytical reference is the current I7 or I8 file by hash. Every
     case runs in both modes, and `required_scalar_rows` equals its positives.
   - Per mode there are 78,116 positive assertions, 672 negative assertions
     and 1,652 absences. The positives include 2,094 terminal-evidence
     selectors and 3,304 I8 rows (720 in MPa). The README's family table is
     reproduced exactly.
   - In the pending state, every reference is `pending_independent_review`
     with no review reference, and every profile is a draft. The gate's case
     preparation refuses all 172 cases: "reference not ready or basis absent".
2. **Unchanged inputs.**
   - 164 cases are common with U4a. Their 328 runner-input and
     product-request files are byte-identical to the admitted package.
   - The inventory change is exactly the four retired MILLTOL v2 cases,
     replaced by eight 0.5.0 cases.
   - Every I7 request is its frozen sketch. Every I8 request is the
     reference's own document: v2 under `v3_patch_for_both`, or the 0.5.0
     documents.

## Product evidence

The gate has no pre-admission mode, and at this head it cannot load a reader
binding (F1). So the runner above solved each case's submitted input itself:
344 runs, up to 0.3 s each. The harness used the gate's own submission rule,
raw admission, standing and structural predicates. The Rust reader's verdict
was taken from `openpipestress-runner read pressure` directly.

The harness has its own resolver. Each row selector must find one row by id,
with exactly the same kind, unit, entity, basis and metadata, and a unique
signature. The resolver agreed with the gate's `resolve_row` and
`resolve_evidence` everywhere.

Results in each mode:

- positives: 78,116 of 78,116 pass, the worst at 0.0035 of its tolerance;
- negatives: 672 of 672 are excluded, the closest at 23,293 tolerances;
- absences: 1,652 of 1,652 hold;
- structural checks: 1,376 of 1,376 match;
- standing: `checks_passed` in all 172 cases;
- reader: consistent on all 172 envelopes.

In the coincident cases, all 976 twin pairs per mode are bit-equal in the
product. Where a re-frozen case's two frozen values coincide (zeros, −p), the
product's twins can differ at noise level (950 sparse and 1,006 dense pairs),
each within its rule.

  `f91e678e95115acc613c6c422b444ba00887fec20689fda51961dc7351fd9177` without
  re-pinning it.
- A gate run on the pending package therefore blocks all 172 cases (78,788
  assertions, 1,376 structural checks) at `bound bytes differ … physics_evidence.rs`.
- At the head, `tests/test_qualification_exact_pressure.py` has 15 failures and
  1 error: `PinTests`, 14 runs blocked at the binding, and the expected
  missing admission.
- The first-static and load/reference-state adapters use the same pin.

Repair: the reader's owner reviews that change and re-pins. Nothing in the
package changes. The runner reviewed here includes the change, and its reader
found every envelope consistent.

**F2: the README's run guidance is stale.** The README says "Set
`--output-limit-bytes` to at least 16 MiB. The largest CLI output observed is
12.8 MB". Under D-5 that no longer holds:

- six U-ANCH-ALL-K2-SKEW cases on 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 exceed 16 MiB
  (16,777,216 bytes) in both modes;
- the largest output is 17,276,452 bytes (`U2-U-ANCH-ALL-K2-SKEW-X7P3E6@0.4.0`,
  dense);
- at 16 MiB, these 12 runs fail closed.

Repair: state the observed maximum, and a limit of at least 17,276,452 bytes
(for example 32 MiB; the transport allows 64 MiB).

**F3: the README's floor tally cannot be reproduced.** The README says "Across
1,074 matched groups, 143 rose …, 467 fell … and 464 are unchanged". My
grouping does not reproduce it:

- package floors: 1,241, of which 173 rose, 518 fell and 550 are unchanged;
- reference: 894 same-named groups (162 rose, 275 fell, 457 unchanged) and 426
  per-basis groups (38 rose, 252 fell, 136 unchanged).

The bounds (×1.17 and 0.87) agree. Repair: correct the tally or name its
grouping.
