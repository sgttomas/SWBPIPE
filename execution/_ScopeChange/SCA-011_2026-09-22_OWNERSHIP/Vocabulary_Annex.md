# SCA-011 implementation-landing successor

This amendment changes delivery accountability only under the SCA-011 Group 2 decision. The accepted SCA-009 Vocabulary Annex, read with its Gate-5 ACCEPTANCE_RECORD, retains the vocabulary classes, normative content, sequencing and historical implementation evidence. Its historical candidate header does not undo its later acceptance. No original annex bytes or historical coverage cells change.

DEL-07-09 owns vocabulary coverage and single-palette organization. Its R-005/R-006 landing is the coverage-accountability home, not editor implementation. Load-case/combination editor behavior belongs to DEL-07-03; support creation and editing belongs to DEL-07-02; material/section/component/library editors belong to DEL-07-03, with generic tree/table/inspector editing in DEL-07-02. All editor changes use the PKG-16 seam. No dedicated support-editor slice is activated.
