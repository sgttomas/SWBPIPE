## Common GUI requirements applied to every panel

DEL-07-12 owns rendering, review interaction and explicit dispatch only. Show record family and version, originating model/state/run and source basis; unknown or unsupported versions must not masquerade as an actual accepted record. Preserve diagnostic severity and affected references, units/dimensions and conversion/withheld-witness status, stable IDs and losses/omissions, provenance/rights/private-data classification, and professional-boundary distinctions. For every download or handoff use DEL-12-02's export-context classification: public/shared routes redact or block protected/private/unknown-rights content; explicit local-private intent permits only the governed local-private path with warning/audit evidence. Assignment of a GUI owner supplies no new data permission, destination, service, runtime or external target.

`GUI_CONTRACT_BINDINGS.csv` distinguishes the required future contract binding from observed source labels. Source inspection is not product execution; no current runtime pass/fail result is claimed. Native delivery/cancellation/recovery must have native-host witnesses where the route requires them; browser screenshots do not establish it. Preview/reference/fixture states must stay visible, and actual generated records require schema and identity/content evidence. Existing test/readiness records remain at their original owners; new GUI evidence cross-references them rather than overwriting their meaning.
