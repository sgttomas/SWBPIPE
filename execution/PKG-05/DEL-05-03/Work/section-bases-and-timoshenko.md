## 1. Section geometry under D-5

For an exact-contract pipe section with outside diameter D, wall t, mill tolerance c_m and corrosion allowance c_a (each a finite length, c_m, c_a ≥ 0, c_m + c_a < t; the existing refusals apply unchanged):

| Name | Wall | Inner radius | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nominal section** | t | r_i,n = D/2 − t | stiffness (A, I, J and the Timoshenko shear area), pipe metal mass, the area As in ε_p = (1−2ν)pAi/(E·As), nominal-basis stresses |
| **Reduced section** | t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a | r_i,r = D/2 − t_r | reduced-basis stresses |
| **Pressure bore** | — | r_i,r | end-cap and joint-face area Ai = π r_i,r², contents mass, the equal-bore region check, `source_bore_key` |

The binary64 order is fixed as written: t_r = (t − c_m) − c_a, then r_i,r = D/2 − t_r (the ruling's algebraically equal OD/2 − t + c_m + c_a rounds differently). Producer, Rust readers and Python verifiers use this order. With c_a = 0 it reproduces today's t − c_m bit for bit.

r_o = D/2 for both sections. Each section's A, I, J = 2I and Z = I/r_o come from `SourceAnnulus::from_od_wall(D, wall)` with that section's wall. Today's single section uses t − c_m, so with c_a = 0 the reduced section is bit-identical to today's section, and with c_m = c_a = 0 both sections are.

**Every geometry site.** The producer derives the three geometries once per pipe on the exact route (`derive_pipe_section`'s exact branch and `build_model_for_members`) and threads them by name. No site re-derives a wall from `wall − mill_tolerance` on the exact route after this change.

| Site (today) | Basis after D-5 |
|---|---|
| `build_model_for_members` stiffness section, `StraightPipeSectionProperties::new`, `CurvedBendMacroElement::new` (arc A, I, J) | nominal |
| Thermal/fit eigenloads E·A·ε (`build_thermal_element_loads`, `load_state_eigen_loads`, `FreeStrainStraight`) | nominal A |
| Straight Poisson eigen pair 2νpAi, terminal caps ±pAi, joint face p(Ae − Ai), `PressureOperand`, `certified_pressure_area_product`, `pressure_adjustment`, `arc_pressure_wall` | Ai on the pressure bore |
| Arc `PressureFreeStrainCurved` / `arc_pressure_strain` | Ai on the pressure bore, As = nominal arc area (the element's own area, N-3); `ARC_PRESSURE_STRAIN_ROUNDINGS` is re-derived for two walls |
| `recover_wall_effective_membrane` | split: N_w = N_m + 2νpAi and S = N_m + (2ν−1)pAi once, with Ai on the pressure bore; then σ_m = N_w/A_b per basis. Calling today's function on the nominal annulus would put the cap on the nominal bore |
| `surface_stresses`, `straight_stress_spans`, `exact_straight_summary_extrema`, `fallback_circular_maximum`, `recover_section_stress`, `append_stress_mpa`, endpoint/station stress pushes, arc membrane | once per basis (section 2) |
| `stable_mass_per_length`, FK `MassPrimitive` / `mass_reformation` | a new exact branch: metal area on the nominal wall, contents on the pressure bore, the allowance as an operand, and a new method id; the preview branch keeps `source_od_effective_wall_areas/v1` |
| `PRESSURE_REGION_BORE_MISMATCH`, `source_bore_key` | pressure bore |
| Evidence `pipe_sections` / `exact_section_evidence` in every identity (pressure-1, pressure-source-1 `exact_cases`, W1-E's overlay), stress-neutral geometry, arc evidence | all three, by name (section 2.3) |
| Rust readers (`physics_evidence.rs`, `arc_evidence.rs`, `physics_source.rs`, `load_reference.rs`, `retained_precision.rs` G5b/G8) and Python verifiers (`pressure_1.py`, `physics_evidence.py`, `physics_source.py`, `load_reference_evidence.py`, `retained_precision.py`) | rederive all three from the captured invocation and bind them bit for bit |

`derive_pipe_section` and `stable_mass_per_length` are shared by both routes today; their exact branches are new code. The preview route's behaviour (wall − mill for every use), its mass method and every preview row stay exactly as they are, and route L's pricing decisions zero the new branches.

**Consequences for the one mechanical solution.** Forces, moments, displacements, reactions, the effective axial force S and the wall force N_w = S + pAi (on straights N_w = N_m + 2νpAi and S = N_m + (2ν−1)pAi from the recovered mechanical force N_m; on arcs S = N_el) come from one solve with nominal stiffness and pressure-bore loads; none of them is per basis. Moving the stiffness section from t − c_m to t changes them wherever the response depends on stiffness: displacements always, and forces and reactions in indeterminate systems (an anchored L-line redistributes with the ratio of EA to EI). Only under collinear straight restraint are the restrained axial forces independent of As, because As in ε_p is the stiffness As (T4-I8 RV1 N-5); there N_w and S do not move.

## 2. Outputs

### 2.1 Stress rows on two bases

On an exact-contract document every row whose pressure-1 table family is `stress` is published twice, once per section basis, at the same entity, location and component. That covers `element_local_axial_normal_stress` (members outside every pressure region), `element_local_bending_normal_stress_y/z`, `element_local_torsional_shear_stress`, `pipe_axial_membrane_stress_v2`, `pipe_lame_radial_stress_v2`, `pipe_lame_hoop_stress_v2` and `pipe_elastic_normal_stress_maximum_v2`, on straight members and realized arcs alike. Both families are published even where the bases coincide (H4). Force, moment, displacement, rotation, reaction, wall-force and effective-force rows are published once.

Per basis b (section A_b, Z_b, J_b, r_i,b):
- axial normal σ = N/A_b; membrane σ_m = N_w/A_b;
- bending σ = M_y/Z_b, M_z/Z_b; torsional shear τ = T·r_o/J_b;
- Lamé, the closed annulus solution of the basis's own section with internal pressure p at r_i,b: radial −p (inner) and 0 (outer); hoop at the outer surface 2p·π r_i,b²/A_b and at the inner surface that plus p. The pressure-load bore governs Ai in the loads; it does not move the stress solution of the nominal section onto another annulus. On the nominal basis the two are therefore not one closed-end annulus: σ_m = N_w/A_n carries the cap thrust on the pressure bore while the hoop uses r_i,n, so σ_m is not hoop/2 there. Limitation 0 discloses this, so a rule pack does not infer one from the other;
- the member maximum max over stations of |N_w/A_b| (or |N/A_b|) + hypot(M_y, M_z)/Z_b, with the existing certified circular-maximum machinery evaluated with that basis's A_b and Z_b. On realized arcs the Lamé rows and the maximum stayed withheld on both bases, with the existing warning once per basis, until T4-U4b published the toroidal hoop and radial rows and the arc maximum on each basis instead (u4b-u5b-implementation.md §§2.3 and 2.5).

**Naming.** Each stress row names its basis in `metadata.basis`, by appending `; section_basis=nominal_section` or `; section_basis=reduced_section` to its existing recovery token (the solver-mode row already uses this `; key=value` form). For example `recovered_from_open_mechanics_stress_components; section_basis=reduced_section`, `recovered_from_local_element_stiffness; section_basis=nominal_section`, and on a selected source case `retained_source_endpoint_normal_max_v1; section_basis=…` for the endpoint maximum; source-derived straight stress rows carry the `recovered_from_*` tokens (`retained_source_straight_stress_v1` is their receipt recipe id, not a row token). Stress row ids gain a final basis segment for both families, so neither family is privileged; which spelling each id family takes is fixed in section 2.5. Non-stress ids and tokens are unchanged. The new tokens and segments are emitted only from exact-route code; the shared preview helpers keep their arguments and bytes.

## 3. The Timoshenko straight member (T4-U8)

### 3.1 Section shear rigidity

For `timoshenko_annulus_energy_v1`, from the nominal section's binary64 leaves r_i = r_i,n, r_o and the member's selected ν (the same E/ν basis that gives G; per resolved case on load-state documents):

```
m  = r_i / r_o;  m2 = m*m;  a = 1 + m2;  b = 1 - m2;  n1 = 1 + nu
c  = (8*nu + 14)*nu + 7
D  = c*(b*b) + 48*(m2*(n1*n1))           // SHEAR_REFERENCE's cancellation-free form
kappa = (6*(n1*n1))*(a*a) / D             // kappa_E, energy-matched (not Cowper, not 5/6)
A_s = kappa * A_n                         // shear area leaf; Ks = G * A_s
```

The operation order is fixed and uses no fused multiply-add, so the Rust and Python readers reproduce κ and A_s bit for bit from the evidence. A_s is a binary64 leaf like A and I: the intended K-D5 system is formed exactly from it. A missing ν or G basis blocks a Timoshenko solve by name; no default factor exists.

### 3.2 Representation in FK (H5: a re-priced FrameElement)

`FrameSection` gains `beam_theory: BeamTheory`, an enum `EulerBernoulli | Timoshenko { shear_area: f64 }`. `FrameSection::new` keeps building Euler–Bernoulli sections, so its 72 call sites and every Euler–Bernoulli formation are unchanged bit for bit. `FrameSection::timoshenko(E, G, A, Iy, Iz, J, shear_area)` checks a finite positive shear area. `StraightPipeSectionProperties` and `StraightPipeElement` carry the same leaf into `frame_section()`, so `pipe.frame_element()`, every load primitive's frame copy and every recovery frame carry it. The layout growth (FrameSection 48 → 64 B, FrameElement 136 → 152 B) is priced (section 9). `core/solver/frame_kernel/README.md`, which describes the realization as Euler–Bernoulli, is updated to state both.

### 3.3 Stiffness

Per bending plane with I (Iz for v = u_y, θ = r_z; Iy for v = u_z, θ = −r_y, as in the existing frame convention), φ = 12EI/(G·A_s·L²):

```
k_vv = 12EI/(L³(1+φ))   k_vθ = 6EI/(L²(1+φ))   k_θθ = (4+φ)EI/(L(1+φ))   k_θθ' = (2−φ)EI/(L(1+φ))
```

placed exactly where `add_bending_z` / `add_bending_y` place the Euler–Bernoulli coefficients. Axial EA/L and torsion GJ/L are unchanged (A is the metal area, never A_s; κ never touches GJ). Each intermediate is a named K2a checked formation. The difference (2 − φ) may be exactly zero (φ = 2), so it and its product are checked for finiteness only; a zero there is not an underflow. ## 4. The K-D5 certificate with a Timoshenko element

The certificate bounds published member, section, support and pipe-wall groups against the intended system K_int u = f_int formed exactly from the binary64 leaves. Its only premise is A1 (the Hager-estimate inverse bound), and the formation error ε_E is measured exactly after the fact; no a-priori per-entry bound enters. Nothing in the bound uses Euler–Bernoulli structure. With the changes below it stays sound:

1. `frame_matrix` (formation_check.rs) forms φ, 1+φ, 4+φ and 2−φ at p from the frame's own leaves when `beam_theory` is Timoshenko. It feeds ρ, s, s₂, ε_E, certified recovery, support reactions and the stress spans, so all of them see the same intended element. ε_E absorbs any binary64 Timoshenko formation error, including cancellation in (2−φ); a larger ε_E fails closed (Sensitive), never open.
2. **Frame binding.** `frame_binding` includes the leaf, and a new fail-closed check requires every straight load primitive's frame and every certified-recovery frame to be bit-equal to the FormationSource frame of the same member, keyed by member identity (parallel members on one node pair are admitted). This closes the split-source hazard (a primitive or recovery frame without the leaf would certify a mixed system).
3. **K2b force scaling.** A_s is an area, so φ is bit-invariant under the exact 2^b scaling of E and G. `ForceScaleCensus::frame` uses the actual binary64 φ, which is scale-invariant, for the exponents of 1+φ, 4+φ and 2−φ, which cannot be predicted from leaf exponents; an exact zero 2−φ is excluded from the census.
4. **Load equivalents.** Full-span uniform loads are unchanged (section 3.4), so the S11-G guard and `uniform_straight_balls` keep their exact formulas. A Timoshenko partial span has two checks. (i) S11-G: its held operands are the element's binary64 q_g, L, a, b, T and the stiffness's own φ_y and φ_z; the intended slot value I_r carries ρ = φ/(1+φ), so with D = (1+φ_y)(1+φ_z) ≥ 1 the scaled defect S_r = s_r·D·(v_r − I_r) is formed exactly as an expansion, and each slot is `Bounded` with B_r = RU(RU(|S_r|)/RD(s_r·D)); a range failure makes every slot `CannotBound` (the case demotes).
