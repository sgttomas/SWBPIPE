## Audit D02: physical interpretation before replacement

**Recommended direction: objective small-displacement finite-end connector, preceded by a bounded formulation proof and manufacturer-meaning review.** The intended component is a freely moving physical expansion joint; a rigid-body nullspace is therefore a necessary verification property under that interpretation. It is insufficient by itself to identify the correct constitutive law or stiffness calibration.

External primary documentation distinguishes fixed-direction two-node springs from line-following springs and warns that rotation semantics need care; connector elastic behavior acts on explicitly defined relative motion. This supports keeping the two interpretations distinct, not copying another solver's formulation. [Abaqus Springs, vendor-authored documentation hosted by Virginia Tech](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEELMRefMap/simaelm-c-spring.htm); [Connector Elastic Behavior](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEELMRefMap/simaelm-c-connelastbehav.htm).

| Option | Concrete content and tradeoff |
|---|---|
| A — objective finite-end linear connector (recommended investigation) | Prove a compatibility operator, energy, end-force recovery and frame transformation. A project-original **candidate**, not an adopted vendor model, is r=xj−xi; delta=u_j−u_i−((theta_i+theta_j)/2)×r; phi=theta_j−theta_i. Project delta and phi into the declared initial local frame and set q=Bd. Candidate energy is 1/2 qᵀDq with user directional stiffnesses and K=BᵀDB. For u=t+omega×x, theta=omega, both delta and phi vanish. The energy derivative supplies end moments associated with the lever arm, avoiding force-only transfer. The symmetric average is a modeling choice; manufacturer lateral/angular stiffness definitions may require different reference points or coupled D terms. Do not adopt merely because the nullspace proof passes. |

## Audit D04: separate scale guarantee from seed recovery

**Scale recommendation:** investigate explicit dimensional normalization plus symmetric equilibration and independent residual diagnostics, rather than changing the 1e-12 literal in isolation. A positive scalar rescaling changes pivots while leaving the exact solution unchanged; uniform rescaling alone does not cure heterogeneous conditioning. Translation and rotation DOFs need a declared coordinate/work-conjugate scaling basis, not a unit-mixed diagonal threshold. After any transform, recover physical displacements and assess the original system residual with force/moment dimensions distinguished.

LAPACK's SPD driver provides a concrete numerical precedent: optional symmetric equilibration, factorization, condition estimation, iterative refinement and error bounds, with solution transformed back. This is a method reference, not authority to import its warnings or precision criteria as piping acceptance thresholds. [LAPACK DPOSVX](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d6/d44/group__posvx_gae23dac18e7ec69c36fd2a2648a470dc3.html).

Options: (S-A) retain documented range/guard and structured failure, requiring owner to choose the supported normalized envelope; (S-B, recommended investigation) establish normalization/equilibration and separate singularity/ill-conditioning/finiteness diagnostics, then bring evidence for an owner-ratified guarantee; (S-C) evaluate a rank-revealing or pivoting backend, with greater dependency, determinism and performance implications. Equilibration does not prove well-conditioning; an alternate factorization does not itself prove mechanical stability. Candidate files after ruling: `frame_kernel/src/lib.rs`, `sparse_direct/src/lib.rs` and README, `diagnostics`, numerical performance harness and `product_physics` diagnostic adapters only as required by a selected integration brief. Freeze boundary elimination, original residual and scaling expectations before edits.

**Seed recommendation:** first specify a frictionless unilateral feasibility-recovery guarantee for a bounded class. An active-set seed is a numerical guess, but friction history is physical state; changing the former must not silently reset the latter. (I-A) retain explicit singular inactive-trial failure and require an admissible user seed; (I-B, recommended investigation) on singular frictionless trial, construct a deterministic feasible candidate active set, solve and validate every contact inequality/reaction/complementarity condition; report failure if no admissible solution is found within the separately accepted search contract; (I-C) use temporary stabilization/continuation, requiring additional residual, artificial-force/energy and removal evidence. Brute-force active-set enumeration is suitable as an independent oracle for tiny invented fixtures, not a proposed unrestricted production algorithm or cap.

Initially unconstrained contact is a recognized quasistatic difficulty. Vendor documentation offers prescribed motion, initial configuration and stabilization strategies, and requires stabilization forces to relax so they do not contaminate the final solution. These are alternatives for analysis, not permission to introduce small penetration or hidden springs in Chirality. [COMSOL Quasistatic Contact Analysis](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.sme/sme_ug_modeling.05.149.html); [COMSOL Stabilization](https://doc.comsol.com/6.4/doc/com.comsol.help.sme/sme_ug_solid.07.131.html).
