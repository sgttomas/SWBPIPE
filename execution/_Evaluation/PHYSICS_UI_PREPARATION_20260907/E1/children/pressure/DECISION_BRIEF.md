## Independent primary engineering basis

[MIT, David Roylance, Pressure Vessels](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/082fcd712adffa589a670f9f8dfdd49a_MIT3_11F99_pv.pdf), pp. 3 and 6, derives closed-end axial equilibrium and the plane-stress Poisson relation. Its thin-wall assumptions do not justify silently adopting a thick-wall model or pressure tolerance. Pressure contributes to longitudinal strain through both axial and hoop stress; a bar extension law alone omits the latter. This source is proposed supporting reference, not owner-vetted project acceptance.

[SIMULIA Abaqus Beam Element Library](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEELMRefMap/simaelm-r-beamlibrary.htm), “Section Forces” defines effective axial force as axial force plus external-pressure area force minus internal-pressure area force. This independently supports distinguishing S from Nwall; it is not a numerical oracle for Chirality or authority to copy another product's formulation.

[SIMULIA Distributed Loads](https://docs.software.vt.edu/abaqusv2025/English/SIMACAEPRCRefMap/simaprc-c-loaddistributed.htm), “Pressure on Pipe and Elbow Elements,” states its closed-end load convention, cancellation at ordinary adjacent pipe elements and need for compensating loads when an open end is intended. Applicability: future topology/pressure-boundary tests, not an assumption that Chirality already supports all such boundaries.

No piping-design extracted equations or protected standard tables were used. External sources retrieved 2026-09-07 (local project date); external support is separate from accepted local history.

## Auditable arithmetic and sign/reference table

Local derivation for the current **limited axial bar + cap loads** model: let extension strain be e, thermal strain eT, metal area As and P=pAi. Define tension-positive wall force Nw=EAs(e−eT), effective force S=Nw−P. End nodal actions for wall force are (−Nw,+Nw); current corrected straight actions are (−S,+S). The algebra follows directly from current assembly/recovery, not a full pressure constitutive law. Support reactions must still come from equilibrium of assembled applied loads; replacing their reference by wall force would be wrong.

The existing R `fixtures/pressure.json` has Do=.168 m, t=.007 m, L=2 m, E=200e9 Pa, p=1e6 Pa, node i anchored and j free. `fixtures/pressure_fixed.json` adds the second anchor. Original arithmetic (Python math only, no solver build): Ai=.018626502843133885 m², As=.003540574920595698 m², P=18626.502843133887 N, P/As=5.260869565217391 MPa, PL/EAs=.0526086956521739 mm. Historical `results/pressure_dense.json` confirms .052609 mm extension and zero published axial stresses/forces; `pressure_fixed_dense.json` confirms endpoint +P/−P and ±5.260870 MPa. Current section behaviour is inferred from R08 source and S2, not those historical station values.

| Model/boundary | e or u | Nw (tension+) | S | Current endpoint action i,j | Candidate A section wall stress |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p only; i anchored, j free | u=P L/(EAs) | +P | 0 | 0,0 | +5.260869565 MPa everywhere |
| p only; both ends fixed | u=0 | 0 | −P | +P,−P | 0 everywhere |
| heat + p; both fixed, TP008 invented values | u=0 | −3 N | −12 N | +12,−12 N | −.75 Pa (As=4 m²) |

For pressure-only fixed/fixed, support-on-model actions are +P at i and −P at j under the existing limited model: supports absorb cap thrust; zero wall axial stress does **not** mean zero anchor load. For free/anchored, cap pair is self-equilibrated and support resultant is zero. These proposed expectations do not generalize to supports on the wall away from actual pressure closure, open boundaries, fittings or full Poisson-coupled shells.

At the same fixture geometry current mean-radius longitudinal formula is 5.75 MPa, versus exact cap-area mean wall stress 5.260869565 MPa, a difference .489130435 MPa (~9.2975% relative). This is a geometric-basis difference, not a tolerance comparison. A one-line “unsuppress longitudinal pressure” change would produce 5.75 MPa free and, if applied to signed section S/As, .489130435 MPa fixed instead of zero under this limited law. Endpoint action signs make blind addition still less coherent.

No double counting under A: apply P once in solve; retain thermal eigenstrain subtraction; recover Nw once for wall stress; do not add another p*r/(2t) longitudinal term to that wall stress. If S is also published, derive S=Nw−P with its own explicit meaning. Hoop stress remains a distinct approximate component and is not added to axial wall stress.
