## Pressure recommendation and concrete repair boundary

This is an explicit priority judgment, not a contradiction in the child's evidence. Option A corrects bookkeeping within an axial-bar/cap-load idealization; it omits hoop-Poisson coupling and therefore cannot establish complete pressure-strain behaviour.

Current straight recovery subtracts pressure and thermal equivalent pairs; curved recovery subtracts radial wall loads but retains cap-induced wall tension. This establishes a force-reference inconsistency to resolve, not permission to change a physical model.
