## Decisions and unresolved coverage

- **Friction:** prior-iterate behavior can produce friction acting with observed displacement or exceeding the current normal-force bound; changing normal force produces seed-dependent states. These consequences reinforce the existing policy hold. Owner must decide history/current-normal/convergence semantics before a physical repair.
- **Finite-end user stiffness:** rigid rotation produces nonzero energy/internal moment. Objective finite-end connector and external-frame relative-displacement springs are different contracts. Owner must choose the intended physical interpretation and compatibility treatment.
- **Pressure:** closed-end wall equilibrium and the accepted handcalc's eigenload subtraction conflict; suppression of longitudinal membrane terms compounds the ambiguity. Reconcile force reference/end-cap/wall-stress interpretation before production alteration.
- **Identity/schema:** constant labels have mitigations; durable history and canonical-native compatibility are separate design work. Public schema or migration changes remain Owner-gated.
- **Coverage gaps:** full practical ill-conditioning/rounding envelope, cross-platform determinism, arbitrary curved-contact composition, independent full mixed-support vectors, broader malformed input/resource cases, independent self-weight accuracy and coupled macro thermal/pressure remain unverified. Actual service fault injection now confirms explicitly disclosed browser fallback: default example returns830 fixture rows, edited model blocks; the original native failure is lost and requested versus actual fixture mode differs. App GUI/manifest behavior remains unexecuted, so hidden or misleading GUI substitution is not established. Six product calls independently confirm premature signed-component quantization: factors2 and1000000 yield0 while directly scaled forces produce0.000001mm and0.406023mm respectively in both modes. Preserve full internal arithmetic before existing display rounding; no new precision policy is required for that bounded objective. No remaining gap is converted to PASS.
