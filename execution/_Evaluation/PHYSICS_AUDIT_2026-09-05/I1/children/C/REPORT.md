## Family-level review reasoning

**Materials and units.** Validation checks declared unit dimensions before normalization. Coordinate units come from project metadata; other quantities retain explicit dimensions. Direction vectors are not coordinate positions and are deliberately unscaled. Normalization uses the accepted catalog with no symbol inference; unsupportedGPa/kN rejection is expected. Per-case material resolution copies the base material list, replaces E/G/alpha from an exact point, or sorts temperature points and uses strict adjacent bracketing. Duplicate temperature/extrapolation and missing bracket properties have blocking paths; E/G must remain finite positive. Exact-pointalpha may beNone, creating the reproduced mismatch with pre-selection thermal validation. Fresh exact/interpolatedE and delta-temperature tests plus existingG regressions support a bounded selection verdict; they do not verify every mixed basis or material.

**Sections and mass.** Shared binding resolves exactly onepipe section, allows onlyOD/wall properties, verifies literal cached representation, validates normalized geometry and preserves supplemental mass/tolerance fields. Fresh stale/cache tests exercise the critical disagreement path. Derived mechanics usesnominalwall minus absolute milltolerance and annulus A/I/J/Z. Equivalent-static mass adds metal,contents,insulation using that effective wall; density sign/finite and insulation pair checks fail closed. Selected-pipe selfweight performs its own normalization, endpoint/selection/identity/collision validation, stable sorting and all-or-nothing draft return; it records authored source quantities and sourcehash. Existing selfweight tests cover these branches but reuse the same mass routine; therefore static review plus those tests justify 'assessed with independent mass/geometry gap', not independently verified selfweight accuracy.

**Support parsing and transfer.** Rigid DOF tokens map explicitly; spring quantity dimensions follow translational versus rotational DOF and top-level stiffness precedes hanger stiffness. Nonlinear family parsing demands explicit behavior,DOF,initialstate and behavior-specific sense/gap/friction inputs; simultaneous explicit and derived normalreaction is blocked and source references must resolve. Constant-effort ambiguous shapes are intentionally review-only warnings underDEC049; eligible forces enter the same assembled force seam. The boundary filter excludes nonlinear/constant-effort records before linear preparation. That review led to the rigid-count failure and nonlinear spring omission. Fresh independent gap+spring cases establish the transfer defect; all classifier inequalities/friction-history correctness remain numerical-manager coverage.

**Loads.** Authored nodal/element targets resolve identities, units and quantity category before lower primitive preparation. Supported global axes and DOF tokens differ from the richer canonical local/station records. Straight distributed assembly usesend shares; arc assembly uses the macroelement consistent vector and rejects partialarc rather than chord fallback. Thermal and pressure loads additionally construct axial equivalents fromselectedproperties and effective areas; the thermal builder's silentNone path is now reproduced. Wind/seismic generators require explicit inputs, map per-pipe mass or diameter, retain user direction and emit deterministic identities. Whole-partial/partialoverlap guards were reviewed; duplicate whole references bypass them and duplicategenerated loads accumulate, demonstrated by the independent repeated-selection witness. Mass/shape calculations corroborate generation; lumped response does not independently validate the physical load approximation.
