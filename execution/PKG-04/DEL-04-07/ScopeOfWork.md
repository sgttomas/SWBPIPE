# Scope of Work — DEL-04-07 Product solve integration and nonlinear orchestration

## Purpose and Objective Traceability

This deliverable is the product's solve: the one entry that takes a model document and returns published mechanics results. It reads the model, brings every quantity to consistent units, maps pipes, components, supports and loads onto the solver's elements and load terms, runs each load case as a linear solve or as the nonlinear iteration, and publishes displacements, member actions, member stresses and support loads with their units, diagnostics and the identity of the input they came from. The desktop's Run command and the headless runner both call it. It adds no mechanics of its own: its work is that the primitives are composed correctly, that nothing is silently dropped or assumed on the way, and that one route serves every model.

It serves SOW-005, SOW-011, SOW-012, SOW-052 and SOW-053 under OBJ-003 and OBJ-012, as their product integration only (`execution/_Decomposition/SOFTWARE_DECOMP.md`, PKG-04, row DEL-04-07), and PRD §12.1, §12.2 and §12.8 with FR-SOL-006, FR-SOL-014 and FR-MOD-007, the derivation of a solver-ready analytical model from the model document. In `docs/RELEASE_TARGET_v1.0.md` it owns "one route" of exit criterion 1: the invented reference system solves on the exact pressure route in the desktop with no admitted family refused.

- **OUT-001** — The product solve entry: composition of the model, unit, boundary, section, load and kernel contracts into one solve entry for the desktop and the headless runner, in both solver modes and on both routes, with the published mechanics result and its identity.
- **OUT-002** — The assembled nonlinear iteration.
- **OUT-003** — The product's section and mass adapter.
- **OUT-004** — Diagnostics, units and provenance carried through composition to the result.

## Deliverable Definition — Ontology

**Terms the solve implements.**

- *Solve request.* A model document with its materials and the cases to solve, in one solver mode. A result binds the exact request it was computed from.
- *Routes.* A document without a pressure contract takes the pressure-free route (PF) and carries no pressure. A document with the pressure contract `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` takes the exact route (EX). There is one current pressure contract; any other is refused by name, a zero-pressure document included.
- *Pressure on EX.* Pressure is authored in regions. A region is one chain of members between two terminals, each terminal either transferring its end load to the pipe wall or separately supported or compensated. Pressure enters a case as loads only: the end loads of each member's wall, the terminal end loads, the remainders at bends and joints and, in a case that includes pressure elongation, the free pressure strain. A node where terminals of two or more regions meet, each transferring to the wall, is a pressure junction; its end loads sum to the fluid force on the fitting, −Σ p·A·s over the incident members, whatever the fitting's angles or bores.
- *Pressure elongation.* The closed-end free pressure strain of a member, ε_p = (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s), with A_i on the pressure bore and A_s the nominal section area; it is not a thin-wall mean-diameter form. The model setting `model_settings.pressure_elongation` names the operating load cases that include it. The solve has no case kinds, so the user names the cases.
- *What pressure does not include.* Steady-flow and transient fluid loads, the opening of a bend under pressure, pressure stiffening of flexibility and stress-intensification factors, and ovalization are excluded and disclosed on results. Users enter their own factors.
- *Action.* What a load case applies: a primitive load, an equivalent-static load, a non-empty pressure-region list, a boundary motion, a member state of non-zero free strain, or a preload present in every case (a constant-effort support, a tie rod with a non-zero reference extension, or a connector with a non-zero reference load).
- *Built model.* The solver-ready analytical model the solve derives from the model document: its elements, load terms, supports and prescribed values in consistent units. Derived analyses read it.
- *Nonlinear iteration.* For a model with nonlinear supports: assemble, solve, classify the supports, update the active set, and repeat until the classifier reports convergence or the iteration cap is reached. A sliding friction support's bounded force is applied in the system from the previous iterate's normal reaction.
- *Admissibility confirmation.* Once the iteration has converged, the check that the converged state satisfies DEL-04-04's state law, each support's deciding quantity strictly on its state's side, with its numerical error taken into account.
- *Held-status solve.* One solve of a case with each nonlinear support's boundary formed from a given state, not iterated: what is held is each support's state (closed or open, stuck or sliding, on or off its seat), never its displacement or force. The case's own loads, prescribed values and motion apply.
- *Memory budget.* One solve may use at most a per-invocation budget: 12 GiB on the 16 GiB base machine, with larger qualified budgets on larger machines. All solves in the desktop share one pool, nominally 75 % of physical memory. A larger pool needs the user's explicit acknowledgement and never exceeds physical memory.
- *Section and mass adapter.* The product's own computation of a pipe's metal area, second moments, torsion constant and mass per length from the user's outside diameter, wall, mill tolerance, corrosion allowance and densities.

**Boundary.** Mathematical primitives, convergence policy and engineering acceptance stay with their owners; a product caller or a file's location does not move them.

- DEL-04-01: frame assembly and linear solving, residual acceptance, the solve integrity check with its established recovery and its evaluation on a converged nonlinear state, and the enclosure of the normal-stress maximum along a realized arc. Its code also carries the element families this deliverable composes (see `deliverable.yaml`).
- DEL-04-02: the straight pipe element. DEL-04-03: linear supports and imposed movement. DEL-04-04: the per-iteration classification, the friction law and the state law the admissibility confirmation checks.
- DEL-04-06: what a diagnostic says, including the classification of a condition estimate against warning and failure levels. DEL-05-04: analysis status semantics.
- DEL-05-01 and DEL-05-05: load values and member loads; DEL-05-01 also defines the hydrostatic test condition and what a held support is. DEL-05-02: combinations and the held-status subtrahend. DEL-05-03: stress recovery, the stress rows, member maxima and headlines, and the two section bases. DEL-03-08: the section and mass calculation this adapter is held to.
- DEL-03-03 to DEL-03-06: component definitions. DEL-02-01: the model document. DEL-02-02: units.
- DEL-04-08: retained-precision publication. DEL-04-09, DEL-04-10 and DEL-04-11: modal, response-spectrum and hanger-sizing entries, which sit beside this one and use its built model. DEL-04-10 also defines the snubber as a restraint in dynamic analysis only.
- DEL-07-07: the desktop's solve job, which calls this entry in a worker.
- DEL-10-04: the Windows build and packaging. Windows is not a release platform for v1.0; this deliverable holds the Windows conditions of its own admission and worker custody (REQ-013).

## Completion and Reliance Basis — Epistemology

A published result is a numerical statement about the discrete model in the request. Agreement between entries or solver modes shows consistent composition; it is not numerical validation.

### Requirements

- **CLM-001** — *One entry.* The accepted model, unit, boundary, section, load and kernel contracts are composed into solve entries for the desktop and the headless runner. The built model is derived from the model document deterministically, a result preserves the identity of its input, and every missing or unsupported input, and every document member the built model cannot represent, is an explicit, named diagnostic (PRD FR-MOD-007). Every support type and load type the model admits (the supports, springs and imposed displacements of SOW-011, the nonlinear supports of SOW-012, and the concentrated forces, concentrated moments and distributed loads of SOW-052) is applied on both routes or refused by name, never dropped silently. A snubber is composed as DEL-04-10/REQ-005 defines it: every static load-case solve leaves it out and discloses it. The same model, solver version and settings give byte-identical results and diagnostics through the desktop and the headless entry (PRD §12.2, §12.8).
- **CLM-002** — *Nonlinear iteration.* The assembled nonlinear iteration runs with frame assembly and linear solving left to DEL-04-01 and per-iteration classification left to DEL-04-04, under the governed convergence tolerances and iteration caps, and reports non-convergence. After convergence it runs the admissibility confirmation once on the converged state and publishes the case's standing as DEL-04-04/REQ-013 states it: a state the confirmation establishes is published as confirmed, with the rows established at that state; a case on which the confirmation does not run, or which it does not establish, carries its named reason, and its state is never presented as confirmed.
- **CLM-003** — *Section and mass adapter.* The product's section and mass conversion is held to DEL-03-08's contracts, with the effect of corrosion allowance and mill tolerance assessed explicitly, and an unsupported section input refused or disclosed.
- **CLM-004** — *Nothing lost in composition.* Typed diagnostics, units and provenance are preserved across composition and into the result. A singular, ill-conditioned or non-converged solve publishes the corresponding diagnostic and no unqualified result (SOW-053). Ill-conditioning is raised by classifying the condition estimate of each solve's reduced system as DEL-04-06/REQ-008 defines: beyond the warning level the result carries the ill-conditioning warning, beyond the failure level the solve publishes the blocking diagnostic and no result marked solved. A constant-effort support that a solve consumed publishes the force the solve applied, on its positive global degree of freedom, with zero in the other components. A constant-effort support the solve does not consume applies and publishes no force and carries a named warning stating the unmet condition.
- **REQ-005** — *One route for every model.* The exact route admits every family the pressure-free route admits, so that a model with pressure needs no other route: straight pipe, realized bends, tees with user modifiers, concentric and eccentric reducers, valves and flanges, expansion joints with and without tie rods, linear restraints and springs, variable springs, constant-effort supports, gap, lift-off and friction supports, and imposed displacements. The pressure-free route gets no pressure. The exact route refuses no family the pressure-free route admits; each refusal that remains is named and listed as a release limitation (release target exit criterion 3).
- **REQ-006** — *Pressure boundary.* Only the current pressure contract is solved. On the exact route only a realized bend carrying the user's flexibility factor carries pressure; a geometry-only bend is refused there with a message to realize it. A terminal that is separately supported or compensated cannot be part of a pressure junction and is refused by name. Unequal pressures at a junction, and a pipe at a junction outside every region, are admitted as a closure at the fitting, each with its own warning. The exclusions above are stated on the results they affect.
- **REQ-007** — *No silent zero.* A load case whose stored loads are not applied, and which applies no other action, is refused by name. A model in which no case applies an action is refused by name. An empty case among loaded cases is published with a named warning saying that its result is all zero. A reference state of a load-state document is published as zero without a finding. No case's all-zero result is published without one of these.
- **REQ-008** — *Memory budget.* A solve is admitted only within the per-invocation budget and the shared pool, and a solve that would exceed them is refused by name instead of run. A larger budget admits no larger retained-precision domain (DEL-04-08/REQ-004). A base budget other than 12 GiB on the 16 GiB machine, or another pool policy, is the owner's choice; work on this deliverable does not make it.
- **REQ-009** — *Pressure elongation (Should).* On the exact route a model may include pressure elongation, the closed-end free pressure strain (1 − 2ν)·p·A_i/(E·A_s), in the operating load cases it names and in no other. This is the effect the Bourdon flag of a CAEPIPE model file switches; the opening of a bend under pressure, which some texts also call the Bourdon effect, is not modelled. It is off by default: with the setting absent no case includes it, and nothing is inferred from a case's kind. Leaving it out removes the free pressure strain only; pressure thrust at unbalanced locations is applied in both settings, and the wall force N_w = N_el + p·A_i and the effective force S = N_el hold in both. The setting is labelled where it is set. The result of each case that has pressure regions states whether it includes pressure elongation; a case without pressure regions states nothing, having no pressure to elongate. A result's limitations carry exactly one of three fixed statements, the one true of the document: that no case includes pressure elongation; that it is included only in the operating cases the setting names, with its formula; or that it is set but none of the cases it names carries pressure, so it has no effect. A setting on a document without a pressure contract, a named case the document does not have, and an empty or duplicated list are each refused by name. A stored result computed before the setting existed (`pressure-2`) is refused by name with a message to solve the model again; nothing is migrated.
- **REQ-010** — *Stress publication.* On both routes each solved case publishes, for every member, the stress rows, member maxima and headlines DEL-05-03 defines, each naming its section basis (on the exact route, the nominal and the reduced basis), each formed from the recovery DEL-04-01/REQ-015 assigns to its case, and along a realized arc the normal-stress maximum enclosed as DEL-04-01/REQ-017 states. Publication preserves that recovery: on a case whose rows are certified, no stress row or straight member's maximum is recomputed from the published binary64 actions, which are publication products and not new exact sources. Two kinds are formed from published rows on every case: DEL-05-03's section quantities, by its fixed sequences over the published rows, and a realized arc's normal-stress and directional intensified bending maxima, enclosed from the arc's published end actions as DEL-04-01/REQ-017 states.
- **REQ-011** — *Held supports and generated sources.* In a case that holds supports under DEL-05-01's test condition, each spring, variable spring and constant-effort support is replaced on the degrees of freedom it acts on by a rigid restraint at zero displacement from the authored geometry. Neither its stiffness nor its force acts, and a held constant-effort support publishes no applied force. The replacement's reaction, from the complete equations of the replaced system, is published under the support's own identity. The case's assembly, solve, recovery and publication all use the same replacement; one that cannot be formed (contradicting or duplicate records, a wrong degree of freedom or position) is refused by name with no partial result. On a load and reference-state document each generated load source enters the case's record of load sources exactly once, with its identity and factor, so that a hydrostatic test case and static seismic cases solve in one document. Bringing a test case's contents density into its self-weight is DEL-05-01's.
- **REQ-012** — *Held-status solve.* On request a case is solved once with every nonlinear support held at a given state assignment, the final states of a source case, as DEL-05-02/REQ-013 asks for its held-status subtrahend. The held solve classifies each support once from its own solution and reports each support whose held state that solution would release, without altering it. It runs the admissibility confirmation once and publishes the standing as CLM-002 states. A hold that cannot be formed (a sliding support whose ground differs between the two cases, a gap position that cannot be formed exactly, a support with no held state) is refused by name and publishes nothing.
- **REQ-013** — *Windows admission and custody.* Windows is not a release platform for v1.0. A Windows implementation of this deliverable's memory admission and of a solve worker's custody keeps the budget, the pool policy and every refusal of REQ-008, and also: memory sampling bounds both available physical memory and available commit, and a failed, partial or stale reading refuses instead of supplying a normal sample; a worker is under the custody that ends it with its parent from before it can compute, so that a failure to place it there refuses the job before computation and no parent death leaves it running; cancellation, parent death and startup failure end the worker and every process it started, or report a survivor that cannot be confirmed ended; and footprint and commit accounting are conservative for the Windows allocator and page size, with no macOS measurement reused for Windows.

## Production and Verification Method — Praxeology

**Method choices that define the solve.**

- The nonlinear method is an active-set fixed-point iteration around assemble, solve, classify and update. Relaxation and anti-chatter measures are implementation choices inside the iteration; tolerances and caps are not.
- Convergence uses the governed per-class record of relative tolerance, absolute floor and iteration cap. An unmeasured entry keeps the tolerance-policy-undecided warning active, and a fixture override may only tighten.
- Sparse is the default solver mode and dense scrutiny is selectable. Both go through the same composition.
- Pressure enters as case-constant loads, so the nonlinear iteration takes it exactly as it takes thermal loads, and nothing in the iteration depends on displacement through pressure.
- A pressure junction is recognised from the regions that meet at a node. The model document has no junction token.
- The pressure load cancellation screen refuses a case (`PRESSURE_ASSEMBLY_CANCELLATION_UNRESOLVED`) when 32 ε times the largest per-degree-of-freedom sum of pressure source magnitudes exceeds 10⁻⁹ of the largest assembled translational pressure load, because a response driven by a load smaller than the rounding of the terms that formed it has no accuracy relative to itself. One family is excepted: a case without pressure elongation whose regions hold straight members only, with no connector, in which every pressure term belongs to a cap pair, either a member's own pair at a transferring terminal, which cancels exactly, or the pair of two consecutive members of one region's chain at the node where they meet. Those interior nodes are taken from each region's member chain, never from the terms formed, so each is examined whether or not it carries a term. On an axis where a member's direction as formed from its node coordinates is exactly zero, its published pressure groups contain no term for that component. When a nonzero source component underflows during direction formation, the producer still retains a zero-valued term and its closure operands internally; a formed zero therefore does not imply equality of the recorded coordinates on that axis. The product solves the recorded geometry; it does not claim that the recorded geometry is what the person intended. In the recorded geometry, bore and alignment differences between adjacent members within the region's representation limits (64 ε of the bore radius against the region's first member, 64 ε of the span from the chord between its terminals) produce a real pressure thrust at interior nodes, and the rounding of each member's separately formed direction and bore area leaves a remainder even where the recorded members are exactly collinear and equal in bore. The product does not tell the two sources apart. Inside the family the screen does not operate, and the case is solved with the remainder retained as a pressure load, not with the load set to zero, so that each published load remains the sum of its published terms. The exception claims nothing about the size of the response the remainder drives, and nothing in the product bounds that response. The remainder at each interior node is checked only for consistency with what the representation limits and the rounding admit, by a bound derived from them on the nodal force; this is a check on the evidence, not on the remainder's effect, and every case the region's limits admit is expected to pass it. The standing of the result is whatever the solve integrity check gives on the premise that check records for its own bound (DEL-04-01/REQ-015); nothing here adds to that check's assurance or supplies its premise. A response driven by the remainder alone can be appreciable and published with established standing. An independent review built two admitted cases in which a retained remainder of a fraction of a newton drives a correctly solved displacement of 17 mm (a steel run of 10¹¹ m) and of 3.5 mm (a 1,000 m run of elastic modulus 10⁻¹² Pa). Both lie far from piping practice in length or stiffness; that is an observation about these two cases and places no restriction on what is admitted. The result keeps the screen's value as computed and records the exception with the node, members, lengths, span, measured remainder, its force in newtons and the bound. A published case carries the warning `PRESSURE_REGION_CAP_REMAINDER_RETAINED` whatever its standing, and a case that is not published carries neither. The warning names the case and the node at which the remainder is largest relative to the pressure force on the bore, gives the force there in newtons and says that the force is not a bound on the resulting displacement. The node is chosen by relative size, so a remainder at another node can be larger in newtons. A reader without the model checks that this evidence agrees with itself, including that each pair of consecutive published members of a region is found at exactly one interior node. Only a reader given the model checks it against the model: it takes the interior nodes from the model's region members and re-forms each node's remainder from the model's node coordinates and pressure, with the bore radius taken from the published geometry. Neither reader detects the removal of a member's own cap pair at a transferring terminal, whose terms sum to zero. One member for each straight run leaves no interior node and so no remainder; equal bores and collinear nodes reduce it to rounding. Every case outside the family keeps the screen unchanged, and a case the screen admits does none of the exception's work. This is a practical resolution of a matter of precision within normative engineering practice.
- A load term the integrity check cannot re-form is not published as established: the case fails closed with a named reason.
- Derived analyses (modal, response spectrum, hanger sizing) read the built model; they do not re-implement composition.

**How each criterion is examined.**

- **VER-001** — Comparison with named references, with an executable check: the same accepted and invalid models are traced through each entry; the check observes identical results, input identity and refusals, and no bypass of primitive validation. The references are `validation/references/w9_benchmarks` and the other benchmark packages of PRD §22.2, generated without product code.
- **VER-002** — Comparison with named references, with an executable check: linear and nonlinear switching, converged and non-converged cases and the classifier hand-offs are exercised against `validation/references/rt_a3_nonlinear`, `rt_a3_s6`, `kd5_nonlinear` and `w16_nonlinear_motion`; the check observes the non-convergence failure on every non-converged exit and the governed cap and tolerances.
- **VER-003** — Comparison with named references: the adapter against closed-form annulus geometry and against DEL-03-08's calculator, for geometry, corrosion allowance, mill tolerance, density and mass, with any discrepancy recorded.
- **VER-004** — Executable check, with a comparison with a named reference: observes valid and invalid inputs and their diagnostics through each mode to the result, the classification of a product solve's condition estimate against supplied warning and failure levels, and the unit on every published quantity; the published constant-effort action is compared with `validation/references/rt_a3_constant_effort`.

## Governing Values and Decisions — Axiology

- **AX-001** — *One route.* Better is fewer families refused on the exact route; the target is none that the reference system needs. Admission is never bought by solving a family approximately in place of its definition, or by putting pressure on the pressure-free route.
- **AX-002** — *A silent wrong result is the worst failure.* Better is fewer paths on which a load, a member or a diagnostic can be dropped without a named refusal. Convenience, speed and a shorter result never justify an unannounced zero or default.
- **AX-003** — *Compose, do not re-derive.* Better is less mechanics in this deliverable. A formula duplicated here from a primitive is a defect, even where it is faster.
- **AX-004** — *Within the machine.* Better is a larger model solved within the budget. This is never bought by exceeding the budget unannounced or by weakening a numerical check.
