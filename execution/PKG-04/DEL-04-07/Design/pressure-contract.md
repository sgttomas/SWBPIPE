# Pressure contract and T4 integration

## Current pressure boundary

The owner retired `1.0.0/legacy_pressure_v1` product-wide in #1168. It is not accepted even for zero-pressure documents. The flawed computation, its historical bypass and dependent pressure oracles are removed. A pressure-free model is a document without a pressure contract. On 2026-10-10 the owner decided that each format keeps only its current version ("as you recommended, no-compatibility principle carries"), reversing the 2026-10-08 choice of model formats 0.1.0/0.2.0 as the pressure-free namespace: B7 defines one model format, 0.5.0 (the version chosen in DEL-05-02 Design), that expresses models with or without pressure, and refuses every older version by name ([DEL-05-02 Design, b7-plan.md](../../../PKG-05/DEL-05-02/Design/b7-plan.md)). Until B7 lands, the code still treats 0.1.0/0.2.0 as the pressure-free documents. Any pressure primitive in a non-exact document, including zero, requires re-authoring (`PRESSURE_MODEL_REAUTHOR_REQUIRED`); authoring uses `OP-PRESSURE-PRIMITIVE-RETIRED`.

Pressure on the current exact route is limited to straight pipe and linear supports, without components, combinations or `equivalent_static`. T4 extends that route; it does not revive the legacy computation. Existing PRD/INTENT pressure requirements remain desired scope, not claims of present capability. T4 rebuilds the curved-pressure and membrane validation removed with the flawed computation.

## Accepted T4 direction

- **D-1:** the L-line (T4-U2) is the first usable path; the corrected joint (T4-U3) proceeds in parallel.
- **D-2:** only realized bends carrying the user's k carry pressure. Geometry-only chord bends are refused on the exact route with a realization message and remain pressure-free.
- **D-3:** steady-flow/transient loads, Bourdon opening, pressure stiffening of k/SIF and ovalization are excluded and disclosed on bend results and the v3 approximation. T4-U9 requires a later owner choice.
- **H-1:** all new authoring writes `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` with reserved `pressure-1` semantics, straight-only documents included (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-10, revising the earlier "straight-only documents continue to be authored as v2"). Until T3 joins v3 to retained-source recovery (a priority T3 unit), a v3 case that v2 would route to retained-source recovery carries the named diagnostic `EXACT_PRESSURE_V3_RETAINED_SOURCE_NOT_JOINED`; that is the temporary honest state, not a reason to author v2. v2 `2.0.0/exact_straight_pressure_v2` stays accepted and byte-identical, except the declared p < 0 refusal, only until B7b deletes it; no new v2-specific behaviour is added before then. HELP_HUMAN's ruling D4 (a) (2026-10-10) rebases W1's exact route E onto `pressure-1` inside B7, after which v3 is the only exact contract (DEL-05-02 Design, `b7-plan.md`). The applier and desktop switch to writing v3 in T4-U2's authoring step; until it lands, the desktop still offers v2.
- **SP-1 (transitional):** a straight-only v3 case is bit-equal to its v2 twin in its ordinary-route publication. This is a transitional check that lasts while v2 exists. It applies to v3 cases with zero mill tolerance, zero corrosion allowance and the Euler–Bernoulli formulation (D-5/D-6, DEL-05-03 Design). Retained-source publications are outside the twin clause (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-10).
- **v2 retirement:** T3's B7b deletes `2.0.0/exact_straight_pressure_v2` (its contract, admission branch, the readers' v2 acceptance, fixtures and the SP-1 twin tests); a v2 document is then refused by name, with no migration code (DEL-05-02 Design, `b7-plan.md`).
- **H-2:** retain the pre-cancelled ledger and add member-owned bend `K_b·u_free(ε_p) − c_b` and joint `p(Ae−Ai)` terms. One implementation owns the assembly. T4-U5 must independently compare representation (A), including JR's tie-force double-count control.

## T4-U1b and T4-U2a as implemented

- **T4-U1b (certified arc loads, R-1):** S11-G certifies consistent load vectors on realized arcs with the proven midpoint–radius enclosure of T4-I9 revision 01 (directed binary64 radii; `Exact { scale: 1.0, scaled_intended: split(m) }`; no FK type, priced-layout or PINNED_RECORD change). Every failed precondition stays CannotBound. The arc load vector uses exact-rational series below the s < ½ switch. T15c's natural refusal stays at guard level: no model in the k window gives an ordinary Passed report, so the end-to-end CannotBound sentence is not shown (R-2's narrowing, ruled by HELP_HUMAN; DEL-04-01 Design).
- **T4-U2a (seam and v3 identity):** `PP/src/exact_admission.rs` classifies every family per contract; v2 keeps its codes and texts; under `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` every family not yet admitted is refused with `EXACT_PRESSURE_FAMILY_NOT_ADMITTED`. v3 publishes under `pressure-1` with its own limitations (D-3 exclusions; collapse not assessed) and admits signed pressure. The three readers dispatch on the contract and refuse cross-reads by name. Pressure-1 export is refused by name until T4-U2. The `pressure-1` table is not a reviewed input.
- **SP-1's twin clause** holds on the ordinary route (v2 typed and its v3 twin are bit-equal in every row). v2 documents that select retained-source recovery publish the ordinary route under v3, because pressure-1 has no retained-source counterpart. HELP_HUMAN scoped the clause to ordinary-route publications as a transitional check (SP-1 above).
- **Missing-join diagnostic** (the temporary state H-1 names). On the captured entry, when a model does not join retained-source recovery (`exact_admission::joins_retained_source`), PP adds `EXACT_PRESSURE_V3_RETAINED_SOURCE_NOT_JOINED` to each case that is retained-source eligible and needs recovery: a Sensitive report, a refused attempt or a load-row finding. These are exactly the cases v2's captured entry attempts. The diagnostic is `info`, names the case and has static text. It appears whether the case publishes or is left `MODEL_INCOMPLETE` (n06, which v2 rescues). The typed entry attempts no retained-source recovery under either contract and does not carry it.

## T4-U2 authoring and the desktop reader as implemented

- **Authoring writes v3 only.** `pressure_profile` writes `3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3` (0.3.0, or the contract only on 0.4.0) and refuses a v2 write (`OP-PRESSURE-PROFILE-SUCCESSOR-REQUIRED`); a v2 document stays solvable and is upgraded only by selecting the profile again. Blank 0.4.0 documents declare v3. Regions take signed pressure under v3, refuse a geometry-only bend's span ("realize this bend") and check each realized-bend end against its region neighbour with CB's `kink` and α_tan, as the solve does.
- **Bends (T4-I11 D-A, D-B, D-G).** `insert_bend_at_corner` places the tangent points exactly (L_t = R·tan(Φ/2)), shortens both straights, adds the arc span with y_reference = u_in and a realized bend carrying the user's k, or converts a marker at the corner in place. `bend_realization` writes the solver consumption, k and its source on a span bend whose arc forms, refusing corner markers, an inconsistent angle and an end kink above α_tan at a single-neighbour end. An agent never writes k. Bend creation no longer takes `bend_plane_orientation`.
- **Desktop.** The inspector realizes bends and inserts them at corners; one objective-connector form, offered on v3 documents only, replaces the legacy four-rate joint forms. The desktop does not read pressure-1: such a result is refused as `PRESSURE_1_RESULT_NOT_READABLE_IN_DESKTOP` on the solve job, results panel, standing, notices and every output surface. Rust reads pressure-1 (Python as a test verifier).
- **First usable path.** The L line authored from its corner through the panels equals the applier's document (`fixtures/model_operations/pressure_v3_l_line_authoring.json`); it solves under pressure-1 in both modes in PP and HR and agrees with T4-I7's hand-placed document.
