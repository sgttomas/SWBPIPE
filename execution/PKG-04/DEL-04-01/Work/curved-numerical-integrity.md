# Curved-element numerical integrity

Current requirements for the T4 curved-element replacement. The section "T4-U1 as implemented" records how T4-U1 meets them; it is not an acceptance claim.

Under N-2 (K-D5 forms from (R, y), with no binary64 centre, derived radius or φ) and CB's relative plane admissibility (`AXIS_TOLERANCE` = 1e-12·|y|), the mutant differs from the correct check by at most 2e-8 of the criterion over the whole admissible domain, so no required test can kill it. Basis: T4-I6 round 01's corrected derivation, with T4-RV10's C-1 to C-4 applied, and T4-RV10's independent check. The equivalence depends on CB's relative tolerance; a test pins it (`curved_bend` `axis_tolerance_tests`), and loosening it reopens this ruling. The two former M31b0 kill tests, whose preconditions no longer hold, are deleted.

K1 must exercise the stable T4-U1 form at φ = 1e-8, extending the earlier 1e-4 acceptance floor. If the stable form moves to T4-U1c, K1 moves with it. K2 remains a supplemental mutation check; it **does not satisfy** the conditioning-driven curved-demotion requirement. Construct a curved true positive (RV131's candidate is F122 realized as a bend, R = 10 m, φ ≈ 0.30 rad). If no such positive can be constructed, seek a narrowing rather than silently substituting K2. Build K2 from the frozen reference or K-D5's p = 128 re-formation and verify the claim that it also kills M31a.

For CSKEW_8_5, agree the rule with T3 before running: no Passed breach in either mode; demote exactly when actual > 0.5, with a ±0.05 guard. Substitute CSKEW_9 or CSKEW_10 instead of relaxing the condition. The kernel's curved-positive controls must reflect this requirement. Regenerate the old models from their bow vectors.

## Stable small-angle evaluation

Use the stable form **1 − cos φ = 2s² at p = 128**, rather than raising precision, in K-D5. Include a kernel check at L = 30 m: long nearly straight bends (φ ≤ 2e-9) otherwise produce false demotions. The product element uses stable binary64 series/half-angle evaluation, retains the existing 1e-9 rad floor and introduces no minimum-angle refusal. Its switch must be continuous and bitwise deterministic. Keep Sensitive demotion as the fallback.

Acceptance also requires guard and K-D5 margin from φ = 1e-4 rad to π − ε at both ordinary and UTM coordinates, and I9's probe rerun on the product. K1's φ = 1e-8 requirement above extends this acceptance floor; it does not replace the full-range check.

If the stable form needs more than about two days, or a T3 re-agreement beyond T4-U1's own, land T4-U1 as planned with (c) disclosed and follow with T4-U1c before T4-U2 merges. The conditioning-driven true-positive demotion requirement remains in force.

**Short stiff bends** (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-10). The stable form removes K-D5's false demotions; it cannot remove genuine ill-conditioning. A bend whose arc length is small against its neighbouring members (Rφ/L, L the shorter adjacent member) is a short stiff element whose chord twin behaves identically. The solver's own report is Sensitive below about 6e-3. Above that, a short bend's verdict is the unified member predicate's outcome on its certified rows ([kd5-member-relative-bound.md](kd5-member-relative-bound.md)); the former short-bend rule (R-1 option (c), a Passed limit of 1.6e-2, [arc-recovery-guard.md](arc-recovery-guard.md), "Short bends") is removed on the integrated batch-4 branch after M3 (kd5 §4.3). Below 1e-4 a pivot-screen refusal names the bend with `CURVED_BEND_SHORT_RELATIVE_TO_NEIGHBOURS`, identifying the bend and the length ratio and saying the bend is too short relative to its neighbours for a reliable solution. Series composition with a neighbour is not committed. T4-I22's measurements on the L line (R = 0.2286 m, 3 m/4 m straights), re-measured on the implementing head: solver Passed at 6.6e-3 (5°), Sensitive at 5.3e-3 (4°) down to 7.6e-5 (1e-3 rad), refused from 5.3e-5 (dense) or 4.6e-5 (sparse).

**Open defect on main (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-11).** T4-RV29 and T4-RV30 (90,240 runs) showed that Passed above these thresholds is not assured. Short stiff members, curved or straight, can publish a Passed result that breaches the 1e-9 criterion, because solve error is amplified through the member's end-relative motion, which depends on neighbouring members rather than on Rφ/L. Separately, R-b′'s resolution conjunct can mask gross errors on straight members: a 10 × 1 m skew cantilever publishes Passed at 6e5–7e5 times the criterion. The ruled repair is the K-D5 member-relative bound, option (d): certified member rows recovered at p, with the formed loads folded into D5C-1 ([kd5-member-relative-bound.md](kd5-member-relative-bound.md)). It reached main in #1304 and supersedes this section's thresholds (below); before it, Passed on short stiff members and on small rows of straight members was not a certified claim.

O-3 accepts Lemma 3 with no `CRITERION` change. O-4 makes this `atan_positive`'s first product caller, with N-2's test and A-6's near-π treatment. These are the RV129 O-3/O-4 dispositions, distinct from the M31b0 N-1/N-2 conditions above.

**Superseded by the K-D5 member-relative bound unit** ([kd5-member-relative-bound.md](kd5-member-relative-bound.md)). HELP_HUMAN's ruling of 2026-10-11 (option (d)) supersedes "my short-bend thresholds (ruling 5, plus the withdrawn (c) limit)" and "the t15b 1e-4 rad acceptance", to be named "in the DEL-04-01 Design as superseded by this unit". On the integrated batch-4 branch these are no longer rules (removed after M3, kd5 §4.3):

## T4-U1 as implemented

- **Element.** CB forms the bend from (x_i, x_j, R, y_reference) only: no absolute centre; c from 4R² − |d|² formed from exact products. φ comes from K3a's `atan_positive` at p = 128 rounded once, so the formation path calls no platform libm and its bits are the same on every platform. The cancelling flexibility integrals use exact-rational series below the switch s < 1/2 (a libm-free comparison) and half-angle closed forms above; the jump at the switch is ≤ 5e-15 relative. Before the guarded tip inversion F is scaled by one power of two, so short arcs form down to the 1e-9 rad floor without changing any element that formed before. PP drops the `bend_plane_orientation` requirement (D-B) on every route; B7b deleted the `2.0.0/exact_straight_pressure_v2` contract and with it the SP-1 exception that kept the earlier condition on v2 documents.
- **Global-frame assembly (T4-I33, T4-RV11 N-1).** The element has no local 12×12. The tip stiffness K_t = F⁻¹ is rotated to global (Aᵀ K_t A, symmetrized) and assembled with H over the global chord d = x_j − x_i. The former Tᵀ K T rotation carried rigid motions through binary64 axes orthonormal only to rounding and left P1 up to 4.5e-11 on small-angle chords near a coordinate plane. P1 ≤ 1e-11 is now asserted on P5's 10,000 seeded elbows and on RV11 N-1's four elbows. The consistent uniform-load vector uses the same K_t rotated to global and the same H(d). SA's symmetry-formation trace re-forms these stages and refuses if its bits differ from the element's. The arc-load certificate and K-D5's p = 128 re-formation enclose the same real element and are unchanged. Under this assembly, RV8-1's XY-plane tee no longer distinguished the order of the bend blocks, so its geometry is turned out of the coordinate planes. RV8-BLOCK-ORDER is still killed by that test.
- **K-D5.** `CurvedFormation` carries (R, y_reference); φ and the chord are formed at p = 128 from (d, R, y) with 1 − cos φ = 2s² (N-2 holds by construction). The L = 30 m kernel test kills a reversion to the cancelling form.
- **Kills.** M31b is killed by K1/K1F (SA and PP at UTM) and K2 (FK, frozen binary64 system matrix, fl(cos φ_b) = 1 pinned); M31a by K2 with `shared` from the scattered matrix. M31b0 is ruled equivalent (above); its former kill tests are deleted.
- **Conditioning-driven curved positive.** Selected by the pre-registered rule (first candidate in T4-I6 round 01's rank order that publishes Passed ordinarily and is demoted in both modes with actual > 0.55 and EF/actual within 1e-3; the others stay D5C-1 controls): `C122-R10-Y100` (F122's 3 m member realized at R = 10 m, y = (1,0,0)), actual 1.859 dense / 1.147 sparse. It replaces CSKEW_8_5 in NI's parity and ledger-split tests.
- **CSKEW_8_5** runs as an undemoted D5C-1 control (actual 0.272 dense / 0.219 sparse); no substitution was needed.
- **Small direction changes.** The stable form meets the guard and K-D5 margin from 1e-4 rad when the arc is not much shorter than its neighbours. A standard-radius bend of a few degrees between metre-scale straights is a short, stiff element beside soft ones: the global solve is genuinely ill-conditioned (≈0.67·(Rφ/L)³), the ordinary report is Sensitive below roughly Rφ/L ≈ 6e-3, a Passed report above it is judged by the unified member predicate on certified rows (O-13's short-bend rule, which demoted a Passed case below 1.6e-2, is removed on the integrated batch-4 branch), and the pivot screen refuses below roughly 5e-5. A straight chord element of the same length behaves identically. HELP_HUMAN accepted these limits (Short stiff bends, above). When FK's pivot screen (`screen_pivot`, unchanged) refuses a case on the ordinary linear route, PP adds the blocking `CURVED_BEND_SHORT_RELATIVE_TO_NEIGHBOURS` if the model's realized bend with the smallest ratio Rφ/L (L the shorter adjacent member: a straight's chord or a neighbouring bend's arc) is below 1e-4. That is the measured refusal boundary with a factor-of-two margin for the screen's constants. The diagnostic names the case, component and pipe and states the ratio. The nonlinear-support route does not add it.

## Certified arc loads

R-1 replaces CannotBound for certified curved consistent-load terms with the proven midpoint–radius enclosure; the guard still demotes when the certified defect including radius exceeds the criterion. After RV129, R-1 remains in force **conditional on revision 01 incorporating A-1–A-6 and RV129 confirming it**. Failure to close the directed-rounding omission makes R-1 lapse.

The amendments require directed rounding of binary64 radii (lower-bound denominators, upward constants/sums and subnormal allowance), tests for all nine failed-precondition classes, and the unchanged priced layout: `Exact { scale: 1.0, scaled_intended: split(m) }` with operand bound r plus γ₅ for generated loads. No new FK type or PINNED_RECORD change. Re-derive T15d/MU2 after the small-bend change and use a sharpened L5 bound or angle reflection near π. Failed preconditions remain CannotBound.

R-2 requires a natural refusal for T15c/M16 first. Probe k ∈ {6e35, 1e36, 1e37, 1e38}. A test seam is a last resort needing a narrowing; retaining only the natural guard-level test also needs the stated narrowing if the end-to-end claim is lost. **R-2 narrowed (HELP_HUMAN, 2026-10-10):** no model with k in {6e35, 1e36, 1e37, 1e38}, nor RV15's parallel-path probe, reaches an ordinary Passed report. T15c therefore stays natural at guard level, with no test seam. The end-to-end CannotBound statement is not claimed.
