# Corrected joint and legacy refusal

**D-4:** refuse both legacy joint populations with `LEGACY_FINITE_CONNECTOR_REAUTHOR_REQUIRED`: flexibility joints and app-authored joints previously solved as pipe. No automatic conversion. Explicit `not_solver_consumed` annotation remains disclosed on every pressure-free result. Annotation-only joints are refused on the exact route by the seam's named refusal (v2 and its composition refusal were deleted with B7b), since treating pressurized bellows as pipe loses thrust.

**H-3 / M07 option A:** delete the flawed user-stiffness element and plumbing in the change that lands the live mechanical connector, T4-U3 (J-A plus J-B). Keep no historical copy. Until replacement, no model may silently skip an incomplete or unmapped joint; the residual shape with no connector, pipe reference, rates or annotation also takes the legacy refusal.

The declared SP-1 exception covers only joint-containing models' refused diagnostic codes/text, 0.1.0/0.2.0 app joints becoming refused, and annotation warnings becoming disclosures. No admitted v2/v3 result changes. Anything beyond that requires an explicit decision. B7a removed the old result-row kind and renamed the summary count to `curved_bend_macro_element_count` (#1387; DEL-04-08 Design `b7-plan.md`, H-4 C1–C3).

The replacement uses `replaces_span`, not parallel/series ambiguity. A nonzero resolved eigenstrain or self-weight on the replaced span refuses with `JOINT_REPLACED_SPAN_LOAD_UNOWNED` until its producer is implemented. Applied-set presence matters: an explicit zero eigenstrain and a stored load not applied by a case remain admitted, consistent with the frozen references. Slot-table revision 01 still needs RV130 confirmation before code; its S20 wording must preserve this distinction.

## T4-U3 as implemented

- The objective connector (FK `connector`) forms B from differences r = (xⱼ − xᵢ) + (aⱼ − aᵢ), Ke = BᵀKB, the q_ref residual and its bound; definiteness is decided exactly (LDLᵀ) on both the authored H and the binary64 K, and a K that rounds indefinite from a non-dyadic Ls is refused with its own reason. It is admitted only on exact-contract (`3.0.0/exact_pressure_v3`) documents with `replaces_span` topology; a connector on a pressure-free document is refused (`PREVIEW_CONTRACT_VERSION_MISMATCH`). T4-U3 admitted it unpressurized only. Since T4-U5a, active with B7b's model 0.5.0, it may also be a constant-effective-area joint on a coaxial pressure-region edge ([DEL-04-07 Design, joint-pressure-thrust.md](../../../PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Design/joint-pressure-thrust.md)); any other pressure model or region participation keeps `JOINT_PRESSURE_INTERFACE_UNRESOLVED` or its own named code. Since T4-U5b (model 0.6.0), its `hardware` is `untied` or `explicit_axial_rods_v1`: explicit bilateral linear elastic tie rods between its end nodes with rigid offsets, each its own FK element (`tie_rod`), on pressurized and unpressurized connectors alike; other hardware kinds and rod laws keep `JOINT_HARDWARE_LAW_UNSUPPORTED` ([DEL-04-07 Design, u4b-u5b-implementation.md](../../../PKG-04/DEL-04-07/Design/u4b-u5b-implementation.md) §3).
- **S20 (applied-set rule):** per case, a primitive targeting the replaced span that the case applies is refused by presence (the case's `analysis_state.load_sources` when it carries an analysis state, otherwise every stored primitive; T4-U3 built this on 0.3.0 and 0.4.0, and B7b carried it to 0.5.0), refs `[component, pipe, load]`; on a load/reference-state case a resolved total eigenstrain on the span that is not exactly zero is refused, refs `[component, element_state:<pipe>, pipe]`; a stored primitive the case does not apply and an exactly-zero eigenstrain are admitted. **S21:** the span gets no member rows, maxima, recovery, eigenload or self-weight, and the load/reference-state member-state records do not publish it as consumed.
- Legacy joints take `LEGACY_FINITE_CONNECTOR_REAUTHOR_REQUIRED` on every route; an explicit `not_solver_consumed` joint, even with legacy fields, is an annotation (pressure-free route: analysed as pipe, disclosed; exact route: the seam's refusal). The user-stiffness element, its plumbing and the W4 tie producer are deleted; the tie reduction stays.
