# Root notice: concordance claim granularity (D-GOV-44, kernel Revision 2)

Tranche `ROOT-CONCORDANCE-CLAIM-GRANULARITY-20260922` (basis `a9da9f9719e0deeea015d1fa934d9a8a604d3ca5`, owner direction Ryan Tufts 2026-09-22) amends the shared method `docs/DELIVERABLE_CONCORDANCE_METHOD.md` to Revision 2 by adding §3.1 "Claim granularity", and revises the bundled `reconciliation` workflow (`resources/method.md` R3, R4, R5 and the `resources/contract.md` "Claim-level audit" invariant). The record is `docs/governance_harness/_DECISIONS/D-GOV-44_concordance_claim_granularity.md`.

**The rule.** A deliverable states what the implementation must satisfy; it does not describe the implementation. A statement belongs in a deliverable as a claim when changing the implementation so that it no longer holds would require a decision (an owner ruling, a scope change, or another registered act). Two companion tests: another surface depends on it (interface), or named evidence can check it (verification). A statement failing all three is implementation detail: cited as evidence, never asserted. Reconciliation continues through the lifecycle at that level. A "text out of date" finding has two repairs, **(a)** rewrite the mechanism to match the code or **(b)** lift the claim and move the mechanism to evidence; (b) is the default and (a) needs a stated reason in the ruling. A claim written at mechanism level is a granularity defect in the deliverable, not a code defect. Unmapped implementation is disposed by the same test. Borderline rows stay visible for human ruling.

**Workflow consequence.** R3 clusters mechanism-level wording as a granularity cause. R4 states both executions for every text-changing option, puts the run-level posture to the human before the packets, and sorts packets as dissolved / narrowed / untouched by it. R5 applies the three tests per repaired claim, lifts by default, and records each (a)/(b) choice in the repair manifest for R6.

**Origin.** The App run `RUN_D128_CONCORDANCE_2026-09-21_1614Z` found 1,089 of 3,568 rows "text out of date", largely mechanism-level wording invalidated by ordinary engineering choices while the requirement held.

**What this means for piping.** Piping pins the kernel through `plans/PLAN_2026-07-10_deliverable_implementation_reconciliation.md` and `docs/RECONCILIATION_PROFILE.md`. Any in-flight piping run keeps its pinned revision (kernel §6) and adopts by its own ruling if the owner so directs; a future activation pins Revision 2 unless its ruling says otherwise. Piping's own divergence layers (validation provenance, professional boundary, ISSUED-baseline protection) are untouched: the rule concerns what counts as a claim, not which project layers apply to it.

This notice communicates the change. The piping loop records adoption through its own instruments; nothing here accepts a deliverable, rules a piping packet, publishes a release, or amends the piping loop's accepted basis.
