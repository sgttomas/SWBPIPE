## Reviewer procedure for a later separately authorized act

For each row, the owner/sole maintainer must inspect the exact page and source
identities below, the generator and suite-source identities in
`BASIS_MANIFEST.md`, and the exact named test commands recorded on the page.
The reviewer then records `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` plus a reason
for each item:

1. evidence commands and inputs are recorded;
2. expected results reproduce from repository artifacts or lawful external
   sources;
3. the human rendering traces to an authoritative machine-readable witness;
4. units and assumptions are explicit;
5. warnings, non-convergence, and missing-data behavior are truthful;
6. protected-content/private-data scan is clean or risk-dispositioned;
7. professional reliance remains human-owned; and
8. unresolved gaps remain visible.

A later reviewer conclusion may be
`PROMOTE_TO_MAINTAINER_REVIEWED`, `RETAIN_DRAFT_EVIDENCE`, or
`BLOCKED`, with reviewer identity, date, reason, and exact hash bindings.
No such conclusion is present in this Phase-A instrument. Any page change,
generator/template change, source/test change, witness change, tolerance-policy
change, decomposition/DAG authority change affecting the page, or new
limitation invalidates the affected row and requires re-review.
