## Finding 1 — Organize the product around the working model

**External documentation.** Apple HIG describes toolbars as access to frequently used actions and cautions against overcrowding. Its split-view guidance describes adjacent content panes, including an example of navigation and inspector surfaces surrounding a canvas; persistent selection highlighting clarifies relationships between panes. Panel guidance defines an inspector as details that follow the current selection. These support complementary work surfaces without prescribing a piping application layout. [A1](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars?changes=_2), [A2](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/split-views?changes=_6), [A3](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/panels?changes=_3).

**Proposal.** Give the model canvas the central workspace, a collapsible model tree the leading edge, and a selection inspector the trailing edge. Put detailed diagnostics and tables in purpose-selected drawers or workspaces. Keep an always-visible, concise readiness summary with direct entry to issues. Distinguish “Build,” “Prepare analysis,” and “Review results” as task contexts sharing entity identity and camera state. Switching tasks should not require rediscovering the same pipe. These are hypotheses for DEL-07-01/02/04/05/07/08, not an Apple mandate or an accepted replacement for the present palette contract.

## Finding 2 — Routing needs a visible precision contract

**External documentation.** Plant 3D’s routing instructions establish pipe specification, size, and line identity before point placement. Its compass/snaps documentation describes continuation grips at open ports, a routing plane, angular snapping, numeric distance entry, and explicit object-snap locations. Its command documentation exposes plane, elevation, offset and undo within routing. Documentation also distinguishes connecting from offset placement; snapping is therefore more than a visual cursor convenience. [P1](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Plant3D-UserGuide/files/GUID-08731B7F-2DBA-47C8-966E-D91F0DFAD067.htm), [P2](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2024/ENU/Plant3D-UserGuide/files/GUID-80606EFD-7D5C-43CD-8F90-DBFEF07EE34D.htm), [P3](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Plant3D-UserGuide/files/GUID-E1D7EC73-34DB-41A6-ADA1-8A9CE99D0F51.htm).

**Proposal.** A route tool should show its active plane, coordinate frame, units, start port, snap target and provisional endpoint beside the canvas. Offer pointer placement and typed coordinates/dimensions as equivalent ways to construct the same preview. Differentiate “align with” from “connect to”; preview affected topology before commit. Permit cancel and step-back without leaving partial unexplained geometry. Show incompatible ports or missing required data explicitly. Do not borrow commercial pipe catalogs, fitting tolerances, or automatic selection defaults. The transferable principle is visible intent plus precise feedback. Route-candidate generation remains outside this PKG-07 investigation’s implementation authority.

## Finding 3 — Tree, inspector and tables should share selection

**External documentation.** Bentley’s AutoPIPE input-grid help documents bidirectional grid/graphic selection, changes across selected ranges, and coordinate/offset placement through grids. It also describes cases where hidden or unselected components disappear from grids. These are documented interaction behaviors, not evidence of superior engineering outcomes. [B1](https://bentleysystems.service-now.com/community?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0026084).

**Proposal.** Use one stable selection model across viewport, tree, property inspector and editable tables. A selected row should locate and highlight the exact entity; a canvas selection should reveal its tree path and relevant table row. Offer selection filters for entity types, explicit disambiguation when geometry overlaps, and “frame selection.” Display selected count, filter state and hidden-selection state. When multiple entities have different values, show a mixed-value state rather than one representative value. Batch edits should preview their scope, report excluded entities and resolve through the existing structured operation contract. Preserve units and provenance adjacent to the edited value. This combines DEL-07-01/02 interaction work with DEL-07-09 contract ownership; implementation still lands in its ratified owners.

## Finding 4 — Results are an investigative workspace

**External documentation.** Bentley documents static reports for whole models, selected objects and filtered criteria, plus interactive point and result-grid review. A separate support article describes combining result filters with graphical selection. The overview is dated March 2017 and identifies limitations for some interactive result categories; it is a historical interaction precedent, not verification of every current AutoPIPE edition. [B2](https://bentleysystems.service-now.com/community?id=kb_article&sysparm_article=KB0116822), [B3](https://bentleysystems.service-now.com/community?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0115827).

**Proposal.** Keep a selected run, load case/combination, quantity, units and coordinate frame explicit. Synchronize results tables with graphical highlights, offer visible filters, and label when only selected/filtered entities are shown. Selecting a concerning row should retain the numerical result while framing its physical location. Separate geometry, analytical idealization and result overlays through named display controls. Treat deformation scale as a display setting with a visible value. Model edits should mark older results as belonging to the prior model state rather than silently recoloring new geometry with stale values. Mechanics completion, rule-check availability and human review remain distinct. These recommendations target DEL-07-05/08 and consume upstream run/provenance contracts without claiming those contracts are complete.

## Finding 5 — Use a small, learnable visual vocabulary

**External documentation.** Apple recommends recognizable, simplified interface icons with consistent visual size, detail, weight and perspective, and accessible descriptions for custom icons. Its color guidance calls for consistent meanings, light/dark/increased-contrast treatment, and alternatives to color-only information. Accessibility guidance encourages auditing throughout design and adapting to text-size and assistive-technology needs. [A4](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/icons?changes=l_8_2), [A5](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color?changes=_2_2), [A6](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility/).

**Proposal.** Establish a vector icon inventory keyed to command names: common edit/navigation actions use familiar metaphors; domain actions get simple tested piping glyphs. Keep accessible names and discoverable text labels for unfamiliar tools. Distinguish selection, pending edits, missing inputs, warnings and numerical results with text or shape as well as color. Give all modeling tasks keyboard paths, visible focus and a searchable command entry. Domain geometry need not be navigated exclusively by a pointer: tree and table selection can supply an equivalent precise route. Adopting Apple visual principles does not select SF Symbols assets or license their redistribution. DEL-07-06 owns accessibility checks; DEL-07-09 owns vocabulary consistency.

## Priorities, evidence gaps and acceptance probes

**P0 proposal:** Validate the core sequence: start/continue a pipe, place a branch, inspect/edit a property, identify a missing input, run analysis, locate a result, modify geometry and recognize stale results. Record task success, wrong-target edits, recovery behavior and time spent searching; choose numeric targets after a baseline study. **P1 proposal:** test larger models, overlapping geometry, mixed selections, typed units, reduced window widths, increased text size, keyboard-only use and assistive technology. **P2 proposal:** extend batch authoring and richer comparison once basic continuity is reliable.
