## 3. How the frames were verified

`tools/render.mjs` opens every frame with Playwright's headless Chromium from `{REPO_ROOT}/projects/chirality-piping/node_modules` (read-only, as the design-system child did), with `page.route("**/*")` aborting every request that is not `file://`, with `colorScheme` set to the frame's theme, at three viewports: 1440 × 900 (the nominal window; the stage scales to 0.929 inside the page's padding), 1472 × 964 (the stage at exactly 1.0, captured 1:1 for review), and 960 × 700 (a narrower pane; the stage scales to 0.644). For each it records: blocked requests (none were attempted), console warnings and errors and page errors (none), the page's scroll width and height against the viewport (no scroll in any case), the computed scale, `data-theme` and the resolved `color-scheme` and body background (light `rgb(238, 240, 243)`, dark `rgb(27, 30, 34)`), the smallest text size in the stage (11 px, the caption token; nothing below), every element whose box leaves the 1440 × 900 stage (none), every cell, header, control, tooltip, popover, footer, banner, inspector value, legend, probe, card or comment whose content is wider than its box (none), and every region whose content is taller than its box (none). At 1.0 it then clicks the caption bar's other theme and records that `data-theme` and the body background flip, then "System" and records that the attribute is removed and the emulated scheme applies. A source lint over all fifteen files (attributes and tooltips included) finds no forbidden word (certify, seal, approve, authenticate, comply, compliant, non-authoritative, not authoritative, the fence token, Pass, Fail, OK, Ready, Safe, Acceptable, Verified, validated, Apply, Confirm, Sign off, Final, Released) outside the registered acceptance sentence, no occurrence of the old product name, the acceptance sentence exactly once in `s7_both_light`, `s7_both_dark`, `s9_table_light` and `s9_table_dark` and nowhere else, the maturity sentence exactly once in `s1_table_light` and nowhere else, "Display only, not accepted as input" once in `s5_table_light`, and "draft until accepted" on both state-8 frames. `index.html` is opened the same way in the same run: offline, no request attempted, no error, all eighteen links resolve to files in the directory.

Every 1:1 screenshot was then read and judged by eye, twice, and the frames fixed between passes: the branch riser projecting onto the main riser at the first azimuth (two azimuths chosen instead); node plates landing on tubes (placement now avoids tube segments); glyph plates and the length field leaving the canvas (clamped); the inspector overflowing the slide-over and the Model-view column (compacted, label column narrowed); read-through and results headers clipping their units (header padding and unit size); the edit cell's unit colliding with the number; the results header wrapping to two lines; the failure banner's link and the drawer's selected row hidden under the two open popovers (banner shortened to the solver's sentence, drawer filtered to the Nonlinear class); the proposal card's Accept and Reject controls falling below the agent column (accepted row collapsed, texts shortened); the agent column's tab strip overflowing; the ghost plate overlapping the rod's plate; comment references truncated; and the `Confirm` in an agent comment (reworded). The last pass over all fifteen at 1:1 and over the scaled 960 × 700 renders found nothing further.

After the stylesheets were inlined (the revision above), `render.mjs` was re-run over all fifteen frames and the index exactly as before — network blocked, the three viewports, the frame's theme, the lint — with one addition: a source scan of every HTML file for `<link>`, script `src`, `@import`, external `url()` and `src` attributes, recorded per file as `selfContained` (fifteen frames and the index: none found; one `<style>` block per frame, two in the index, which keeps its own page rules).
