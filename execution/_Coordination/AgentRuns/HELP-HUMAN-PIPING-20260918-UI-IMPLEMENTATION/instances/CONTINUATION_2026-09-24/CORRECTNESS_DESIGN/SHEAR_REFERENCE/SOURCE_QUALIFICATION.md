## Accuracy warrant and scope of “exact”

The independent return derives the annular stress field, integrates its squared shear magnitude over the annulus, and equates its energy to `V²/(2Ks)`. Limits, dimensions, material dependence and comparison with Cowper are checked. The manager derives the Timoshenko block and load part from force/moment equilibrium plus displacement/rotation compatibility. Exact here means the solution of that defined prismatic 1D static model, and the section-energy matching under Saint-Venant assumptions. It does not mean every displacement/rotation of a fully constrained 3D end surface is reproduced.

The constitutive and nodal quantities therefore have an explicit energy-conjugate meaning. `theta` is generalized section rotation; `v'` also contains the effective shear strain. If a validation specimen observes a literal centerline displacement, cross-section average or boundary rotation, its mapping to these model variables and end/warping constraints must be stated. A point load and a real bearing patch are not identical near their application region.

The model is suitable for homogeneous isotropic straight members away from local stress-introduction/end zones, within first-order kinematics and the declared material/section state. It has no pressure geometric stiffness, sectional ovalization, shear-lag enrichment, local shell instability, finite-rotation or dynamic mass/rotary-inertia qualification. A scalar annular coefficient cannot repair elbows or discontinuities. A generic computed-section method becomes appropriate for anisotropy, multi-material layering, warping constraints or noncircular sections; that method must preserve the same chosen generalized-variable/energy definition.
