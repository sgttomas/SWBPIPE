## Independent derivation and relevance checks

The attachment-motion difference under an infinitesimal rigid rotation is omega×r, so subtracting the average endpoint rotation crossed with r cancels it. Differentiating this linear measure supplies B; differentiating the stated potential supplies B^Tg and B^TKB. Expanding the transpose gives the endpoint moment terms in CONTRACT.md. Their force/moment sum vanishes at any reference origin without a corrective force. These facts establish the required first-order kinematics and virtual work independently of the current six-spring implementation.

For pressurized axial changes at reference cross-sections, the axial end/face pressure-work ledger has pipe term `p Ai delta(L)` and the declared calibrated joint term `p Ae delta(qt_x)`. Full pipe volume change also has `L delta(Ai)`; that radial-wall response is separately condensed into the pipe's Poisson constitutive term and is not discarded or counted again as a cap. Collecting the axial face terms gives the area-difference pair plus real terminal caps. Together with the wall law this gives `Nw-Pi=Tb+Tt-Pe` and the tied/untied cases. It also identifies an important test weakness: an anchored ideal tie can absorb a wrong extra pressure pair while leaving anchor reactions unchanged. Its tie force and pressure-work ownership must be checked independently.

The six generalized coordinates and matrix are an **interface model**, not a detailed corrugation-shell design. The source-supplied stiffness must describe the same end motions, frames, reference temperature, pressure basis, material range and installed state. A source with substantial working-rate nonlinearity or unknown test restraints cannot be repaired by merely adding off-diagonal matrix entries. A relevant constitutive transform or new measured/reference data is required.

The first pressurized reference is intentionally coaxial and axial, with other modes restrained. It supports ordinary elastic load-path and compatibility checks at realistic SI dimensions; it does not qualify lateral pressure stability, local shell stress, actual manufacturer movement capacity or a generic tied-joint assembly. General infinitesimal mechanical modes are qualified separately at zero pressure. Subsequent pressurized lateral/hardware compositions need their own physical reference before the application can claim that broader fidelity. This is a bounded physical qualification, not a new numerical-policy hold on unrelated repairs.
