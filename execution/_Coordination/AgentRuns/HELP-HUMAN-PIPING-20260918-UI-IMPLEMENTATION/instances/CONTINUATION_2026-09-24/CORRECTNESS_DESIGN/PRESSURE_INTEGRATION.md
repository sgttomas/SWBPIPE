## Force bookkeeping that must remain connected

For local i→j and tension-positive wall force, use the existing kernel's `Nw=EAs(epsilon_z-alphaDeltaT)+2nuP`, `S=Nw-P`, `P=pAi`, and its independently frozen cap/eigen pairs. Assemble mechanical equivalent loads, thermal/Poisson eigenloads and physical terminal cap transfer separately. Recover wall actions as `K_e d_e - f_mechanical_equivalent - f_eigen`; never subtract cap transfer again. Recover section wall tension from negative i action / positive j action. Publish wall force, effective force and axial membrane stress separately; add no second longitudinal-pressure scalar to `Nw/As`. Lamé radial/hoop surface rows retain their surface identity.

There is no independently unbalanced external pA load merely because a welded line turns. The complete wall/cap/closure free body determines restraint demand. This first straight composition must block an unsupported bend instead of inventing that force. Later curved composition must independently reconcile existing radial wall loads and cap transfer with wall/effective-force recovery; neither blind addition nor deletion of the existing bend term is the contract. Expansion-joint effective-area thrust belongs to its actual interrupted/tied load path and is a separate connector integration.
