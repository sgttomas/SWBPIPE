# Contribution-preserving solve and action recovery

Bounded design, 2026-09-24, HELPS_HUMANS `/root/correctness_design`. This follows the independently qualified N05/N06/NP-A references and KREV repair; it does not redo their derivation, change their 1e-9 intended-answer criterion or allocate public versions. `P/` means `projects/chirality-piping/`; `N/` means the isolated numerical worktree's continuation `SOLVER_MANAGER/NUMERICAL_INTEGRITY/`. Exact origins/hashes and actual worktree locations are under `_run_records/`. No production, Cargo/build, native/UI, endpoint or Git action occurs in this task.

## 1. Decision and actual gap

Select an **adaptive binary multiprecision structural path with preserved input contributions and retained high-precision recovery**, triggered by actual numerical evidence, not fixture IDs or expected answers. Start at 128 significand bits and increase when necessary. Use a backend with explicit precision, exponent/range checks and correctly rounded elementary arithmetic; MPFR is the reference arithmetic contract, not a claim that a Rust binding or native package is already installed/qualified. A bounded all-software double-double/quad-double backend is a valid later implementation alternative only after its operation/range/error model meets the same end-to-end contract. Preserve the ordinary binary64 path for cases it actually resolves under the existing policy.

Current `frame_kernel/src/structural.rs` has useful exact nonoverlapping expansions and source-bound opaque prepared/factor types. It audits contribution loss and intended action. However, `PreparedSystem.matrix/rhs`, factor arithmetic, `StructuralSolution.displacements` and downstream product recovery remain binary64. `contribution_sums` can reject an absorbed positive diagonal before stronger computation. Product reactions and local actions are reconstructed from the returned binary64 displacement vector. Accurate audit of a rounded solve does not repair either lost stabilizing stiffness or lost relative motion.

The new path must therefore be a genuine assembly/reduction/factor/solve/recovery path. Higher-precision residuals alone, promoting an already rounded K, or retaining only an accurate final scalar root rotation cannot complete this repair. An absorbed positive contribution is an escalation reason when the complete source is available; it is not a physical mechanism and must not simply be suppressed.

## 2. Preserve three distinct reference meanings

1. **Authored/model basis:** accepted finite input quantities, units, geometry, material/section laws, loads, constraints and their supported mathematical formulation. Existing inputs are binary64 unless the owning input contract says otherwise; promote their exact binary values, not a decimal spelling guessed from display. No recovery of information already lost before that boundary is promised.
2. **Intended contribution system:** the sum and transformations of those supported element/support/load contributions before lossy global accumulation. The independent NP-A seam uses exact recorded binary64 contribution values. Product physics additionally requires consistent evaluation of the actual element/constraint maps; an arbitrary bag of rounded global entries does not establish that its soft mode represents the intended physical model.
3. **Historical represented matrix:** the old binary64 coalesced K and its exact solution. Preserve it as a baseline/oracle/diagnostic. Do not call its changed effective spring the user's intended k, or change the reference to obtain a pass.

All three identities must remain source-bound. Keep the original NP-A hex coefficient and its stored-matrix expectations untouched. For a product run, capture the actual produced coefficients: a separately rounded product torsional stiffness is not automatically the isolated NP-A hex value. The original physical expressions, rather than the rounded matrix, own N05/N06 intended-answer accuracy.

## 3. Generic source representation

Retain immutable, individually identified stiffness/load contributions with entity, DOF/frame, case, material/section and formulation basis. Use checked expansions to preserve sums of represented scalar contributions, including mirrored off-diagonal terms and force cancellation. Convert every expansion component into the stronger representation before coalescing there. A dense binary64 matrix is a derivative, never the stronger path's source.

For supported derived elements, retain/rebuild their **basic deformation map and constitutive operator** at the chosen precision, then assemble `K=sum(B_e^T D_e B_e)+support terms`. For ordinary straight frames, B describes axial extension, two midpoint transverse deformation measures and three relative rotations; D contains their axial, bending, torsional stiffnesses (or the full qualified coupled constitutive matrix). This is an algebraic representation of the already selected element, not a new beam theory. Compute coordinate differences, orientation normalization, rigid offsets and transformations coherently at that precision from the actual input basis. Recompute derived section/material coefficients where their source arithmetic can affect the required quantity accuracy.

This avoids promoting a rounded transformed element matrix whose artificial rigid-mode stiffness can already exceed the small spring. In particular, a skew element's nearly cancelling rows must retain the source geometry/energy relationships. Preserve the existing energy/nullspace model proof and symmetry checks; do not enforce correctness by deleting arbitrary small entries or projecting a corrupted matrix onto a desired nullspace.

Distinguish supported primitive-derived contributions from explicitly authored matrix entries and from legacy rounded-only derived matrices. An authored finite matrix is its declared numerical model; a rounded-only derived matrix cannot be upgraded to a primitive-accuracy claim. If the required source is unavailable, return a specific source-fidelity unresolved result rather than invent the missing small terms. First supported family is the passive straight-frame/bilateral-positive-spring system with exact prescribed constraints used by the existing references, plus other families only when their contribution and recovery contracts are actually carried through. Nonlinear/contact and curved/connector producers need equivalent source ownership before claiming the stronger guarantee.

## 4. Stronger structural path

The implementing owner supplies typed internal equivalents of `ContributionSystem`, `PreparedExtended`, `PositiveExtendedFactor` and `RetainedStructuralSolution`. These remain opaque/source-bound: no caller can supply an arbitrary LU closure, a label or mismatched original system to completion. The existing KREV trust-boundary repairs must survive.

1. Admit the immutable source, supported family, free/prescribed map and contribution coverage. Detect true geometric mechanisms independently; greater precision must not regularize one. Preserve finite/range and symmetry checks.
2. Assemble and reduce at precision p, including **load contributions and `K_fc*u_c`**. Apply the same work-conjugate/radix congruence and DOF ordering, represented without dropping tails. Scaling does not create stiffness or information. Round neither the matrix nor reduced RHS back to f64.
3. Factor the actual stronger SPD system using positive Cholesky or LDL. A generic dense LDL reference implementation is acceptable first; profile/sparse storage can use the same arithmetic with tested structural ordering. Do not densify silently beyond the actual supported resource limit. No speed, memory or scale claim is made here.
4. Solve and retain the complete p-bit displacement and prescribed state. Evaluate residuals at a greater precision against immutable intended contributions, not factor workspaces. Refine with corrections computed from this same intended system; never use only the old rounded K as the authoritative correction equation. A low-precision preconditioner may be an optimization later, subject to convergence qualification.
5. Recover element deformations/actions, support laws and reactions **before publication rounding**. Independently recompute original free equilibrium, prescribed compatibility, element energy and complete force/moment balances from that retained state. Keep the existing protected residual/accuracy comparisons separately; a method-specific arithmetic evaluation allowance cannot replace the 1e-9 answer check.
6. Compare a candidate solve/recovery at p against an independently recomputed stronger solve/recovery, and test the relevant output quantities, not just global displacement norm. Agreement is evidence of numerical convergence, not proof of source fidelity or guaranteed forward digits. Existing independently derived reference cases must still meet their original predicates.

Proposed bounded precision schedule: candidate p=128,256,512 bits, with verification/re-evaluation at 2p and residual evaluation at at least p+64. Thus the actual backend and recovery verification ceiling is **1024 bits**, not512. These are resource/algorithm choices, not physical tolerances. Stop on actual verified candidate acceptance; if the maximum supported precision/resource limit is insufficient, report numerical accuracy unresolved with retained evidence. A finite p-bit answer is not automatic success. The implementation must record actual arithmetic precision/rounding and effective exponent limits, and must not silently accept overflow, underflow, low-term erasure or a failed refinement.

Working-precision screens must identify the arithmetic to which they apply. A binary64 rcond cutoff cannot be used to reject a resolved 128-bit factor as though it were still a binary64 factor, nor may an old factor be relabelled high precision. ROOT owns the reviewed prospective method-policy registration. Ordinary binary64 M03 observations and protected DEC-050/053 records remain unchanged; this design adds no global tolerance and makes no certified-inertia/forward-error claim.

### Error and conditioning limits

If the supported normal-range arithmetic has unit roundoff u_p, operation-level accumulation uses its actual count/error model (for correctly rounded basic binary arithmetic, u_p=2^-p). A rough `condition_estimate*roundoff_estimate` is a planning/sensitivity indicator, not a forward-error theorem or a substitute for intended quantity checks. Data/model uncertainty remains separate. A large condition number under arbitrary matrix-entry perturbations can greatly overstate sensitivity to the independently authored parameters a,k,T; preserving their structure is exactly the purpose of this repair.

For a linear recovered quantity g=c^T*u+g0, exact-source error can be related to an adjoint `K^T*z=c`: its solve-error contribution is z^T*r, with sign depending on residual convention. Include source-map/constitutive/load and output-rounding errors too. An approximate adjoint supplies an estimate, not a rigorous bound unless its own error is controlled. This is an available quantity-aware diagnostic, not a mandatory inverse certificate for every ordinary model. For the scalar torsion action c=[-a,a], the exact adjoint is [0,1]; the action error depends directly on the tip-equilibrium residual even when global rotation conditioning is severe. The global displacement norm alone therefore neither bounds every action tightly nor demonstrates action accuracy.

Escalation cannot recover unknown omitted contributions, imprecise source geometry already collapsed to identical input coordinates, an incorrect element law, a physical mechanism, or unquantified material/support uncertainty. Finite precision/resource limits remain real; unresolved output outside the supported limit must remain visible. Named intended references must meet their unchanged1e-9 criteria, including tiny relative/internal actions, before this bounded repair is complete.

## 5. Retained relative actions versus public f64 displacements

For the isolated torsional pair, the intended energy is `(a*(theta_j-theta_i)^2+k*theta_i^2)/2`. Existing references give `theta_i=T/k`, `delta=theta_j-theta_i=T/a`, spring-on-pipe action `-T`, and element torsion T. In N06, delta is far smaller than the spacing of a binary64 global rotation around 1e-4 rad. Both published rotations can legitimately round to the same binary64 value while the accurate element torsion remains nonzero.

`RetainedStructuralSolution` must therefore own high-precision global coordinates **and/or exact-source-derived basic deformations**, with immutable source/method identity. Compute `q_e=B_e u-q_ref`, constitutive actions, load-corrected end actions and support reactions in the retained representation; only then round each public scientific quantity once to f64. Do not recover torque by subtracting the two published rotations, and do not recover reactions using rounded K times published u. Public displacement values are correctly rounded **from the checked retained p-bit result**; this does not prove correct rounding of the exact-source mathematical solution or claim to encode every relative observable.

Keep load/eigen/reference subtraction at the same precision as the elastic action. For cancellation-sensitive end actions, prefer directly evaluated basic deformations/constitutive forces with source-owned load terms. The result rows and their provenance must distinguish computed actions from a reader's approximation reconstructed from rounded global fields. Cast failure or a nonzero physical result outside the public carrier's representable range is explicit, not a silent zero/infinity.

Residual evidence must name its state basis. The solve gate uses the retained internal state; a residual or reconstructed action using the public f64 displacement projection is a separate observation and can be worse. Do not relabel one as the other or claim the rounded public vector preserves every small relative action. External `finite_binary64` value representation can remain true while the internal solver uses greater precision; report the two facts separately through the source-owned semantic/evidence interface. A matching input/run ID is not sufficient to bind an unrelated retained state.

Persist a hash-bound retained-state/recovery evidence record sufficient to replay the actions—normalized binary significands/exponents or round-trip precision-tagged strings, plus source contributions/basis, maps and algorithm. It is typed numerical evidence, not a replacement interpretation of existing force/moment rows. If later operations only display stored f64 rows, preserve them; if they recompute actions/combinations, use the retained supported state or execute a new solve. Existing result-family/semantic registry owners coordinate any internal/report fields through the reserved versions; no public version is allocated here. Historical hashes and values are never rewritten or “restored” to hypothetical precision.

## 6. Comparison with alternatives

**Double-double plus exact expansions:** a genuine end-to-end two-limb factor/solve/recovery can resolve much more than f64 and is a viable bounded alternative. QD's approximately 106-bit significand does not mean the present `Expansion` audit is already such a solver, nor that arbitrary hand-written addition/division/square-root algorithms inherit correct rounding. N06's condition scale and cancellation leave far less margin than a 128/256-bit path. Retain range/renormalization, operation-error and all-recovery checks; if it fails the original reference, increase precision or improve formulation, never adjust the reference. The selected adaptive backend offers a clearer escalation and arithmetic contract, at an unmeasured deployment/runtime cost.

**Relative-coordinate formulation:** for this pair, choose `z=[theta_i,delta]` and `u=Pz`, `P=[[1,0],[1,1]]`. Then `P^T K_intended P=diag(k,a)` and `P^T f=[T,T]`. Solve z=[T/k,T/a] directly and retain delta. This is an excellent independent oracle and demonstrates why preserving physical deformation coordinates matters. Applying P to already rounded K does not restore k. A general tree/basic-deformation transformation can reduce cancellation, but loops require compatibility, supports/MPCs change admissible coordinates, and a transformation can be ill-conditioned or destroy sparsity. It needs a generic graph/nullspace/rank implementation, not a branch that recognizes the two-node fixture or its expected answer. Defer it as a production optimization; preserve element-local basic deformations now.

**Mixed-precision refinement alone:** high-precision residuals can improve a nonsingular approximate factor when its convergence assumptions hold. They cannot reconstruct an omitted contribution, make a singular rounded N06 factor invertible, or preserve a displacement increment immediately recast to f64. General GMRES-based refinement is a possible later optimization, not the first repair's proof.
