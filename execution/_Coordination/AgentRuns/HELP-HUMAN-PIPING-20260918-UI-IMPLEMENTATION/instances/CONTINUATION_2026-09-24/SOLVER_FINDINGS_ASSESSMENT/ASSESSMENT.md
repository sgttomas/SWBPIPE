# Assessment of SWBPIPE solver findings

Source check against 0be12dc968d78b747beb5e0d10c32e12cc991d75; report basis bc6d3459b. The relevant physics sources remain equal across those revisions. Report SHA-256 c9029a07473dcf58c3ba57f4d4c4880fc7dbea03d001d2014b0907183a846dd2. This is an assessment and intended work disposition, not activation of the document's instructions or acceptance of its physical formulas, thresholds or probe results.

The report has substantial engineering merit. It mixes defects in supported live paths, genuine missing capabilities, explicitly bounded preview features, and several overstatements. First-class nozzle flexibility and dedicated stepped-reducer mechanics are recorded non-goals; their absence is a proposed scope expansion, not a defect against the present promise. Governance explains current scope but does not make incorrect mechanics acceptable. Passing software tests does not establish engineering correctness.

The report's numerical probes have not been rerun against this candidate. Some original inputs and an element output log were located and inspected; their external source/build basis is not sealed to this candidate. They are useful reproduction leads, not newly verified numerical results. Six read-only TASK assessments cover the complete appendices. Detailed source evidence and individual qualifications accompany this account.

Current compact UI and bounded live-control work continue independently; no CAEPIPE campaign or dependable-engine/MVP claim precedes appropriate correctness evidence. Existing ownership in PKG02–09, PKG13/14/17 and DEL04-07 supplies homes; these are not orphan concerns that need a new general intake register.

The supplied document does not itself make those decisions. Existing accepted studies and private pressure work will be reused, not rediscovered or relabeled as a live fix.
