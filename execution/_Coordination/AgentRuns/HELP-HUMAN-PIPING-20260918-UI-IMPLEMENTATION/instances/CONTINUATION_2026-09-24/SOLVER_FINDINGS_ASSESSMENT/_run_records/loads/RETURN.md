The three highest-risk confirmed silent-result behaviors in this appendix are **LOAD-01** (straight pressure stress/recovery inconsistency), **LOAD-02** (repeated constant-effort contribution in algebra), and **LOAD-06** (accepted hydrotest pressure silently ignored). **LOAD-03** and **LOAD-15** are also clear repair candidates because accepted UI operations prevent a native solve; they fail visibly rather than silently underestimating a load.

Recommendations requiring rejection or qualification: do not implement “always add longitudinal pressure everywhere,” “thrust only at ends,” or “free and anchored outputs must both equal PD/4t” without separating physical wall stress, effective force and code-check quantities; do not change a protected oracle to obtain a pass. Macro-bend pressure recovery already contains wall tension. Do not use arbitrary large stiffness multipliers as a default rigid/prescribed-displacement implementation. Do not automatically remove all algebra for nonlinear models: combinations of independently solved result states and simultaneous-load superposition are distinct operations, and the latter needs explicit applicability controls. Do not treat case names, gravity presence, assumed +Y, cold spring exclusion, wind projection law, or an SRSS recipe as sufficient engineering definitions.

The existing dormant pressure helper is useful implementation groundwork, not a correction of the live path. ROOT reported that D66/D67 already provide the relevant pressure investigation/activation route; this assessment did not independently reopen those authority records.
