| Q1 thrust, published N | **tp_phys_008's effective axial force.** The cap pair {−pA·e at i, +pA·e at j} enters the force vector as nodal loads (`PP` `add_pressure_thrust_loads`). Recovery adds +pA at end i and −pA at end j of the local force vector (`corrected_local_forces_for_axial_effects`), so N = EA·ext/L − pA (tension positive): fixed-fixed gives N = −pA, and a free end gives N = 0. The arc wall-tension form (+pA) applies only to realized curved macro spans, which are out of scope. Displacements and reactions are as you say | `PP:8588-8628`, `:8721-8740`; `straight_pipe` axial-effect recovery `:674-687` |
| Q1 thrust, area | A = π/4·ID², with ID = OD − 2t, where t is the **effective** wall: t_nom − m when a mill tolerance m is given, otherwise t_nom. For the N section with no mill tolerance, ID = 0.18 m. Expansion-joint user effective areas are out of scope | `PP` `derive_pipe_section` `:7993-8036`, `build_pressure_thrust_loads` `:8477-8513` |
| Q2 generated self-weight | **Not generated at solve time.** Self-weight generation is an authoring-time model operation (`product_physics/src/self_weight.rs`, `generate_self_weight_operations`). It writes a `distributed_force` element load, with intensity = mass per length × user gravity along an explicit global axis, into the model document. The solve consumes the stored binary64 intensity as an input. **So drop item 2 as a solve-time family.** Weight is covered by item 1 as a user-given intensity. Record this in your README | `self_weight.rs:122-380` |
| Q3 local frame | Every pipe carries a required explicit `y_reference`. Its absence is blocking. x = (x_j − x_i)/L; y = normalize(y_ref − (y_ref·x)·x); z = x × y. Your plan (an explicit y_reference, publishing the equivalent global intensity) is right | `FK` `FrameOrientation::from_x_axis_and_y_reference` `:511-525`; `PP` pipe y_reference check |
| Q4 global intensity on a skew member | Per unit **member** length. The load vector is transformed into local axes and integrated over the member length, with no projection. Wind likewise: w = p·Cs·D_exp per unit member length, along a global axis, whatever the member's orientation. Keeping wind on members perpendicular to its direction is fine, and also add one skew-member wind case so the no-projection rule is exercised (the projected form is a negative control) | `primitive_loads` wind generator `:1971-2110`; `straight_pipe` global equivalent loads |
| Q5 generated loads in the solve | They take the same consistent route. The generators emit `uniform_element_load` or `partial_uniform_element_load` (sub-span, with extent). These are appended to the case's loads (`PP:6998`, `:7247`) and enter `equivalent_nodal_loads_with_spans`, with consistent Hermite loads including FEMs, and partial spans by shape-function integration. The 50/50 and lever-rule forms are negative controls only | `PP:2889`, `:6998`, `:7247`; `straight_pipe:568-583` |
| Q6 section for generated cases | **One effective wall t = t_nom − m (absolute length) for everything:** stiffness (A, I, J), thrust area and mass. There is no nominal-stiffness/effective-mass split. Mass per length = ρ_metal·π/4·(OD² − ID²) + ρ_contents·π/4·ID² + ρ_ins·π/4·((OD + 2t_ins)² − OD²), with ID = OD − 2t. Contents and insulation are optional; insulation needs both thickness and density. With no mill tolerance, t = t_nom | `PP` `derive_pipe_section` `:7993-8036`; `compute_pipe_mass_per_length` `:7008-7110` |
| Q7 thermal | Both enter as the same axial effect P = E·A·ε*, with the equivalent pair ±P and recovery giving N = EA(ext/L − ε*). **Legacy:** ε* = α·ΔT, with E and α from the member's selected material and A from the effective-wall section. **0.4.0 resolved:** ε* = λ_fit·λ_thermal − 1 (fit composed with thermal), with each member's own E per resolved case and the exact annulus area. The λ_thermal laws (engineering secant and others) are as T1's ANALYTICAL_REFERENCE defines them; use its engineering-secant form. One E per member per state. So yes, they differ only in how ε* is defined | `PP` `build_thermal_element_loads` `:8426-8475`, `load_state_eigen_loads` `:2006-2025`, notes `:982-983` |
