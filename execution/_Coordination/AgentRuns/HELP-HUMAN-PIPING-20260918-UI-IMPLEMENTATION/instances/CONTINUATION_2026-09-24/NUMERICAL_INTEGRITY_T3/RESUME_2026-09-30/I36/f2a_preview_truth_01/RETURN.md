## 2. Actual ordinary readout and source boundary

For this assignment, the relevant ordinary subset is the already admitted
straight-member, nodal-load, rigid/global-spring W1a source. T0's broader arcs,
constant effort, nonlinear supports, distributed/thermal loads and combinations
are not brought into it by this reconciliation. Keep every existing selection
and no-retirement/coexistence gate.

On that subset, the public mechanical readout is a global component of the
linear displacement state; a same-frame signed local end or section-cut station
action; or an attributed support-law action. Stress uses that member's section
and the signed N, My, Mz or T of that same cut. The source-annulus interpretation
has c=D/2, ri=c−t, circular A and I, J=2I, Z=I/c. Its basis is the normalized D
and **actual effective-wall t** already handed to section construction. This is
the least-change ordinary association with accepted B1C. It does not promote the
rounded inner diameter/radii, product PI, A_hat or Z_hat to mathematical truth.

For materials, distinguish three ordinary source cases:

1. No temperature basis: normalized explicit base E and G.
2. Exact-id basis: normalized explicit E and G on that selected point.
3. Interpolated basis: normalized adjacent source points, actual strictly
   bracketed normalized solve temperature, and the declared linear law for
   each of E and G. Actual E_hat/G_hat returned by the resolver are captured
   separately as the K primitives. A conservative certificate should include
   the exact linear interpolation on these normalized source values and the
   actual rounded resolver values. This encloses the finer numerical ambiguity
   without replacing the selected physical method. Positive bounds and source
   association still have to be established.

No nu is required or invented for these ordinary cases. In particular the
ordinary user may provide E and G without any selected isotropic E/nu pair.
The I33 source coefficient formulas must be instantiated from these ordinary
E/G operands, not copied from the exact-profile E/[2(1+nu)] recipe.

The present evidence does **not** warrant an exact unrounded authored
nominal-wall-minus-tolerance promise. B1C already preserves the effective-value
boundary; it should remain so. If another accepted ordinary instrument is later
found to require unrounded subtraction, reopen that source association and
include its enclosure. Do not silently select that stronger contract now.
The older hand-calculation's corrosion allowance is a different input surface;
it is not an extra preview wall reduction.
