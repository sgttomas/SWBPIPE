   stop rule, R7/A1 gates and once-rounded native publication remain unchanged

   They are not observed convergence of the different final y/n. Product admission
   requires native admission **and** a direct certificate of the final frozen

   exact maxima, original-operand coupling, class thresholds, small/zero-scale
