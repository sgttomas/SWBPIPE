# I44 ownership and work boundary

This is a private conditional native diagnostic component. No complete memory
profile, byte allowance, tariff, facade price, visit permit, kernel budget debit,
PP custody, or product admission is selected. Counts below describe this code,
not an inherited I35/I42 three-pass or 177-slot reservation.

## Actual owners and phases

The existing RetainedSolve, source, publication/radii, factors, verification state,
ordering, blocks, ledger, source identity and proposed-law slice are borrowed.
SourceBridgeView's existing data buffer persists. Its original construction also
allocates a temporary prescribed flag vector and performs library searches; those
old allocation/visit behaviors are not newly qualified here. No model, publication,
coefficient or frame array is copied/cached by the production source-residual path.

Let N be free positions, C constraints, b free blocks and q native rows. Each
Enclosure owns two p1024 endpoints. New vectors use checked Layout and fallible
reserve/resize, except the explicitly separated test-only traces below. The
nine-entry capacity receipt is a fixed array, not another growing vector.

1. F1 visits every law and checks member pointer/ordinal and all coefficient
   operands. MemberEnclosures is fixed local scratch and is dropped each visit.
2. F2 retains center[N] and eta[N], returns initial residual[N enclosures], then
   creates alpha[b] and epsilon[b]. Thus these heap arrays can overlap as 4N+2b
   endpoint capacities. F2 combines the unchanged conservative eta contraction
   with a signed exact-source residual. Eta is dropped after alpha is checked.
3. Midpoint[N] overlaps center, initial residual and alpha/epsilon (4N+2b endpoint
   slots by requested lengths). Each midpoint is RN1024(lo+hi), then exact /2.
   Initial residual is dropped before the correction entry. The matching-width
   RHS overlaps midpoint during RN-even P casts, then midpoint and its cast
   context are explicitly dropped.
4. One matching retained factor borrows RHS. factor.rs is unchanged: its internal
   collect allocates permuted x[N]; its later vec allocates out[N] while x and
   RHS remain live. Those are three simultaneous P-width owners, beside center[N]
   and alpha/epsilon. Actual RHS and returned-out capacities are observed; x's
   internal allocator capacity is not measured. Its infallible collect/vec
   allocation behavior remains unqualified. A successful small witness does not
   qualify allocator failure, M1, or a large-source profile. No adaptation was
   necessary to execute this bounded witness and no factor hunk was made.
5. After the factor returns, its exact producing context is recorded and dropped;
   RHS is dropped before conversion. P-width out overlaps the new p1024 correction,
   the original p1024 center and alpha/epsilon. Widening copies L source limbs
   into a zero-initialized sixteen-limb endpoint exactly. Center is updated by
   RN1024(x+2^s z) only on data blocks; no-data free centers remain zero. P out is
   dropped on return; correction drops after center formation in non-test builds.
6. F3 overlaps center[N], residual[N enclosures] and alpha/epsilon. It independently
   rebuilds exact-source coefficients/frame and recomputes every source residual,
   including all individual free loads, constrained columns and springs. It does
   not use factor equations or the prior residual. Rho drops before production
   recovery, after epsilon is formed from the original alpha and beta=2B.
7. F4 retains center[N], alpha/epsilon, rows[q enclosures] and reactions[C
   enclosures]. It shares each member's B*y and D*B*y for H-transpose ends and
   B-transpose reactions, then emits checked station/spring/reaction/support rows.
   Center drops on successful non-test return. Result owns rows, alpha, epsilon
   and the view's data flags, borrowing the owner and proposed laws.

These are logical owners and recorded capacities, not allocator byte peaks or
compiler overlays. Layout/count/storage refusals remain separate from actual
lost-work status. Existing source-sized lookups/allocations are not hidden by
calling them zero-cost.

Fixed local scratch includes MemberEnclosures (its c; ri/p/q/geometry/A/I/J/Z/E/G
intervals; four coefficients; four admitted products; four differences; optional
represented-Z interval), and Member (five three-axis interval arrays, inv-L,
four D intervals, four delta endpoints and lower-L). Frame construction keeps
chord, y-reference, ex/ey/ez, projected transverse and cross-product triples;
length, projection, inverse and transverse/L temporaries. Member coefficient
scratch remains live while the frame is built. Each contraction has twelve
input intervals, six basic intervals, and local/global twelve-interval outputs;
recovery holds basic/local/global outputs together for scatter/stations.
Norm/signed-corner routines hold their endpoint products and comparisons;
each directed scalar owns a fresh WideContext and ExactWideSum. Sqrt owns the
existing helper's context/sum. Rust may retain additional return/copy stack
slots; no summed stack-byte claim or inherited overlay is made.

Test builds additionally retain initial residual, returned correction, actual
cast RHS, final rho and center for the receipt. The test-only actual RHS uses
collect; these diagnostic allocations are not production qualification.
Publication clone and closed-reference/control temporaries are test-owned.
The alpha-parity test deliberately overlaps the old bridge diagnostic and new
result; that overlap is separate from a future streamed facade profile.

## Arithmetic and entered work

All production source operations use fixed p1024 intervals; there is no host
hypot/sqrt, runtime pi, retry or arbitrary-precision fallback. Signed multiply
and positive-denominator signed divide actually enter all four corners in each
direction. Add/sub use corresponding endpoint pairs. Range square has zero lower
when crossing zero. DQ has its own seventh NumericWork entry and merges the
actual existing sqrt-helper AttemptWork/SumWork once on each return, including
failure. Source normalizations whose lower denominator is nonpositive refuse as
finite certificate insufficiency, not physical singularity.

The source module reports separate named loop events, coefficient/frame builds,
B/D/H products, directed entry categories, actual fixed-core work, exact-sum work,
point arithmetic, view visits/f64 work, and correction cast/factor/call/copy
categories. Heterogeneous quantities join status; they are not silently summed
into an invented price. All original numeric errors survive simultaneous work
loss and successful extraction checks joined status.

Four coefficient builds and three exact-frame builds occur per member on the
successful path. Both residual passes use signed B(48), D(10), B-transpose(48);
recovery uses B(48), D(10), H-transpose(16), B-transpose(48). Eta additionally
uses the original Bbar/Ddelta/Bbar contraction, with constrained output rows
omitted. Its original alpha is bitwise equal to the old bridge on the loaded
witness. These counts are scalar/loop descriptions, not safety uppers passed
off as actual spent work. Every failed prefix reports only entered operations.

The six-free-DOF loaded witness has actual P256 casts=6, factor calls=1,
factor Mul=30/Sub=30/Div=6, point p1024 Add=12, four member builds and three frame
builds. Actual scalar/sum entry totals are preserved in final logs. Zero skips
factor/cast work but still executes complete source recovery. An extreme RHS
fails in the real factor after Mul=15/Sub=15/Div=5. A cast-overflow control fails
after two actual rounds and before any factor call. A further control seeds
exact homogeneous work just below overflow, executes the same failed factor,
and proves numeric Exponent plus accounting loss both survive actual collection.

Core round internals, factor indexing/permutation/initialization, fixed copies,
range checks, pointer/source/map searches and other library/bit operations are
explicit additional bounded auxiliary work. Named visits are not a complete
facade visit ledger. Core arithmetic AttemptWork is retained, not reconstructed
from 2*ell Mul+2*ell Sub+N Div. Qualification/pricing of these auxiliary operations
and original allocations remains necessary before product/resource reliance.
