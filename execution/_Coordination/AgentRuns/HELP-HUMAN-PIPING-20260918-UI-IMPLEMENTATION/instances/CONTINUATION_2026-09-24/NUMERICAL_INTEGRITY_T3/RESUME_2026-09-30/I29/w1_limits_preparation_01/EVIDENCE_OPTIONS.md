No such product composition or refusal path is supplied by this packet. A per-case fit never authorizes an invocation fit; sum/max accounting must follow actual lifetimes and shared ownership. These byte candidates are revisable technical allowances, not an owner decision on supported target-machine memory, and are not RSS ceilings.
