Zmij's finite-f64 buffer and spelling branches bound total bytes by 24 and exponent digits by three. We do not assume generated numbers always take lexical's fast path.

For long parsing, scratch reconstructs mantissa digits only. parse_decimal_overflow's synthetic zeros replace consumed fractional zeros; they do not expand the explicit exponent. Consequently scratch length is at most 24 for this route. Concise parsing adjusts the exponent by consumed fraction digits and converts at most twenty u64 digits through inline itoa. Truncated parsing computes scientific_exponent and subtracts at most 24 digits. The conservative scaled interval [-1047,1023] covers both paths.

Bigint::default reserves 20 limbs. build.rs selects 64-bit limbs for aarch64. A 24-digit mantissa has at most 80 bits (two limbs). The halfway comparator derives a mantissa below 2^54 and exponent in [-1075,970] from binary64 masks, bias and bh_extended. The maximum power-five exponent is 1047; its highest indexed cached power has 38 limbs. Since initial length<=2 and 2+38<64, imul_pow5 selects only scalar in-place multiplication. The long_mul/Karatsuba allocation family is unreachable under these premises.

Using 5^n<2^(3n), theory has at most 3195 bits/50 limbs after multiplication, so capacity at most 80. The subsequent positive shift is at most 2017 bits, yielding at most 5212 bits/82 limbs and capacity at most 160. Opposite shifting of the real mantissa is at most 1074 bits, yielding 1154 bits/19 limbs within its original 20 slots. In the nonnegative decimal branch, 80+4*1023=4172 bits/66 limbs fits capacity 80. ishl_bits may append one carry, then ishl_limbs reserves the prefix and splices an exact repeat/take iterator. The stock Vec splice source explicitly leaves an empty leftover collection when that lower bound is exact; there is no third heap buffer.
