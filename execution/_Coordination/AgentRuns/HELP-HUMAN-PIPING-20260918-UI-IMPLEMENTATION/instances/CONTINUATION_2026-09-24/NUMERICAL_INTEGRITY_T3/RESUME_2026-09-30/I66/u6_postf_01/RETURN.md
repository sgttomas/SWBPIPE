## 3. RV88's U6a N-3: the seam guard's scope (repaired)

`u6_doc_hidden_seams_have_no_product_callers` now scans each product `.rs` file's **product text**: the whole file, minus only its `#[cfg(test)]`-gated items.

**How a gated item is found and dropped.**
- After each `#[cfg(test)]` that appears in code, any further attributes are skipped. The item is then dropped through:
  - its `;` or `,` at depth 0, for a `use`, `mod x;`, const or field;
  - or the brace matching its first `{`.
- **While matching, comments and literals are skipped.** That covers line comments, nested block comments, strings, raw strings with any number of `#`, byte strings and char literals including escapes. Lifetimes and labels are not mistaken for chars.
- **A gated item that never closes fails the test** and names the file, so the scan can never silently hide the rest of a file.

**What is counted.** The **bare seam name** as a whole identifier, so a path, an `as` alias or a comment naming it all count.
