## Minimal two-sided seam

Keep the numerical evaluator inside frame_kernel's retained module, adjacent to its existing source/precision/radius owner. A proposed internal `product_certificate` module contains only the F2a closed source/action/recipe grammar and its workspace. Product_physics owns normalized source truth and final-row custody. It supplies a checked, borrowed case adapter from the actual captured normalized model/material/section and final immutable rows. A local shape may be:

    RetainedSolve::check_product_certificate(case_adapter, final_rows, admitted_counts)
        -> Spent<product_verdict>

Names are illustrative. The facade is an algorithm entry point for the specific certificate, **not** add/mul/div/sqrt as a public service. The adapter yields one bounded member operand record or one actual final-row descriptor at a time; no new m-element copy of normalized source operands is required. Its methods have source-count bounds and no hidden allocation. The Rust-facing trait/type may need public visibility to cross crates, but fields/constructors and intended producer use remain restricted; visibility is not a security boundary.

The kernel side binds its actual source+ledger, selected p/policy, matching successful P=2p cache/scaling, free order/block/body map, associated upward B, validated data/zero facts, and actual row/value/radius. A final recipe asks for a closed quantity identity, not caller-supplied (value,radius). The product side binds invocation/route/case or own combination/material basis, D/effective-wall/E/nu, actual K property bits, maps, and actual immutable y/unit/id/component/location/recipe. No free proof endpoint or radius constructor is exported. Lifetimes retain owner storage; explicit identities protect two live solves with identical numerical values.

The entry point may orchestrate F0–F5 internally with the bounded adapter, or expose phase-specific consuming cursors that enforce exactly this schedule. Prefer one entry point unless actual crate borrowing needs cursors. Either way the scope uses one existing solve, not a solve clone, second operator, or second accounting pass over kernel work. The verdict is usable only for the borrowed final row snapshot; mutable publication after certification invalidates it. Work is returned on failure as well as success.

Avoid an extra b-element data Vec by factoring the **same** `bound::data_blocks` predicate into a private fill/visit operation over the admitted block tags; the legacy allocating caller can wrap that operation unchanged. This is a bounded plumbing seam, not a changed predicate or global service. Source correspondence must check individual ledger terms, nonzero prescriptions at pattern adjacency and the selected verification state; an all-zero output shortcut is forbidden.
