## 3. Actual diagonal deletion and exact primitive inequalities

K/assemble.rs:706–735 applies F03 to each diagonal with more than one tagged
contribution. It selects one absolute-minimum member/global/directional term,
omits exactly that term, and re-sums the rest. It leaves off-diagonal entries
unchanged. Stable structure construction (:590–635) tags members before springs
but also includes zero directional entries; a minimum can be zero or tied.

For **every case**, inspect global component X at node 0: Rx for torsion and Ux
for axial. There is one incident root member M1, the intended soft spring
S.N0.0, and, where present, hard springs with a nonzero component at this row.
This root coordinate is free. The script checks its primitive member/spring and
constraint identities, separately from the reported factor-screen coordinate.

Let the exact root chord be d, its length L, and a=d_x/L>0. CHAIN is axis-aligned;
the SKEW chords are exactly (3,4,0), L=5, or (1,2,2), L=3. These primitive lengths
and axial normalizations need no geometry approximation in the mathematical
bound. The actual retained length is also exactly 3 or 5 on those integer
SKEW chords, and CHAIN root lengths are exact dyadics. E/G/A/J are lifted from
their exact binary64 primitive bits.

The root member diagonal contains the nonnegative axial/torsion contribution

    D0 = (GJ/L) a^2     for Rx,
    D0 = (EA/L) a^2     for Ux,

plus nonnegative bending contributions. Its **formed retained** member term is
at least D0/32 at every attempted precision p≥128:

- the positive formed axial/torsion coefficient is at least half its exact value;
- each formed e_x component is at least half its exact positive value;
- DB's one rounded product and the final KE rounded sum each preserve at least
  half of that positive contribution;
- this gives (1/2)(1/2)^2(1/2)(1/2)=1/32.

This deliberately coarse bound is justified directly by the actual B/DB/KE
code (:298–349), not a solver emulation. At these root diagonals the axial/
torsion product and each bending self-term have a common sign before their
final square/product, so another term cannot cancel the retained positive
contribution. The same input magnitudes are far from retained exponent/span
limits. Rounding-to-nearest of a positive normal retained number is within a
factor two; these are vastly looser factors than its p-bit unit roundoff.

For S.N0.0, a global-axis term is exactly k. A directional term is
`s=k n_x^2/(n·n)`. Actual form_directional (:495–514) forms this with exact
small-integer direction products, exact lifted k times those products at p≥128,
then one retained division. Thus its formed diagonal lies in [s/2,2s], and is
strictly positive whenever n_x≠0. Every named soft root here has n_x≠0.
Any additional contributing hard directional term has the same positive lower
bound s_h/2; global-axis terms are exact.

Exact Fraction comparisons from the raw primitives establish for all 66 rows:

    soft_upper < member_lower,
    soft_upper < every other contributing hard_spring_lower.

The smallest member_lower/soft_upper ratio over the full roster is exactly

    13441051728854037109375 / 387165452765786800128 > 34.

There is therefore ample separation despite the coarse rounding factors.
**S.N0.0 is the unique positive minimum at root X in all 66 cases.** This is
a statement about an actual formed contribution under the source inequalities,
not a guess from an output row or a claim that every dropped term is a spring.
The attached ROOT_DIAGONAL_INEQUALITIES.json records each bound and original
case/source identity. No private factor value or numerical solve was recreated.

For a directional spring F03 deletes its root-X **diagonal contribution** only;

## 4. CHAIN: formed-matrix consequence independent of the reported pivot

For every CHAIN torsion/axial case, let t_i>0 be the **actual formed** GJ/L or
EA/L of member i in its decoupled X block. Axis alignment makes each member's

precision: multiplying by the exact ±1 axis introduces no further discrepancy.
The root has two contributions t_1 and k, with k uniquely smaller (§3), so F03
leaves t_1. Each interior node has two positive member contributions t_i,t_(i+1)
and F03 leaves exactly one, namely their maximum. The last diagonal is t_n.
The off-diagonal entries remain −t_i.

The constant free X vector z=(1,...,1) therefore has the **exact formed-matrix**
Rayleigh expression

    z^T K_F03 z = −sum_(interior i) min(t_i,t_(i+1)) < 0.

No initial rounded diagonal sum survives: after deletion each diagonal above
has one term, and each cross-entry has one term. This remains true for tied
minima; dropping either equal positive term has the same result. The original
root-spring energy k has been removed, and at least two interior losses exist.
The source has thus destroyed positive definiteness of the very axial/torsion
soft-root system. An SPD factor screen cannot correctly accept that matrix.
