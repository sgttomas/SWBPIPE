### 5.2 The lifts

Each coefficient is an intermediate of nonzero finite operands divided by Lⁿ:

| Coefficient | Intermediate | Lift |
|---|---|---|
| EA/L | E·A | 1/L |
| GJ/L | G·J | 1/L |
| 4EI/L, 2EI/L | (4·E)·I, (2·E)·I | 1/L |
| 6EI/L² | (6·E)·I | 1/L² |
| 12EI/L³ | (12·E)·I | 1/L³ |

- With L > 2^-39.863 (§5.1): 1/L < 2^39.863, 1/L² < 2^79.73 and 1/L³ < 2^119.59.
- **Exact zero.** Under round-to-nearest-even, a product rounds to 0 exactly when its exact value is at most 2^-1075 in magnitude (the tie rounds to the even 0). So a zeroed intermediate has |exact| ≤ 2^-1075, and the zeroed coefficient's intended value is at most 2^-1075/Lⁿ:
  - 1/L: < 2^-1035.14;
  - 1/L²: < 2^-995.27;
  - 1/L³: < 2^-955.41.
- **Nonzero subnormal intermediate** p, with 0 < |p| < 2^-1022: the coefficient p/Lⁿ is formed normal, or subnormal, with |p/Lⁿ| < 2^-1022/Lⁿ:
  - 1/L: < 2^-982.14;
  - 1/L²: < 2^-942.27;
  - 1/L³: < 2^-902.41.

  Its relative error is up to 2^-1075/|p|, which is 100 % at the least subnormal.
- **The 2/L relation.** The intended values satisfy 12EI/L³ = (2/L)·(6EI/L²) exactly, because 12·E = 2·(6·E) exactly. The formed values need not: rounding in the subnormal range is absolute. The 12EI/L³ defect therefore dominates the 6EI/L² defect by 2/L only when L < 2 m. For L ≥ 2 m both are tiny in absolute terms (< 2^-1078 when zeroed), but not relative to an arbitrarily small ground spring. There the limit is K-D5's trigger (§5.6).

### 5.3 M03's element-entry floor, and what it does not bound

- **The floor.** `transform_roundoff` (FK `structural.rs:1832-1866`) is called for every frame on the product route, in `AssemblyEvidence::new`. It forms bounds[i][j] = g·(inherited/(1 − g) + second)·(1 + 64ε), with g = `gamma(24)` = 24u/(1 − 24u), u = 2^-53, so g ≈ 2^-48.415. It then applies `checked_value`, which refuses a subnormal result (`structural.rs:393-399`), and `checked_product` (`:377-384`), which refuses a zero or subnormal product of nonzero operands.
  - For an entry c of an axis-aligned element (one nonzero rotation entry of magnitude 1 per row), inherited = second = |c|, so the bound is about 2g·|c| = 2^-47.415·|c|. It is subnormal, and refused as `Range("arithmetic outside normal range")`, when 0 < |c| < 2^-974.585.
  - A subnormal c is refused earlier, at `checked_product`, as `Range("product overflow or underflow")`.
  - For a rotated element the same test applies to each transformed entry's magnitude sum, which is of the same order.
  - **Measured:** `main_threshold_scan.jsonl` refuses at a smallest element entry of 2^-981.8 and accepts at 2^-971.8.
  - **Exact zero entries are exempt:** a zero operand is not refused.
- **What the floor does not bound.** The floor applies to element entries only. **Ground springs are added to K directly and are not subject to it.** Main accepted ground springs down to 2^-1015 (`main_gj_probe.jsonl`, `main_six2_probe.jsonl`). So the floor does not bound the relative effect of a zeroed coefficient against the retained stiffness of its DOF. That effect is limited on the linear route only by K-D5's trigger (§5.6).

### 5.7 K2a's benefit and cost on the product route (ROOT, correction 2 ruling 3; correction 3)

- **Benefit.** K2a corrects no trusted published value on the product route. Its value is a **formation-layer guarantee, independent of M03 and K-D5.** Every zeroed or subnormal-derived coefficient is refused at formation, earlier and by name. This includes the reach_zero and reach_lef cases, which main publishes wrong (untrusted) and flags only downstream. It also protects every `local_stiffness` consumer that M03 does not cover (§4):
  - SP `recover_local_forces` and `bending_formation_bound`;
  - PP `source_recovery::prepare_sources` and `source_receipt` commitments;
  - the performance harness and the validation benchmarks, which assemble and solve without M03;

### 5.9 Other stiffness-forming paths with 1/L² or 1/L³ terms (T3-close item; not probed or fixed)

From the caller scan and reading only:
- **curved_bend** `CurvedBendMacroElement::end_flexibility` / `local_stiffness` (`curved_bend/src/lib.rs:206-252`). It forms unchecked binary64 rigidities E·I, G·J and E·A (`:210-212`) and a flexibility R·Σ quad(…)/rigidity. The unit-load actions carry R and R² terms, so the flexibility scales as R³/EI. It then inverts (`invert_symmetric6`), so the stiffness scales as EI/R³: a 1/R³ lift. A zeroed rigidity gives a division by zero there. There is no intermediate check.
- **K-D5's `formation_check.rs`** frame and curved re-formation in `Wide<2>` (extended exponent). It is not limited by binary64 range, but its binary64 conversions of inputs and outputs are outside K2a.
- **Load formations (not stiffness).** SP distributed-load fixed-end terms (`straight_pipe/src/lib.rs:1404-1414`) and PP self-weight carry length powers; these are S11 territory.
- User-stiffness elements, linear springs and the gap supports have no length lift.
