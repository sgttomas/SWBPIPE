# Independent baseline reference

For the invented uniform annular pipe, derive metal area by subtracting disk areas: A=π(Do²−Di²)/4, Di=Do−2t. With explicitly entered density rho and signed acceleration g, q=rho*A*g. Dimensions are (kg/m³)(m²)(m/s²)=N/m. The density/acceleration/newton dimensions are checked against [NIST SP811 chapter4](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-chapter-4-two-classes-si-units-and-si-prefixes), tables2–3; no obsolete base-kilogram definition is relied on.

For a straight horizontal cantilever of length L with uniform q in global Y and a tip force P, external resultant is qL+P and external moment about the fixed origin is qL²/2+PL in global Z. Support reactions oppose these. This follows directly by integrating q dx and x q dx and summing the tip force/moment, consistent with [MIT statics force/resultant and moment equilibrium](https://web.mit.edu/4.441/1_lectures/1_lecture16/1_lecture16.html). It does not use a production mass helper or stiffness result as oracle.

The baseline uses Do0.1m, t0.01m, rho1000kg/m³, explicit g=-7m/s² and P=-11N at L2m. Density doubling must double only the generated distributed weight; length change to3m changes weight force linearly and its first moment quadratically. The manual tip force remains11N. Existing product output rounds to six decimal places, so the retained force/moment comparator accounts for that documented publication resolution; the large stale-load discrepancy is not a rounding artifact. No tolerance is changed during repair.

These equilibrium identities remain appropriate for this small-displacement, uniform straight member and are independent of a newer beam stiffness model. Large deformation, nonuniform/curved density distribution, pressure force conventions or new layered mass recipes require their own source/formulation checks and are outside this baseline. No newer theory is needed to identify stale explicit density; this does not qualify those other physical capabilities.
