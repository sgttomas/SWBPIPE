## Accepted design facts

1. “Exposure” is a write to filesystem, stdout, browser download, DOM/iframe
   preview, print surface, downstream handoff, or process/UI boundary.
   In-process builders may construct diagnostic representations, but callers
   may not expose their raw result.
2. Each route uses an explicit metadata projection, the existing control, and
   a schema/format-aware materializer. Container metadata must never stop
   traversal of nested value carriers.
3. Structured artifacts may materialize redacted values. Specialized
   text/binary formats are withheld when a redaction/omission would make the
   format misleading or invalid; explicit local-private intent may retain
   private values with warnings where the route is classified local-private.
4. Existing unit, provenance, diagnostics, sandbox, protected-content/report,
   hash, and professional-boundary gates compose with redaction and are never
   weakened or replaced.
5. Plugin and bug-report routes are future assertions only. The DEC-059
   public-source release exporter remains separately governed and unchanged.
