# Owner engineering direction V2

> Is there a reason why you can exercise your own judgment for those engineering choices? All of the outputs from this app will be validated against industry standard solvers, and we can even do that in a testing and validation phase. So make good judgments from the necessary engineering perspective.

Root accepts this current Owner act as explicit authority for agents to make defensible engineering model and method selections within the selected tranche. Choices must be grounded in vetted primary engineering evidence, state assumptions and limits, receive independent refutation before source effect, and identify future validation against industry-standard solvers. That future validation is planned and has not passed.

This direction removes automatic escalation solely because a choice was previously held as an engineering decision. Root will escalate a consequential tradeoff that requires Owner priorities or a choice for which the evidence cannot support a defensible judgment. It does not retroactively cure prior acts, constitute a per-choice Owner ruling, select a source fence, update a formal decision register, or change decomposition, DAG, dependency, lifecycle, public-contract, or acceptance state.

The standing Git authority and the requirement that every Agent 1 and Agent 2 use `gpt-5.6-sol` with high reasoning continue unchanged. No `gpt-6-astra` exception is granted.
