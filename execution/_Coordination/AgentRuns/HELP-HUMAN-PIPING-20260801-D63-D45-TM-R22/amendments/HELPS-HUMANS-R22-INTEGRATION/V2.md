# Plan amendment V2 — correct N4 role and write ownership

**Run:** `HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260801-D63-D45-TM-R22`
**SelectionAuthority:** `HUMAN`
**Classification:** non-consequential control-plane role/write-ownership
correction within exact accepted scope
**Integration owner:** `HELPS_HUMANS`
**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Trigger

Version 1 combined two serialized acts in `N4_D62_CURRENCY_REPAIR` under
`HELPS_HUMANS`: promotion of the owner-directed Decisions-domain row in the
Piping Task Management register, and the narrow D-62 decision-register
currency repair. That assignment conflicts with K-TM ownership: the invoking
loop's `TASK_MANAGEMENT` role owns writes to its Action Item register, while a
different manager may own the cited decision surface.

## Exact correction

Replace former `N4_D62_CURRENCY_REPAIR` with two serialized nodes:

1. `N4A_TM_D62_PROMOTION`, owned by `TASK_MANAGEMENT`, depends on completed
   `N3_D45_CODIFICATION`, and may write only
   `execution/_Coordination/_TaskManagement/REGISTER.csv` plus its unique
   `instances/N4A_TM_D62_PROMOTION/` records. It performs only the
   owner-directed promotion of the Decisions-domain row with source
   provenance and no authority effect.
2. `N4B_D62_CURRENCY_REPAIR`, owned by `HELPS_HUMANS`, depends on completed
   `N4A_TM_D62_PROMOTION`, and may write only the D-62 row text in
   `execution/_Coordination/_DECISIONS/_REGISTER.md` plus its unique
   `instances/N4B_D62_CURRENCY_REPAIR/` records. It performs only the directed
   register-currency correction without reopening, rewriting, or
   reinterpreting D-62.

`N5_TM_CLOSURE_NOTICE` now depends on `N4B_D62_CURRENCY_REPAIR`. `N4A` is
`READY`; `N4B` and all later nodes remain `PENDING`. The graph remains fully
serialized.
