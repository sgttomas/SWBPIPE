## Ruling

**The lifecycle-semantics amendment is adopted:** `docs/TYPES.md` §9 is
amended to the governed change-control model with layered `CHECKING` entry,
per the reference formulation in repo-root
`docs/DELIVERABLE_CONCORDANCE_METHOD.md` §4 (ratified 2026-07-11) —
lifecycle states as governed production and change-control regimes with
maturity/readiness entry conditions, not percentage-complete scores;
`IN_PROGRESS` as ordinary authorized edits and the honest holding state
whenever warranted open scope exists; `CHECKING` as a frozen candidate under
review against a declared basis, reversal to `IN_PROGRESS` the only edit
path; layered `CHECKING` entry (warranted-empty `_STATUS.md` `## Remaining`
with a current evidence basis bound to the candidate source state + a
candidate-specific declared checking basis + human declaration and freeze);
no disclosed-deferral carve-outs (rescope via the decision register before
freeze); failed checks exit by reversal with findings becoming `## Remaining`
items; rebaseline asymmetry (demotion needs no criteria beyond absence of a
current accepted basis, promotion needs a contemporary declared basis,
corrections are human-authorized administrative acts through the register);
`ISSUED` as accepted baseline changed only through the governed scope-change
process. `docs/AGENTIC_DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md` receives the consistency
edits aligning its phase table, heuristics, Phase 4, and Phase 7 wording to
the amended §9.

## Scope not granted

concordance work; no `CHECKING` → `ISSUED` transitions; the suspension of
piping work is unaffected.
