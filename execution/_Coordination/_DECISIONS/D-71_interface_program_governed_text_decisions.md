# D-71 — the interface program's nine governed-text decisions

Status: **PROPOSAL / AWAITING_RULING**, 2026-09-18. Prepared by HELP_HUMAN Agent 0 of the run [`HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/). No option takes effect without the owner's ruling. Form follows [D-69](D-69_ui_foundation_performance_disposition.md): decision and recommendation, evidence and consequence, why this is an owner decision, on-ruling mechanism, bound evidence. Nine items, each ruled separately; the owner may accept a recommendation, choose the other option, amend, or defer, item by item.

The redesign of the SWB Piping Designer interface (formerly OpenPipeStress) was accepted as a direction on 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-18 ([direction record](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/ROOT/DIRECTION_DECISION_2026-09-17.md) §2, §8 to §11). Nine of its choices touch governed text, registered vocabulary or a classification the governance leaves open. The design program inventoried exactly what binds each item ([RESEARCH-F](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/RESEARCH/F_packet_bindings.md), every statement cited to file and line) and drew the fifteen mock frames that show where each text would appear ([frames](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/index.html), [screenshots](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/shots/)). Nothing here is an implementation instruction: implementation of the redesign stays behind the baseline characterization tranche under [D-70](D-70_RULING_2026-09-17.md).

Three kinds of act appear below, in RESEARCH-F's words: a **confirmation** (no governed text changes; the ruling records a reading), a **registry act** (a change to ruled `DEC-081` vocabulary in `docs/claims_registry.md`: a `DEC` row codified from this ruling), and a **PRD-level act** (a change to PRD text on the scope-change path, SCA-007 being the precedent). Items 6 and 9 need no act at all and are included so the owner sees the choice the design made.

## Decision and recommendation, item by item

### Item 1 — where the maturity line lives

**Binds:** registry `BS-MATURITY`, "Technical preview — not a released product.", "Belongs on: the app shell banner/footer" (F §1.1); the lint anchor `MISSING_MATURITY_BANNER` checks that the sentence is present anywhere in `App.tsx` (F §1.2). The sentence itself changes only when the stage record changes; this item does not touch the sentence.

**The design asked:** whether an on-demand popover satisfies "banner/footer". The frames put the sentence in the status bar's information popover ([`s1_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s1_table_light.html)) and nowhere permanent.

**Options.** A — the status bar carries the sentence permanently at its right end in the secondary text style, and About repeats it; the 24 px status bar is the shell's footer, so the registry clause is met as written. B — popover and About only, with the registry's placement clause revised to allow an on-demand placement.

**Recommendation: A**, a confirmation. One short sentence in a bar the engineer already reads costs nothing the design cares about and keeps the maturity visible, which is the clause's purpose. B buys 200 px of status bar for a registry act. The design system and frames are revised to draw A.

### Item 2 — one visible placement of the acceptance sentence per surface class

**Binds:** registry `BS-ACCEPT`, canonical text and four listed short variants, "Belongs on: results, rule-check, comparison, solve, report-preview surfaces; any place a computed outcome is shown" (F §2.1). Today the sentence sits on 53 lines in 21 product files, each with a test assertion (F §2.3); D-48 §3.4 records that placement is registry matter and no PRD change is implied (F §2.5).

**The design asked:** whether a disclosure that must be opened counts as the surface carrying the sentence. The frames carry the canonical text once in the results header's information disclosure ([`s7_both_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s7_both_light.html)) and once at the top of the Review page ([`s9_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s9_table_light.html)); the report carries it in its notice section.

**Options.** A — three homes, each visible without a click: the results header carries listed short variant 2 ("acceptance and professional judgment remain with the responsible engineer") as a one-line caption under the run identity, the Review page carries the canonical text once at its top, the report carries it once; no other product surface carries it (not the inspector, the probe, the canvas, the agent panel, the Model or Loads pages). B — as A, but the results-surface placement stays inside the information disclosure, with the registry's placement clause revised to say that an on-demand disclosure counts.

**Recommendation: A**, a confirmation that one visible placement per surface class satisfies "belongs on … any place a computed outcome is shown", which requires presence, not repetition. The 53 placements collapse to three at implementation; each removal moves a test (F §2.2 point 14), which is ordinary tranche work. The design system's disclosure home for M-02 changes from the disclosure to the visible caption.

### Item 3 — the product name

**Binds** (F §3.1, §3.2): the PRD §19.3 required report notice, whose first word is the name (changes "require a PRD-level owner act", `report_notice_template.md:37-38`); registry `BS-IP`'s canonical text, which carries the name (its two short variants do not); `DEC-089`'s App ID description "OpenPipeStress Technical Preview" and bundle ID; the seven governance document titles; and an identity layer that is not prose: 46 schema `$id` URLs under `openpipestress.org`, 61 `openpipestress.*` document-kind constants asserted by `const` and by tests, package, crate and binary names. The lint anchors (`MISSING_PRD_NOTICE`, `MISSING_RENDERER_NOTICE`) match fragments that do not contain the name. `SWBPIPE` appears today in no product, schema, packaging or governance file (F §3.3). No trademark or naming clearance record exists; the legal-review authority is `PB-TBD-004` (F §3.6).

**The design needs:** "SWB Piping Designer" as the product name in the window title, About, the report identity block and every product string; "SWBPIPE" as the wordmark and file badge; the old name only inside a registered sentence quoted by ID until that sentence is replaced. Every frame carries the new name and none the old.

**Options.** A — scope the rename to the display layer now and defer the identity layer: (i) a PRD-level act on the scope-change path replacing the first word of the §19.3 notice, so that it reads "SWB Piping Designer is decision-support software for piping design, flexibility, and stress-analysis workflows." with the rest unchanged, and retitling the PRD; (ii) a registry act replacing `BS-IP`'s canonical text with "SWB Piping Designer ships no protected standards content. All code-specific values, tables, allowables, and factors are supplied by the user or user-controlled private sources, with provenance recorded." and the template's metadata slot with "SWB Piping Designer version or commit basis"; (iii) a packaging act changing the App ID description to "SWB Piping Designer Technical Preview" and leaving the bundle identifier as `DEC-089` fixed it; (iv) the schema namespace, document-kind constants, crate, package and binary names unchanged until a separate schema-compatibility act, since changing them breaks every emitted document's identity for no user-facing gain; (v) the other governance documents take the new name in their titles at their next amendment, not by a bulk rewrite; historical records never change. B — a full rename of both layers in one act. C — defer the rename; the redesign ships under the old name.

**Recommendation: A.** It gives the product its name everywhere a person reads it and leaves the machine identity stable. Before (i) is executed the owner should state whether a naming clearance is required under `PB-TBD-004`; the design program treats "SWB Piping Designer / SWBPIPE" as the owner's working name until then.

### Item 4 — short status labels, the evidence labels, and where "Human review required" shows

**Binds** (F §4.1, §4.2, §4.5): `TYPES.md` §4 fixes the six automatic tokens and bars five others; `analysis_status.schema.yaml` requires `human_review_required: const true` on every status envelope, a data obligation; `PROFESSIONAL_BOUNDARY` §6 lists the distinctions product language must preserve as prose ("Mechanics solved | User-rule checked | Human review required | Human accepted for project"); the registry defines the two evidence labels `INTERNALLY_VERIFIED` and `PROVER_CORRELATED` and reserves `ENGINEER_ACCEPTED`. No registry entry, listed variant or governed sentence gives a token a display form, and no governed sentence requires "Human review required" to be rendered on every surface (F §4.5, the absence found by search). Today the workspace status pills render two curated labels, "Review required" for `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and "Inputs needed" for `RULE_INPUTS_INCOMPLETE`, and a lowercased de-underscored form of the other four tokens, with the raw token beneath each; seven Vitest assertions pin that displayed text (F §4.2 point 14, §4.3, difference D-7). The evidence labels are not emitted by any product component (F §4.3).

**The design needs:** a short label per token with the raw token one click away and the authority domain beside it ([`s1_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s1_table_light.html) status bar); two chips after a solve, one per authority domain; no chip when the model is complete but unsolved; "Human review required" on the Review page ([`s9_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s9_table_light.html)) and in the report; the evidence label beside the run name when evidence exists.

**Options.** A — a registry act adding a display-form table to `claims_registry.md` §2: `MODEL_INCOMPLETE` "Model incomplete", `MECHANICS_SOLVED` "Mechanics solved", `RULE_INPUTS_INCOMPLETE` "Rule inputs incomplete", `USER_RULE_CHECKED` "User-rule checked", `USER_RULE_FAILED` "User-rule failed", `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` "Human review required", `INTERNALLY_VERIFIED` "Internally verified", `PROVER_CORRELATED` "Prover correlated"; with three rules: a label is never shown without its token reachable in place, a label is always shown with its authority domain, and no label exists outside the table (no seventh status, no "Engineer accepted"). The two hyphenated forms follow `PROFESSIONAL_BOUNDARY` §6's own spelling. The registered forms replace the product's two curated labels; "Review required" is not kept because it drops the word that carries the distinction §6 preserves. The seven pinned assertions move with the labels at implementation. And a confirmation that the display obligation for `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` is met by the Review page chip and the report, the results surfaces carrying the distinction through the acceptance sentence (item 2); that an absent chip when the model is complete but unsolved is consistent with M-08, which governs vocabulary and not presence; and that the evidence chip ships only when a run record carries evidence, so M-09 becomes implemented rather than a registry rule with no product behind it. B — leave short labels as ungoverned microcopy under the claims lint, with the same display rules stated only in the design system.

**Recommendation: A.** Labels that a professional reads on every solve deserve to be ruled vocabulary, and the registry already has the shape for it. The design system and the UX specification change "User rules checked" and "User rule failed" to the hyphenated forms.

### Item 5 — vendor hanger tables as a library class

**Binds** (F §5.1, §5.2): `DEC-049` (user-entered, code-neutral, no catalog sizing); SCA-009 Vocabulary Annex §1.4 and item 23 (spring-hanger selection over user-imported libraries only; "No hanger catalog is bundled"); `BS-IP`'s placement rule for import and library surfaces; `OPS-K-DATA-1`, `OPS-K-IP-1`. The source has moved past the annex's citation: `LibraryKind::Hanger`, `hanger.schema.yaml` and a `HangerSelectionPanel` already exist (F §0.3 difference D-5), though no decision or coverage-ledger row records that extension (F uncertainty 5), and the hanger-selection surface carries no `BS-IP` text (F §5.3).

**The design needs:** hanger tables imported like materials and sections, with the seven provenance fields, and a hanger table under Results that is empty of sizes until a library is imported ([`s8_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s8_table_light.html)).

**Recommendation: confirm the framing**, a confirmation ruling: vendor hanger tables are a user-imported library class under `BS-IP` (M-05), never bundled; the confirmation names the landed `LibraryKind::Hanger` and schema as the extension the annex anticipated, so the `DEL-07-09` coverage ledger can record it; and the hanger-selection surface carries the `BS-IP` short variant at implementation, ordinary alignment work. No amendment.

### Item 6 — the historical-run wording

**Binds:** nothing in the registry or the governance documents; the wording is component copy in `HistoricalRunContext.tsx` (F §6.1). The constraints sheet had taken a source comment ("reopening cannot establish a current solve basis") for rendered text; the component renders the title "Historical saved run" and two sentences: "Run a fresh solve to establish current results." and "Historical results cannot drive current overlays, rule checks, comparisons or report readiness." (F §0.3 difference D-4).

**Recommendation: use the rendered text**, no act. The results header band reads "Historical saved run · Run a fresh solve to establish current results." and its popover carries the second sentence; the source comment's phrase is not used. The design system's tooltip and the UX specification's §10.4 are corrected accordingly. Recorded here so the owner sees the choice; silence lets it stand.

### Item 7 — the Checked mark

**Binds** (F §7.1, §7.2): PRD §16.3 permits names, tags and notes as state labelling and bars an enforced status lifecycle; PRD §21.3 keeps any human acceptance record separate from solver, rule, comparison and report outputs and requires no acceptance workflow in the MVP; `OPS-K-AUTH-2` binds any acceptance record to hashes; `PROFESSIONAL_BOUNDARY` §7 lists five requirements on any stored acceptance record; the registry reserves `ENGINEER_ACCEPTED` for a future acceptance record. No code carries a Checked affordance today.

**The design needs:** a mark the engineer sets on a row, or on many rows, after checking work whoever authored it; bound to the row's content so it lapses visibly on change; a filter for unchecked rows; never a status and never exported ([`s4_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s4_table_light.html), the state slot and its tooltip). The owner has already settled that the mark stays (direction record §8 (d)); what is open is its standing and its words.

**Options.** A — a classification ruling: the mark is a PRD §16.3 tag, human-authored, per row, recording who and when and a hash of the row's content, shown stale when the content changes, set and cleared by the engineer only, carried in the project's interface state and not in the model payload, never emitted in any status, never written to an export or a run record, and never rendered in a report's fixed sections or its review/signoff block; it is not the §21.3 acceptance record, `ENGINEER_ACCEPTED` stays reserved and `PB-TBD-002` is not triggered. Its words: the control "Check", the mark "Checked", the tooltip "Checked by name · date time · bound to this row's content · not a software status", stale "the row changed since · Check again or Clear", the filter "Unchecked rows". B — classify it as a human acceptance record under §21.3, which attaches `PROFESSIONAL_BOUNDARY` §7's five requirements and the open storage workflow of `PB-TBD-002` before it can exist.

**Recommendation: A.** The mark's value is that it is cheap and honest: a working aid for checking an agent's or a colleague's rows, visibly lapsing when the row changes. Making it an acceptance record would give it a weight the owner has said it must not carry and would block it on an open TBD.

### Item 8 — agent feedback on the Review page

**Binds** (F §8.1, §8.2): `PROFESSIONAL_BOUNDARY` §3's agent-output row permits five classes, "drafts, proposals, evidence summaries, checks, and open issues", and bars agent output presented as accepted work or approval; `OPS-K-AGENT-4`; PRD §11.8 FR-AGENT-001 to 005, the last a Must; a runtime guard with seven blocking patterns over agent rationale text; the `BS-ACCEPT` surface class is "any place a computed outcome is shown", and no governed text names a Review page. `DEC-042` and `DEC-091` hold any live agent binding (F §8.5).

**The design needs:** a Review page where the agent's checks and open issues appear as comment cards by reference, never altering a table; agent-drafted report text that the engineer inserts or discards; the acceptance sentence once at the page's top ([`s9_table_light`](../AgentRuns/HELP-HUMAN-PIPING-20260916-UI-DESIGN-PROGRAM/instances/MOCKS/frames/s9_table_light.html)).

**Recommendation: two confirmations**, no amendment. First, the wording envelope: every agent card on the Review page carries one of the five class words as its label, "Check", "Open issue", "Draft", "Proposal" or "Evidence summary" (the design's agent-authored "Note" is renamed "Evidence summary"); the runtime guard's seven patterns apply to comment and draft text as they apply to rationale; the words accepted, approved, verified, correct and compliant never appear in agent text. Second, the Review page is inside the existing `BS-ACCEPT` surface class because it shows computed outcomes as live result blocks, so its one placement needs no clause change. Shipping a live agent remains a separate owner stage act under `DEC-042`; the design specifies the seam only.

### Item 9 — what the export is called before compatibility evidence exists

**Binds** (F §9.1, §9.2): PRD §21.1's permitted claims do not include interoperability; `software_makes_caepipe_compatibility_claim` is `"const": false` in two schemas, two producers, two panels and three tests, so no surface can assert compatibility without a schema change; M-04's supplement is automatic when a report includes export metadata; `IP_AND_DATA_BOUNDARY` bars commercial examples and templates without permission; naming clearance is `PB-TBD-004`.

**The design needs:** an export that produces a model batch file in the documented CAEPIPE grammar, described by what it writes, drops and transforms, with a loss report (UX specification §8.3), and a name for the dialog and the menu.

**Recommendation: name it by what it produces**, no act: the command "Export model batch file (.mbf)…", the dialog title "Model batch file (.mbf)", one line under it "Written in the CAEPIPE model batch file grammar as documented in the vendor's manual", the rendered flag "Compatibility with CAEPIPE: not claimed; no evidence recorded", and the `BS-VALID` short variant once. The existing panel already takes this posture. Two things are deferred, not decided here: an interoperability evidence standard, which would be a PRD-level act adding a claim class to §21.1 and is premature before a real round trip exists; and naming clearance under `PB-TBD-004`, which the owner may raise with the legal-review authority when one is named.

## Evidence and practical consequence

The inventory behind every "binds" line is RESEARCH-F, 2,002 lines, 96 enforcement points, nine differences between the constraints sheet and its sources tabulated in its §0.3 (the constraints sheet cited a stale footer line, took a source comment for rendered text, and had not seen the landed hanger kind). The frames are the fifteen accepted mock drafts, one sample model through every state, in both themes; each shows the whole shell so the placement of every text is seen in context. The UX specification is the behaviour the implementers would build and names, in its §9.1, the surface, trigger and wording source of all seventeen mandatory disclosures.

If the recommendations are ruled as proposed: five items are confirmations (1, 2, 5, 8 and the second half of 4), two are registry acts (3(ii) and 4), one is a PRD-level act on the scope-change path (3(i)) plus a packaging act (3(iii)), one is a classification ruling (7), and two need no act (6, 9). None changes the engine, a schema or a test now; all of it becomes tranche work behind the D-70 baseline when the redesign is implemented. The heaviest execution consequence is item 2 (53 placements to three, each with a test) and item 3's display-layer rename (188 product files carry the name today, of which the prose occurrences change and the identifiers do not).

What the packet does not do: it makes no claim about the product, releases nothing, changes no scope item, does not promote vocabulary item 23, and does not reopen `DEC-042`, `DEC-089` or `DEC-091` except where an option says so.

## Why this is an owner decision

The registry is ruled vocabulary under `DEC-081`; only the human project authority rules on it (`_REGISTER.md:6-15`). The §19.3 notice changes only by a PRD-level owner act (`report_notice_template.md:37-38`). The Checked mark's standing turns on a classification the PRD leaves to a future record (§21.3). The design program can recommend and prepare; it cannot confirm a reading of governed text or rename the product.

## On-ruling mechanism

The owner rules item by item, in chat or on this packet. Agent 0 then appends the ruling verbatim to this packet as its Human Ruling section, writes the `D-71` ruling record, flips the register row, and codifies each ruled registry act and classification as the next free `DEC` rows in `SOFTWARE_DECOMP.md` §12. For item 3(i) Agent 0 prepares the scope-change bundle (brief, impact assessment, amendment preview, amendment actions) on the SCA-007 pattern for the owner's separate acceptance; nothing in §19.3 changes until that acceptance. The design system, the UX specification and the frames are revised to the rulings in the program's next revision pass. Cross-loop notice: RESEARCH-F found no other project loop pinning any of the nine texts; the piping session, which owns implementation, receives a notice of the rulings on the form of the D-70 notices. Silence on an item leaves the current governed text and the design's conditional placement in force, except items 6 and 9, which proceed as recommended because no act is needed.
