## Adopted bounded effect

1. **Item 1 accepted.** The five D-68 numeric limits and the settled-frame and owned-resource obligations carry to the redesigned product unchanged. Interaction latency and Chromium-reported presentation intervals are the gated quantities; main-frame trace work and GPU-thread trace evidence are recorded and never gate; presentation feedback is not physical scanout; no observer overhead is subtracted; there is no CPU-only gate.
2. **Item 2 accepted**, read with the owner's stated intent that the product work on a laptop's own display and on an external monitor. Reference host, pinned browser and production build as the packet states; the laptop's internal display is the gated reference; device pixel ratio 2, Light appearance and Comfortable density gate; the external monitor in its 60 Hz mode, device pixel ratio 1 and the Dark theme are each measured once per size by the same method as observations that are published and do not gate.
3. **Item 3 accepted**, with the same intent: window 1440 × 900; the two canvas configurations (Both view 603 × 828, Model view 1000 × 828); boundary recording of canvas size, pixel ratio and rendered label count; label populations Off and Budget with caps of 138 and 230.
4. **Item 4 accepted**, with the owner's gloss: "we need not pin ourselves to previous test results or methods.  Those are just evidence to know what was the case before changes are made." The shared core workload and the redesigned-only workloads with their perception limits stand. The earlier records are a before-picture and nothing more.
5. **Item 5 NOT RULED.** No cohort size or pass rule is in force. The packet's supplement gives the rationale the owner asked for and proposes a lighter plan (S-1 repeat only when a result is within 20 % of its limit; S-2 no fresh baseline cohort; S-3 a gated latency check on the external monitor, and the separate Dark and pixel-ratio-1 runs dropped). S-2 and S-3 would refine accepted items 2 to 4 and need the owner's word like S-1.
6. **Item 6 accepted.** No number is frozen for deformation animation, ghosted proposals, large-selection halos, theme switch with results, first coloured paint, per-node colour, memory or cold start until the behaviour exists and can be observed; a supplementary proposal is ruled before it is used. Implementing those features does not wait for measuring them.

## What this does and does not do

The accepted criteria are frozen as of this ruling; a later change is a new owner act made before the runs it applies to. Nothing can be qualified yet, because item 5 is open and because the redesigned product does not exist. The original D-70 failures, the successor demonstration and their separate attribution are unchanged. The independent-usability holds PDU-045 and PDU-046, the WCAG 2.2 AA criteria for touched controls and the redesign's functional acceptance are outside this ruling. No implementation, release, lifecycle promotion or minimum-hardware statement follows.
