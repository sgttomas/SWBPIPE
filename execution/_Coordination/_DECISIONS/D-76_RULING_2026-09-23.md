# D-76 ruling — nonlinear-suite moment units are N·mm

## Owner act

After the `D-75` translation-delta correction, the owner was told that the
nonlinear verification suite labels moments, moment reactions and work/energy
residuals `N-m`, although its fixtures measure length in millimetres (`D-75`),
so these quantities are N·mm. The owner was told where the labels live (the
benchmark code, the governed DEC-046 records, the hand-calc notes and the
public case pages built from them; not the solver, the desktop app or the
product preview, which solve in SI metres) and was offered a fix. The owner's
reply, verbatim:

```text
yes fix it
```

## Interpretation

The owner directed the fix without choosing among written options. Following
the `D-75` principle (state quantities in their true unit, keep the enforced
envelope), the fix is a relabel: every numeric value and limit is already a
consistent N·mm value, so only the unit label changes. Rescaling the values by
1/1000 to keep an `N-m` label was not chosen, because it would mix length units
inside a fixture set declared to have no unit conversions.

## Adopted bounded effect

Within the nonlinear benchmark suite only, `N-m` becomes `N-mm`, with no
numeric change:

1. `validation/benchmarks/nonlinear/src/lib.rs`: the fixture unit basis
   (`rotational_reaction_unit`), the DEC-046 moment-reaction-delta,
   free-DOF moment residual, free-DOF work residual and general-energy residual
   units, and the rotational fixture observations.
2. The ten `validation/benchmarks/nonlinear/*.dec046.json` records that carry a
   moment, work or energy unit. Record ids, status, scope and all limits are
   unchanged (for example the multi-support moment-reaction delta limit stays
   `3.0`, now `N-mm`).
3. `validation/hand_calcs/nonlinear/*.md`, `validation/benchmarks/nonlinear/README.md`
   and `tests/test_nonlinear_support_regression.py`.

## Boundary

The solver, the desktop app, the product preview and its DEC-046 records, the
mechanics and stress suites (which are in metres) and the release-scope record
are unchanged. No limit, tolerance, lifecycle, release, professional or
code-compliance position changes. Historical records keep their words.
