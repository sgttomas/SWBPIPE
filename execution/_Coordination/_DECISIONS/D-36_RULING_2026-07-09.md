## Ruling

**CAND-4 is adopted as in-scope workflow-physics work**, three separable
items, each its own bounded P3 tranche under
`plans/PLAN_2026-07-09_physical_model_mechanics.md`:

1. **Modulus basis per load case.** A load case may name which user-entered
   temperature-dependent material property values (E, α) it solves with, and
   expansion-range combinations record their modulus basis explicitly —
   giving the existing `schemas/material.schema.yaml` temperature-dependent
   slots solve semantics. All values remain user-entered; the schema's open
   `temperature_interpolation_policy` question is resolved inside this
   tranche or minted as its own `D-XX` if optioned.
2. **Static-equivalent occasional-load generation.** Seismic: user-entered
   g-factors per global axis generate distributed loads from the model's own
   computed mass distribution. Wind: user-entered pressure/shape parameters
   project onto exposed diameter per user-marked spans. Pure mechanics from
   user inputs — no code-content values, no defaults, blocking diagnostics
   on missing inputs (PRD §6.2).
3. **Mill-tolerance slot.** A user-entered mill-tolerance dimension in
   `schemas/section.schema.yaml` consumed by the effective-wall calculation
   in `core/section_properties/` alongside corrosion allowance, with
   provenance.
