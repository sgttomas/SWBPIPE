## Instrument: an owner ruling, not a scope-change amendment

This ruling departs from the proposal on one point. The proposal (§4,
"Instrument") recommended applying the pick as a scope-neutral amendment,
SCA-012. `D-77` is recorded as an owner ruling instead, for two reasons:

- **No Piping or Root instrument requires scope-change for this act.** The
  governed scope-change process is mandatory for changes to an `ISSUED`
  deliverable (`docs/TYPES.md` §9; Root `docs/SPEC.md` §3.2–§3.4), and
  retiring, merging, splitting or creating deliverables routes to
  `scope-change` (`docs/RECONCILIATION_PROFILE.md` §8). This act reallocates
  the Package home of five scope items only. It creates, retires, merges,
  splits or remaps no Deliverable. It changes no file of an `ISSUED`
  deliverable: the only one is DEL-01-01, and every path this change touches
  was checked against its deliverable's `_STATUS.md` (99 `IN_PROGRESS`, 1
  `OPEN`, none `ISSUED`). Otherwise the human project authority rules scope and
  allocation, and the ruling binds when recorded in the proper surface
  (`docs/AGENTIC_DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md`, Authority and Role maps).
- **The owner's answers selected homes, a validator rule and a §6 label, not
  an instrument.** Root D-GOV-48's Adoption section leaves the adoption to the
  loop: "A project loop adopts this change by its own ruling." Piping records
  such rulings as a `D-XX` row with a `DEC` entry, as `D-74`/`DEC-112` did when
  it edited ledger rows and §9 cells directly without an SCA.

So `D-77` takes no SCA number, writes no checkpoint snapshot and does not move
`execution/_ScopeChange/_LATEST.md`, which still names SCA-011. Revision 0.14
is the first `SOFTWARE_DECOMP.md` revision not recorded by an SCA.
