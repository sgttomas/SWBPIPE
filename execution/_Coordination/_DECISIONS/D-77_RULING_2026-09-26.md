## Why no scope-change amendment

No Piping or Root rule requires the governed scope-change process for this
act:

- the process is mandatory for changes to `ISSUED` deliverables (`docs/TYPES.md`
  §9; Root `docs/SPEC.md` §3.3). The only `ISSUED` deliverable is DEL-01-01,
  which this ruling does not touch. All 15 refreshed deliverables are
  `IN_PROGRESS`;
- retiring, merging, splitting or creating deliverables routes to
  `scope-change` (`docs/RECONCILIATION_PROFILE.md` §8). None happens here;
- otherwise the human project authority rules scope and allocation, and the
  ruling binds when recorded in the proper surface
  (`docs/AGENTIC_DEVELOPMENT_WORKFLOW.md`, Authority and Role maps). Piping's
  practice records such rulings as `D-XX` rows with a `DEC` entry, as `D-43`
  did for the scope-neutral SCA-006 decomposition edit and `D-74`/`DEC-112`
  did for ledger and §9 edits.

So `D-77` is recorded as an owner-ruled allocation. It takes no `SCA` number,
writes no checkpoint snapshot and does not move `execution/_ScopeChange/_LATEST.md`,
which still names SCA-011.
