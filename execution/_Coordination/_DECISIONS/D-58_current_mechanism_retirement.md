## Proposed ruling

Adopt all of the following as one bounded current-effect disposition:

1. `D-30` and `D-31` remain valid historical owner acts concerning the
   App-era `D-APP-48` O-A SHA-pinned pull mechanism as it existed on
   2026-07-04. Their records, the historical `DEC-041` text, and the historical
   `DEC-063` text are not rewritten.
2. Retire current reliance on the claim that the App-era `D-APP-48` / `D-30`
   synchronized-consumption mechanism is a working or current mechanism for
   Piping.
3. Consequently, the `D-31` declaration that the `DEC-041` no-manual-toil
   automation condition is met is no longer current by virtue of that
   mechanism. The mechanism by which the automation condition could be met is
   unresolved pending a later exact owner-accepted mechanism.
4. Piping remains outside the current Root-runtime client set and outside the
   current App-harness source-client set. This ruling creates no Root-runtime,
   App-harness, package, sidecar, daemon, API, or compatibility obligation for
   Piping.
5. Piping PRD requirement R7 and its agent-assisted product outcomes remain
   unchanged. Retiring the mechanism does not retire the product intention.
   Work that would rely on a live embedded-agent mechanism remains gated until
   a later accepted mechanism and any required Piping scope are present.
6. No Root successor contract is adopted, inferred, or accepted by this
   ruling. No future Piping client status is decided.
7. Current-effect reconciliation belongs in a forward Piping decomposition
   decision row through `SCOPE_CHANGE`. The forward row must cite this ruling
   and must reconcile the current-effect reading of `DEC-063` without editing
   or erasing `DEC-041`, `DEC-063`, `D-30`, or `D-31`.
