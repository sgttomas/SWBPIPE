## Ruling

**CAND-3 is adopted as ordinary in-scope mechanics-repair work:**

1. **Complementarity test switches with support state.** Released
   one-way/lift-off supports classify on penetration/displacement into the
   support; engaged supports (including closed gaps) classify on reaction
   sign — replacing the reaction-only / displacement-only tests of
   `core/solver/nonlinear_supports/src/lib.rs` (~543–565) so re-engagement
   and lift-off transitions cannot be missed and results do not depend on
   seeded initial states.
2. **Bounded Coulomb sliding force.** A support classified Sliding applies
   the bounded `±μN` tangential force opposing motion (replacing full DOF
   release in `core/solver/nonlinear_integration/src/lib.rs` ~601–606), with
   the normal reaction taken from the current iterate rather than solely
   caller-supplied.
3. **Fixture extension.** The nonlinear hand-calc suite gains transition
   cases (re-engagement of a lifted one-way support; lift-off of a closed
   gap; sliding with bounded friction force), with thresholds entering under
   the `DEC-046` class-tiered convergence policy, tighten-only per
   `DEC-024`/`DEC-026`.
