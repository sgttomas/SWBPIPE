- `reconciliation` routes only a single-deliverable lifecycle transition to the `review` workflow; other review acts go to a bounded independent TASK review or to the owner or engineering holder.
- Activation must merge before any discovery dispatch; independent review of the activation diff before merge is permitted and recorded.

- A recovery and continuation section defines append-only run state, idempotent briefs, no promotion of interrupted returns to PASS, and a `BACKCHECK/<id>/` successor derivative with its own multiset proof.
- Auxiliary populations are accounted separately, coverage checkers show negative controls, R0–R2 carry the practices from both R6 lessons, and the R5 fence covers project governing corpora.
- Remaining-based wording becomes unfulfilled obligations (legacy Remaining only where pinned); the representation-migration profile moves unchanged to an on-demand resource.
- `bounded-reconciliation` cites the Concordance §3.1 claim-granularity tests, returns edits to the owning path for CHECKING, ISSUED or frozen-run deliverables, and distinguishes navigation pointers from authority pins.
- Catalog display names change: `reconciliation` is "Run corpus concordance program" and `review` is "Lifecycle transition review".
