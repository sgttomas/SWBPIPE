## Scenario/storyboard

The engineer creates a short straight pipe, restrains its start and applies a force at its end. In a real project every engineering value is supplied deliberately by that engineer or lawful input. For automated/native smoke, freeze a public invented input card before execution: use the existing invented_preview_model.json material quantities/provenance and explicitly copied geometry/section quantities as synthetic test inputs, not product defaults or engineering acceptance. Choose one concentrated-force member only, omit pressure/thermal/distributed terms and combinations deliberately, record the exact projection and source hash. Do not reuse its precomputed sample result as native solve evidence. A fresh invented one-case payload/input-card freeze and successful common-engine validation are witness prerequisites; this planning run does not claim they already exist. IDs may be new unique test IDs; no user-created model is committed.
