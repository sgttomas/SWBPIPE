# DEL-01-03: contributor workflow files

`CONTRIBUTING.md` and `governance/CONTRIBUTOR_CERTIFICATION_TEMPLATE.md`, the
contributor workflow and certification template this deliverable declares among
its outputs, are not in the repository. The files produced earlier were
withdrawn on 2026-11-10 on the owner's ruling:

> we don't have contributor rules defined properly so the deliverables aren't
> wrong but the way it was executed was because it didn't consult me.

The commitments in [ScopeOfWork.md](../ScopeOfWork.md) stand unchanged. What
was withdrawn is the files, because they were written without consulting the
owner. Statements in ScopeOfWork.md that the files exist describe the withdrawn
files, not the current standing. The contribution review checklist this
workflow routes to was withdrawn with them
([DEL-01-02](../../DEL-01-02/Work/contribution-review-checklist.md)).

The output remains to be produced. Its input is the owner's contributor rules,
which are the owner's to supply; nothing is drafted until they are.
